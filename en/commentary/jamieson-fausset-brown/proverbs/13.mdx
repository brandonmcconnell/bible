## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 13:1-25)</p>
<p>(Compare Pro 6:1-5; Pro 10:1, Pro 10:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v2)</div>
<p>shall eat--that is, obtain (Pro 12:14).</p>
<p>transgressors--as in Pro 2:22.</p>
<p>violence--or, "mischief" to themselves.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v3)</div>
<p>He . . . mouth . . . life--because evil speeches may provoke violence from others.</p>
<p>he that openeth wide his lips shall have destruction--On last clause, compare Pro 10:14.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v4)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 12:11, Pro 12:27).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v5)</div>
<p>loathsome . . . shame--better, causeth shame and reproach (compare Pro 19:26), by slander, &amp;c., which the righteous hates.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v6)</div>
<p>A sentiment of frequent recurrence, that piety benefits and sin injures.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v7)</div>
<p>In opposite ways men act hypocritically for gain of honor or wealth.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v8)</div>
<p>Riches save some from punishment, while others suffer because they will not heed the rebuke of sloth, which makes and keeps them poor.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v9)</div>
<p>light . . . lamp--prosperity; the first, the greater, and it</p>
<p>rejoiceth--burns brightly, or continues, while the other, at best small, soon fails.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v10)</div>
<p>The obstinacy which attends self-conceit, produces contention, which the well-advised, thus evincing modesty, avoid.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v11)</div>
<p>by vanity--or, "nothingness," that is, which is vain or useless to the public (as card playing or similar vices).</p>
<p>gathereth . . . labour--(Compare Margin), little by little, laboriously.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v12)</div>
<p>desire cometh--is realized.</p>
<p>a tree of life--or, "cause of happiness."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v13)</div>
<p>the word--that is, of advice, or, instruction (compare Pro 10:27; Pro 11:31).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v14)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:11).</p>
<p>fountain--or, "source of life."</p>
<p>to depart--(compare Pro 1:2-4), or, "for departing," &amp;c., and so gives life.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v15)</div>
<p>Right perception and action secure good will, while evil ways are difficult as a stony road. The wicked left of God find punishment of sin in sinning.</p>
<p>hard--or, "harsh" (compare Hebrew: Deu 21:4; Jer 5:15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v16)</div>
<p>dealeth--acts with foresight.</p>
<p>a fool . . . folly--for want of caution.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v17)</div>
<p>A wicked--or, "unfaithful"</p>
<p>messenger falleth into mischief--or, "by mischief," or "evil," and so his errand fails. Contrasted is the character of the faithful, whose faithfulness benefits others.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v18)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:17; Pro 12:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v19)</div>
<p>Self-denial, which fools will not endure, is essential to success.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v20)</div>
<p>The benefits of good and evil of bad society are contrasted.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v21)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 11:31).</p>
<p>good . . . repaid--or, "He (God) will repay good."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v22)</div>
<p>wealth . . . just--While good men's estates remain in their families, God so orders that the gains of sinners enure to the just (compare Pro 28:8; Psa 37:18, Psa 37:22, Psa 37:26, &amp;c.).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v23)</div>
<p>The laboring poor prosper more than those who injudiciously or wickedly strive, by fraud and violence, to supersede the necessity of lawful labor.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v24)</div>
<p>spareth--or, "withholds."</p>
<p>rod--of correction.</p>
<p>hateth--or, acts as if he hated him (compare Pro 3:12; Pro 8:36).</p>
<p>chasteneth . . . betimes--or, "diligently seeks for him all useful discipline."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 13:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/13#v25)</div>
<p>The comparative temporal prosperity of the righteous and wicked, rather than contentment and discontent, is noted.</p>
<p>Next: Proverbs Chapter 14</p>
</div>

</div>
