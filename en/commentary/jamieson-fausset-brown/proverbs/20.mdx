## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 20:1-30)</p>
<p>mocker--scorner. Such men are made by wine.</p>
<p>strong drink--made by spicing wine (compare Isa 5:11, Isa 5:22); and it may include wine.</p>
<p>raging--or boisterous as a drunkard.</p>
<p>deceived--literally, "erring," or reeling.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v2)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 19:12). Men who resist authority injure themselves (Rom 13:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v3)</div>
<p>to cease from strife--or, better, "to dwell from or without strife," denoting the habit of life.</p>
<p>fool . . . meddling-- (Pro 17:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v4)</div>
<p>shall . . . beg--literally, "ask" (in this sense, Psa 109:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v5)</div>
<p>Counsel . . . water--that is, deeply hidden (Pro 18:4; Psa 13:2). The wise can discern well.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v6)</div>
<p>Boasters are unreliable.</p>
<p>goodness--or, "kind disposition."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v7)</div>
<p>The conduct of good men proclaims their sound principles. God's covenant and their good example secure blessing to their children (Pro 4:26; Psa 112:1-2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v8)</div>
<p>As in Pro 14:35; Pro 16:10, Pro 16:15, this is the character of a good king, not of all kings.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v9)</div>
<p>The interrogation in the affirmative strengthens the implied negation (compare Job 15:14; Ecc 7:20).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v10)</div>
<p>Various measures, implying that some are wrong (compare Pro 11:1; Pro 16:11).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v11)</div>
<p>The conduct of children even is the best test of principle (compare Mat 7:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v12)</div>
<p>Hence, of course, God will know all you do (Psa 94:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v13)</div>
<p>Activity and diligence contrasted with sloth (Pro 6:9; Pro 10:11).</p>
<p>lest . . . poverty--literally, "be deprived of inheritance."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v14)</div>
<p>when . . . his way--implying that he goes about boasting of his bargains.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v15)</div>
<p>The contrast denotes the greater value of knowledge (compare Pro 3:14-16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v16)</div>
<p>Take his garment--implies severe exaction, justified by the surety's rashness.</p>
<p>a strange woman--by some readings "strangers," but the former here, and in Pro 27:13, is allowable, and strengthens the sense. The debauchee is less reliable than the merely careless.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v17)</div>
<p>Bread . . . sweet--either as unlawfully (Pro 9:17) or easily obtained.</p>
<p>mouth . . . gravel--well expresses the pain and grief given at last.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v18)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 15:22). Be careful and considerate in important plans.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v19)</div>
<p>Those who love to tell news will hardly keep secrets.</p>
<p>flattereth . . . lips--(compare Margin; Pro 1:10).</p>
<p>meddle . . . him--literally, "join," or "associate with."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v20)</div>
<p>his lamp--(Compare Pro 13:9; Pro 24:20).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v21)</div>
<p>gotten hastily--contrary to God's providence (Pro 28:20), implying its unjust or easy attainment; hence the man is punished, or spends freely what he got easily (compare Pro 20:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v22)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 27:14; Rom 12:17-19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v23)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 20:10; Pro 11:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v24)</div>
<p>Man's goings--literally, "Stately steppings of a strong man."</p>
<p>a man--any common man.</p>
<p>understand--or, "perceive."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v25)</div>
<p>devoureth . . . holy--or, better, "who rashly speaks promises," or "devotes what is holy," consecrating any thing. This suits better the last clause, which expresses a similar view of the results of rashly vowing.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v26)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 20:8).</p>
<p>bringeth . . . over them--The wheel was used for threshing grain. The figure denotes severity (compare Amo 1:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v27)</div>
<p>The spirit . . . Lord--Men's minds are God's gifts, and thus able to search one another (compare Pro 20:5; Pro 18:8, Pro 18:17; Co1 2:11).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v28)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 3:3; Pro 16:6, Pro 16:12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v29" data-v="29">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:29](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v29)</div>
<p>The glory of young men . . . the beauty of old men--Each age has its peculiar excellence (Pro 16:31).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v30" data-v="30">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 20:30](/en/kjv/proverbs/20#v30)</div>
<p>blueness--literally, "joining," the process of uniting the edges of a wound throws off purulent matter.</p>
<p>stripes . . . belly--So punishment provides healing of soul (Pro 18:8), by deterring from evil courses.</p>

</div>
