## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 16:1-33)</p>
<p>preparations--schemes.</p>
<p>in man--or literally, "to man," belonging, or pertaining to him.</p>
<p>the answer . . . Lord--The efficient ordering is from God: "Man proposes; God disposes."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v2)</div>
<p>clean--or, "faultless."</p>
<p>weigheth--or, "tries," "judges," implying that they are faulty (Pro 21:2; Pro 24:12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v3)</div>
<p>(Compare Margin). Rely on God for success to your lawful purposes.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v4)</div>
<p>for himself--"for its answer," or "purpose," that is, according to God's plan; the wicked are for the day of evil (Psa 49:5; Jer 17:18); sinning and suffering answer to each other, are indissolubly united.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v5)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 3:32).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v6)</div>
<p>By mercy and truth--that is, God's (Psa 85:10); He effects the atonement, or covering of sin; and the principles of true piety incline men to depart from evil; or, "mercy" and "truth" may be man's, indicative of the gracious tempers which work instrumentally in procuring pardon.</p>
<p>purged--expiated (as in Lev 16:33; Isa 27:9, Hebrew).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v7)</div>
<p>Persecutions, of course, excepted.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v8)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 15:6, Pro 15:16-17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v9)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 16:3).</p>
<p>directeth--establisheth.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v10)</div>
<p>The last clause depends on the first, expressing the importance of equity in decisions, so authoritative.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v11)</div>
<p>are the Lord's . . . his work--that is, what He has ordered, and hence should be observed by men.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v12)</div>
<p>Rulers are rightly expected, by their position, to hate evil; for their power is sustained by righteousness.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v13)</div>
<p>A specification of the general sentiment of Pro 16:12.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v14)</div>
<p>This wrath, so terrible and certain, like messengers of death (Kg1 2:25), can be appeased by the wise.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v15)</div>
<p>light of . . . countenance--favor (Psa 4:6).</p>
<p>life--preserves it, or gives blessings which make it valuable.</p>
<p>the latter rain--fell just before harvest and matured the crop; hence specially valuable (Deu 11:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v16)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 3:16; Pro 4:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v17)</div>
<p>The highway--A common, plain road represents the habitual course of the righteous in departing from evil.</p>
<p>keepeth--observes.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v18)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 15:33). Haughtiness and pride imply self-confidence which produces carelessness, and hence</p>
<p>a fall--literally, "sliding."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v19)</div>
<p>divide the spoil--that is, conquer. Avoid the society of the proud (Jam 4:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v20)</div>
<p>handleth a matter--wisely considers "the word," that is, of God (compare Pro 13:13).</p>
<p>trusteth--(Compare Psa 2:12; Psa 118:8-9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v21)</div>
<p>wise in heart--who rightly consider duty.</p>
<p>sweetness of the lips--eloquent discourse, persuades and instructs others.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v22)</div>
<p>Understanding--or, "discretion," is a constant source of blessing (Pro 13:14), benefiting others; but fools' best efforts are folly.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v23)</div>
<p>The heart is the source of wisdom flowing from the mouth.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v24)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 15:26). Gentle, kind words, by soothing the mind, give the body health.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v25)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 14:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v26)</div>
<p>Diligence is a duty due to one's self, for his wants require labor.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v27)</div>
<p>ungodly man--(Compare Pro 6:12).</p>
<p>diggeth up evil--labors for it.</p>
<p>in his lips . . . fire--His words are calumniating (Jam 3:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v28)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 6:14; Pro 10:31).</p>
<p>whisperer--prater, talebearer (Pro 18:8; Pro 26:20).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v29" data-v="29">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:29](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v29)</div>
<p>violent man--or, "man of mischief" (Pro 3:31).</p>
<p>enticeth-- (Pro 1:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v30" data-v="30">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:30](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v30)</div>
<p>He shutteth his eyes--denoting deep thought (Psa 64:6).</p>
<p>moving his lips--or, "biting his lips"--a determined purpose (Pro 6:13).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v31" data-v="31">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:31](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v31)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 20:29).</p>
<p>if--or, which may be supplied properly, or without it the sense is as in Pro 3:16; Pro 4:10, that piety is blessed with long life.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v32" data-v="32">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:32](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v32)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 14:29).</p>
<p>taketh a city--that is, by fighting.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v33" data-v="33">
