## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 15:1-33)</p>
<p>soft--tender or gentle.</p>
<p>turneth . . . wrath--from any one.</p>
<p>stir up--as a smouldering fire is excited.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v2)</div>
<p>useth . . . aright--commends knowledge by its proper use.</p>
<p>poureth out--utters abundantly (Pro 12:23), and so disgusts others.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v3)</div>
<p>beholding--watching (compare Pro 5:21; Psa 66:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v4)</div>
<p>A wholesome tongue--(Compare Margin), pacifying and soothing language.</p>
<p>tree of life-- (Pro 3:18; Pro 11:30).</p>
<p>perverseness therein--cross, ill-natured language.</p>
<p>breach . . . spirit--(compare Isa 65:14, Hebrew), grieves, instead of appeasing.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v5)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 4:1; Pro 10:17; Pro. 13:1-18).</p>
<p>is prudent--acts discreetly.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v6)</div>
<p>treasure--implying utility.</p>
<p>trouble--vexation and affliction.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v7)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:20-21).</p>
<p>heart . . . not so--not right, or vain.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v8)</div>
<p>The sacrifice &#91;and&#93; prayer--are acts of worship.</p>
<p>way . . . followeth . . . righteousness--denote conduct. God's regard for the worship and deeds of the righteous and wicked respectively, so stated in Psa 50:17; Isa 1:11.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v10)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:17).</p>
<p>the way--that in which God would have him to go (Pro 2:13; Psa 119:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v11)</div>
<p>Hell-- (Psa 16:10).</p>
<p>destruction--or, "Abaddon," the place of the destroyer. All the unseen world is open to God, much more men's hearts.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v12)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 9:8).</p>
<p>go unto the wise--to be instructed.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v13)</div>
<p>maketh . . . countenance--or, "benefits the countenance."</p>
<p>spirit is broken--and so the countenance is sad.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v14)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:21-22). The wise grow wiser, the fools more foolish (Pro 9:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v15)</div>
<p>The state of the heart governs the outward condition.</p>
<p>evil--sad, contrasted with the cheerfulness of a feast.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v16)</div>
<p>trouble--agitation, implying the anxieties and perplexities attending wealth held by worldlings (Pro 16:18; Ti1 6:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v17)</div>
<p>dinner--or, "allowance" (Kg2 25:30) --</p>
<p>of herbs--and that the plainest.</p>
<p>and hatred--(compare Pro 10:12, Pro 10:18).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v18)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 14:29; Pro 16:32).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v19)</div>
<p>The difficulties of the slothful result from want of energy; the righteous find a</p>
<p>plain &#91;and open&#93; way--literally, "a highway," by diligence (Sa1 10:7; Psa 1:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v20)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v21)</div>
<p>walketh uprightly--and so finds his joy (Pro 3:6; Pro 10:23).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v22)</div>
<p>Without counsel--or, "deliberation," implying a wise deference to the opinions of the wise and good, contrasted with rashness.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v23)</div>
<p>Good advice blesses the giver and receiver.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v24)</div>
<p>(Compare Col 3:2). Holy purposes prevent sinning, and so its evils.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v25)</div>
<p>The most desolate who have God's aid have more permanent good than the self-reliant sinner (Pro 2:22; Pro 12:7).</p>
<p>border--or, "boundary for possessions" (Psa 78:54).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v26)</div>
<p>are pleasant words--that is, pleasing to God (Pro 8:8-9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v27)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 11:17). Avarice brings trouble to him and his.</p>
<p>hateth gifts--or, "bribes" (Exo 23:8; Psa 15:5), and is not avaricious.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v28)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 15:14; Pro 10:11). Caution is the fruit of wisdom; rashness of folly.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v29" data-v="29">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:29](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v29)</div>
<p>far . . . wicked--in His love and favor (Psa 22:11; Psa 119:155).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v30" data-v="30">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:30](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v30)</div>
<p>light of the eyes-- (Pro 13:9). What gives light rejoiceth the heart, by relieving from anxiety as to our course; so</p>
<p>good report--or, "doctrine" (Isa 28:9; Isa 53:1),</p>
<p>maketh . . . fat--or, "gives prosperity" (Pro 3:13-17; Pro 9:11). The last clause is illustrated by the first.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v31" data-v="31">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:31](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v31)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:17).</p>
<p>reproof of life--which leads to life.</p>
<p>abideth . . . wise--is numbered among them.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v32" data-v="32">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:32](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v32)</div>
<p>refuseth--or, "neglects," "passes by" (Pro 1:25; Pro 4:15).</p>
<p>despiseth . . . soul--so acts as if esteeming its interests of no value.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v33" data-v="33">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:33](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v33)</div>
<p>The fear . . . wisdom--Wisdom instructs in true piety.</p>
<p>before . . . humility--(compare Luk 24:26; Pe1 1:11); opposite (compare Pro 16:18).</p>
<p>Next: Proverbs Chapter 16</p>
</div>
