## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>Men are invited to seek wisdom because it teaches those principles by which they may obtain God's guidance and avoid the society and influence of the wicked, whose pernicious courses are described. (Pro. 2:1-22)</p>
<p>Diligence in hearing and praying for instruction must be used to secure the great principle of godliness, the fear of God.</p>
<p>hide . . . with thee--lay up in store (compare Pro 7:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v2)</div>
<p>Listen attentively and reflect seriously (Pro 1:24; Psa 130:2).</p>
<p>understanding--right perception of truth.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v3)</div>
<p>Yea, if--literally, "When if," that is, in such a case.</p>
<p>knowledge--or, "discrimination."</p>
<p>understanding--as in Pro 2:2.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v4)</div>
<p>There must be earnest prayer and effort.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v5)</div>
<p>understand--or, "perceive intelligently."</p>
<p>find--obtain.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v6)</div>
<p>For--God is ready (Jam 1:5; Jam 4:8).</p>
<p>out of his mouth--by revelation from Him.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v7)</div>
<p>sound wisdom--literally, "substance," opposed to what is fictitious. According to the context, this may be assistance, as here corresponding with</p>
<p>buckler--or safety, or wisdom, which procures it (compare Pro 3:21; Pro 8:14; Pro 18:1; Job 6:13; Job 12:13).</p>
<p>layeth up--provides, ever ready.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v8)</div>
<p>keepeth . . . way--God defends the right way, and those in it.</p>
<p>saints--objects of favor (compare Psa 4:3, &amp;c.). He guides and guards them.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v9)</div>
<p>Then--emphatic, in such a case.</p>
<p>righteousness . . . path--all parts of duty to God and man.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v10)</div>
<p>Idea of Pro 2:9, amplified; on terms, compare Pro 2:2 and Pro 2:4.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v12)</div>
<p>To deliver--as from great danger (Pro 6:5).</p>
<p>way . . . man-- (Psa 1:1).</p>
<p>froward things--perversity (Pro 6:14; Pro 23:23), what is opposed to truth.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v13)</div>
<p>paths of uprightness--or, "plainness."</p>
<p>walk--habitually act;</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v14)</div>
<p>and that with pleasure, in ignorance of good and pursuit of evil.</p>
<p>frowardness--Not only their own perversity, but that of others is their delight. They love most the worst things.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v15)</div>
<p>crooked--tortuous, unprincipled.</p>
<p>froward--literally, (they) are going back, not only aside from right, but opposite to it.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v16)</div>
<p>Deliverance from another danger.</p>
<p>the strange woman--This term is often used for harlot, or loose woman (Jdg 11:1-2), married (Pro 7:5, Pro 7:19) or not (Kg1 11:1), so called, because such were, perhaps at first, foreigners, though "strange" may also denote whatever is opposed to right or proper, as "strange fire" (Num 3:4); "strange incense" (Exo 30:9).</p>
<p>flattereth--literally, "smooths."</p>
<p>her words-- (Psa 5:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v17)</div>
<p>guide . . . youth--lawful husband (Jer 3:4).</p>
<p>covenant . . . God--of marriage made in God's name.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v18)</div>
<p>inclineth--sinks down (compare Num 13:31).</p>
<p>the dead--or shades of the departed (Psa 88:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v19)</div>
<p>that is, such as remain impenitent (compare Ecc 7:26).</p>
<p>paths of life-- (Psa 16:11), opposed to paths unto the dead.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v20)</div>
<p>That . . . way of good--that is, Such is the object of these warnings.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v21)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 37:3, Psa 37:9, Psa 37:22, Psa 37:27).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 2:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/2#v22)</div>
<p>transgressors--or impious rebels (compare Jer 9:2).</p>
<p>rooted out--utterly destroyed, as trees plucked up by the roots.</p>

</div>
