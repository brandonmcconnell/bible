## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 11:1-31)</p>
<p>(Compare Margin). The Hebrews used stones for weights.</p>
<p>just--complete in measure.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v2)</div>
<p>Self-conceit is unteachable; the humble grow wise (compare Pro 16:18; Pro 18:12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v3)</div>
<p>guide--to lead, as a shepherd (Pro 6:7; Psa 78:52).</p>
<p>perverseness--ill-nature.</p>
<p>destroy--with violence.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v4)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:2).</p>
<p>wrath--that is, of God.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v5)</div>
<p>direct--or, "make plain"; wicked ways are not plain (Pro 13:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v6)</div>
<p>deliver them--that is, from evil, which the wicked suffer by their own doings (Pro 5:22; Psa 9:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v7)</div>
<p>expectation . . . perish--for death cuts short all his plans (Luk 16:25).</p>
<p>hope of unjust--better, "hope of wealth," or "power" (compare Isa 40:29, Hebrew). This gives an advance on the sentiment of the first clause. Even hopes of gain die with him.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v8)</div>
<p>Perhaps the trouble prepared by the wicked, and which he inherits (compare Pro 11:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v9)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 35:16; Dan 11:32). The just is saved by superior discernment.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v10)</div>
<p>The last may be a reason for the first. Together, they set forth the relative moral worth of good and bad men.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v11)</div>
<p>By the blessing--implying active benevolence.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v12)</div>
<p>despiseth--or, "reviles," a course contrasted with the prudent silence of the wise.</p>
<p>holdeth his peace--as if neither hearing nor telling.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v13)</div>
<p>tale-bearer--(Compare Margin), one trading as a peddler in scandal, whose propensity to talk leads him to betray confidence.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v14)</div>
<p>counsel--the art of governing (Pro 1:5).</p>
<p>counsellors--literally, "one giving counsel"; the participle used as a collective.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v15)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 6:1).</p>
<p>suretiship--(Compare Margin), the actors put for the action, which may be lawfully hated.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v16)</div>
<p>retaineth--or literally, "lay hold of as a support." Honor is to a feeble woman thus as valuable as riches to men.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v17)</div>
<p>merciful--kind to others; opposed to cruel. Such benefit themselves by doing good to others (compare Pro 24:5), while the cruel injure themselves as well as others.</p>
<p>flesh--that is, his body, by penuriousness (Col 2:23).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v18)</div>
<p>a deceitful work--or, "wages," which fail to satisfy, or flee away (Pro 10:2; Pro 23:5).</p>
<p>sure reward--or, "gain," as from trading (Hos 10:12; Gal 6:8-9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v19)</div>
<p>Inference from Pro 11:18 (compare Pro 11:5-6; Pro 10:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v20)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 11:5).</p>
<p>froward--as in Pro 2:15, opposed to the simplicity and purity of the upright.</p>
<p>in their way--or, "conduct."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v21)</div>
<p>The combined power of the wicked cannot free them from just punishment, while the unaided children of the righteous find deliverance by reason of their pious relationship (Psa 37:25-26).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v22)</div>
<p>Jewels were often suspended from the nose (Gen 24:47; Isa 3:21). Thus adorned, a hog disgusts less than a fair and indiscreet woman.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v23)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:28).</p>
<p>wrath--is that of God.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v24)</div>
<p>The scope of the whole is a comment on Pro 11:23. Thus liberality, by God's blessing, secures increase, while penuriousness, instead of expected gain, procures poverty.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v25)</div>
<p>liberal soul--(Compare Margin).</p>
<p>made fat--prospers (Pro 28:25; Deu 32:15; Luk 6:38).</p>
<p>watereth . . . watered--a common figure for blessing.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v26)</div>
<p>Another example of the truth of Pro 11:23; the miser loses reputation, though he saves corn.</p>
<p>selleth it--that is, at a fair price.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v27)</div>
<p>good &#91;and&#93; mischief--that is, of others.</p>
<p>procureth . . . seeketh--implying success.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v28)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:15; Psa 49:6; Ti1 6:17).</p>
<p>righteous . . . branch-- (Psa 1:3; Jer 17:8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v29" data-v="29">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:29](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v29)</div>
<p>troubleth--as Pro 15:27 explains, by greediness for gain (compare Pro 11:17).</p>
<p>inherit . . . wind--Even successful, his gains are of no real value. So the fool, thus acting, either comes to poverty, or heaps up for others.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v30" data-v="30">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:30](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v30)</div>
<p>a tree of life--Blessings to others proceed from the works of the righteous (Pro 3:18).</p>
<p>winneth souls--(Compare Margin) to do them good as opposed to Pro 6:25; Eze 13:18 (compare Luk 5:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v31" data-v="31">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 11:31](/en/kjv/proverbs/11#v31)</div>
<p>Behold--Thus calling attention to the illustrations (compare Pro 11:23), the sentiment of which is confirmed even in time, not excluding future rewards and punishments.</p>
<p>Next: Proverbs Chapter 12</p>
</div>

</div>
