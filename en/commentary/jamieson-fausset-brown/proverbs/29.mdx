## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 29:1-27)</p>
<p>hardeneth . . . neck--obstinately refuses counsel (Kg2 17:14; Neh 9:16).</p>
<p>destroyed--literally, "shivered" or "utterly broken to pieces."</p>
<p>without remedy--literally, "without healing" or repairing.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v2)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 11:10; Pro 28:28).</p>
<p>in authority--(Compare Margin), increased in power.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v3)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 4:6-7; Pro 10:1, &amp;c.).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v4)</div>
<p>by judgment--that is, righteous decisions, opposed to those procured by gifts (compare Pro 28:21), by which good government is perverted.</p>
<p>land--for nation.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v5)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 26:28).</p>
<p>spreadeth . . . feet--By misleading him as to his real character, the flatterer brings him to evil, prepared by himself or others.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v6)</div>
<p>In--or, "By"</p>
<p>the transgression--he is brought into difficulty (Pro 12:13), but the righteous go on prospering, and so sing or rejoice.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v7)</div>
<p>considereth--literally, "knows," as Psa 1:6.</p>
<p>the cause--that is, in courts of justice (compare Pro 29:14). The voluntary neglect of it by the wicked (Pro 28:27) occasions oppression.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v8)</div>
<p>Scornful men--those who contemptuously disregard God's law.</p>
<p>bring--(Compare Margin), kindle strife.</p>
<p>turn away wrath--that is, "abate wrath."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v9)</div>
<p>contendeth--that is, in law.</p>
<p>whether . . . laugh--The fool, whether angry or good-humored, is unsettled; or referring the words to the wise man, the sense is, that all his efforts, severe or gentle, are unavailing to pacify the fool.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v10)</div>
<p>bloodthirsty--(Compare Margin), murderers (Psa 5:6; Psa 26:9).</p>
<p>hate, &amp;c.-- (Pro 1:11; Gen 3:4).</p>
<p>seek . . . soul--that is, to preserve it.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v11)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 12:16; Pro 16:32).</p>
<p>mind--or, "spirit," for anger or any ill passion which the righteous restrain.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v12)</div>
<p>His servants imitate him.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v13)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 22:2).</p>
<p>deceitful man--literally, "man of vexations," an exactor.</p>
<p>the Lord . . . their eyes--sustains their lives (Sa1 14:27; Psa 13:3); that is, both depend on Him, and He will do justice.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v14)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 20:28; Pro 25:5). Such is the character of the King of kings (Psa 72:4, Psa 72:12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v15)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 13:24; Pro 23:13).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v16)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 29:2, Pro 29:12; Psa 12:1-8).</p>
<p>shall see . . . fall--and triumph in it (Psa 37:34-38; Psa 58:10-11).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v17)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 29:3, Pro 29:15; Pro 19:18).</p>
<p>give thee rest--peace and quiet (compare Pro 29:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v18)</div>
<p>no vision--instruction in God's truth, which was by prophets, through visions (Sa1 3:1).</p>
<p>people perish--(Compare Margin), are deprived of moral restraints.</p>
<p>keepeth the law--has, and observes, instruction (Pro 14:11, Pro 14:34; Psa 19:11).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v19)</div>
<p>A servant--who lacks good principle.</p>
<p>corrected--or discovered.</p>
<p>will not answer--that is, will not obey.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v20)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 21:5).</p>
<p>hasty in . . . words?--implying self-conceit (Pro 26:12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v21)</div>
<p>become his son--assume the place and privileges of one.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v22)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 15:18). Such are delighted by discord and violence.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v23)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 16:18; Pro 18:12).</p>
<p>honour . . . spirit--or, "such shall lay hold on honor" (Pro 11:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v24)</div>
<p>hateth . . . soul--(Compare Pro 8:36).</p>
<p>heareth cursing-- (Lev 5:1), risks the punishment, rather than reveal truth.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v25)</div>
<p>The fear . . . snare--involves men in difficulty (compare Pro 29:6).</p>
<p>shall be safe--(Compare Margin; Pro 18:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v26)</div>
<p>(Compare Margin; Psa 27:8). God alone will and can do exact justice.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 29:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/29#v27)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 3:32). On last clause, compare Pro 29:16; Psa 37:12.</p>

</div>
