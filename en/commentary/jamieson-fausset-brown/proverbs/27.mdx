## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 27:1-27)</p>
<p>Do not confide implicitly in your plans (Pro 16:9; Pro 19:21; Jam 4:13-15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v2)</div>
<p>Avoid self-praise.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v3)</div>
<p>heavy--The literal sense of "heavy," applied to material subjects, illustrates its figurative, "grievous," applied to moral.</p>
<p>a fool's wrath--is unreasonable and excessive.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v4)</div>
<p>envy--or, "jealousy" (compare Margin; Pro 6:34), is more unappeasable than the simpler bad passions.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v5)</div>
<p>secret love--not manifested in acts is useless; and even, if its exhibition by rebukes wounds us, such love is preferable to the frequent (compare Margin), and hence deceitful, kisses of an enemy.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v7)</div>
<p>The luxury of wealth confers less happiness than the healthy appetite of labor.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v8)</div>
<p>Such are not only out of place, but out of duty and in danger.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v9)</div>
<p>rejoice the heart--the organ of perceiving what pleases the senses.</p>
<p>sweetness . . . counsel--or, "wise counsel is also pleasing."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v10)</div>
<p>Adhere to tried friends. The ties of blood may be less reliable than those of genuine friendship.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v11)</div>
<p>The wisdom of children both reflects credit on parents and contributes to their aid in difficulties.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v12)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 20:16; Pro 22:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v14)</div>
<p>Excessive zeal in praising raises suspicions of selfishness.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v15)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 19:13).</p>
<p>very . . . day--literally, "a day of showers."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v16)</div>
<p>hideth--or, "restrains" (that is, tries to do it); is as fruitless an effort, as that of holding the wind.</p>
<p>the ointment of his right hand--the organ of power (Psa 17:7; Psa 18:35). His right hand endeavors to repress perfume, but vainly. Some prefer: "His right hand comes on oil," that is, "cannot take hold." Such a woman cannot be tamed.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v17)</div>
<p>a man sharpeneth . . . friend--that is, conversation promotes intelligence, which the face exhibits.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v18)</div>
<p>Diligence secures a reward, even for the humble servant.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v19)</div>
<p>We may see our characters in the developed tempers of others.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v20)</div>
<p>Men's cupidity is as insatiable as the grave.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v21)</div>
<p>Praise tests character.</p>
<p>a man to his praise--according to his praise, as he bears it. Thus vain men seek it, weak men are inflated by it, wise men disregard it, &amp;c.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v22)</div>
<p>The obstinate wickedness of such is incurable by the heaviest inflictions.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v23)</div>
<p>flocks--constituted the staple of wealth. It is only by care and diligence that the most solid possessions can be perpetuated (Pro 23:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v25)</div>
<p>The fact that providential arrangements furnish the means of competence to those who properly use them is another motive to diligence (compare Psa 65:9-13).</p>
<p>The hay appeareth--literally, "Grass appeareth" (Job 40:15; Psa 104:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 27:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/27#v27)</div>
<p>household--literally, "house," the family (Act 16:15; Co1 1:16).</p>
<p>Next: Proverbs Chapter 28</p>
</div>

</div>
