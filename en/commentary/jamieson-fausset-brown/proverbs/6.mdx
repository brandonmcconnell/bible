## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>After admonitions against suretyship and sloth (compare Pro 6:6-8), the character and fate of the wicked generally are set forth, and the writer (Pro. 6:20-35) resumes the warnings against incontinence, pointing out its certain and terrible results. This train of thought seems to intimate the kindred of these vices. (Pro. 6:1-35)</p>
<p>if--The condition extends through both verses.</p>
<p>be surety--art pledged.</p>
<p>stricken . . . hand--bargained (compare Job 17:3).</p>
<p>with a stranger--that is, for a friend (compare Pro 11:15; Pro 17:18).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v3)</div>
<p>come . . . friend--in his power.</p>
<p>humble . . . sure thy friend--urge as a suppliant; that is, induce the friend to provide otherwise for his debt, or secure the surety.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v4)</div>
<p>The danger requires promptness.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v6)</div>
<p>The improvident sluggards usually want sureties. Hence, such are advised to industry by the ant's example.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v9)</div>
<p>Their conduct graphically described;</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v11)</div>
<p>and the fruits of their self-indulgence and indolence presented.</p>
<p>as . . . travelleth--literally, "one who walks backwards and forwards," that is, a highwayman.</p>
<p>armed man--that is, one prepared to destroy.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v12)</div>
<p>A naughty person--literally, "A man of Belial," or of worthlessness, that is, for good, and so depraved, or wicked (compare Sa1 25:25; Sa1 30:22, &amp;c.). Idleness and vice are allied. Though indolent in acts, he actively and habitually (walketh) is ill-natured in speech (Pro 4:24).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v13)</div>
<p>If, for fear of detection, he does not speak, he uses signs to carry on his intrigues. These signs are still so used in the East.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v14)</div>
<p>Frowardness--as in Pro 2:14.</p>
<p>deviseth--literally, "constructs, as an artisan."</p>
<p>mischief--evil to others.</p>
<p>discord--especially litigation. Cunning is the talent of the weak and lazy.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v15)</div>
<p>Suddenness aggravates evil (compare Pro 6:11; Pro 29:1).</p>
<p>calamity--literally, "a crushing weight."</p>
<p>broken--shivered as a potter's vessel; utterly destroyed (Psa 2:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v16)</div>
<p>six . . . seven--a mode of speaking to arrest attention (Pro 30:15, Pro 30:18; Job 5:19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v17)</div>
<p>proud look--literally, "eyes of loftiness" (Psa 131:1). Eyes, tongue, &amp;c., for persons.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v19)</div>
<p>speaketh--literally, "breathes out," habitually speaks (Psa 27:12; Act 9:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v20)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 1:8; Pro 3:3, &amp;c.).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v22)</div>
<p>it--(compare Pro 6:23); denotes the instruction of parents (Pro 6:20), to which all the qualities of a safe guide and guard and ready teacher are ascribed. It prevents the ingress of evil by supplying good thoughts, even in dreams (Pro 3:21-23; Psa 19:9; Pe2 1:19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v23)</div>
<p>reproofs-- (Pro 1:23) the convictions of error produced by instruction.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v24)</div>
<p>A specimen of its benefit. By appreciating truth, men are not affected by lying flattery.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v25)</div>
<p>One of the cautions of this instruction, avoid alluring beauty.</p>
<p>take--or, "ensnare."</p>
<p>eyelids--By painting the lashes, women enhanced beauty.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v26)</div>
<p>The supplied words give a better sense than the old version: "The price of a whore is a piece of bread."</p>
<p>adulteress--(Compare Margin), which the parallel and context (Pro 6:29-35) sustain. Of similar results of this sin, compare Pro 5:9-12.</p>
<p>will hunt--alluding to the snares spread by harlots (compare Pro 7:6-8).</p>
<p>precious life--more valuable than all else.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v27)</div>
<p>The guilt and danger most obvious.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v30" data-v="30">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:30](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v30)</div>
<p>Such a thief is pitied, though heavily punished.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v31" data-v="31">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:31](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v31)</div>
<p>sevenfold--(compare Exo 22:1-4), for many, ample (compare Gen 4:24; Mat 18:21), even if all his wealth is taken.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v32" data-v="32">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:32](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v32)</div>
<p>lacketh understanding--or, "heart"; destitute of moral principle and prudence.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v33" data-v="33">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:33](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v33)</div>
<p>dishonour--or, "shame," as well as hurt of body (Pro 3:35).</p>
<p>reproach . . . away--No restitution will suffice;</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v34" data-v="34">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 6:34](/en/kjv/proverbs/6#v34)</div>
<p>nor any terms of reconciliation be admitted.</p>
<p>regard--or, "accept" any ransom.</p>

</div>
