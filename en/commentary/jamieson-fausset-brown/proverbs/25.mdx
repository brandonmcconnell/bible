## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 25:1-28)</p>
<p>The character of these proverbs sustains the title (see Introduction).</p>
<p>also--refers to the former part of the book.</p>
<p>copied out--literally, "transferred," that is, from some other book to this; not given from memory.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v2)</div>
<p>God's unsearchableness impresses us with awe (compare Isa 45:15; Rom 11:33). But kings, being finite, should confer with wise counsellors;</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v3)</div>
<p>Ye wisely keeping state secrets, which to common men are as inaccessible heights and depths.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v4)</div>
<p>As separating impurities from ore leaves pure silver, so taking from a king wicked counsellors leaves a wise and beneficent government.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v5)</div>
<p>before--or, "in presence of," as courtiers stood about a king.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v6)</div>
<p>Do not intrude into the presence of the king, for the elevation of the humble is honorable, but the humbling of the proud disgraceful (Luk 14:8-10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v8)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 3:30).</p>
<p>lest . . . shame--lest you do what you ought not, when shamed by defeat, or "lest thou art shut out from doing any thing."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v9)</div>
<p>(Compare Mat 5:25, Margin).</p>
<p>secret--that is, of your opponent, for his disadvantage, and so you be disgraced, not having discussed your difficulties with him.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v11)</div>
<p>a word fitly--literally, "quickly," as wheels roll, just in time. The comparison as apples . . . silver gives a like sense.</p>
<p>apples, &amp;c.--either real apples of golden color, in a silver network basket, or imitations on silver embroidery.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v12)</div>
<p>Those who desire to know and do rightly, most highly esteem good counsel (Pro 9:9; Pro 15:31). The listening ear is better than one hung with gold.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v13)</div>
<p>Snow from mountains was used to cool drinks; so refreshing is a faithful messenger (Pro 13:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v14)</div>
<p>clouds--literally, "vapors" (Jer 10:13), clouds only in appearance.</p>
<p>a false gift--promised, but not given.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v15)</div>
<p>Gentleness and kindness overcome the most powerful and obstinate.</p>
<p>long forbearing--or, "slowness to anger" (Pro 14:29; Pro 15:18).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v16)</div>
<p>A comparison, as a surfeit of honey produces physical disgust, so your company, however agreeable in moderation, may, if excessive, lead your friend to hate you.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v18)</div>
<p>A false witness is as destructive to reputation, as such weapons to the body (Pro 24:28).</p>
<p>beareth . . . witness--literally, "answereth questions," as before a judge, against his neighbor.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v19)</div>
<p>Treachery annoys as well as deceives.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v20)</div>
<p>Not only is the incongruity of songs (that is, joyful) and sadness meant, but an accession of sadness, by want of sympathy, is implied.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v21)</div>
<p>(Compare Mat 5:44; Rom 12:20). As metals are melted by heaping coals upon them, so is the heart softened by kindness.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v23)</div>
<p>Better, "As the north wind bringeth forth (Psa 90:2) or produces rain, so does a concealed or slandering tongue produce anger."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v24)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 21:9, Pro 21:19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v25)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 25:13).</p>
<p>good news--that is, of some loved interest or absent friend, the more grateful as coming from afar.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v26)</div>
<p>From troubled fountains and corrupt springs no healthy water is to be had, so when the righteous are oppressed by the wicked, their power for good is lessened or destroyed.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v27)</div>
<p>Satiety surfeits (Pro 25:16); so men who are self-glorious find shame.</p>
<p>is not glory--"not" is supplied from the first clause, or "is grievous," in which sense a similar word is used (Pro 27:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v28)</div>
<p>Such are exposed to the incursions of evil thoughts and successful temptations.</p>
<p>Next: Proverbs Chapter 26</p>
</div>

</div>
