## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>The commendation of wisdom is continued, under the figure of a liberal host, and its provisions under that of a feast (compare Luk 14:16-24). The character of those who are invited is followed by a contrasted description of the rejectors of good counsel; and with the invitations of wisdom are contrasted the allurement of the wicked woman. (Pro. 9:1-18)</p>
<p>house--(compare Pro 8:34).</p>
<p>her--or, "its" (the house).</p>
<p>seven pillars--the number seven for many, or a sufficiency (Pro 6:31).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v2)</div>
<p>mingled--to enhance the flavor (Pro 23:30; Isa 5:22).</p>
<p>furnished--literally, "set out," "arranged."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v3)</div>
<p>maidens--servants to invite (compare Psa 68:11; Isa 40:9).</p>
<p>highest places--ridges of heights, conspicuous places.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v4)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 1:4; Pro 6:32). Wisdom not only supplies right but forbids wrong principles.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v7)</div>
<p>shame--(Compare Pro 3:35).</p>
<p>a blot--or, "stain on character." Both terms denote the evil done by others to one whose faithfulness secures a wise man's love.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v9)</div>
<p>The more a wise man learns, the more he loves wisdom.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v10)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 1:7).</p>
<p>of the holy--literally, "holies," persons or things, or both. This knowledge gives right perception.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v11)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 3:16-18; Pro 4:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v12)</div>
<p>You are mainly concerned in your own conduct.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v13)</div>
<p>foolish woman--or literally, "woman of folly," specially manifested by such as are described.</p>
<p>clamorous--or, "noisy" (Pro 7:11).</p>
<p>knoweth nothing--literally, "knoweth not what," that is, is right and proper.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v14)</div>
<p>on a seat--literally, "throne," takes a prominent place, impudently and haughtily.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v15)</div>
<p>to allure those who are right-minded, and who are addressed as in Pro 9:4, as</p>
<p>simple--that is, easily led (Pro 1:4) and unsettled, though willing to do right.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v17)</div>
<p>The language of a proverb, meaning that forbidden delights are sweet and pleasant, as fruits of risk and danger.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v18)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 2:18-19; Pro 7:27).</p>

</div>
