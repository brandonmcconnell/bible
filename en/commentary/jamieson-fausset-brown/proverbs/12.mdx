## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 12:1-28)</p>
<p>loveth knowledge--as the fruit of instruction or training (Pro 1:2).</p>
<p>hateth reproof-- (Pro 10:17).</p>
<p>brutish--stupid, regardless of his own welfare (Psa 49:10; Psa 73:22).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v3)</div>
<p>Wickedness cannot give permanent prosperity.</p>
<p>root . . . not be moved--firm as a flourishing tree-- (Psa 1:3; Psa 15:5; Jer 17:8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v4)</div>
<p>A virtuous woman--in the wide sense of well-disposed to all moral duties (Pro 31:10).</p>
<p>maketh ashamed--that is, by misconduct.</p>
<p>rottenness--an incurable evil.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v5)</div>
<p>thoughts--or, "purposes."</p>
<p>are right--literally, "are judgment," that is, true decisions.</p>
<p>counsels--(Compare Pro 11:14).</p>
<p>deceit--contrary to truth and honesty.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v6)</div>
<p>The words--or, "expressed designs" of the wicked are for evil purposes.</p>
<p>the mouth--or, "words" of the righteous delivering instead of ensnaring men.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v7)</div>
<p>Such conduct brings a proper return, by the destruction of the wicked and well-being of the righteous and his family.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v8)</div>
<p>despised--as opposed to commended (Pro 11:12).</p>
<p>perverse heart--or, "wicked principles," as opposed to one of wisdom.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v9)</div>
<p>despised--held in little repute, obscure (Sa1 18:23; Isa 3:5).</p>
<p>hath a servant--implying some means of honest living.</p>
<p>honoureth himself--is self-conceited.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v10)</div>
<p>regardeth--literally, "knoweth" (Psa 1:6).</p>
<p>mercies . . . cruel--as acts of compassion ungraciously rendered to the needy. The righteous more regards a beast than the wicked a man.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v11)</div>
<p>The idler's fate is the result of indolence and want of principle (Pro 6:32; Pro 7:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v12)</div>
<p>the wicked . . . evil--They love the crafty arts of deception.</p>
<p>the root . . . fruit--their own resources supply them; or, it may be rendered: "He (God) giveth, or, sets (Eze 17:22) the root of the righteous," and hence it is firm: or, the verb is impersonal; "As to the root . . . it is firm" (Pro 17:19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v13)</div>
<p>The wicked is snared, &amp;c.--The sentiment expanded. While the wicked, such as liars, flatterers, &amp;c., fall by their own words, the righteous are unhurt. Their good conduct makes friends, and God rewards them.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v15)</div>
<p>The way . . . eyes--The fool is self-conceited (compare Pro 12:1; Pro 1:32; Pro 10:17; Jam 3:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v16)</div>
<p>prudent . . . shame--He is slow to denounce his insulters (Jam 1:19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v18)</div>
<p>speaketh--literally, "speaketh hastily," or indiscreetly (Psa 106:33), as an angry man retorts harsh and provoking invectives.</p>
<p>tongue . . . health--by soothing and gentle language.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v19)</div>
<p>Words of truth are consistent, and stand all tests, while lies are soon discovered and exposed.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v20)</div>
<p>that imagine--or, "plan" (Pro 3:29). They design a deceitful course, to which, with all its evils and dangers to others and themselves, the happiness of peace-makers is opposed (compare Mat 5:9; Rom 12:18).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v21)</div>
<p>no evil--(as in Psa 91:10), under God's wise limitations (Rom 8:28).</p>
<p>mischief--as penal evil.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v22)</div>
<p>deal truly--or, "faithfully," that is, according to promises (compare Joh 3:21).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v23)</div>
<p>concealeth--by his modesty (Pro 10:14; Pro 11:13).</p>
<p>heart . . . proclaimeth--as his lips speak his thoughts (compare Ecc 10:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v24)</div>
<p>slothful--(Compare Margin), so called because he fails to meet his promises.</p>
<p>under tribute--not denoting legal taxes, but the obligation of dependence.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v25)</div>
<p>a good word--one of comfort.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v26)</div>
<p>more excellent--(Compare Margin); or, "more successful," while the wicked fail; or, we may read it: "The righteous guides his friend, but," &amp;c., that is, The ability of the righteous to aid others is contrasted with the ruin to which the way of the wicked leads themselves.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v27)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 12:24).</p>
<p>took in hunting--or, "his venison." He does not improve his advantages.</p>
<p>the substance . . . precious--or, "the wealth of a man of honor is being diligent," or "diligence."</p>
<p>precious--literally, "honor" (Ecc 10:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 12:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/12#v28)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 8:8, Pro 8:20, &amp;c.). A sentiment often stated; here first affirmatively, then negatively.</p>

</div>
