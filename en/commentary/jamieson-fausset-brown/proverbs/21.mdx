## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 21:1-31)</p>
<p>rivers--irrigating channels (Psa 1:3), whose course was easily turned (compare Deu 11:10). God disposes even kings as He pleases (Pro 16:9; Psa 33:15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v2)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 14:2; Pro. 16:2-25).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v3)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 50:7-15; Isa 1:11, Isa 1:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v4)</div>
<p>high look--(Compare Margin; Psa 131:1).</p>
<p>proud heart--or, "heart of breadth," one that is swollen (compare Psa 101:5).</p>
<p>ploughing--better "lamp," a frequent figure for prosperity (Pro 20:20); hence joy or delight.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v5)</div>
<p>The contrast is between steady industry and rashness (compare Pro 19:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v6)</div>
<p>The getting--or, "what is obtained" (compare Job 7:2; Jer 22:13, Hebrew).</p>
<p>vanity . . . to and fro--as fleeting as chaff or stubble in the wind (compare Pro 20:17-21; Psa 62:10). Such gettings are unsatisfactory.</p>
<p>them . . . death--act as if they did (Pro 8:36; Pro 17:19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v7)</div>
<p>robbery--or, "destruction," especially oppression, of which they are authors.</p>
<p>shall destroy--literally, "cut with a saw" (Kg1 7:9), that is, utterly ruin them. Their sins shall be visited on them in kind.</p>
<p>to do judgment--what is just and right.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v8)</div>
<p>of man--any one; his way is opposed to truth, and also estranged from it. The pure proves himself such by his right conduct.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v9)</div>
<p>corner--a turret or arbor on the roof.</p>
<p>brawling--or contentious.</p>
<p>wide house--literally, "house of fellowship," large enough for several families.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v10)</div>
<p>So strongly does he desire to do evil (Psa 10:3; Ecc 8:11), that he will not even spare his friend if in his way.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v11)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 19:25). That which the simple learn by the terrors of punishment, the wise learn by teaching.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v12)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 37:35-38; Psa 73:17, Psa 73:20).</p>
<p>house--family or interests.</p>
<p>overthroweth--either supply "God" (compare Pro 10:24), or the word is used impersonally.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v13)</div>
<p>The principles of retribution, often taught (compare Psa 18:26; Mat 7:1-12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v14)</div>
<p>The effect of bribery (Pro 17:23) is enhanced by secrecy, as the bribed person does not wish his motives made known.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v15)</div>
<p>But the just love right and need no bribes. The wicked at last meet destruction, though for a time happy in concealing corruption.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v16)</div>
<p>the way of understanding--(Compare Pro 12:26; Pro 14:22).</p>
<p>remain--that is, rest as at a journey's end; death will be his unchanging home.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v17)</div>
<p>Costly luxuries impoverish.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v18)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 11:8). By suffering what they had devised for the righteous, or brought on them, the wicked became their ransom, in the usual sense of substitutes (compare Jos 7:26; Est 7:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v19)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 21:9).</p>
<p>wilderness--pasture, though uninhabitable ground (Psa 65:12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v20)</div>
<p>The wise, by diligence and care, lay up and increase wealth, while fools</p>
<p>spend--literally, "swallow it up," greedily.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v21)</div>
<p>He who tries to act justly and kindly (Psa 34:14) will prosper and obtain justice and honor.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v22)</div>
<p>"Wisdom is better than strength" (Ecc 7:19; Ecc 9:15).</p>
<p>strength . . . thereof--that in which they confide.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v23)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 13:2-3; Jam 3:6-10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v24)</div>
<p>The reproachful name is deserved by those who treat others with anger and contempt.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v25)</div>
<p>desire--that is, of ease and idleness brings him to starvation.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v26)</div>
<p>The sin of covetousness marks the sluggard, as the virtue of benevolence the righteous.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v27)</div>
<p>God regards the heart, and hypocrisy is more odious than open inconsistency.</p>
<p>wicked mind--or, "design" (Pro 1:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v28)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 19:5).</p>
<p>that heareth--or heeds instruction, and so grows wise.</p>
<p>speaketh constantly--or sincerely (compare Hab 1:5), and hence is believed (Pro 12:19; Jam 1:19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v29" data-v="29">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:29](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v29)</div>
<p>hardeneth his face--is obstinate.</p>
<p>directeth . . . way--considers it, and acts advisedly.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v30" data-v="30">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 21:30](/en/kjv/proverbs/21#v30)</div>
<p>Men's best devices and reliances are vain compared with God's, or without His aid (Pro 19:21; Psa 20:7; Psa 33:17).</p>

</div>
