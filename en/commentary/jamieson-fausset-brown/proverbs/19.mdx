## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 19:1-29)</p>
<p>(Compare Pro 28:6). "Rich" for fool here. Integrity is better than riches (Pro 15:16-17; Pro 16:8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v2)</div>
<p>The last illustrates the first clause. Rashness, the result of ignorance, brings trouble.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v3)</div>
<p>perverteth . . . way--turns him back from right (Pro 13:6; Jam 1:13); and he blames God for his failures.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v4)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 14:20). Such facts are often adduced with implied disapprobation.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v5)</div>
<p>Compare Pro 19:9, where perish explains not escape here (compare Psa 88:9-10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v8)</div>
<p>(Compare Margin; Pro 15:32).</p>
<p>loveth . . . soul--or, "himself," which he evinces by regarding his best interests.</p>
<p>keepeth--or, "regards."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v10)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 17:7). The fool is incapable of properly using pleasure as knowledge, yet for him to have it is less incongruous than the undue elevation of servants. Let each abide in his calling (Co1 7:20).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v11)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 14:29; Pro 16:32). This inculcation of a forgiving spirit shows that true religion is always the same (Mat 5:22-24).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v12)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 16:14-15; Pro 20:2). A motive to submission to lawful authority.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v13)</div>
<p>calamity--literally, "calamities," varied and many.</p>
<p>continual dropping--a perpetual annoyance, wearing out patience.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v14)</div>
<p>A contrast of men's gifts and God's, who, though author of both blessings, confers the latter by His more special providence.</p>
<p>and--or, "but," implying that the evils of Pro 19:13 are only avoided by His care.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v15)</div>
<p>a deep sleep--a state of utter indifference.</p>
<p>idle soul--or, "person" (compare Pro 10:4; Pro 12:24).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v16)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:17; Pro 13:13).</p>
<p>despiseth . . . ways--opposed to keeping or observing, neglects (Pro 16:17) (as unworthy of regard) his moral conduct.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v17)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 14:21; Psa 37:26).</p>
<p>hath pity--shown by acts (compare Margin).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v18)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 13:24; Pro 23:13).</p>
<p>let not . . . spare--literally, "do not lift up thy soul" (Psa 24:4; Psa 25:1), that is, do not desire to his death; a caution to passionate parents against angry chastisement.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v19)</div>
<p>Repeated efforts of kindness are lost on ill-natured persons.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v20)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 13:18-20).</p>
<p>latter end-- (Pro 5:11). In youth prepare for age.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v21)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 16:1, Pro 16:9; Psa 33:10-11). The failure of man's devices is implied.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v22)</div>
<p>desire--that is, to do good, indicates a kind disposition (Pro 11:23); and the poor thus affected are better than liars, who say and do not.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v23)</div>
<p>The fear . . . life--(Compare Pro 3:2).</p>
<p>abide--or, "remain contented" (Ti1 4:8).</p>
<p>not visited with evil-- (Pro 10:3; Psa 37:25), as a judgment, in which sense visit is often used (Psa 89:32; Jer 6:15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v24)</div>
<p>bosom--literally, a wide dish in which the hand was plunged in eating (Mat 26:23). Compare Pro 26:15, the sentiment expressed with equal irony and less exaggeration.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v25)</div>
<p>Such is the benefit of reproof; even the simple profit, much more the wise.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v26)</div>
<p>Unfilial conduct often condemned (Pro 17:21-25; Pro 20:20; Deu 21:18, Deu 21:21).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v27)</div>
<p>Avoid whatever leads from truth.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v28)</div>
<p>ungodly witness--(Compare Margin), one false by bad principles (compare Pro 6:12).</p>
<p>scorneth judgment--sets at naught the dictates of justice.</p>
<p>devoureth--literally, "swalloweth," as something delightful.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v29" data-v="29">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:29](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v29)</div>
<p>Their punishment is sure, fixed, and ready (compare Pro 3:34; Pro 10:13).</p>
<p>Next: Proverbs Chapter 20</p>
</div>

</div>
