## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 26:1-28)</p>
<p>The incongruities of nature illustrate also those of the moral world. The fool's unworthiness is also implied (Pro 17:7; Pro 19:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v2)</div>
<p>Though not obvious to us,</p>
<p>the bird--literally, "sparrow"--and</p>
<p>swallow--have an object in their motions, so penal evil falls on none without a reason.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v3)</div>
<p>The rod is as much needed by fools and as well suited to them, as whips and bridles are for beasts.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v4)</div>
<p>Answer not--that is, approvingly by like folly.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v5)</div>
<p>Answer--by reproof.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v6)</div>
<p>A fool fails by folly as surely as if he were maimed.</p>
<p>drinketh damage--that is, gets it abundantly (Job 15:16; Job 34:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v7)</div>
<p>legs . . . equal--or, "take away the legs," or "the legs . . . are weak." In any case the idea is that they are the occasion of an awkwardness, such as the fool shows in using a parable or proverb (see Introduction; Pro 17:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v8)</div>
<p>A stone, bound in a sling, is useless; so honor, conferred on a fool, is thrown away.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v9)</div>
<p>As vexatious and unmanageable as a thorn in a drunkard's hand is a parable to a fool. He will be as apt to misuse is as to use it rightly.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v10)</div>
<p>Various versions of this are proposed (compare Margin). Better perhaps--"Much He injures (or literally, "wounds") all who reward," &amp;c., that is, society is injured by encouraging evil men.</p>
<p>transgressors--may be rendered "vagrants." The word "God" is improperly supplied.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v11)</div>
<p>returneth . . . folly--Though disgusting to others, the fool delights in his folly.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v12)</div>
<p>The self-conceited are taught with more difficulty than the stupid.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v13)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 22:13).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v14)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 6:10; Pro 24:33).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v15)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 19:24).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v16)</div>
<p>The thoughtless being ignorant of their ignorance are conceited.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v17)</div>
<p>meddleth--as in Pro 20:19; Pro 24:21; as either holding a dog by the ears or letting him go involves danger, so success in another man's strife or failure involves a useless risk of reputation, does no good, and may do us harm.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v18)</div>
<p>Such are reckless of results.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v20)</div>
<p>The talebearers foster (Pro 16:28), and the contentious excite, strife.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v22)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 18:8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v23)</div>
<p>Warm professions can no more give value to insincerity than silver coating to rude earthenware.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v24)</div>
<p>dissembleth--though an unusual sense of the word (compare Margin), is allowable, and better suits the context, which sets forth hypocrisy.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v25)</div>
<p>Sentiment of Pro 26:24 carried out.</p>
<p>seven abominations in his heart--that is, very many (compare Pro 24:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v26)</div>
<p>Deceit will at last be exposed, and the wicked by their own arts often bring on retribution (compare Pro 12:13; Psa 7:16; Psa 9:17, &amp;c.).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v28)</div>
<p>Men hate those they injure.</p>
<p>A lying tongue--"lips" for the persons (compare Pro 4:24; Psa 12:3).</p>
<p>Next: Proverbs Chapter 27</p>
</div>
