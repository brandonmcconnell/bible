## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>Here begins the second part of the book, Pro. 10:1-22:16, which, with the third, Pro. 22:16-25:28, contains series of proverbs whose sense is complete in one or two verses, and which, having no logical connection, admit of no analysis. The parallelisms of Pro. 10:1-15:33 are mostly antithetic; and those of Pro. 16:1-22:16, synthetic. The evidences of art in the structure are very clear, and indicate, probably, a purpose of facilitating the labor of memorizing. (Pro. 10:1-32)</p>
<p>wise &#91;and&#93; foolish--as they follow or reject the precepts of wisdom.</p>
<p>maketh . . . father--or, "gladdens a father."</p>
<p>heaviness--or, "grief."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v2)</div>
<p>Treasures . . . nothing--that is, Ill-gotten gains give no true happiness (compare Pro 4:17; Mat 6:19).</p>
<p>righteousness--especially beneficence (Psa 112:9).</p>
<p>death--the greatest of all evils.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v3)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 37:16-20). The last clause is better: "He will repel the greedy desires of the wicked."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v4)</div>
<p>slack--literally, "deceitful," failing of its purpose (compare Hos 7:16).</p>
<p>maketh rich--(compare Pro 10:22).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v5)</div>
<p>son--as Pro 1:8, Pro 1:10, and often.</p>
<p>sleepeth--in indolence, and not for rest.</p>
<p>causeth shame--literally, "is base" (compare Pro 14:35; Pro 17:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v6)</div>
<p>Blessings--literally, "Praises." The last clause is better: "The mouth of the wicked covereth (or concealeth) violence (or mischievous devices)" to be executed in due time (Psa 5:9; Psa 10:7; Rom 3:14), and hence has no praises (compare Pro 10:11).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v7)</div>
<p>blessed--literally, "for a blessing," or praise.</p>
<p>shall rot--literally, "be worm-eaten," useless and disgusting.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v8)</div>
<p>wise, &amp;c.--(compare Pro 9:8-9, Pro 9:16), opposed to</p>
<p>prating fool--or, "fool of lips of wicked language."</p>
<p>fall--headlong, suddenly.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v9)</div>
<p>perverteth his ways--acts deceitfully.</p>
<p>known--discovered and punished.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v10)</div>
<p>Two vices contrasted; hypocrisy, or insinuating evil against one (Pro 6:13; Psa 35:19), and rashness of speech. In each case, the results are on the evildoers.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v11)</div>
<p>a well--or, "source" of good to himself and others (Joh 7:37-38). On last clause, see on Pro 10:6.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v12)</div>
<p>strifes--or, "litigations."</p>
<p>covereth--by forgiveness and forbearance.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v13)</div>
<p>In the lips . . . found--hence, not beaten, as the wicked-speaking fool.</p>
<p>void of understanding-- (Pro 6:32; Pro 7:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v14)</div>
<p>lay up knowledge--that is, as treasures for good use.</p>
<p>mouth . . . destruction--or, "as to the mouth," &amp;c., destruction is near; they expose themselves to evil by prating.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v15)</div>
<p>Both by trusting in "uncertain riches" (Ti1 6:17), or by the evils of poverty (Pro 30:9), men, not fearing God, fall into dangers.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v16)</div>
<p>The industry of the righteous is alone truly successful, while the earnings of the wicked tempt and lead to sin.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v17)</div>
<p>keepeth--observes (Pro 3:18; Pro 4:22).</p>
<p>refuseth--or, "turns from reproof," which might direct him aright.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v18)</div>
<p>Both vices must one day be known and punished, and hence their folly.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v19)</div>
<p>Much speech involves risk of sin; hence the wisdom of restraining the tongue (Psa 39:1; Jam 1:26).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v20)</div>
<p>Right speech is the fruit of a good heart, but the wicked show theirs to be useless.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v21)</div>
<p>Fools not only fail to benefit others, as do the righteous, but procure their own ruin (compare Pro 10:11, Pro 10:17; Hos 4:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v22)</div>
<p>it maketh, &amp;c.--"it" is emphatic. Riches from God are without the sorrow of ill-gotten wealth (compare Ecc 2:21-23; Ti1 6:9-10, Ti1 6:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v23)</div>
<p>Sin is the pleasure of the wicked; wisdom that of the good.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v24)</div>
<p>it--the very thing. The wicked get dreaded evil; the righteous, desired good.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v25)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 1:4; Psa 37:9-10, Psa 37:36).</p>
<p>righteous . . . foundation--well laid and firm (Mat 7:24-25).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v26)</div>
<p>that is, causes vexation.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v27)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 9:11; Psa 55:23).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v28)</div>
<p>gladness--in confidence of realizing it.</p>
<p>expectation . . . perish--in disappointment.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v29" data-v="29">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:29](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v29)</div>
<p>The way, &amp;c.--that is, God's providence sustains the righteous and overthrows the wicked (Hos 14:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v30" data-v="30">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:30](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v30)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 12:3; Psa 37:9-11; Psa 102:28).</p>
<p>earth--or, "land of promise."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v31" data-v="31">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:31](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v31)</div>
<p>bringeth forth--literally, "germinates" as a plant.</p>
<p>froward--(Compare Pro 2:12, Pro 2:14).</p>
<p>cut off--as an unproductive plant.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v32" data-v="32">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 10:32](/en/kjv/proverbs/10#v32)</div>
<p>know--regard and provide for (Psa 1:6).</p>
<p>frowardness--all kinds of deceit and ill-nature. The word is plural.</p>
