## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 24:1-34)</p>
<p>(Compare Pro 23:3, Pro 23:17; Psa 37:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v2)</div>
<p>studieth--meditateth.</p>
<p>talk . . . mischief--Their expressed purposes are to do evil.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v3)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 14:1; Isa 54:14).</p>
<p>house--including the family.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v4)</div>
<p>by knowledge . . . riches-- (Pro 8:18; Pro 21:20).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v5)</div>
<p>The general statement (Ecc 9:16, Ecc 9:18) is specially illustrated (compare Pro 21:22; Psa 144:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v7)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 14:16).</p>
<p>in the gate--(Compare Pro 22:22).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v8)</div>
<p>So called even if he fails to do evil.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v9)</div>
<p>Same thought varied.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v10)</div>
<p>Literally, "If thou fail in the day of straits (adversity), strait (or, small) is thy strength," which is then truly tested.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v11)</div>
<p>Neglect of known duty is sin (Jam 4:17).</p>
<p>ready--literally, "bowing down"</p>
<p>to be slain--that is, unjustly. God's retributive justice cannot be avoided by professed ignorance.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v13)</div>
<p>As delicious food whets the appetite, so should the rewards of wisdom excite us to seek it.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v14)</div>
<p>reward--literally, "after part," the proper result (compare Pro 23:18; Psa 37:37-38).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v15)</div>
<p>The plots of the wicked against the good, though partially, shall not be fully successful (Psa 37:24); while the wicked, falling under penal evil, find no help.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v16)</div>
<p>seven times--often, or many (Pro 6:16, Pro 6:31; Pro 9:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v17)</div>
<p>Yet let none rejoice over the fate of evildoers, lest God punish their wrong spirit by relieving the sufferer (compare Pro 17:5; Job 31:29).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v19)</div>
<p>(Psa 37:1, Psa 37:38; Psa 18:28).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v20)</div>
<p>candle--or, "prosperity"; it shall come to an end (Pro 13:9; Pro 20:20).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v21)</div>
<p>A warning against impiety and resistance to lawful rule (Rom 13:1-7; Pe1 2:17).</p>
<p>meddle . . . change--(Compare Margin), literally, "mingle not yourself," avoid the society of restless persons.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v22)</div>
<p>their calamity, &amp;c.--either what God and the king inflict, or what changers and their company suffer; better the first.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v23)</div>
<p>These . . . wise--literally, "are of the wise," as authors (compare "Psalms of David," Hebrew). "These" refers to the verses following, Pro 24:24-34.</p>
<p>to have respect--literally, "to discern faces," show partiality,</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v24)</div>
<p>of which an example is justifying the wicked, to which is opposed, rebuking him, which has a blessing.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v26)</div>
<p>kiss his lips--love and obey, do homage (Psa 2:12; Sol 8:1).</p>
<p>right answer--literally, "plain words" (compare Pro 8:9), opposed to deceptive, or obscure.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v27)</div>
<p>Prepare . . . in the field--Secure, by diligence, a proper support, and then build; provide necessaries, then comforts, to which a house rather pertained, in a mild climate, permitting the use of tents.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v28)</div>
<p>Do not speak even truth needlessly against any, and never falsehood.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v29" data-v="29">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:29](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v29)</div>
<p>Especially avoid retaliation (Mat 5:43-45; Rom 12:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v30" data-v="30">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:30](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v30)</div>
<p>A striking picture of the effects of sloth.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v32" data-v="32">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 24:32](/en/kjv/proverbs/24#v32)</div>
<p>From the folly of the sluggard learn wisdom (Pro 6:10-11).</p>

</div>
