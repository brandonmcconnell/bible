## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 17:1-28)</p>
<p>sacrifices--or, "feasts" made with part of them (compare Pro 7:14; Lev 2:3; Lev 7:31).</p>
<p>with--literally, "of."</p>
<p>strife--its product, or attendant.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v2)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 14:35).</p>
<p>causeth shame-- (Pro 10:5).</p>
<p>shall . . . inheritance--that is, share a brother's part (compare Num 27:4, Num 27:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v3)</div>
<p>God only knows, as He tries (Psa 12:6; Psa 66:10) the heart.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v4)</div>
<p>Wicked doers and speakers alike delight in calumny.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v5)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 14:31).</p>
<p>glad at calamities--rejoicing in others' evil. Such are rightly punished by God, who knows their hearts.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v6)</div>
<p>Prolonged posterity is a blessing, its cutting off a curse (Pro 13:22; Psa 109:13-15), hence children may glory in virtuous ancestry.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v7)</div>
<p>Excellent speech--(Compare Margin). Such language as ill suits a fool, as lying (ought to suit) a prince (Pro 16:12-13).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v8)</div>
<p>One so corrupt as to take a bribe evinces his high estimate of it by subjection to its influence (Pro 18:16; Pro 19:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v9)</div>
<p>seeketh love--(Compare Margin). The contrast is between the peace-maker and tale-bearer.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v10)</div>
<p>Reproof more affects the wise than severe scourging, fools.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v11)</div>
<p>Such meet just retribution (Kg1 2:25).</p>
<p>a cruel messenger--one to inflict it.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v12)</div>
<p>They are less rational in anger than wild beasts.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v13)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 7:4; Psa 35:12).</p>
<p>evil--injury to another (Pro 13:21).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v14)</div>
<p>letteth . . . water--as a breach in a dam.</p>
<p>before . . . meddled with--before strife has become sharp, or, by an explanation better suiting the figure, before it rolls on, or increases.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v15)</div>
<p>abomination . . . Lord--as reversing His method of acting (Pro 3:32; Pro 12:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v16)</div>
<p>Though wealth cannot buy wisdom for those who do not love it, yet wisdom procures wealth (Pro 3:16; Pro 14:24).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v17)</div>
<p>To the second of these parallel clauses, there is an accession of meaning, that is, that a brother's love is specially seen in adversity.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v18)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 6:1-5; Pro 11:15).</p>
<p>in the presence, &amp;c.--that is, he either fails to consult his friend, or to follow his advice.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v19)</div>
<p>strife--contention is, and leads to, sin.</p>
<p>he that exalteth his gate--gratifies a vain love of costly building.</p>
<p>seeketh--or, "findeth," as if he sought (compare "loveth death," Pro 8:36).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v20)</div>
<p>The second clause advances on the first. The ill-natured fail of good, and the cavilling and fault-finding incur evil.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v21)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 23:24). Different words are rendered by "fool," both denoting stupidity and impiety.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v22)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 14:30; Pro 15:13). The effect of the mind on the body is well known.</p>
<p>medicine--or, "body," which better corresponds with "bone."</p>
<p>drieth--as if the marrow were exhausted.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v23)</div>
<p>a gift . . . bosom--Money and other valuables were borne in a fold of the garment, called the bosom.</p>
<p>to pervert--that is, by bribery.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v24)</div>
<p>Wisdom . . . him--ever an object of regard, while a fool's affections are unsettled.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v25)</div>
<p>a grief--or cross, vexation (compare Pro 17:21; Pro 10:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v26)</div>
<p>Also--that is, Equally to be avoided are other sins: punishing good subjects, or resisting good rulers.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v27)</div>
<p>Prudence of speech is commended as is an excellent or calm spirit, not excited to vain conversation.</p>
<p>Next: Proverbs Chapter 18</p>
</div>

</div>
