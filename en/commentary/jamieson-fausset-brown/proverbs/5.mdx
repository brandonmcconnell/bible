## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>A warning against the seductive arts of wicked women, enforced by considering the advantages of chastity, and the miserable end of the wicked. (Pro. 5:1-23)</p>
<p>This connection of wisdom and understanding is frequent (Pro 2:2; Pro 3:7); the first denotes the use of wise means for wise ends; the other, the exercise of a proper discrimination in their discovery.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v2)</div>
<p>regard--or, "observe."</p>
<p>keep--preserve constantly.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v3)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 2:16). Her enticing promises are deceitful.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v4)</div>
<p>her end--literally, "her future," in sense of reward, what follows (compare Psa 37:37; Psa 73:17). Its nature is evinced by the use of figures, opposite those of Pro 5:3. The physical and moral suffering of the deluded profligate are notoriously terrible.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v5)</div>
<p>feet . . . , steps--that is, course of life ends in death.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v6)</div>
<p>her ways . . . know--Some prefer, "that she may not ponder the path of life," &amp;c.; but perhaps a better sense is, "her ways are varied, so as to prevent your knowledge of her true character, and so of true happiness."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v8)</div>
<p>Avoid the slightest temptation.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v9)</div>
<p>thine honour--in whatever consisting, strength (Pro 3:13) or wealth.</p>
<p>thy years--by cutting them off in dissipation.</p>
<p>unto the cruel--for such the sensual are apt to become.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v10)</div>
<p>wealth--literally, "strength," or the result of it.</p>
<p>labours--the fruit of thy painful exertions (Psa 127:2). There may be a reference to slavery, a commuted punishment for death due the adulterer (Deu 22:22).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v11)</div>
<p>at the last--the end, or reward (compare Pro 5:4).</p>
<p>mourn--roar in pain.</p>
<p>flesh and . . . body--the whole person under incurable disease.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v12)</div>
<p>The ruined sinner vainly laments his neglect of warning and his sad fate in being brought to public disgrace.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v14)</div>
<p>evil--for affliction, as in Gen 19:20; Gen 49:15.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v15)</div>
<p>By figures, in which well, cistern, and fountain &#91;Pro 5:15, Pro 5:18&#93; represent the wife, and rivers of waters &#91;Pro 5:16&#93; the children, men are exhorted to constancy and satisfaction in lawful conjugal enjoyments. In Pro 5:16, fountains (in the plural) rather denote the produce or waters of a spring, literally, "what is from a spring," and corresponds with "rivers of waters."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v17)</div>
<p>only thine own--harlots' children have no known father.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v18)</div>
<p>wife . . . youth--married in youth.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v19)</div>
<p>loving . . . roe--other figures for a wife from the well-known beauty of these animals.</p>
<p>breasts--(Compare Sol 1:13; Eze 23:3, Eze 23:8).</p>
<p>ravished--literally, "intoxicated," that is, fully satisfied.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v21)</div>
<p>The reason, God's eye is on you,</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v22)</div>
<p>and He will cause sin to bring its punishment.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 5:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/5#v23)</div>
<p>without instruction--literally, "in want of instruction," having refused it (compare Job 13:18; Heb 11:24).</p>
<p>go astray--literally, "be drunken." The word "ravished" (Pro 5:19) here denotes fulness of punishment.</p>

</div>
