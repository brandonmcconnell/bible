## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>The subject continued, by a delineation of the arts of strange women, as a caution to the unwary. (Pro. 7:1-27)</p>
<p>Similar calls (Pro 3:1-3; Pro 4:10, &amp;c.).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v2)</div>
<p>apple . . . eye--pupil of eye, a custody (Pro 4:23) of special value.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v3)</div>
<p>Bind . . . fingers--as inscriptions on rings.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v5)</div>
<p>The design of the teaching (compare Pro 2:16; Pro 6:24).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v6)</div>
<p>For--or, "Since," introducing an example to illustrate the warning, which, whether a narrative or a parable, is equally pertinent.</p>
<p>window--or, "opening"</p>
<p>looked--literally, "watched earnestly" (Jdg 5:28).</p>
<p>casement--or, "lattice."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v7)</div>
<p>simple--as in Pro 1:4.</p>
<p>void of, &amp;c.--(Compare Pro 6:32).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v8)</div>
<p>her corner--where she was usually found.</p>
<p>went . . . house--implying, perhaps, confidence in himself by his manner, as denoted in the word</p>
<p>went--literally, "tread pompously."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v9)</div>
<p>The time, twilight, ending in darkness.</p>
<p>black . . . night--literally, "pupil," or, "eye," that is, middle of night.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v10)</div>
<p>attire--that of harlots was sometimes peculiar.</p>
<p>subtile--or, "wary," "cunning."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v11)</div>
<p>loud--or, "noisy," "bustling."</p>
<p>stubborn--not submissive.</p>
<p>without . . . streets, . . . corner--(Compare Ti1 5:13; Tit 2:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v13)</div>
<p>The preparations for a feast do not necessarily imply peculiar religious professions. The offerer retained part of the victim for a feast (Lev 3:9, &amp;c.). This feast she professes was prepared for him whom she boldly addresses as one sought specially to partake of it.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v16)</div>
<p>my bed--or, "couch," adorned in the costliest manner.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v17)</div>
<p>bed--a place for sleeping.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v18)</div>
<p>There is no fear of discovery.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v20)</div>
<p>the day appointed--perhaps, literally, "a full moon," that is, a fortnight's time (compare Pro 7:19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v21)</div>
<p>caused . . . yield--or, "inclines."</p>
<p>flattering--(Compare Pro 5:3).</p>
<p>forced him--by persuasion overcoming his scruples.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v22)</div>
<p>straightway--quickly, either as ignorant of danger, or incapable of resistance.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v23)</div>
<p>Till--He is now caught (Pro 6:26).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v24)</div>
<p>The inferential admonition is followed (Pro 7:26-27), by a more general allegation of the evils of this vice.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 7:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/7#v26)</div>
<p>Even the mightiest fail to resist her deathly allurements.</p>
<p>Next: Proverbs Chapter 8</p>
</div>
