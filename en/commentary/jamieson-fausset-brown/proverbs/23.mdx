## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 23:1-35)</p>
<p>Avoid the dangers of gluttony.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v2)</div>
<p>put a knife--an Eastern figure for putting restraint on the appetite.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v3)</div>
<p>are deceitful meat--though well tasted, injurious.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v4)</div>
<p>(Compare Ti1 6:9-10).</p>
<p>thine own wisdom--which regards riches intrinsically as a blessing.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v5)</div>
<p>Wilt . . . eyes--As the eyes fly after or seek riches, they are not, that is, either become transitory or unsatisfying; fully expressed by their flying away.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v6)</div>
<p>Beware of deceitful men, whose courtesies even you will repent of having accepted.</p>
<p>evil eye--or purpose (Pro 22:9; Deu 15:9; Mat 6:23).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v8)</div>
<p>The morsel . . . words--that is, disgusted with his true character, all pleasant intercourse will be destroyed.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v9)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 9:8). "Cast not your pearls before swine" (Mat 7:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v10)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 22:22-23).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v11)</div>
<p>redeemer--or avenger (Lev 25:25-26; Num 35:12), hence advocate (Job 19:25).</p>
<p>plead . . . thee--(Compare Job 31:21; Psa 35:1; Psa 68:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v12)</div>
<p>Here begins another series of precepts.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v13)</div>
<p>While there is little danger that the use of the "divine ordinance of the rod" will produce bodily harm, there is great hope of spiritual good.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v15)</div>
<p>The pleasure afforded the teacher by the pupil's progress is a motive to diligence.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v16)</div>
<p>my reins--(Compare Psa 7:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v17)</div>
<p>(Compare Margin). The prosperity of the wicked is short.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v18)</div>
<p>an end--or, "hereafter," another time, when apparent inequalities shall be adjusted (compare Psa 37:28-38).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v19)</div>
<p>guide . . . way--or direct thy thoughts to a right course of conduct (compare Pro 4:4; Pro 9:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v20)</div>
<p>riotous . . . flesh--prodigal, or eating more than necessary. Instead of "their flesh" (compare Margin), better, "flesh to them," that is, used for pleasure.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v21)</div>
<p>drowsiness--the dreamy sleep of the slothful.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v22)</div>
<p>Hearken--that is, obey (Pro 1:8; Eph 6:1).</p>
<p>despise . . . old--Adults revere the parents whom, as children, they once obeyed.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v23)</div>
<p>Buy--literally, "get" (Pro 4:5).</p>
<p>truth--generally and specially as opposed to errors of all kinds.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v24)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 10:1; Pro 17:21, Pro 17:25).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v26)</div>
<p>A solemn warning against whoredom and drunkenness (Hos 4:11).</p>
<p>give me--This is the address of that divine wisdom so often presented (Pro 8:1; Pro 9:3, &amp;c.).</p>
<p>heart--confidence.</p>
<p>observe--keep.</p>
<p>my ways--such as I teach you (Pro 3:17; Pro 9:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v27)</div>
<p>deep ditch--a narrow pit, out of which it is hard to climb.</p>
<p>lieth in wait--to ensnare men into the pit, as hunters entrap game (compare Pro 22:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v28)</div>
<p>increaseth . . . transgressors-- (Pro 5:8-10). The vice alluded to is peculiarly hardening to the heart.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v29" data-v="29">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:29](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v29)</div>
<p>This picture is often sadly realized now.</p>
<p>mixed wine--(Compare Pro 9:2; Isa 5:11).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v31" data-v="31">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:31](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v31)</div>
<p>when . . . red--the color denoting greater strength (compare Gen 49:11; Deu 32:14).</p>
<p>giveth . . . cup--literally, "gives its eye," that is, sparkles.</p>
<p>moveth . . . aright--Perhaps its foaming is meant.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v32" data-v="32">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:32](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v32)</div>
<p>The acute miseries resulting from drunkenness contrasted with the temptations.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v33" data-v="33">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:33](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v33)</div>
<p>The moral effects: it inflames passion (Gen 19:31, Gen 19:35), lays open the heart, produces insensibility to the greatest dangers, and debars from reformation, under the severest sufferings.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v35" data-v="35">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 23:35](/en/kjv/proverbs/23#v35)</div>
<p>awake--that is, from drunkenness (Gen 9:24). This is the language rather of acts than of the tongue.</p>

</div>
