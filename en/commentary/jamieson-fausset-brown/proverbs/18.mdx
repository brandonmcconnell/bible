## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>(Pro. 18:1-24)</p>
<p>Through desire . . . seeketh--that is, seeks selfish gratification.</p>
<p>intermeddleth . . . wisdom--or, "rushes on" (Pro 17:14) against all wisdom, or what is valuable (Pro 2:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v2)</div>
<p>that his heart . . . itself--that is, takes pleasure in revealing his folly (Pro 12:23; Pro 15:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v3)</div>
<p>So surely are sin and punishment connected (Pro 16:4).</p>
<p>wicked, for "wickedness," answers to</p>
<p>ignominy, or the state of such; and</p>
<p>contempt, the feeling of others to them; and to</p>
<p>reproach, a manifestation of contempt.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v4)</div>
<p>Wise speech is like an exhaustless stream of benefit.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v5)</div>
<p>accept the person--(Compare Psa 82:2). "It is not good" is to be supplied before "to overthrow."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v6)</div>
<p>The quarrelsome bring trouble on themselves. Their rash language ensnares them (Pro 6:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v8)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 16:28).</p>
<p>as wounds--not sustained by the Hebrew; better, as "sweet morsels," which men gladly swallow.</p>
<p>innermost . . . belly--the mind, or heart (compare Pro 20:27-30; Psa 22:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v9)</div>
<p>One by failing to get, the other by wasting wealth, grows poor.</p>
<p>waster--literally, "master of washing," a prodigal.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v10)</div>
<p>name of the Lord--manifested perfections (Psa 8:1; Psa 20:2), as faithfulness, power, mercy, &amp;c., on which men rely.</p>
<p>is safe--literally, "set on high, out of danger" (Psa 18:2; Psa 91:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v11)</div>
<p>contrasts with Pro 18:10 (compare Pro 10:15). Such is a vain trust (compare Psa 73:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v12)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 15:33; Pro 16:18).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v13)</div>
<p>Hasty speech evinces self-conceit, and ensures shame (Pro 26:12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v14)</div>
<p>infirmity--bodily sickness, or outward evil. The spirit, which sustains, being wounded, no support is left, except, as implied, in God.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v15)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 1:5, Pro 1:15, Pro 1:31).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v16)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 17:8, Pro 17:23). Disapproval of the fact stated is implied.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v17)</div>
<p>One-sided statements are not reliable.</p>
<p>searcheth--thoroughly (Pro 17:9, Pro 17:19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v18)</div>
<p>The lot--whose disposal is of God (Pro 16:13), may, properly used, be a right mode of settling disputes.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v19)</div>
<p>No feuds so difficult of adjustment as those of relatives; hence great care should be used to avoid them.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v20)</div>
<p>(Compare Pro 12:14; Pro 13:2). Men's words are the fruit, or, increase of his lips, and when good, benefit them.</p>
<p>satisfied with--(Compare Pro 1:31; Pro 14:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v21)</div>
<p>Death and life--or, the greatest evil and good.</p>
<p>that love it--that is, the tongue, or its use for good or evil.</p>
<p>eat . . . fruit--(Compare Pro 18:19; Jam 1:19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v22)</div>
<p>The old versions supply "good" before the "wife," as the last clause and Pro 19:14 imply (compare Pro 31:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v23)</div>
<p>the rich . . . roughly--He is tolerated because rich, implying that the estimate of men by wealth is wrong.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 18:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/18#v24)</div>
<p>A man . . . friendly--better, "A man . . . (is) to, or, may triumph (Psa 108:9), or, shout for joy (Psa 5:11), that is, may congratulate himself." Indeed, there is a Friend who is better than a brother; such is the "Friend of sinners" &#91;Mat 11:19; Luk 7:34&#93;, who may have been before the writer's mind.</p>
