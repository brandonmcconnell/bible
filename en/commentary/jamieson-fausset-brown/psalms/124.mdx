## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>To praise for God's favor to His people is added a prayer for its continued manifestation. (Psa 126:1-6)</p>
<p>When the Lord, &amp;c.--The joy of those returned from Babylon was ecstatic, and elicited the admiration even of the heathen, as illustrating God's great power and goodness.</p>
<p>turned again the captivity--that is, restored from it (Job 39:12; Psa 14:7; Pro 12:14). HENGSTENBERG translates: "When the Lord turned Himself to the turning of Zion" (see Margin), God returns to His people when they return to Him (Deu 30:2-3).</p>
</div>
