## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>In the grandeur and solemnity of a divine judgment, God is introduced as instructing men in the nature of true worship, exposing hypocrisy, warning the wicked, and encouraging the pious. (Psa. 50:1-23)</p>
<p>The description of this majestic appearance of God resembles that of His giving the law (compare Exo 19:16; Exo 20:18; Deu 32:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:4](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v4)</div>
<p>above--literally, "above" (Gen 1:7).</p>
<p>heavens . . . earth--For all creatures are witnesses (Deu 4:26; Deu 30:19; Isa 1:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:5](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v5)</div>
<p>my saints-- (Psa 4:3).</p>
<p>made--literally, "cut"</p>
<p>a covenant, &amp;c.--alluding to the dividing of a victim of sacrifice, by which covenants were ratified, the parties passing between the divided portions (compare Gen 15:10, Gen 15:18).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:6](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v6)</div>
<p>The inhabitants of heaven, who well know God's character, attest His righteousness as a judge.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:7](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v7)</div>
<p>I will testify--that is, for failure to worship aught.</p>
<p>thy God--and so, by covenant as well as creation, entitled to a pure worship.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:8](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v8)</div>
<p>However scrupulous in external worship, it was offered as if they conferred an obligation in giving God His own, and with a degrading view of Him as needing it &#91;Psa 50:9-13&#93;. Reproving them for such foolish and blasphemous notions, He teaches them to offer, or literally, "sacrifice," thanksgiving, and pay, or perform, their vows--that is, to bring, with the external symbolical service, the homage of the heart, and faith, penitence, and love. To this is added an invitation to seek, and a promise to afford, all needed help in trouble.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:16](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v16)</div>
<p>the wicked--that is, the formalists, as now exposed, and who lead vicious lives (compare Rom 2:21, Rom 2:23). They are unworthy to use even the words of God's law. Their hypocrisy and vice are exposed by illustrations from sins against the seventh, eighth, and ninth commandments.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:21](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v21)</div>
<p>God, no longer (even in appearance) disregarding such, exposes their sins and threatens a terrible punishment.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:22](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v22)</div>
<p>forget God--This denotes unmindfulness of His true character.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:23](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v23)</div>
<p>offereth praise-- (Psa 50:14), so that the external worship is a true index of the heart.</p>
<p>ordereth . . . aright--acts in a straight, right manner, opposed to turning aside (Psa 25:5). In such, pure worship and a pure life evince their true piety, and they will enjoy God's presence and favor.</p>

</div>
