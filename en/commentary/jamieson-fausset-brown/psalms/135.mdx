## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>This Psalm records the mourning of the captive Israelites, and a prayer and prediction respecting the destruction of their enemies. (Psa 137:1-9)</p>
<p>rivers of Babylon--the name of the city used for the whole country.</p>
<p>remembered Zion--or, Jerusalem, as in Psa 132:13.</p>
