<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 148:2](/en/kjv/psalms/148#v2)</div>
<p>hosts--(compare Psa 103:21).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 148:4](/en/kjv/psalms/148#v4)</div>
<p>heavens of heavens--the very highest.</p>
<p>waters--clouds, resting above the visible heavens (compare Gen 1:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 148:5](/en/kjv/psalms/148#v5)</div>
<p>praise the name--as representing His perfections.</p>
<p>he commanded--"He" is emphatic, ascribing creation to God alone.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 148:6](/en/kjv/psalms/148#v6)</div>
<p>The perpetuity of the frame of nature is, of course, subject to Him who formed it.</p>
<p>a decree . . . pass--His ordinances respecting them shall not change (Jer 36:31), or perish (Job 34:20; Psa 37:36).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 148:7](/en/kjv/psalms/148#v7)</div>
<p>The call on the earth, as opposed to heaven, includes seas or depths, whose inhabitants the dragon, as one of the largest (on leviathan, see on Psa 104:26), is selected to represent. The most destructive and ungovernable agents of inanimate nature are introduced.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 148:8](/en/kjv/psalms/148#v8)</div>
<p>fulfilling his word--or, law, may be understood of each. Next the most distinguished productions of the vegetable world.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 148:9](/en/kjv/psalms/148#v9)</div>
<p>fruitful trees--or, "trees of fruit," as opposed to forest trees. Wild and domestic, large and small animals are comprehended.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 148:11](/en/kjv/psalms/148#v11)</div>
<p>Next all rational beings, from the highest in rank to little children.</p>
<p>princes--or, military leaders.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 148:13](/en/kjv/psalms/148#v13)</div>
<p>Let them--all mentioned.</p>
<p>excellent--or, exalted (Isa 12:4).</p>
<p>his glory--majesty (Psa 45:3).</p>
<p>above the earth and heaven--Their united splendors fail to match His.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 148:14](/en/kjv/psalms/148#v14)</div>
<p>exalteth the horn--established power (Psa 75:5-6).</p>
<p>praise of--or literally, "for"</p>
<p>his saints--that is, occasions for them to praise Him. They are further described as "His people," and "near unto Him," sustaining by covenanted care a peculiarly intimate relation.</p>

</div>
