## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>A prayer for deliverance from cunning and malicious enemies, with a confident view of their overthrow, which will honor God and give joy to the righteous. (Psa 64:1-10)</p>
<p>preserve . . . fear--as well as the danger producing it.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 64:2](/en/kjv/psalms/64#v2)</div>
<p>insurrection--literally, "uproar," noisy assaults, as well as their secret counsels.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 64:3](/en/kjv/psalms/64#v3)</div>
<p>Similar figures for slander (Psa 57:4; Psa 59:7).</p>
<p>bend--literally, "tread," or, "prepared." The allusion is to the mode of bending a bow by treading on it; here, and in Psa 58:7, transferred to arrows.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 64:4](/en/kjv/psalms/64#v4)</div>
<p>the perfect--one innocent of the charges made (Psa 18:23).</p>
<p>fear not-- (Psa 55:19), not regarding God.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 64:5](/en/kjv/psalms/64#v5)</div>
<p>A sentiment here more fully presented, by depicting their deliberate malice.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 64:6](/en/kjv/psalms/64#v6)</div>
<p>This is further evinced by their diligent efforts and deeply laid schemes.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 64:7](/en/kjv/psalms/64#v7)</div>
<p>The contrast is heightened by representing God as using weapons like theirs.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 64:8](/en/kjv/psalms/64#v8)</div>
<p>their . . . tongue to fall, &amp;c.--that is, the consequences of their slanders, &amp;c. (compare Psa 10:2; Psa 31:16).</p>
<p>all that see . . . away--Their partners in evil shall be terrified.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 64:9](/en/kjv/psalms/64#v9)</div>
<p>Men, generally, will acknowledge God's work, and the righteous, rejoicing in it, shall be encouraged to trust Him (Psa 58:10).</p>

</div>
