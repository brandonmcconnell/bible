## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>God's government is especially exercised in and for His Church, which should praise Him for His gracious dealings. (Psa 99:1-9)</p>
<p>sitteth . . . cherubim--(compare Sa1 4:4; Psa 80:1).</p>
<p>tremble . . . be moved--inspired with fear by His judgments on the wicked.</p>
