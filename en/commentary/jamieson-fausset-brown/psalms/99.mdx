## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>As closing this series (see on Psa 93:1), this Psalm is a general call on all the earth to render exalted praise to God, the creator, preserver, and benefactor of men. (Psa 100:1-5)</p>
<p>With thankful praise, unite service as the subjects of a king (Psa 2:11-12).</p>
