## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>See on Psa 4:1, title; Psa 32:1, title; for the history, see Sa1 23:19, Sa1 23:29; 1Sa. 26:1-25. After an earnest cry for help, the Psalmist promises praise in the assurance of a hearing. (Psa 54:1-7)</p>
<p>by thy name-- (Psa 5:11), specially, power.</p>
<p>judge me--as in Psa 7:8; Psa 26:1.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 54:2](/en/kjv/psalms/54#v2)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 4:1; Psa 5:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 54:3](/en/kjv/psalms/54#v3)</div>
<p>strangers--perhaps Ziphites.</p>
<p>oppressors--literally, "terrible ones" (Isa 13:11; Isa 25:3). Such were Saul and his army.</p>
<p>not set . . . them--acted as atheists, without God's fear (compare Psa 16:8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 54:4](/en/kjv/psalms/54#v4)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 30:10).</p>
<p>with them--on their side, and for me (compare Psa 46:11).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 54:5](/en/kjv/psalms/54#v5)</div>
<p>He shall . . . evil--or, "Evil shall return on" (Psa 7:16) my enemies or watchers, that is, to do me evil (Psa 6:7).</p>
<p>in thy truth--Thy verified promise.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 54:6](/en/kjv/psalms/54#v6)</div>
<p>I will freely, &amp;c.--or, present a freewill offering (Lev 7:16; Num 15:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 54:7](/en/kjv/psalms/54#v7)</div>
<p>mine eye . . . desire--(compare Psa 59:10; Psa 112:8), expresses satisfaction in beholding the overthrow of his enemies as those of God, without implying any selfish or unholy feeling (compare Psa 52:6-7).</p>
