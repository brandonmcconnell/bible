## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>On Neginoth, that is, stringed instruments, as the kind of musical accompaniment. On other parts of title, see Introduction., The historical occasion was probably the same as that of the foregoing &#91;see on Psa 3:1&#93;. The writer, praying for further relief, admonishes his enemies of the vanity of attacking God's servant, exhorts them to repentance, and avows his confidence and peace in God's favor. (Psa 4:1-8)</p>
<p>Hear--as in Psa 3:4.</p>
<p>God of my righteousness--or, "my righteous God, as my holy hill" (Psa 2:6), who will act towards me on righteous principles.</p>
<p>thou hast enlarged--expresses relief afforded in opposition to "distress," which is expressed by a word denoting straits or pressure. Past favor is a ground of hope for the future.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 4:2](/en/kjv/psalms/4#v2)</div>
<p>sons of men--men of note or prominence (compare Ch2 21:9).</p>
<p>turn my glory--or, "royal dignity."</p>
<p>into shame--or, "reproach."</p>
<p>vanity--a foolish and hopeless enterprise (Psa 2:1).</p>
<p>leasing--a lie.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 4:3](/en/kjv/psalms/4#v3)</div>
<p>godly--an object as well as subject of divine favor (compare Psa 105:14-15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 4:4](/en/kjv/psalms/4#v4)</div>
<p>Stand in awe-- (Eph 4:26), from Septuagint, "be angry." Both clauses are qualified by "not."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 4:5](/en/kjv/psalms/4#v5)</div>
<p>Not only repent, but manifest penitence by sacrifices or righteousness or righteous sacrifices, &amp;c.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 4:6](/en/kjv/psalms/4#v6)</div>
<p>Contrast true with vain confidence.</p>
<p>light of thy countenance upon us--figure for favor (Num 6:26; Psa 44:3; Psa 81:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 4:7](/en/kjv/psalms/4#v7)</div>
<p>corn and wine--literally, "new corn and wine."</p>
<p>increased--an abundant harvest giving great joy (Isa 9:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 4:8](/en/kjv/psalms/4#v8)</div>
<p>both lay me down, &amp;c.--or, will lie down at once, and sleep in sure confidence and quiet repose (Psa 3:5).</p>
