## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>The historical occasion referred to by the title was probably during Absalom's rebellion (compare Sa2 15:23, Sa2 15:28; Sa2 16:2). David expresses an earnest desire for God's favor, and a confident expectation of realizing it in his deliverance and the ruin of his enemies. (Psa 63:1-11)</p>
<p>early . . . seek thee--earnestly (Isa 26:9). The figurative terms--</p>
<p>dry and thirsty--literally, "weary," denoting moral destitution, suited his outward circumstances.</p>
<p>soul--and--flesh--the whole man (Psa 16:9-10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 63:2](/en/kjv/psalms/63#v2)</div>
<p>The special object of desire was God's perfections as displayed in his worship (Psa 27:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 63:3](/en/kjv/psalms/63#v3)</div>
<p>Experiencing God's mercy, which exceeds all the blessings of life, his lips will be opened for his praise (Psa 51:15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 63:4](/en/kjv/psalms/63#v4)</div>
<p>Thus--literally, "Truly."</p>
<p>will I bless--praise Thee (Psa 34:1).</p>
<p>lift up my hands--in worship (compare Psa 28:2).</p>
<p>in thy name--in praise of Thy perfections.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 63:5](/en/kjv/psalms/63#v5)</div>
<p>Full spiritual blessings satisfy his desires, and acts of praise fill his thoughts and time.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 63:6](/en/kjv/psalms/63#v6)</div>
<p>night--as well as day. Past favors assure him of future, and hence he presses earnestly near to God, whose power sustains him (Psa 17:8; Psa 60:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 63:9](/en/kjv/psalms/63#v9)</div>
<p>those . . . to destroy it--or literally, "to ruin," or, "for ruin"; that is, such as seek to injure me (are) for ruin, appointed to it (compare Psa 35:8).</p>
<p>shall go . . . earth--into the grave, or, to death; as their bodies are represented as a portion for--</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 63:10](/en/kjv/psalms/63#v10)</div>
<p>foxes--literally, "jackals."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 63:11](/en/kjv/psalms/63#v11)</div>
<p>the king--that is, David himself, and all who reverence God, "shall share a glorious part," while treacherous foes shall be for ever silenced (Psa 62:4).</p>
