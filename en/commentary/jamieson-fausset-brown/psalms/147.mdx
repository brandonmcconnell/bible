<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:3](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v3)</div>
<p>Though applicable to the captive Israelites, this is a general and precious truth.</p>
<p>wounds--(Compare Margin).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:4](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v4)</div>
<p>God's power in nature (Isa 40:26-28, and often) is presented as a pledge of His power to help His people.</p>
<p>telleth . . . stars--what no man can do (Gen 15:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:6](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v6)</div>
<p>That power is put forth for the good of the meek and suffering pious, and confusion of the wicked (Psa 146:8-9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:7](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v7)</div>
<p>His providence supplies bountifully the wild animals in their mountain homes.</p>
<p>Sing . . . Lord--literally, "Answer the Lord," that is, in grateful praise to His goodness, thus declared in His acts.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:10](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v10)</div>
<p>The advantages afforded, as in war by the strength of the horse or the agility of man, do not incline God to favor any; but those who fear and, of course, trust Him, will obtain His approbation and aid.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:13](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v13)</div>
<p>strengthened . . . gates--or, means of defense against invaders,</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:14](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v14)</div>
<p>maketh . . . borders--or, territories (Gen 23:17; Isa 54:12).</p>
<p>filleth thee, &amp;c.--(Compare Margin).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:15](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v15)</div>
<p>God's Word, as a swift messenger, executes His purpose, for with Him to command is to perform (Gen 1:3; Psa 33:9), and He brings about the wonders of providence as easily as men cast crumbs.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:17](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v17)</div>
<p>morsels--used as to food (Gen 18:5), perhaps here denotes hail.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:19](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v19)</div>
<p>This mighty ruler and benefactor of heaven and earth is such especially to His chosen people, to whom alone (Deu 4:32-34) He has made known His will, while others have been left in darkness. Therefore unite in the great hallelujah.</p>
