## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>Upon Nehiloth--flutes or wind instruments. The writer begs to be heard, on the ground of God's regard for His covenant-people and true worshippers as contrasted with His holy hatred to the wicked. He prays for divine guidance, on account of his watchful, malignant, and deceitful enemies; and for their destruction as being also God's enemies. At the same time he expresses his confidence that God will extend aid to His people. (Psa 5:1-12)</p>
<p>meditation--moanings of that half-uttered form to which deep feeling gives rise--groanings, as in Rom 8:26-27.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 5:2](/en/kjv/psalms/5#v2)</div>
<p>Hearken--incline the ear (Psa 10:17; compare Psa 61:2) --give close attention.</p>
<p>my cry--that is, for help (Psa 61:2; Jer 8:19).</p>
<p>my King--thus by covenant relation interested in my cause.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 5:3](/en/kjv/psalms/5#v3)</div>
<p>direct--literally, "set in order," as the showbread was placed or set in order (Exo 40:23).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 5:4](/en/kjv/psalms/5#v4)</div>
<p>For, &amp;c.--God only regards sincere worshippers.</p>
<p>evil--or, "the evil man."</p>
<p>dwell--lodge, remain under protection.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 5:5](/en/kjv/psalms/5#v5)</div>
<p>foolish--vainglorious and insolent.</p>
<p>iniquity--especially such as denotes a negation, or defect, that is, of moral principle.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 5:6](/en/kjv/psalms/5#v6)</div>
<p>leasing--a lie.</p>
<p>the bloody . . . man--literally, "man of blood"--murderer.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 5:7](/en/kjv/psalms/5#v7)</div>
<p>But--as in Psa 2:6, literally, "and."</p>
<p>house-- (Ch1 9:23), the tabernacle.</p>
<p>temple--literally, "palace," applied to God's residence, the Holy of Holies (Sa1 3:3; Sa2 22:7); the inner part of the tabernacle.</p>
<p>toward--not in; the high priest alone was allowed to enter.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 5:8](/en/kjv/psalms/5#v8)</div>
<p>enemies--literally, "watchers" (Psa 27:11), hence special need of guidance.</p>
<p>in thy righteousness--an attribute implying faithfulness in promises as well as threatenings.</p>
<p>make thy way straight--that is, make the way of providence plain.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 5:9](/en/kjv/psalms/5#v9)</div>
<p>The wicked are not reliable because by nature they are full of wickedness, or literally, "wickednesses," of every kind (Rom 8:7).</p>
<p>sepulchre--a dwelling-place of corruption, emitting moral putridness.</p>
<p>flatter--or, "make smooth."</p>
<p>their tongue--speaks deceitfully.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 5:10](/en/kjv/psalms/5#v10)</div>
<p>Destroy--or, "condemn" them to destruction as guilty.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 5:11](/en/kjv/psalms/5#v11)</div>
<p>defendest--(compare Margin).</p>
<p>love thy name--Thy manifested perfections (Psa 9:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 5:12](/en/kjv/psalms/5#v12)</div>
<p>with favour--or, "acceptance," alluding to the favor shown to an acceptable offering and worshipper (Lev 7:18; Lev 19:7).</p>
<p>shield--(compare Psa 3:3).</p>

</div>
