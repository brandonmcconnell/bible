## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>In this Psalm the profession of the principles of his domestic and political government testifies, as well as actions in accordance with it, David's appreciation of God's mercy to him, and His judgment on his enemies: and thus he sings or celebrates God's dealings. (Psa 101:1-8)</p>
<p>He avows his sincere purpose, by God's aid, to act uprightly (Gen 17:1; Psa 18:30).</p>
