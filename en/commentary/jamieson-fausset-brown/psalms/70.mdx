## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>The Psalmist, probably in old age, appeals to God for help from his enemies, pleading his past favors, and stating his present need; and, in confidence of a hearing, he promises his grateful thanks and praise. (Psa. 71:1-24)</p>
<p>(Compare Psa 30:1-3).</p>
