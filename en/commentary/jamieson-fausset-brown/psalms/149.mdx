<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 149:2](/en/kjv/psalms/149#v2)</div>
<p>God had signalized His relation as a sovereign, in restoring them to their land.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 149:3](/en/kjv/psalms/149#v3)</div>
<p>in the dance-- (Psa 30:11). The dance is connected with other terms, expressive of the great joy of the occasion. The word may be rendered "lute," to which the other instruments are joined.</p>
<p>sing praises--or, sing and play.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 149:4](/en/kjv/psalms/149#v4)</div>
<p>taketh pleasure--literally, "accepts," alluding to acceptance of propitiatory offerings (compare Psa 147:11).</p>
<p>beautify, &amp;c.--adorn the humble with faith, hope, joy, and peace.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 149:5](/en/kjv/psalms/149#v5)</div>
<p>in glory--the honorable condition to which they are raised.</p>
<p>upon their beds--once a place of mourning (Psa 6:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 149:6](/en/kjv/psalms/149#v6)</div>
<p>high praises--or, "deeds." They shall go forth as religious warriors, as once religious laborers (Neh 4:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 149:7](/en/kjv/psalms/149#v7)</div>
<p>The destruction of the incorrigibly wicked attends the propagation of God's truth, so that the military successes of the Jews, after the captivity, typified the triumphs of the Gospel.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 149:9](/en/kjv/psalms/149#v9)</div>
<p>the judgment written--either in God's decrees, or perhaps as in Deu 32:41-43.</p>
<p>this honour--that is, to be thus employed, will be an honorable service, to be assigned</p>
<p>his saints--or, godly ones (Psa 16:3).</p>

</div>
