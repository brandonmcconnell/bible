## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>In view of the wonders of grace and righteousness displayed in God's salvation, the whole creation is invited to unite in praise. (Psa 98:1-9)</p>
<p>gotten . . . victory--literally, "made salvation," enabled Him to save His people.</p>
<p>right hand, and . . . arm--denote power.</p>
<p>holy arm--or, "arm of holiness," the power of His united moral perfections (Psa 22:3; Psa 32:11).</p>
