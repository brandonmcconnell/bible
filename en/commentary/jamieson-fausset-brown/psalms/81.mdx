## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>Before the great Judge, the judges of the earth are rebuked, exhorted, and threatened. (Psa 82:1-8)</p>
<p>congregation--(Compare Exo 12:3; Exo 16:1).</p>
<p>of the mighty--that is, of God, of His appointment.</p>
<p>the gods--or, "judges" (Exo 21:6; Exo 22:9), God's representatives.</p>
