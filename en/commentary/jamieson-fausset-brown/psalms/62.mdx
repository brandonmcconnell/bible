## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>To Jeduthun--(See on Psa 39:1, title). The general tone of this Psalm is expressive of confidence in God. Occasion is taken to remind the wicked of their sin, their ruin, and their meanness. (Psa 62:1-12)</p>
<p>waiteth--literally, "is silent," trusts submissively and confidently as a servant.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 62:2](/en/kjv/psalms/62#v2)</div>
<p>The titles applied to God often occur (Psa 9:9; Psa 18:2).</p>
<p>be greatly moved-- (Psa 10:6). No injury shall be permanent, though devised by enemies.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 62:3](/en/kjv/psalms/62#v3)</div>
<p>Their destruction will come; as a tottering wall they already are feeble and failing.</p>
<p>bowing wall shall ye be--better supply "are." Some propose to apply these phrases to describe the condition of "a man"--that is, the pious suffer: thus, "Will ye slay him," &amp;c.; but the other is a good sense.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 62:4](/en/kjv/psalms/62#v4)</div>
<p>his excellency--or, elevation to which God had raised him (Psa 4:2). This they try to do by lies and duplicity (Psa 5:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 62:5](/en/kjv/psalms/62#v5)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 62:1-2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 62:6](/en/kjv/psalms/62#v6)</div>
<p>not be moved--not at all; his confidence has increased.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 62:7](/en/kjv/psalms/62#v7)</div>
<p>rock of my strength--or strongest support (Psa 7:10; Psa 61:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 62:8](/en/kjv/psalms/62#v8)</div>
<p>pour out your heart--give full expression to feeling (Sa1 1:15; Job 30:16; Psa 42:4).</p>
<p>ye people--God's people.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 62:9](/en/kjv/psalms/62#v9)</div>
<p>No kind of men are reliable, compared with God (Isa 2:22; Jer 17:5).</p>
<p>altogether--alike, one as the other (Psa 34:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 62:10](/en/kjv/psalms/62#v10)</div>
<p>Not only are oppression and robbery, which are wicked means of wealth, no grounds of boasting; but even wealth, increasing lawfully, ought not to engross the heart.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 62:11](/en/kjv/psalms/62#v11)</div>
<p>once; twice--(as in Job 33:14; Job 40:5), are used to give emphasis to the sentiment. God's power is tempered by His mercy, which it also sustains.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 62:12](/en/kjv/psalms/62#v12)</div>
<p>for thou renderest--literally, "that Thou renderest," &amp;c., connected with "I heard this," as the phrase--"that power," &amp;c. &#91;Psa 62:11&#93; --teaching that by His power He can show both mercy and justice.</p>

</div>
