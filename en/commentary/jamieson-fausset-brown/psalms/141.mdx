## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>This and the remaining Psalms have been represented as specially designed to celebrate the rebuilding of Jerusalem (compare Neh 6:16; Neh 12:27). They all open and close with the stirring call for praise. This one specially declares God's providential care towards all creatures, and particularly His people. (Psa. 147:1-20)</p>
<p>(Compare Psa 92:1; Psa 135:3).</p>
