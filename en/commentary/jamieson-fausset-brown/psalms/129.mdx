## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>This Psalm, while expressive of David's pious feelings on assuming the royal office, teaches the humble, submissive temper of a true child of God. (Psa 131:1-3)</p>
<p>eyes lofty--a sign of pride (Psa 18:27).</p>
<p>exercise myself--literally, "walk in," or "meddle with."</p>
