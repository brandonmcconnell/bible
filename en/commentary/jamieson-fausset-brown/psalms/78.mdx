## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>This Psalm, like the seventy-fourth, probably depicts the desolations of the Chaldeans (Jer 52:12-24). It comprises the usual complaint, prayer, and promised thanks for relief. (Psa 79:1-13)</p>
<p>(Compare Psa 74:2-7).</p>
