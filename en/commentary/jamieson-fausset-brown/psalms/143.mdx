## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>This Psalm sustains a close connection with the foregoing. The chosen people are exhorted to praise God, in view of past favors, and also future victories over enemies, of which they are impliedly assured. (Psa 149:1-9)</p>
<p>(Compare Psa 96:1).</p>
