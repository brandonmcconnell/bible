## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>David thanks God for His benefits, and anticipating a wider extension of God's glory by His means, assures himself of His continued presence and faithfulness. (Psa 138:1-8)</p>
<p>I will praise thee with my whole heart--(Compare Psa 9:1).</p>
<p>before the gods--whether angels (Psa 8:5); or princes (Exo 21:6; Psa 82:6); or idols (Psa 97:7); denotes a readiness to worship the true God alone, and a contempt of all other objects of worship.</p>
