## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>Upon Jonath-elem-rechokim--literally, "upon the dove of silence" of distant places; either denoting a melody (see on Psa 9:1) of that name, to which this Psalm was to be performed; or it is an enigmatical form of denoting the subject, as given in the history referred to (Sa1 21:11, &amp;c.), David being regarded as an uncomplaining, meek dove, driven from his native home to wander in exile. Beset by domestic and foreign foes, David appeals confidently to God, recites his complaints, and closes with joyful and assured anticipations of God's continued help. (Psa 56:1-13)</p>
<p>would swallow--literally, "pants as a raging beast" (Act 9:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 56:2](/en/kjv/psalms/56#v2)</div>
<p>enemies--watchers (Psa 54:5).</p>
<p>most High--As it is not elsewhere used absolutely for God, some render the word here, arrogantly, or proudly, as qualifying "those who fight," &amp;c.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 56:3](/en/kjv/psalms/56#v3)</div>
<p>in--or literally, "unto."</p>
<p>thee--to whom he turns in trouble.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 56:4](/en/kjv/psalms/56#v4)</div>
<p>in God . . . his word--By His grace or aid (Psa 60:12; Psa 108:13), or, "I will boast in God as to His word"; in either case His word is the special matter and cause of praise.</p>
<p>flesh--for mankind (Psa 65:2; Isa 31:3), intimating frailty.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 56:5](/en/kjv/psalms/56#v5)</div>
<p>A vivid picture of the conduct of malicious enemies.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 56:7](/en/kjv/psalms/56#v7)</div>
<p>Shall they escape? &amp;c.--or better, "Their escape is by iniquity."</p>
<p>cast . . . people--humble those who so proudly oppose Thy servant.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 56:8](/en/kjv/psalms/56#v8)</div>
<p>God is mindful of his exile and remembers his tears. The custom of bottling the tears of mourners as a memorial, which has existed in some Eastern nations, may explain the figure.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 56:9](/en/kjv/psalms/56#v9)</div>
<p>God is for me--or, "on my side" (Psa 118:6; Psa 124:1-2); hence he is sure of the repulse of his foes.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 56:12](/en/kjv/psalms/56#v12)</div>
<p>I will render praises--will pay what I have vowed.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 56:13](/en/kjv/psalms/56#v13)</div>
<p>The question implies an affirmative answer, drawn from past experience.</p>
<p>falling--as from a precipice.</p>
<p>before God--in His favor during life.</p>
