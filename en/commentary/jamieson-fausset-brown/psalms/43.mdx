## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>Excepting the recurrence of the refrain, there is no good reason to suppose this a part of the preceding, though the scope is the same. It has always been placed separate. (Psa 43:1-5)</p>
<p>Judge--or, "vindicate" (Psa 10:18).</p>
<p>plead, &amp;c.-- (Psa 35:1).</p>
<p>ungodly--neither in character or condition objects of God's favor (compare Psa 4:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 43:2](/en/kjv/psalms/43#v2)</div>
<p>God of my strength--by covenant relation my stronghold (Psa 18:1).</p>
<p>cast me off--in scorn.</p>
<p>because--or, "in," that is, in such circumstances of oppression.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 43:3](/en/kjv/psalms/43#v3)</div>
<p>light--as in Psa 27:1.</p>
<p>truth--or, "faithfulness" (Psa 25:5), manifest it by fulfilling promises. Light and truth are personified as messengers who will bring him to the privileged place of worship.</p>
<p>tabernacles--plural, in allusion to the various courts.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 43:4](/en/kjv/psalms/43#v4)</div>
<p>the altar--as the chief place of worship. The mention of the harp suggests the prominence of praise in his offering.</p>
