## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>On Neginoth (See on Psa 4:1, title) upon Sheminith--the eighth--an instrument for the eighth key; or, more probably, the bass, as it is contrasted with Alamoth (the treble, Psa 46:1) in Ch1 15:20-21. In deep affliction the Psalmist appeals to God's mercy for relief from chastisement, which otherwise must destroy him, and thus disable him for God's service. Sure of a gracious answer, he triumphantly rebukes his foes. (Psa 6:1-10)</p>
<p>He owns his ill desert in begging a relief from chastisement.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 6:2](/en/kjv/psalms/6#v2)</div>
<p>I am weak--as a culled plant (Isa 24:4).</p>
<p>my bones--the very frame.</p>
<p>are vexed-- (Psa 2:5) --shaken with fear.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 6:3](/en/kjv/psalms/6#v3)</div>
<p>how long?--shall this be so (compare Psa 79:5).</p>
<p>but--or, "and."</p>
<p>thou--The sentence is incomplete as expressive of strong emotion.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 6:4](/en/kjv/psalms/6#v4)</div>
<p>Return--that is, to my relief; or, "turn," as now having His face averted.</p>
<p>for thy mercies' sake--to illustrate Thy mercy.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 6:5](/en/kjv/psalms/6#v5)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 115:17-18; Isa 38:18). There is no incredulity as to a future state. The contrast is between this scene of life, and the grave or Sheol, the unseen world of the dead.</p>
<p>give . . . thanks--or, "praise for mercies."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 6:6](/en/kjv/psalms/6#v6)</div>
<p>By a strong figure the abundance as well as intensity of grief is depicted.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 6:7](/en/kjv/psalms/6#v7)</div>
<p>consumed--or, "has failed," denoting general debility (Psa 13:3; Psa 38:10).</p>
<p>waxeth old--or, "dim."</p>
<p>grief--mingled with indignation.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 6:8](/en/kjv/psalms/6#v8)</div>
<p>Assured of God's hearing, he suddenly defies his enemies by an address indicating that he no longer fears them.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 6:10](/en/kjv/psalms/6#v10)</div>
<p>and knows they will be disappointed and in their turn (compare Psa 6:3) be terror-stricken or confounded.</p>

</div>
