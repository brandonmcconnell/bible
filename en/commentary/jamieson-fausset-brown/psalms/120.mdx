## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>This Psalm might well express the sacred joy of the pilgrims on entering the holy city, where praise, as the religious as well as civil metropolis, is celebrated, and for whose prosperity, as representing the Church, prayer is offered. (Psa 122:1-9)</p>
<p>Our feet shall stand--literally, "are standing."</p>
