## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>Praise is given to God for victory, perhaps that recorded (Ch2 20:20-30); and His dominions over all people, Jews and Gentiles, is asserted. (Psa 47:1-9)</p>
<p>clap . . . hands . . . people--literally, "peoples," or "nations" (compare Deu 32:43; Psa 18:49; Psa 98:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 47:2](/en/kjv/psalms/47#v2)</div>
<p>His universal sovereignty now exists, and will be made known.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 47:3](/en/kjv/psalms/47#v3)</div>
<p>under us--that is, His saints; Israel's temporal victories were types of the spiritual conquests of the true Church.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 47:4](/en/kjv/psalms/47#v4)</div>
<p>He shall . . . inheritance--the heathen to be possessed by His Church (Psa 2:8), as Canaan by the Jews.</p>
<p>excellency of Jacob--literally, "pride," or, that in which he glories (not necessarily, though often, in a bad sense), the privileges of the chosen people--</p>
<p>whom he loved--His love being the sole cause of granting them.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 47:5](/en/kjv/psalms/47#v5)</div>
<p>God, victorious over His enemies, reascends to heaven, amid the triumphant praises of His people, who celebrate His sovereign dominion. This sovereignty is what the Psalm teaches; hence he adds,</p>
<p>sing . . . praises with understanding--literally, "sing and play an instructive (Psalm)." The whole typifies Christ's ascension (compare Psa 68:18).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 47:8](/en/kjv/psalms/47#v8)</div>
<p>The instruction continued.</p>
<p>throne of . . . holiness--or, "holy throne" (see on Psa 2:6; Psa 23:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 47:9](/en/kjv/psalms/47#v9)</div>
<p>princes--who represent peoples. For--</p>
<p>even--supply, "as," or, "to"--that is, they all become united under covenant with Abraham's God.</p>
<p>shields--as in Hos 4:18, "rulers" &#91;Margin&#93;.</p>
<p>Next: Psalms Chapter 48</p>
</div>
