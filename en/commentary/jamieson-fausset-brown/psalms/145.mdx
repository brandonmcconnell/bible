<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:3](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v3)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 18:3; Psa 48:1).</p>
<p>greatness--as displayed in His works.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:4](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v4)</div>
<p>shall declare--literally, "they shall declare," that is, all generations.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:5](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v5)</div>
<p>I will speak--or, "muse" (Psa 77:12; Psa 119:15).</p>
<p>thy wondrous works--or, "words of thy wonders," that is, which described them (Psa 105:27, Margin).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:6](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v6)</div>
<p>terrible acts--which produce dread or fear.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:7](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v7)</div>
<p>memory-- (Psa 6:5), remembrance, or what causes to be remembered.</p>
<p>righteousness--as in Psa 143:1, goodness according to covenant engagement.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:8](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v8)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 103:8; Psa 111:4).</p>
<p>over all, &amp;c.--rests on all His works.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:10](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v10)</div>
<p>bless--as in Psa 145:1, to praise with reverence, more than merely to praise.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:11](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v11)</div>
<p>The declaration of God's glory is for the extension of His knowledge and perfections in the world.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:13](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v13)</div>
<p>(Compare Dan 4:3, Dan 4:34).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:14](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v14)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 37:17; Psa 54:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:15](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v15)</div>
<p>eyes of . . . thee--or, look with expecting faith (Psa 104:27-28).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:17](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v17)</div>
<p>holy . . . works--literally, "merciful" or "kind, goodness" (Psa 144:2) is the corresponding noun.</p>
<p>righteous--in a similar relation of meaning to "righteousness" (Psa 145:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:18](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v18)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 34:7, Psa 34:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:20](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v20)</div>
<p>Those who fear Him (Psa 145:19) are those who are here said to love Him.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 145:21](/en/kjv/psalms/145#v21)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 33:21).</p>
<p>all flesh-- (Psa 65:2). The Psalm ends, as it began, with ascriptions of praise, in which the pious will ever delight to join.</p>

</div>
