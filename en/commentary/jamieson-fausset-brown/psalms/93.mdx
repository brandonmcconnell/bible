## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>The writer, appealing to God in view of the oppression of enemies, rebukes them for their wickedness and folly, and encourages himself, in the confidence that God will punish evildoers, and favor His people. (Psa. 94:1-23)</p>
<p>God's revenge is His judicial infliction of righteous punishment.</p>
