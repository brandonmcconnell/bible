## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>This Psalm may be regarded as an exposition of Psa 111:10, presenting the happiness of those who fear and obey God, and contrasting the fate of the ungodly. (Psa 112:1-10)</p>
<p>True fear produces obedience and this happiness.</p>
