## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>With a general strain of confidence, hope, and joy, especially in God's worship, in the midst of dangers, the Psalmist introduces prayer for divine help and guidance. (Psa 27:1-14)</p>
<p>light--is a common figure for comfort.</p>
<p>strength--or, "stronghold"--affording security against all violence. The interrogations give greater vividness to the negation implied.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 27:2](/en/kjv/psalms/27#v2)</div>
<p>eat . . . my flesh-- (Job 19:22; Psa 14:4). The allusion to wild beasts illustrates their rapacity.</p>
<p>they stumbled--"they" is emphatic; not I, but they were destroyed.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 27:3](/en/kjv/psalms/27#v3)</div>
<p>In the greatest dangers.</p>
<p>in this--that is, then, in such extremity.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 27:4](/en/kjv/psalms/27#v4)</div>
<p>The secret of his confidence is his delight in communion with God (Psa 16:11; Psa 23:6), beholding the harmony of His perfections, and seeking His favor in His temple or palace; a term applicable to the tabernacle (compare Psa 5:7). There he is safe (Psa 31:21; Psa 61:5). The figure is changed in the last clause, but the sentiment is the same.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 27:6](/en/kjv/psalms/27#v6)</div>
<p>head be lifted up--I shall be placed beyond the reach of my enemies. Hence he avows his purpose of rendering joyful thank offerings.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 27:7](/en/kjv/psalms/27#v7)</div>
<p>Still pressing need extorts prayer for help.</p>
<p>cry with my voice--denotes earnestness. Other things equal, Christians in earnest pray audibly, even in secret.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 27:8](/en/kjv/psalms/27#v8)</div>
<p>The meaning is clear, though the construction in a literal translation is obscure. The English Version supplies the implied clause. To seek God's face is to seek His favor (Psa 105:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 27:9](/en/kjv/psalms/27#v9)</div>
<p>Hide not, &amp;c.-- (Psa 4:6; Psa 22:24). Against rejection he pleads former mercy and love.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 27:10](/en/kjv/psalms/27#v10)</div>
<p>In the extremity of earthly destitution (Psa 31:11; Psa 38:11), God provides (compare Mat 25:35).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 27:11](/en/kjv/psalms/27#v11)</div>
<p>thy way--of providence.</p>
<p>a plain path-- (Psa 26:12).</p>
<p>enemies--literally, "watchers for my fall" (Psa 5:8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 27:12](/en/kjv/psalms/27#v12)</div>
<p>will--literally, "soul," "desire" (Psa 35:25).</p>
<p>enemies--literally, "oppressors." Falsehood aids cruelty against him.</p>
<p>breathe out--as being filled with it (Act 9:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 27:13](/en/kjv/psalms/27#v13)</div>
<p>The strong emotion is indicated by the incomplete sentence, for which the English Version supplies a proper clause; or, omitting that, and rendering, "yet I believed," &amp;c., the contrast of his faith and his danger is expressed.</p>
<p>to see--is to experience (Psa 22:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 27:14](/en/kjv/psalms/27#v14)</div>
<p>Wait, &amp;c.--in confident expectation. The last clause is, literally, "and wait," &amp;c., as if expecting new measures of help.</p>

</div>
