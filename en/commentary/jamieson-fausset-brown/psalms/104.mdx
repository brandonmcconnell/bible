## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>After an exhortation to praise God, addressed especially to the chosen people, the writer presents the special reason for praise, in a summary of their history from the calling of Abraham to their settlement in Canaan, and reminds them that their obedience was the end of all God's gracious dealings. (Psa. 105:1-45)</p>
<p>call . . . name-- (Psa 79:6; Rom 10:13). Call on Him, according to His historically manifested glory. After the example of Abraham, who, as often as God acquired for Himself a name in guiding him, called in solemn worship upon the name of the Lord (Gen 12:8; Gen 13:4).</p>
<p>among the people--or, "peoples" (Psa 18:49).</p>
<p>deeds--or, "wonders" (Psa 103:7).</p>
</div>
