## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>Upon Alamoth--most probably denotes the treble, or part sung by female voices, the word meaning "virgins"; and which was sung with some appropriately keyed instrument (compare Ch1 15:19-21; see on Psa 6:1, title). The theme may be stated in LUTHER'S well-known words, "A mighty fortress is our God." The great deliverance (Kg2 19:35; Isa 37:36) may have occasioned its composition. (Psa 46:1-11)</p>
<p>refuge--literally, "a place of trust" (Psa 2:12).</p>
<p>strength-- (Psa 18:2).</p>
<p>present help--literally, "a help He has been found exceedingly."</p>
<p>trouble--as in Psa 18:7.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 46:2](/en/kjv/psalms/46#v2)</div>
<p>The most violent civil commotions are illustrated by the greatest physical commotions.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 46:3](/en/kjv/psalms/46#v3)</div>
<p>swelling--well represents the pride and haughtiness of insolent foes.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 46:4](/en/kjv/psalms/46#v4)</div>
<p>God's favor is denoted by a river (compare Psa 36:8; Zac 14:8; Rev 22:1).</p>
<p>city of God, the holy place--His earthly residence, Jerusalem and the temple (compare Psa 2:6; Psa 3:4; Psa 20:2; Psa 48:2, &amp;c.). God's favor, like a river whose waters are conducted in channels, is distributed to all parts of His Church.</p>
<p>most High--denoting His supremacy (Psa 17:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 46:5](/en/kjv/psalms/46#v5)</div>
<p>right early--literally, "at the turn of morning," or change from night to day, a critical time (Psa 30:5; compare Isa 37:36).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 46:6](/en/kjv/psalms/46#v6)</div>
<p>(Compare Psa 46:2).</p>
<p>earth melted--all powers dissolved by His mere word (Psa 75:3; Hos 2:22).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 46:7](/en/kjv/psalms/46#v7)</div>
<p>with us--on our side; His presence is terror to our enemies, safety to us.</p>
<p>refuge--high place (Psa 9:9; compare also Psa 24:6, Psa 24:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 46:8](/en/kjv/psalms/46#v8)</div>
<p>what desolations--literally, "who hath put desolations," destroying our enemies.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 46:9](/en/kjv/psalms/46#v9)</div>
<p>The usual weapons of war (Psa 7:12), as well as those using them, are brought to an end.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 46:10](/en/kjv/psalms/46#v10)</div>
<p>Be still, &amp;c.--literally, "Leave off to oppose Me and vex My people. I am over all for their safety." (Compare Isa 2:11; Eph 1:22).</p>
