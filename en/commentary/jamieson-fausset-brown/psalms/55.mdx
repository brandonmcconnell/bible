## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>In great terror on account of enemies, and grieved by the treachery of a friend, the Psalmist offers an earnest prayer for relief. He mingles confident assurances of divine favor to himself with invocations and predictions of God's avenging judgments on the wicked. The tone suits David's experience, both in the times of Saul and Absalom, though perhaps neither was exclusively before his mind. (Psa. 55:1-23)</p>
<p>hide not thyself, &amp;c.--(compare Psa 13:1; Psa 27:9), withhold not help.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:2](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v2)</div>
<p>The terms of the last clause express full indulgence of grief.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:3](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v3)</div>
<p>oppression--literally, "persecution."</p>
<p>they . . . iniquity--literally, "they make evil doings slide upon me."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:4](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v4)</div>
<p>express great alarm.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:5](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v5)</div>
<p>come upon--or literally, "into."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:6](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v6)</div>
<p>be at rest--literally, "dwell," that is, permanently.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:7](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v7)</div>
<p>Even a wilderness is a safer place than exposure to such evils, terrible as storm and tempest.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:9](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v9)</div>
<p>Destroy--literally, "swallow" (Psa 21:9).</p>
<p>divide their tongues--or, "confound their speech," and hence their counsels (Gen 11:7).</p>
<p>the city--perhaps Jerusalem, the scene of anarchy.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:10](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v10)</div>
<p>which is described in detail (compare Psa 7:14-16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:11](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v11)</div>
<p>Wickedness--literally, "Mischief," evils resulting from others (Psa 5:9; Psa 52:2, Psa 52:7).</p>
<p>streets--or literally, "wide places," markets, courts of justice, and any public place.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:12](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v12)</div>
<p>This description of treachery does not deny, but aggravates, the injury from enemies.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:13](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v13)</div>
<p>guide--literally, "friend" (Pro 16:28; Pro 17:9).</p>
<p>acquaintance--in Hebrew, a yet more intimate associate.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:14](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v14)</div>
<p>in company--literally, "with a crowd," in a festal procession.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:15](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v15)</div>
<p>Let death, &amp;c.--or, "Desolations are on them."</p>
<p>let them go--literally, "they will go."</p>
<p>quick--or, living in the midst of life, death will come (compare Num 16:33).</p>
<p>among them--or, "within them," in their hearts (Psa 5:9; Psa 49:11).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:16](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v16)</div>
<p>God answers his constant and repeated prayers.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:18](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v18)</div>
<p>many with me--that is, by the context, fighting with me.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:19](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v19)</div>
<p>God hears the wicked in wrath.</p>
<p>abideth--or, "sitteth."</p>
<p>of old--enthroned as a sovereign.</p>
<p>Because . . . no changes--Prosperity hardens them (Psa 73:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:20](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v20)</div>
<p>The treachery is aggravated by hypocrisy. The changes of number, Psa 55:15, Psa 55:23, and here, enliven the picture, and imply that the chief traitor and his accomplices are in view together.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:22](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v22)</div>
<p>thy burden--literally, "gift," what is assigned you.</p>
<p>he shall sustain--literally, "supply food," and so all need (Psa 37:25; Mat 6:11).</p>
<p>to be moved--from the secure position of His favor (compare Psa 10:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:23](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v23)</div>
<p>bloody . . . days--(compare Psa 5:6; Psa 51:14), deceit and murderous dispositions often united. The threat is directed specially (not as a general truth) against the wicked, then in the writer's view.</p>

</div>
