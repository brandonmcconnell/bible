## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>On the title compare Sa1 21:13. Abimelech was the general name of the sovereign (Gen 20:2). After celebrating God's gracious dealings with him, the Psalmist exhorts others to make trial of His providential care, instructing them how to secure it. He then contrasts God's care of His people and His punitive providence towards the wicked. (Psa. 34:1-22)</p>
<p>Even in distress, which excites supplication, there is always matter for praising and thanking God (compare Eph 5:20; Phi 4:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:2](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v2)</div>
<p>make her boast--"glory" (Psa 105:3; compare Gal 6:14).</p>
<p>humble--"the pious," as in Psa 9:12; Psa 25:9.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:3](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v3)</div>
<p>magnify the Lord--ascribe greatness to Him, an act of praise.</p>
<p>together--"alike" (Psa 33:15), or, equally, without exception.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:4](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v4)</div>
<p>delivered . . . fears--as well as actual evil (Psa 64:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:5](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v5)</div>
<p>God's favor to the pious generally, and to himself specially, is celebrated.</p>
<p>looked--with desire for help.</p>
<p>lightened--or, "brightened," expressing joy, opposed to the downcast features of those who are ashamed or disappointed (Psa 25:2-3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:6](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v6)</div>
<p>This poor man--literally, "humble," himself as a specimen of such.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:7](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v7)</div>
<p>angel--of the covenant (Isa 63:9), of whom as a leader of God's host (Jos 5:14; Kg1 22:19), the phrase--</p>
<p>encampeth, &amp;c.--is appropriate; or, "angel" used collectively for angels (Heb 1:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:8](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v8)</div>
<p>taste and see--try and experience.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:9](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v9)</div>
<p>that fear him--who are pious--fear and love (Pro 1:7; Pro 9:10).</p>
<p>saints--consecrated to His service (Isa 40:31).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:10](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v10)</div>
<p>not want any good--"good" is emphatic; they may be afflicted (compare Psa 34:10); but this may be a good (Co2 4:17-18; Heb 12:10-11).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:11](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v11)</div>
<p>children--subjects of instruction (Pro 1:8, Pro 1:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:12](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v12)</div>
<p>What man--Whoever desires the blessings of piety, let him attend.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:13](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v13)</div>
<p>Sins of thought included in those of speech (Luk 6:45), avoiding evil and doing good in our relations to men are based on a right relation to God.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:15](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v15)</div>
<p>eyes of the Lord are upon-- (Psa 32:8; Psa 33:18).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:16](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v16)</div>
<p>face . . . against--opposed to them (Lev 17:10; Lev 20:3).</p>
<p>cut off the remembrance--utterly destroy (Psa 109:13).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:17](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v17)</div>
<p>Humble penitents are objects of God's special tender regard (Psa 51:19; Isa 57:15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:20](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v20)</div>
<p>bones--framework of the body.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:21](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v21)</div>
<p>Contrast in the destiny of righteous and wicked; the former shall be delivered and never come into condemnation (Joh 5:24; Rom 8:1); the latter are left under condemnation and desolate.</p>
