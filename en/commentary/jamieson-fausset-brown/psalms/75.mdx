## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>On Neginoth--(See on Psa 4:1, title). This Psalm commemorates what the preceding anticipates: God's deliverance of His people by a signal interposition of power against their enemies. The occasion was probably the events narrated in Kg2 19:35; Isa. 37:1-28. (Compare Psa 46:1-11). (Psa 76:1-12)</p>
<p>These well-known terms denote God's people and Church and His intimate and glorious relations to them.</p>
