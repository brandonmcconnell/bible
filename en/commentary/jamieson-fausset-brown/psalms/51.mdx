## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>On the occasion, compare Sa2 11:12. The Psalm illustrates true repentance, in which are comprised conviction, confession, sorrow, prayer for mercy, and purposes of amendment, and it is accompanied by a lively faith. (Psa. 51:1-19)</p>
<p>A plea for mercy is a confession of guilt.</p>
<p>blot out--as from a register.</p>
<p>transgressions--literally, "rebellions" (Psa 19:13; Psa 32:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:2](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v2)</div>
<p>Wash me--Purity as well as pardon is desired by true penitents.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:3](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v3)</div>
<p>For . . . before me--Conviction precedes forgiveness; and, as a gift of God, is a plea for it (Sa2 12:13; Psa 32:5; Jo1 1:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:4](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v4)</div>
<p>Against thee--chiefly, and as sins against others are violations of God's law, in one sense only.</p>
<p>that . . . judgest--that is, all palliation of his crime is excluded; it is the design in making this confession to recognize God's justice, however severe the sentence.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:5](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v5)</div>
<p>His guilt was aggravated by his essential, native sinfulness, which is as contrary to God's requisitions of inward purity as are outward sins to those for right conduct.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:6](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v6)</div>
<p>thou shalt make, &amp;c.--may be taken to express God's gracious purpose in view of His strict requisition; a purpose of which David might have availed himself as a check to his native love for sin, and, in not doing so, aggravated his guilt.</p>
<p>truth . . . and . . .wisdom--are terms often used for piety (compare Job 28:28; Psa 119:30).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:7](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v7)</div>
<p>A series of prayers for forgiveness and purifying.</p>
<p>Purge . . . hyssop--The use of this plant in the ritual (Exo 12:22; Num 19:6, Num 19:18) suggests the idea of atonement as prominent here; "purge" refers to vicarious satisfaction (Num 19:17-20).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:8](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v8)</div>
<p>Make . . . joy--by forgiving me, which will change distress to joy.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:9](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v9)</div>
<p>Hide, &amp;c.--Turn from beholding.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:10](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v10)</div>
<p>Create--a work of almighty power.</p>
<p>in me--literally, "to me," or, "for me"; bestow as a gift, a heart free from taint of sin (Psa 24:4; Psa 73:1).</p>
<p>renew--implies that he had possessed it; the essential principle of a new nature had not been lost, but its influence interrupted (Luk 22:32); for Psa 51:11 shows that he had not lost God's presence and Spirit (Sa1 16:13), though he had lost the "joy of his salvation" (Psa 51:12), for whose return he prays.</p>
<p>right spirit--literally, "constant," "firm," not yielding to temptation.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:12](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v12)</div>
<p>free spirit--"thy" ought not to be supplied, for the word "free" is, literally, "willing," and "spirit" is that of David. "Let a willing spirit uphold me," that is, with a soul willingly conformed to God's law, he would be preserved in a right course of conduct.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:13](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v13)</div>
<p>Then--Such will be the effect of this gracious work.</p>
<p>ways--of providence and human duty (Psa 18:21, Psa 18:30; Psa 32:8; Luk 22:32).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:14](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v14)</div>
<p>Deliver--or, "Free me" (Psa 39:8) from the guilt of murder (Sa2 12:9-10; Psa 5:6).</p>
<p>righteousness--as in Psa 7:17; Psa 31:1.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:15](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v15)</div>
<p>open . . . lips--by removing my sense of guilt.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:16](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v16)</div>
<p>Praise is better than sacrifice (Psa 50:14), and implying faith, penitence, and love, glorifies God. In true penitents the joys of pardon mingle with sorrow for sin.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:18](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v18)</div>
<p>Do good, &amp;c.--Visit not my sin on Thy Church.</p>
<p>build . . . walls--is to show favor; compare Psa 89:40, for opposite form and idea.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 51:19](/en/kjv/psalms/51#v19)</div>
<p>God reconciled, material sacrifices will be acceptable (Psa 4:5; compare Isa 1:11-17).</p>
<p>Next: Psalms Chapter 52</p>
</div>

</div>
