## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>A prayer that, by God's blessing on His people, His salvation and praise may be extended over the earth. (Psa 67:1-7)</p>
<p>cause his face to shine--show us favor (Num 6:24-25; Psa 31:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 67:2](/en/kjv/psalms/67#v2)</div>
<p>thy way--of gracious dealing (Isa 55:8), as explained by--</p>
<p>saving health--or literally, "salvation."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 67:3](/en/kjv/psalms/67#v3)</div>
<p>Thanks will be rendered for the blessings of His wise and holy government (compare Isa 2:3-4; Isa 11:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 67:6](/en/kjv/psalms/67#v6)</div>
<p>The blessings of a fruitful harvest are mentioned as types of greater and spiritual blessings, under which all nations shall fear and love God.</p>
