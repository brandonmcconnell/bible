## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>This triumphal song was probably occasioned by the same event as the forty-sixth &#91;see on Psa 46:1, title&#93;. The writer celebrates the glory of the Church, as the means of spiritual blessing to the nation. (Psa 87:1-7)</p>
<p>His--that is, God's</p>
<p>foundation--or, what He has founded, that is, Zion (Isa 14:32).</p>
<p>is in the holy mountains--the location of Zion, in the wide sense, for the capital, or Jerusalem, being on several hills.</p>
