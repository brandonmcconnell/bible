## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>On title, see Introduction and see on Psa 6:1. The Psalmist laments the decrease of good men. The pride and deceit of the wicked provokes God's wrath, whose promise to avenge the cause of pious sufferers will be verified even amidst prevailing iniquity. (Psa 12:1-8)</p>
<p>the faithful--or literally, "faithfulness" (Psa 31:23).</p>
</div>
