## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>The general tone of this Psalm is that of prayer for help from enemies. Distress, however, exciting a sense of sin, humble confession, supplication for pardon, preservation from sin, and divine guidance, are prominent topics. (Psa. 25:1-22)</p>
<p>lift up my soul-- (Psa 24:4; Psa 86:4), set my affections (compare Col 3:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:2](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v2)</div>
<p>not be ashamed--by disappointment of hopes of relief.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:3](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v3)</div>
<p>The prayer generalized as to all who wait on God--that is, who expect His favor. On the other hand, the disappointment of the perfidious, who, unprovoked, have done evil, is invoked (compare Sa2 22:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:4](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v4)</div>
<p>On the ground of former favor, he invokes divine guidance, according to God's gracious ways of dealing and faithfulness.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:6](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v6)</div>
<p>Confessing past and present sins, he pleads for mercy, not on palliations of sin, but on God's well-known benevolence.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:8](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v8)</div>
<p>upright--acting according to His promise.</p>
<p>sinners--the general term, limited by the</p>
<p>meek--who are penitent.</p>
<p>the way--and his way--God's way of providence.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:9](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v9)</div>
<p>in judgment--rightly.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:10](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v10)</div>
<p>paths--similar sense--His modes of dealing (compare Psa 25:4).</p>
<p>mercy and truth-- (Job 14:1-22), God's grace in promising and faithfulness in performing.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:11](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v11)</div>
<p>God's perfections of love, mercy, goodness, and truth are manifested (his name, compare Psa 9:10) in pardoning sin, and the greatness of sin renders pardon more needed.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:12](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v12)</div>
<p>What he asks for himself is the common lot of all the pious.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:13](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v13)</div>
<p>inherit the earth--(compare Mat 5:5). The phrase, alluding to the promise of Canaan, expresses all the blessings included in that promise, temporal as well as spiritual.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:14](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v14)</div>
<p>The reason of the blessing explained--the pious enjoy communion with God (compare Pro 3:21, Pro 3:12), and, of course, learn His gracious terms of pardon.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:15](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v15)</div>
<p>His trust in God is fixed.</p>
<p>net--is frequently used as a figure for dangers by enemies (Psa 9:15; Psa 10:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:16](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v16)</div>
<p>A series of earnest appeals for aid because God had seemed to desert him (compare Psa 13:1; Psa 17:13, &amp;c.), his sins oppressed him, his enemies had enlarged his troubles and were multiplied, increasing in hate and violence (Psa 9:8; Psa 18:48).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:20](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v20)</div>
<p>keep my soul-- (Psa 16:1).</p>
<p>put my trust--flee for refuge (Psa 2:12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:21](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v21)</div>
<p>In conscious innocence of the faults charged by his enemies, he confidently commits his cause to God. Some refer--</p>
<p>integrity, &amp;c.--to God, meaning His covenant faithfulness. This sense, though good, is an unusual application of the terms.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 25:22](/en/kjv/psalms/25#v22)</div>
<p>Extend these blessings to all Thy people in all their distresses.</p>
<p>Next: Psalms Chapter 26</p>
</div>

</div>
