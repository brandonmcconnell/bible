## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>The writer invites all men to unite in praise, cites some striking occasions for it, promises special acts of thanksgiving, and celebrates God's great mercy. (Psa. 66:1-20)</p>
<p>Make . . . noise--or, "Shout."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:2](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v2)</div>
<p>his name--as in Psa 29:2.</p>
<p>make his praise glorious--literally, "place honor, His praise," or, "as to His praise"; that is, let His praise be such as will glorify Him, or, be honorable to Him.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:3](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v3)</div>
<p>A specimen of the praise.</p>
<p>How terrible--(Compare Psa 65:8).</p>
<p>submit--(Compare Margin), show a forced subjection (Psa 18:44), produced by terror.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:5](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v5)</div>
<p>The terrible works illustrated in Israel's history (Exo 14:21). By this example let rebels be admonished.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:7](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v7)</div>
<p>behold the nations--watch their conduct.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:8](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v8)</div>
<p>Here is, perhaps, cited a case of recent deliverance.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:9](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v9)</div>
<p>holdeth . . . in life--literally, "putteth our soul in life"; that is, out of danger (Psa 30:3; Psa 49:15).</p>
<p>to be moved--(Compare Psa 10:6; Psa 55:22).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:10](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v10)</div>
<p>Out of severe trials, God had brought them to safety (compare Isa 48:10; Pe1 1:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:11](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v11)</div>
<p>affliction--literally, "pressure," or, as in Psa 55:3, "oppression," which, laid on the</p>
<p>loins--the seat of strength (Deu 33:11), enfeebles the frame.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:12](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v12)</div>
<p>men to ride over our heads--made us to pass.</p>
<p>through fire, &amp;c.--figures describing prostration and critical dangers (compare Isa 43:2; Eze 36:12).</p>
<p>wealthy--literally, "overflowing," or, "irrigated," and hence fertile.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:13](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v13)</div>
<p>These full and varied offerings constitute the payment of vows (Lev 22:18-23).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:15](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v15)</div>
<p>I will offer--literally, "make to ascend," alluding to the smoke of burnt offering, which explains the use of "incense."</p>
<p>incense--elsewhere always denoting the fumes of aromatics.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:16](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v16)</div>
<p>With these he unites his public thanks, inviting those who fear God (Psa 60:4; Psa 61:5, His true worshippers) to hear. He vindicates his sincerity, inasmuch as God would not hear hypocrites, but had heard him.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:17](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v17)</div>
<p>he was extolled with my tongue--literally, "exaltation (was) under my tongue," as a place of deposit, whence it proceeded; that is, honoring God was habitual.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:18](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v18)</div>
<p>If I regard iniquity in my heart--literally, "see iniquity with pleasure."</p>
<p>Next: Psalms Chapter 67</p>
</div>
