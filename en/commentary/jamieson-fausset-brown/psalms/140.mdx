## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>A Psalm of praise to God for His mighty, righteous, and gracious government of all men, and of His humble and suffering people in particular. (Psa. 145:1-21)</p>
<p>(Compare Psa 30:1).</p>
<p>bless thy name--celebrate Thy perfections (Psa 5:11). God is addressed as king, alluding to His government of men.</p>
