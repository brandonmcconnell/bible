## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>An earnest cry for divine aid against his enemies, as being also those of God, is followed by the Psalmist's praise in assurance of a favorable answer, and a prayer for all God's people. (Psa 28:1-9)</p>
<p>my rock-- (Psa 18:2, Psa 18:31).</p>
<p>be not silent to me--literally, "from me," deaf or inattentive.</p>
<p>become like them, &amp;c.--share their fate.</p>
<p>go down into the pit--or, "grave" (Psa 30:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 28:2](/en/kjv/psalms/28#v2)</div>
<p>lift up my hands--a gesture of prayer (Psa 63:4; Psa 141:2).</p>
<p>oracle--place of speaking (Exo 25:22; Num 7:89), where God answered His people (compare Psa 5:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 28:3](/en/kjv/psalms/28#v3)</div>
<p>Draw me not away--implies punishment as well as death (compare Psa 26:9). Hypocrisy is the special wickedness mentioned.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 28:4](/en/kjv/psalms/28#v4)</div>
<p>The imprecation is justified in Psa 28:5. The force of the passage is greatly enhanced by the accumulation of terms describing their sin.</p>
<p>endeavours--points out their deliberate sinfulness.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 28:5](/en/kjv/psalms/28#v5)</div>
<p>Disregard of God's judgments brings a righteous punishment.</p>
<p>destroy . . . build . . . up--The positive strengthened by the negative form.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 28:6](/en/kjv/psalms/28#v6)</div>
<p>supplications--or, "cries for mercy."</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 28:7](/en/kjv/psalms/28#v7)</div>
<p>The repetition of "heart" denotes his sincerity.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 28:8](/en/kjv/psalms/28#v8)</div>
<p>The distinction made between the people.</p>
<p>their strength--and the anointed--may indicate Absalom's rebellion as the occasion.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 28:9](/en/kjv/psalms/28#v9)</div>
<p>The special prayer for the people sustains this view.</p>
<p>feed them--as a shepherd (Psa 23:1, &amp;c.).</p>

</div>
