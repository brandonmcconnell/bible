## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>Those who are fit for communion with God may be known by a conformity to His law, which is illustrated in various important particulars. (Psa 15:1-5)</p>
<p>abide--or, "sojourn" (compare Psa 5:4), where it means under God's protection here, as (Psa 23:6, Psa 27:4, Psa 27:6) communion.</p>
<p>tabernacle--seat of the ark (Sa2 6:17), the symbol of God's presence.</p>
<p>holy hill--(Compare Psa 2:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 15:2](/en/kjv/psalms/15#v2)</div>
<p>walketh--(Compare Psa 1:1).</p>
<p>uprightly--in a complete manner, as to all parts of conduct (Gen 17:1), not as to degree.</p>
<p>worketh--or, "does."</p>
<p>righteousness--what is right.</p>
<p>in his heart--sincerely (Pro 23:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 15:3](/en/kjv/psalms/15#v3)</div>
<p>He neither slanders nor spreads slander.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 15:4](/en/kjv/psalms/15#v4)</div>
<p>Love and hate are regulated by a regard to God.</p>
<p>sweareth . . . hurt--or what so results (compare Lev 5:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 15:5](/en/kjv/psalms/15#v5)</div>
<p>(Compare Lev 25:37; Deu 23:19-20).</p>
<p>usury is derived from a verb meaning "to bite." All gains made by the wrongful loss of others are forbidden.</p>
<p>taketh reward, &amp;c.--The innocent would not otherwise be condemned (compare Exo 23:8; Deu 16:19). Bribery of all sorts is denounced.</p>
<p>doeth these, &amp;c.--Such persons admitted to God's presence and favor shall never be moved (Psa 10:6; Psa 13:5).</p>
