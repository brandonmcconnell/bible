## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>Neginah--or, Neginoth (see on Psa 4:1, title). Separated from his usual spiritual privileges, perhaps by Absalom's rebellion, the Psalmist prays for divine aid, and, in view of past mercies, with great confidence of being heard. (Psa 61:1-8)</p>
<p>From the end--that is, places remote from the sanctuary (Deu 28:64).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 61:2](/en/kjv/psalms/61#v2)</div>
<p>heart is overwhelmed--literally, "covered over with darkness," or, "distress."</p>
<p>to the rock-- (Psa 18:2; Psa 40:2).</p>
<p>higher than I--which otherwise I cannot ascend.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 61:3](/en/kjv/psalms/61#v3)</div>
<p>shelter . . . and strong tower--repeat the same sentiment.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 61:4](/en/kjv/psalms/61#v4)</div>
<p>I will abide--So I desire to do (compare Psa 23:6).</p>
<p>trust in the covert, &amp;c.--make my refuge, in the shadow (compare Psa 17:8; Psa 36:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 61:5](/en/kjv/psalms/61#v5)</div>
<p>the heritage--or, part in the spiritual blessings of Israel (Psa 21:2-4).</p>
<p>vows--implies prayers.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 61:6](/en/kjv/psalms/61#v6)</div>
<p>the king--himself and his royal line ending in Christ. Mercy and truth personified, as in Psa 40:11; Psa 57:3.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 61:7](/en/kjv/psalms/61#v7)</div>
<p>abide before God--literally, "sit as a king in God's presence," under His protection.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 61:8](/en/kjv/psalms/61#v8)</div>
<p>Thus for new blessings will new vows of praise ever be paid.</p>
<p>Next: Psalms Chapter 62</p>
</div>
