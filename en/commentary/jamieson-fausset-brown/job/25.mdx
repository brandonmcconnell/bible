## Introduction

<div className="cm-intro">
<p>BILDAD'S REPLY. (Job 25:1-6)</p>
<p>Power and terror, that is, terror-inspiring power.</p>
<p>peace in his high places--implying that His power is such on high as to quell all opposition, not merely there, but on earth also. The Holy Ghost here shadowed forth Gospel truths (Col 1:20; Eph 1:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Job 25:3](/en/kjv/job/25#v3)</div>
<p>armies--angels and stars (Isa 40:26; Jer 33:22; Gen 15:5; "countless," Dan 7:10).</p>
<p>his light-- (Jam 1:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Job 25:4](/en/kjv/job/25#v4)</div>
<p>(Job 4:17-18; Job 14:4; Job 15:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Job 25:5](/en/kjv/job/25#v5)</div>
<p>"Look up even unto the moon" (Job 15:15). "Stars" here answer to "saints" (angels) there; "the moon" here to "the heavens" there. Even the "stars," the most dazzling object to man's eye, and the angels, of which the stars are emblems (Job 4:18; Rev 9:1), are imperfect in His sight. Theirs is the light and purity but of creatures; His of the Creator.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Job 25:6](/en/kjv/job/25#v6)</div>
<p>(Job 4:19-21; Job 15:16).</p>
<p>worm . . . worm--Two distinct Hebrew words. The first, a worm bred in putridity; alluding to man's corruption. The second a crawling worm; implying that man is weak and grovelling.</p>
