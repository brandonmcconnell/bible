<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Thessalonians 3:1](/en/kjv/1-thessalonians/3#v1)</div>
<p>3:1-5 After preaching in Berea, Paul had departed from Macedonia and sailed to Athens, instructing Silas and Timothy to join him soon (Acts 17:13-15). Timothy returned to Thessalonica (1 Thes 3:2), as Paul’s anxiety for the Thessalonian church had intensified (3:1, 5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Thessalonians 3:3](/en/kjv/1-thessalonians/3#v3)</div>
<p>3:3 we are destined for such troubles: Suffering for the faith is often part of a Christian’s life (1:6; 2:14; Phil 1:29; 1 Pet 1:6; 2:21; 3:17; 4:19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Thessalonians 3:5](/en/kjv/1-thessalonians/3#v5)</div>
<p>3:5 Paul was concerned that Satan, the tempter (Mark 1:13; 1 Cor 7:5), had undermined the Thessalonians’ faith (1 Thes 3:2, 6, 8) and rendered his work among them futile (Gal 2:2; Phil 2:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Thessalonians 3:6](/en/kjv/1-thessalonians/3#v6)</div>
<p>3:6 Paul wrote 1 Thessalonians from Corinth just after Timothy returned from Thessalonica (3:2, 5; Acts 18:5). Timothy’s report provided good news about the Thessalonians’ faith and love, steadfastness (1 Thes 3:8), and hope (see also 5:8; 2 Thes 1:3-4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Thessalonians 3:8](/en/kjv/1-thessalonians/3#v8)</div>
<p>3:8 Paul was distressed by his present circumstances (3:7) and deeply concerned for the church (3:1, 5). He was relieved to learn that the Thessalonians remained strong and were standing firm in the Lord even though they were also suffering (2:14; 3:3-4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Thessalonians 3:11](/en/kjv/1-thessalonians/3#v11)</div>
<p>3:11 Paul’s prayer to return to Thessalonica very soon (3:10) is directed both to the Father and the Lord Jesus, implying Jesus’ divinity.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Thessalonians 3:12](/en/kjv/1-thessalonians/3#v12)</div>
<p>3:12 A characteristic of this church was their love for one another as members of God’s family (4:9; 2 Thes 1:3-4). Paul prays that their love will grow and overflow toward those outside the Christian community (cp. 1 Thes 5:15; Gal 6:10; 2 Tim 2:24; Titus 3:2) even in the face of persecution.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Thessalonians 3:13](/en/kjv/1-thessalonians/3#v13)</div>
<p>3:13 In the final part of this prayer (3:11-13), Paul prays that their hearts—their conduct and moral resolve (Matt 5:8; Acts 15:9; Heb 10:22)—might be strong, blameless, and holy. • When Jesus comes, he will be accompanied by all his holy people, possibly a reference to angels (Deut 33:2; Ps 89:5, 7; Dan 4:13; 8:13; Matt 13:41; Mark 8:38; 13:26-27; 2 Thes 1:7).</p>
</div>
