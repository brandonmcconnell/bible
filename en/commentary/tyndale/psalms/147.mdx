<p>Ps 147 The psalmist calls for the Lord to be praised for restoring and blessing Zion, caring for the poor, displaying his power over nature, and revealing himself to his people.147:1 This verse is a call to worship.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:2](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v2)</div>
<p>147:2-6 The psalmist recalls the restoration of Jerusalem and the Lord’s afflicted exiles.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:4](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v4)</div>
<p>147:4-5 The Lord created the heavens. • He counts the stars: God’s people can feel secure in knowing that the Lord attends to every detail of his creation; he knows all that belongs to him.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:6](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v6)</div>
<p>147:6 God measures out his justice on the poor and on the wicked (55:22; 140:12; 145:14; 146:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:7](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v7)</div>
<p>147:7-11 Human achievements fail to impress the God who commands nature; rather, he takes pleasure in those who fear him.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:8](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v8)</div>
<p>147:8 God holds sovereign power over the weather (77:17-18; 78:26; 104:4; 148:5-6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:10](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v10)</div>
<p>147:10-11 human might: Any physical or intellectual achievement is a gift from the Lord, just like the strength of a horse. These gifts are a reason to praise the Lord, not to boast (Jer 9:23-24).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:13](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v13)</div>
<p>147:13 The bars of the city gates symbolize defenses against enemies. • God blessed his people by empowering them with safety, prosperity, peace, and provisions (see 133:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:15](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v15)</div>
<p>147:15-18 Simply by speaking his command, the Lord can change the season from unbearable winter to refreshing spring (see 147:8; 148:5-6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 147:19](/en/kjv/psalms/147#v19)</div>
<p>147:19-20 words . . . decrees . . . regulations: The Lord gave powerful words to Israel so that they might obey him (105:45).</p>
</div>
