<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 67:1](/en/kjv/psalms/67#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 67 In this anonymous prayer for God’s blessing, the psalmist addresses the knowledge and worship of God among the nations (cp. 64:9; 65:8). The prayer requests that God’s name be kept holy and that his kingdom come (cp. Matt 6:9-13).67:1-2 This prayer is based on the priestly blessing found in Num 6:24-26. God’s goodness to Israel affects his reputation among the nations (see Ps 96:1-3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 67:3](/en/kjv/psalms/67#v3)</div>
<p>67:3 God’s international reputation leads to his praise among the nations (see 138:4), whose turmoil and rebelliousness will cease when they join Israel in praise of God. • One God rules over all, and the nations will give thanks for God’s just rule (see 67:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 67:4](/en/kjv/psalms/67#v4)</div>
<p>67:4 God brings the ultimate justice that human beings desire (96:10; 98:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 67:6](/en/kjv/psalms/67#v6)</div>
<p>67:6-7 Bountiful harvests are evidence of God’s blessing (Gen 26:12; Isa 30:23). • all over the world: See Pss 2:8; 96:1-3. • All will fear him, joining with Israel to acknowledge the God of Israel.</p>
