<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:1](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 66 This anonymous psalm includes two hymns (66:1-12, 13-20) that praise God for his redemption.66:1-4 The psalmist exhorts nature to join in a mighty chorus to praise God’s glory, name, and power.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:2](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v2)</div>
<p>66:2 The glory of his name represents the perfection of God’s character.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:3](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v3)</div>
<p>66:3 God conquers his enemies (see 18:44; 59:11; 81:15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:5](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v5)</div>
<p>66:5-7 The story of the Exodus and God’s victory over the sea evokes responses of astonishment (66:5) and worship (66:6). God works miracles . . . for people in order to redeem them for himself.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:7](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v7)</div>
<p>66:7 God’s power brings redemption. The same word occurs in 65:6 for God’s power in creation (see also 80:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:8](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v8)</div>
<p>66:8-10 The orderly lives of the redeemed reflect the order of God’s world. The Lord protects them from evil and purifies them.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:9](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v9)</div>
<p>66:9 God guides his people into wisdom and away from folly (17:5; 37:31; 121:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:10](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v10)</div>
<p>66:10 You have tested us, O God: As the silversmith heats silver to remove the dross, God’s discipline removes sin from his people (see 12:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:11](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v11)</div>
<p>66:11 God decrees and permits the many trying circumstances his people experience.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:12](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v12)</div>
<p>66:12 Then you put a leader over us: Or You made people ride over our heads. This expression possibly refers to a foreign ruler who kept the Israelites in submission.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:15](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v15)</div>
<p>66:15 Vows often included sacrifices of thanksgiving, including burnt offerings (see 20:3; 40:6; 50:8; 51:19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:16](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v16)</div>
<p>66:16-20 The private testimony in the conclusion of the psalm parallels the public praise of its opening (66:1-4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 66:18](/en/kjv/psalms/66#v18)</div>
<p>66:18 Confession leads to restoration (see 32:5).</p>
</div>
