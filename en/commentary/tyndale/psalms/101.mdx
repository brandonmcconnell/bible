<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 101:1](/en/kjv/psalms/101#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 101 This hymn expresses a commitment to live with integrity and to avoid evil. Jesus meets this ideal.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 101:2](/en/kjv/psalms/101#v2)</div>
<p>101:2 in my own home (literally in my house): This commitment to integrity might refer to private life at home. Some interpreters view my house as referring to the king’s palace; the commitment would then refer to the policies of his administration. • Leading a life of integrity means choosing a wise lifestyle.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 101:4](/en/kjv/psalms/101#v4)</div>
<p>101:4-5 The psalmist hates what the Lord hates (Prov 6:16-19; see Pss 5:5; 11:4-5). • Perverse ideas come from corrupt human hearts (cp. 18:26).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 101:6](/en/kjv/psalms/101#v6)</div>
<p>101:6 The psalmist surrounds himself with people who are also committed to integrity (101:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 101:7](/en/kjv/psalms/101#v7)</div>
<p>101:7-8 The psalmist commits himself to ridding his house and the city of the Lord—representing the entire kingdom—of all evil.</p>
</div>
