<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 48:1](/en/kjv/psalms/48#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 48 This song of Zion (see also Pss 46, 76, 87, 122) explicitly views Jerusalem (Zion) as the city of the Great King where the godly find protection (48:1-3). God rules from Zion in faithfulness, righteousness, and justice, and he inspires his subjects with confidence and joy (48:9-11). They commit themselves to seeing the glory of Zion for themselves so that they can tell the next generation about it (48:8, 12-14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 48:3](/en/kjv/psalms/48#v3)</div>
<p>48:3 God dwells in Zion to protect it from all enemies (see 46:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 48:7](/en/kjv/psalms/48#v7)</div>
<p>48:7 The ships of Tarshish, built for long voyages, represent human accomplishment (see also 2 Chr 9:21; Isa 2:16). Their destruction might be the event mentioned in 1 Kgs 22:48.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 48:9](/en/kjv/psalms/48#v9)</div>
<p>48:9-11 The psalmist joyfully celebrates the Lord’s presence in the midst of the community, reflecting on the faithfulness, righteousness, and justice of God’s rule.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 48:12](/en/kjv/psalms/48#v12)</div>
<p>48:12-13 towers . . . fortified walls . . . citadels: These structures provide a visual tour of Zion’s strength.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 48:14](/en/kjv/psalms/48#v14)</div>
<p>48:14 No one is like God (35:10; 71:19; 86:8; 89:6, 8; 113:5; see Isa 40:18, 25). He is the shepherd King (Ps 95:7) who will guide (see 5:8; 23:2) the sheep of his flock (77:20).</p>
</div>
