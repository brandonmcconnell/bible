<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 98:1](/en/kjv/psalms/98#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 98 This psalm celebrates the Lord’s kingship. The Lord is Israel’s true King, its champion and savior. His people sing a new song celebrating his victorious power and faithfulness to them. The Lord displays his righteous rule to all the nations throughout the earth, which will culminate in a worldwide kingdom of justice (98:2, 9). Consequently, all the earth must worship and rejoice together because the Lord is coming (98:4-6).</p>
</div>
