<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 92:1](/en/kjv/psalms/92#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 92 Thanksgiving and praise open this psalm. The Lord—the Most High—is faithful and just in discerning between the godly and the wicked. The godly will enjoy a glorious, vigorous future, while the wicked will perish. The wise see and understand the Lord’s works as they reveal his justice (92:6-7, 11, 15). The wicked, like grass, are quickly destroyed (92:7-10).92:title on the Sabbath Day: Jewish tradition assigned one psalm to each day of the week: Sunday (Ps 24), Monday (Ps 48), Tuesday (Ps 82), Wednesday (Ps 94), Thursday (Ps 81), Friday (Ps 93), and the Sabbath (Ps 92). This is the only psalm that indicates this use in its title.92:1-3 The psalmist offers praise and thanksgiving for God’s unfailing love and faithfulness.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 92:4](/en/kjv/psalms/92#v4)</div>
<p>92:4-7 Fools perish because they do not understand the Lord’s marvelous works and eternal plans.92:4-5 The Lord acts and the psalmist responds in praise (92:4). God’s actions spring from his thoughts (92:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 92:5](/en/kjv/psalms/92#v5)</div>
<p>92:5-6 The Lord’s thoughts do not make sense to the simpleton or the fool, the immoral person who desires no wisdom (Prov 1:22; 10:23).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 92:7](/en/kjv/psalms/92#v7)</div>
<p>92:7 Even when the ungodly seem to grow luxuriantly (73:3-5), they have no future (92:9; see 1:6; 90:5-6; Isa 40:6-8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 92:8](/en/kjv/psalms/92#v8)</div>
<p>92:8-10 The glory of the Lord provides the reason for the poet’s renewed strength and glory, and for the imminent defeat of the enemy.92:8 The transcendent Lord stays above the world of human experience. From this exalted place, he rules and judges (7:7), celebrates his victories (68:18; see Ps 47), comes to rescue the needy (102:19), brings an end to the wicked (92:7-8), and receives praise (148:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 92:10](/en/kjv/psalms/92#v10)</div>
<p>92:10 as strong as a wild ox: The Lord allows the psalmist to share his exaltation (92:8), thus ensuring victory.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 92:11](/en/kjv/psalms/92#v11)</div>
<p>92:11-15 The psalmist understands the Lord’s mighty acts. He shares in the glory of the wise of heart.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 92:12](/en/kjv/psalms/92#v12)</div>
<p>92:12 Carvings of palm trees (1 Kgs 6:29, 32, 35; 7:36) adorned the Temple, and builders made rich use of cedars (1 Kgs 5:6). The trees represent the godly, who are planted in the very courts of the Temple.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 92:13](/en/kjv/psalms/92#v13)</div>

</div>

<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 92:15](/en/kjv/psalms/92#v15)</div>
