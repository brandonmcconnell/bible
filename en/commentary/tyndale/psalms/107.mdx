<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:1](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 107 This psalm is a hymn of praise to God the Redeemer. After an introduction exhorting the redeemed to praise God for his goodness (107:1-3), the psalmist brings together four case studies of people whom the Lord redeemed from adversity (107:4-32). He then sums up the Lord’s sovereignty (107:33-42) and concludes with an invitation to discern God’s unfailing love in all areas of life (107:43).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:2](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v2)</div>
<p>107:2 The redeemed might be all who came out of exile.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:3](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v3)</div>
<p>107:3 exiles: People came from all nations where the people of Israel and Judah had been scattered.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:4](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v4)</div>
<p>107:4-9 Those who were lost in the wilderness thank God for his rescue. The wilderness might be a metaphor for leaving the path of wisdom (1:1; Prov 4:10-15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:6](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v6)</div>
<p>107:6 The psalmist does not specify the occasion of his trouble and distress. The generic nature of the adversity permits readers in a variety of circumstances to identify with the laments.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:7](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v7)</div>
<p>107:7-8 God led them like a shepherd (see 23:1). • Verse 8 is a refrain that is repeated in 107:15, 21, 31.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:10](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v10)</div>
<p>107:10-16 The psalmist calls for thanksgiving and praise from people in despair over the consequences of their rebellion.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:11](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v11)</div>
<p>107:11 God’s counsel includes his instruction and will.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:12](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v12)</div>
<p>107:12 Exhausted by their hard labor (Lam 5:13), the people fell. While this might be expected of the wicked (Pss 9:3; 27:2), it was unlike the Israelites’ flight from Egypt (105:37).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:17](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v17)</div>
<p>107:17-22 Fools who received what they deserved but were rescued from death offer thanksgiving to the Lord.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:20](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v20)</div>
<p>107:20 The Lord creates (Gen 1:3) and sustains the universe (Heb 1:3) simply by speaking (Ps 147:18); when he speaks, people are also healed (Matt 8:8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:23](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v23)</div>
<p>107:23-32 Sailors give thanks to the Lord when he saves them from the chaos and peril of the sea.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:25](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v25)</div>
<p>107:25-29 The same God who speaks and brings about the sailors’ distress can also calm the storm with just a word (cp. Jon 1:4; Matt 8:26).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v32" data-v="32">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:32](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v32)</div>
<p>107:32 This call for public praise expands the refrains in 107:21-22 and 107:31.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v33" data-v="33">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:33](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v33)</div>
<p>107:33-42 These two sections (107:33-38, 39-42) expand the theme of the Lord’s sovereignty over things that humans cannot control.107:33-35 The Lord can choose to make the earth fertile or barren (74:15; Isa 35:6-7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v38" data-v="38">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:38](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v38)</div>
<p>107:38 God’s presence can be seen in the abundance of his people’s families and herds (127:3; 133:3; cp. 109:8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v39" data-v="39">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:39](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v39)</div>
<p>107:39 People often become impoverished when their rulers practice oppression.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v40" data-v="40">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:40](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v40)</div>
<p>107:40 In his contempt for human rulers, the Lord rectifies their corruption on earth.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v41" data-v="41">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:41](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v41)</div>
<p>107:41 The large families of the poor are evidence of God’s blessing.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v42" data-v="42">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 107:42](/en/kjv/psalms/107#v42)</div>
<p>107:42-43 Justice prevails in God’s world (1:6), and Israel’s history demonstrates God’s faithful love.</p>
</div>

</div>
