<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 33:1](/en/kjv/psalms/33#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 33 This hymn of creation (see also Pss 8, 19, 104, 145) might be a continuation of Ps 32. It exhorts readers to praise God (33:1-3), to recognize the power of his word in creation (33:4-7), and to fear the Creator (33:8-11). It offers hope to forgiven sinners (Ps 32) that they can live a new life in the fear of the Lord and under divine protection.33:1-3 The psalmist exhorts the godly to praise the Lord with melodies, with a new song, and with skillfully-played music.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 33:4](/en/kjv/psalms/33#v4)</div>
<p>33:4-7 God’s word displays his character and his power.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 33:7](/en/kjv/psalms/33#v7)</div>
<p>33:7 The nations surrounding Israel thought that the sea had divine power, but it is under God’s control, within boundaries that he set (see 104:8-10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 33:8](/en/kjv/psalms/33#v8)</div>
<p>33:8-11 Fear of the Lord begins with awe for God and for his powerful word. His word created everything and continues to order and sustain creation. He frustrates the plans of people who oppose his will.33:8 The power of the Lord extends to all the nations (see 2; 96:1-3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 33:10](/en/kjv/psalms/33#v10)</div>
<p>33:10 The plans and schemes of the mighty will not prevail, except by God’s will (see Isa 8:9-10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 33:11](/en/kjv/psalms/33#v11)</div>
<p>33:11 God’s word still orders creation and will do so forever. • God’s intentions are marvelous (40:5; 92:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 33:12](/en/kjv/psalms/33#v12)</div>
<p>33:12 People who fear God are chosen as his inheritance (see 33:13-19); this excludes rebellious people (78:62).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 33:13](/en/kjv/psalms/33#v13)</div>
<p>33:13-19 From the heavens, the Lord searches out and cares for anyone who fears him (see 14:2-3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 33:16](/en/kjv/psalms/33#v16)</div>
<p>33:16-17 army . . . great strength . . . warhorse: Even military might is under God’s control (see 33:10-11; Eccl 9:11).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 33:18](/en/kjv/psalms/33#v18)</div>
<p>33:18 God watches over all who live by his counsel (32:8). He protects, rescues, and remains close to them (34:15-22).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 33:20](/en/kjv/psalms/33#v20)</div>
<p>33:20-22 The godly respond to this magnificent vision of God’s power in creation and in the affairs of the world with an affirmation of trust, commitment, joy, and hope as they pray and wait for God’s rescue.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 33:22](/en/kjv/psalms/33#v22)</div>
<p>33:22 Using a play on words, the psalmist contrasts his people’s sure hope (Hebrew yakhal) in the Lord with the supposed strength (33:17; Hebrew khayil) of the horses of the wicked.</p>
</div>
