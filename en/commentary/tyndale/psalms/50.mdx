<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:1](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 50 God appears as judge to remind the godly and the ungodly in turn that outward performance of sacrificial rituals and recitation of the law are worthless without thankfulness, repentance, and justice.50:1-3 The whole universe must pay attention when God approaches to bring justice into the world.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:4](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v4)</div>
<p>50:4-6 The Lord is coming to bring righteousness. This hope encourages his faithful people, but those who have been unfaithful will not experience blessing when the heavens above and earth below testify against them (cp. Deut 30:19; 31:28; 32:1; Isa 1:2). The Lord comes to restore order to the world (Pss 5:8; 22:31; 33:5; 40:9; 50:6; 71:16; 97:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:7](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v7)</div>
<p>50:7-13 The psalmist delivers a message against those who regard the sacrificial rituals as more important than having a proper heart attitude.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:9](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v9)</div>
<p>50:9 Unlike pagan gods, God does not need food; instead, he wants his people to serve him with a right heart (50:14-15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:10](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v10)</div>
<p>50:10 I own the cattle on a thousand hills: God has no needs; he possesses everything.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:14](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v14)</div>
<p>50:14 Thankfulness means looking beyond self-interest to express gratitude for God’s blessings (see 51:15-17; 54:6). • True sacrifice requires offering one’s self to the glory of God (50:23; Rom 12:1-2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:15](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v15)</div>
<p>50:15 God’s response to his people’s cry is to rescue them.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:16](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v16)</div>
<p>50:16-21 This message is against the wicked, who only pretend to listen to God.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:17](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v17)</div>
<p>50:17 my discipline . . . my words: The wicked may observe rituals, but they reject more important matters such as justice, mercy, and faithfulness (cp. Matt 23:23-24).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:21](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v21)</div>
<p>50:21 Because he is patient, God remained silent.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 50:22](/en/kjv/psalms/50#v22)</div>
