<p>Ps 13 The psalmist’s piercing cry for help becomes a confident song of hope.13:1-2 How long? The fourfold repetition expresses agitation and deep anguish (79:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 13:2](/en/kjv/psalms/13#v2)</div>
<p>13:2 A sense of anguish and sorrow closes off any perspective of hope (6:3). Unless the Lord answers the psalmist’s prayers (22:1), death seems inescapable, torturous, and slow (6:6; 32:3; 55:4; 61:2; 102:5; 116:3). The psalmist hardly knows how to pray; he longs for God’s redemption (25:17-18; 31:10; 38:8; 39:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 13:3](/en/kjv/psalms/13#v3)</div>
<p>13:3-4 Only the Lord can bring enlightenment and relief. The psalmist longs for the Lord’s vindication so that his foes will not rejoice.13:3 The psalmist’s affliction feels like death (22:15; 55:4; 88:15; 94:17; 116:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 13:5](/en/kjv/psalms/13#v5)</div>
<p>13:5-6 The psalmist no longer hears the ridicule of his foes. Instead, because of his trust in the Lord, he is full of joy.</p>
