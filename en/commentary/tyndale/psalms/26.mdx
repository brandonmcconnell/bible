<p>Ps 26 In this individual lament, the psalmist prays for redemption on the basis of his own innocence and the Lord’s justice.26:1-3 The psalmist opens himself to divine examination of his life, especially in the areas of his character and devotion.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 26:3](/en/kjv/psalms/26#v3)</div>
<p>26:3 For God’s truth to become part of one’s character requires a habitual commitment.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 26:4](/en/kjv/psalms/26#v4)</div>
<p>26:4-5 Because of his devotion to the Lord (26:1-3), the psalmist dissociates himself from evil people (see 1:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 26:6](/en/kjv/psalms/26#v6)</div>
<p>26:6-8 The psalmist demonstrates his loyalty to the Lord through pious rituals, praise, and acknowledgment of the Lord’s presence in the Temple.26:6 Ritual purity required the worshiper to wash their hands, symbolic of separation from evil and evildoers (see 73:13). • The altar symbolizes God’s presence (43:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 26:8](/en/kjv/psalms/26#v8)</div>

</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 26:9](/en/kjv/psalms/26#v9)</div>

</div>
