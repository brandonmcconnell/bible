<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 118:1](/en/kjv/psalms/118#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 118 With an exultant testimony, the psalmist gives thanks for the Lord’s goodness and encourages others to trust in his faithful love (118:1-4, 29). This poem is the last of the Egyptian Hallel (Pss 113–118).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 118:2](/en/kjv/psalms/118#v2)</div>
<p>118:2-4 Israel . . . Aaron’s descendants . . . all who fear the Lord: These three groups represent the whole of the covenant community (cp. 115:9-11; 135:19-20).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 118:5](/en/kjv/psalms/118#v5)</div>
<p>118:5-9 The Lord alone is the psalmist’s helper.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 118:6](/en/kjv/psalms/118#v6)</div>
<p>118:6-7 The Lord is for me: Cp. Rom 8:31; Heb 13:6. • God’s presence cancels out the need to fear mere people (Ps 56:11). The psalmist joyfully expects God’s rescue (59:10; 112:8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 118:8](/en/kjv/psalms/118#v8)</div>
<p>118:8-9 Trusting in the Lord is better than placing confidence in even the most powerful human leaders (118:6; 146:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 118:10](/en/kjv/psalms/118#v10)</div>
<p>118:10-12 Strength comes in the name of the Lord, declared by the threefold mention of the attackers, the Lord’s name, and victory. • Even when hostile people blazed . . . like a crackling fire, the Lord caused them to subside like burning thorns (see Isa 9:18; 10:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 118:14](/en/kjv/psalms/118#v14)</div>
<p>118:14-18 The psalmist celebrates the victory of the Lord’s strong right arm, which kept him from death.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 118:17](/en/kjv/psalms/118#v17)</div>
<p>118:17-21 God has restored the psalmist from death to life, so he praises the Lord publicly (cp. 116:6, 12-19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 118:22](/en/kjv/psalms/118#v22)</div>
<p>118:22-24 The psalmist sees his own salvation as the beginning of a new day in which the Lord will do great things.118:22 The cornerstone (or keystone) of a building or an arch gives the structure its shape and foundation. It is the most important of all the stones. The Lord chooses and places this stone for his sanctuary (Isa 28:16). Jesus referred to himself as this cornerstone (Matt 21:42; Mark 12:10-11; Luke 20:17; see Acts 4:11; Eph 2:20; 1 Pet 2:6-7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 118:24](/en/kjv/psalms/118#v24)</div>
<p>118:24 This is the day the Lord has made: The day of the Lord’s victory has already arrived, although its completion awaits another day (118:25).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 118:25](/en/kjv/psalms/118#v25)</div>
<p>118:25-29 The psalmist concludes with prayer and thanksgiving, anticipating an even greater revelation of God’s victory and rescue.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 118:26](/en/kjv/psalms/118#v26)</div>
<p>118:26 The one who comes in the name of the Lord brings the Lord’s complete victory (see 118:10-12). • During Jesus’ triumphant entry into Jerusalem, the crowds called out various blessings, including phrases taken from this verse (Matt 21:9; Mark 11:9; Luke 19:38; John 12:13). Jesus himself quoted this passage as he wept over Jerusalem (Matt 23:39; Luke 13:35).</p>
</div>
