<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 24:1](/en/kjv/psalms/24#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 24 The shepherd of Ps 23, who is also the King of the whole world, takes possession of Zion.24:1-2 The kingdom belongs to the Creator, the Great King (cp. 22:27).24:1 The Lord created and now rules the earth and everything in it (see 1 Cor 10:25-26). He sees all its people (Ps 33:14), regardless of their status (49:1-2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 24:2](/en/kjv/psalms/24#v2)</div>
<p>24:2 God created everything in an orderly and stable manner (see 89:11; 102:25; 104:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 24:3](/en/kjv/psalms/24#v3)</div>
<p>24:3-6 The citizens of the King’s realm include all who seek him. The Lord invites them to enter his presence.24:3 These questions invite readers to look away from their problems with the wicked and to examine themselves (see 15:1). • God’s holy place is the holy mountain, a reference both to Jerusalem and to heaven.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 24:4](/en/kjv/psalms/24#v4)</div>
<p>24:4 Those with pure hands have not shed blood or injured relationships between people (see 9:12; 15:2-5). • Those with pure hearts commit themselves fully to the Lord, as evidenced in strength of character, transparency, and selflessness (51:10; 73:1; see Matt 5:8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 24:10](/en/kjv/psalms/24#v10)</div>
<p>24:10 The Lord of Heaven’s Armies reigns from his home, the Temple in Jerusalem (84:3-5). He excludes sin and sinners (24:3-6) but protects all who dwell in Zion.</p>
</div>
