<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 54:1](/en/kjv/psalms/54#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 54 In this individual lament, occasioned by violent and godless people (54:3), the psalmist turns to God in prayer and maintains his focus on God’s help (54:4). He prays for God to save him from evil and to judge his enemies (54:5, 7).54:title This psalm refers to the account of 1 Sam 23:19-20, when the Ziphites were bent on violence.</p>
