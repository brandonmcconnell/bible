<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 55:1](/en/kjv/psalms/55#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 55 The psalmist is faced with adversaries whose leader is a former friend (55:12-14), and he is overwhelmed with anguish. He turns to the Lord in prayer and commits himself to the Lord for redress (55:22-23). Jesus experienced similar suffering when his friend (Judas) betrayed him. He also experienced the answer to the psalmist’s prayer: (1) by taking the place of the wicked and receiving their judgment (Isa 53:7-9), and (2) by committing himself to the Father, who rescued (Luke 23:46) and rewarded him (Isa 53:10-12).55:1-3 The occasion for this prayer is an attack by an enemy.</p>
</div>
