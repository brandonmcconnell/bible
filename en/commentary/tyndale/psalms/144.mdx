<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 144:1](/en/kjv/psalms/144#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 144 This lament begins with an exclamation of love for the Lord. The community then reflects on the transitory nature of life (144:3-4). The psalmist prays for divine intervention and anticipates victory (144:5-11). A new song (144:9-10) and a prayer for rescue (144:11) open up the theme of the Lord’s blessing through his provisions and protection (144:12-15).144:1-2 The psalmist’s descriptive names for the Lord reveal his deep love for him.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 144:3](/en/kjv/psalms/144#v3)</div>
<p>144:3-4 what are human beings? The same question in 8:4-6 elicits the answer that humans are mere mortals but are also glorious rulers. The psalmist depicts humans as having a transitory, empty existence (see 90:3, 7-10; 102:11; 109:23; 146:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 144:5](/en/kjv/psalms/144#v5)</div>
<p>144:5-8 The psalmist prays for God’s intervention against his enemies (cp. 18:7-19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 144:12](/en/kjv/psalms/144#v12)</div>
<p>144:12-15 This prayer for the Lord’s blessing brings the laments of Pss 140–144 to a close. It offers a vision of God’s provision and protection (see Ps 127), not only for the psalmist, but for his descendants and those of all the godly.144:12 The psalmist compares sons and daughters to plants and pillars, which are images of strength and beauty.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 144:13](/en/kjv/psalms/144#v13)</div>
<p>144:13-14 no enemy . . . no cries of alarm: These images depict peace and security.</p>
</div>
