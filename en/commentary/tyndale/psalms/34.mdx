<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:1](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 34 This wisdom psalm includes a thanksgiving hymn (34:1-7) that celebrates the Lord’s care for and protection of godly sufferers. It also includes an invitation to wisdom (34:8-14) and an exposition of wisdom concerning the Lord’s care for the needy and the suffering of the wicked (34:15-22).34:title Abimelech is another name for Achish (see 1 Sam 21:10-15). The body of the psalm makes no explicit connection to this event.34:1-3 The psalmist exhorts the helpless to join him in praise.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:5](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v5)</div>
<p>34:5-6 The psalmist narrates his experiences of answered prayer.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:7](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v7)</div>
<p>34:7 The angel of the Lord represented the Lord’s presence during the wilderness journey. He protected Israel from the forces of Egypt (Exod 14:19-20), and he will guard all the godly (Ps 91:11).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:8](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v8)</div>
<p>34:8-14 The godly encourage everyone to seek wisdom, to fear the Lord, and to place themselves under the protection of the Lord’s angel.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:9](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v9)</div>
<p>34:9-10 Fear of the Lord includes reverence and respect, and it motivates a life of wisdom (see Prov 1:7; 9:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:11](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v11)</div>
<p>34:11-13 Wisdom contains three components: fearing the Lord (34:11), doing good (34:14), and rejecting evil (34:13).34:11 Anyone who listens, even children, can become wise followers of God.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:12](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v12)</div>
<p>34:12-16 Peter quotes this passage in his instructions for peaceful living (1 Pet 3:10-12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:13](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v13)</div>
<p>34:13 Godly people control their words; speaking evil and telling lies are foolish (see 10:7; cp. 17:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:14](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v14)</div>
<p>34:14 The way of peace leads to harmony and order, whereas evil destroys them (see 37:11).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:15](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v15)</div>
<p>34:15-16 The Lord distinguishes between wise and foolish people; he shows his concern for the wise by rescuing them from trouble (34:15, 17-20), but he destroys the foolish (34:16, 21).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:18](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v18)</div>
<p>34:18 Those who are brokenhearted possess a spirit of deep contrition and dependence on the Lord. God accepts this spirit as a proper sacrifice (51:17). The wicked hate the brokenhearted (109:16), but the Lord heals them (147:3; see Isa 57:15; 61:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:19](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v19)</div>
<p>34:19-22 The godly might suffer, but the Lord will reward them in the end. Similarly, the wicked might prosper for a time, but ultimately they will perish (1:6; 34:15-16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 34:20](/en/kjv/psalms/34#v20)</div>
<p>34:20 Bones represent a person’s entire being (see 6:2). • not one of them is broken: John applied this statement to Jesus at the crucifixion (John 19:36).</p>
</div>
