<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 135:1](/en/kjv/psalms/135#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 135 God, the Creator and the Lord of history, “made heaven and earth” (134:3). He deserves praise because, unlike idols, he redeems his people.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 135:4](/en/kjv/psalms/135#v4)</div>
<p>135:4 The synonyms Jacob and Israel include all twelve tribes. The Lord cherishes his chosen people. They are his treasure out of all the kingdoms of the world (Exod 19:5-6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 135:5](/en/kjv/psalms/135#v5)</div>
<p>135:5 The Lord is superior to any real or imagined supernatural being (see 95:3-5; 115:4-8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 135:6](/en/kjv/psalms/135#v6)</div>
<p>135:6 God is sovereign over the whole created order—he does whatever pleases him him. • The phrase heaven and earth refers to all of creation.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 135:7](/en/kjv/psalms/135#v7)</div>
<p>135:7 The psalmist tells of God’s sovereignty over the weather (Pss 29; 77:17-18; 78:26; 104:4; 147:8-9; 148:5-6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 135:8](/en/kjv/psalms/135#v8)</div>
<p>135:8-12 The Lord was sovereign over the Exodus and the conquest of the Promised Land.135:8-11 In the tenth and culminating plague against Egypt, God destroyed the firstborn of each family. • The miraculous signs and wonders are the ten plagues (see Exod 7–11; Deut 4:34; Neh 9:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 135:13](/en/kjv/psalms/135#v13)</div>
<p>135:13-18 The Lord’s name and character stand in contrast to idols.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 135:15](/en/kjv/psalms/135#v15)</div>
<p>135:15-20 This entire section is repeated in 115:4-11.135:15-18 Idols corrupt the thinking because they are merely things made by humans; they cannot speak, see, hear, or breathe (Isa 41:22-24; 44:9, 18; Rom 1:21-23).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 135:19](/en/kjv/psalms/135#v19)</div>

</div>
