<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 39:1](/en/kjv/psalms/39#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 39 This prayer for rescue reflects the psalmist’s discouragement, which comes from having a limited perspective on his situation.39:title Jeduthun was a levitical singer appointed by David along with Asaph (1 Chr 9:16; 16:38; 25:1).39:1-3 Suffering in silence only intensifies the psalmist’s anguish, pain, and inner turmoil.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 39:4](/en/kjv/psalms/39#v4)</div>
<p>39:4-6 Because of his suffering and sin, the psalmist concludes that life is meaningless and fleeting (see Job 7:7-10; Isa 40:6-8). He had expected a wicked life to have those qualities (see Pss 37:2, 20; 103:15-16), but now whether a person is godly or not seems to be irrelevant.39:4-5 how fleeting my life is: This perspective comes out of suffering (see 78:39; 89:47-48; 90:3-10). The human lifetime is but a moment to God (90:4), as temporary as a breath (see 39:11; 144:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 39:8](/en/kjv/psalms/39#v8)</div>
<p>39:8 The idea that rebellion brings on suffering is a common thread in Pss 32, 38–41.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 39:9](/en/kjv/psalms/39#v9)</div>
<p>39:9-11 The psalmist links suffering with God’s discipline, assuming that it is punishment for his sin.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 39:11](/en/kjv/psalms/39#v11)</div>
<p>39:11 The Lord uses discipline to correct his children (38:1, 3, 7; 40:12; see Prov 3:11-12; Heb 12:5-6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 39:12](/en/kjv/psalms/39#v12)</div>
<p>39:12 A guest (or foreigner) had no rights of land ownership (see Lev 25:23). The patriarchs had lived as guests in Canaan (Ps 105:23).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 39:13](/en/kjv/psalms/39#v13)</div>
<p>39:13 Unlike the psalmist’s earlier request that God stay close to him (38:21-22), his present desire for God to leave him alone echoes Job’s disposition (Job 7:17-19).</p>
</div>
