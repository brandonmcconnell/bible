<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 10:1](/en/kjv/psalms/10#v1)</div>
<p>Ps 10 The lament of 9:13-20 continues in Ps 10 (see study note on Pss 9–10), but the mood changes from confident assertion to anguished questioning. The psalmist prays for rescue, believing that the Lord, as a just king, takes care of the needy.10:1 The opening questions set the mood. The Lord does not seem to hear the prayers of his people (22:1, 19; see 2 Kgs 4:27; Isa 1:15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 10:2](/en/kjv/psalms/10#v2)</div>
<p>10:2-11 Wicked and oppressive people might prosper, leaving ugly consequences. Evil can be as dramatic as the psalmist portrays it here, or it could be more subtle.10:2 The wicked brazenly defy God with their acts and speech (17:10; 31:18; 59:12; 73:8; 123:4). Although the Lord will deal with them (31:23), they disturb the righteous (see 5:4-6) prior to their condemnation and judgment.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 10:3](/en/kjv/psalms/10#v3)</div>
<p>10:3 Unlike those who have evil desires, the godly wait for justice (10:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 10:4](/en/kjv/psalms/10#v4)</div>
<p>10:4 God is dead: See 10:11; 14:1.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 10:6](/en/kjv/psalms/10#v6)</div>
<p>10:6-7 Despite their evil ways (5:9; 28:3; Rom 3:14), the wicked expect a future free of trouble such as the righteous will enjoy.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 10:8](/en/kjv/psalms/10#v8)</div>
<p>10:8 The wicked rely on outright violence (11:5; 37:32) and deception (Prov 12:6; 23:27-28).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 10:12](/en/kjv/psalms/10#v12)</div>
<p>10:12-15 With confidence that the wicked will be held responsible for their deeds and that the Lord will care for the helpless, the psalmist prays intensely for rescue from evil.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 10:14](/en/kjv/psalms/10#v14)</div>
<p>10:14 Because the Lord cares for the afflicted, he will take note and punish evildoers (see 72:14; 116:15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 10:15](/en/kjv/psalms/10#v15)</div>
<p>10:15 The psalmist strongly invokes a curse as he prays (see “Prayers for Vengeance” Theme Note). • Arms represent an ability to wage war (18:34; 44:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 10:16](/en/kjv/psalms/10#v16)</div>
<p>10:16 As the true and just king, God will bring the wicked to an end, as he promises (1:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 10:17](/en/kjv/psalms/10#v17)</div>

</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Psalms 10:18](/en/kjv/psalms/10#v18)</div>
