<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Corinthians 16:1](/en/kjv/1-corinthians/16#v1)</div>
<p>16:1-4 Paul answers the Corinthians’ question about the offering for poor Christians in Jerusalem (Acts 24:17; Rom 15:25-27; 2 Cor 8–9; Gal 2:10).16:1 Galatia was a Roman province in Asia Minor (see Acts 16:6; 18:23).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Corinthians 16:2](/en/kjv/1-corinthians/16#v2)</div>
<p>16:2 Christians met on the first day of each week (see Acts 20:7) rather than the last day of the week, as in the Old Testament (Exod 20:8; 34:21; Isa 58:13-14). The first day was the “Lord’s Day,” the day of the Lord’s resurrection (Mark 16:9; Luke 24:13-15; John 20:19; Rev 1:10). • The portion of the money you have earned is not specified; Paul later encouraged them to give as generously as they could (2 Cor 8:1-4; 9:6-13; cp. Luke 12:33-34; 21:1-4; 1 Tim 6:18-19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Corinthians 16:4](/en/kjv/1-corinthians/16#v4)</div>
<p>16:4 Paul did in fact deliver the gift to Jerusalem (Rom 15:25-26).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Corinthians 16:5](/en/kjv/1-corinthians/16#v5)</div>
<p>16:5 Paul had traveled through Macedonia, a Roman province in the northern region of Greece, on his second missionary journey (Acts 15:36–18:22). • planning to travel through Macedonia: See Acts 19:21; 20:1-3.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Corinthians 16:6](/en/kjv/1-corinthians/16#v6)</div>
<p>16:6-7 possibly all winter: Paul evidently made this visit (cp. Acts 20:2-3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Corinthians 16:8](/en/kjv/1-corinthians/16#v8)</div>
<p>16:8 Paul wrote this letter while in Ephesus (about AD 53–56; Acts 19:1–20:1). • Pentecost was in May or June.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Corinthians 16:10](/en/kjv/1-corinthians/16#v10)</div>
<p>16:10 When Timothy comes: See 4:17. Timothy was Paul’s child in the faith and co-worker (see 1 Tim 1:2). • don’t intimidate him: Timothy may have been shy (2 Tim 1:6-8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Corinthians 16:11](/en/kjv/1-corinthians/16#v11)</div>
<p>16:11-12 Apollos: See study note on 1:12.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Corinthians 16:17](/en/kjv/1-corinthians/16#v17)</div>
<p>16:17 Together with Stephanas (see also 1:16; 16:15), Fortunatus and Achaicus had apparently traveled from Corinth to visit Paul in Ephesus.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Corinthians 16:19](/en/kjv/1-corinthians/16#v19)</div>
<p>16:19-24 As in other letters, Paul greets his friends and gives a benediction.16:19 Aquila and Priscilla (Greek Prisca) had previously moved from Corinth to Ephesus, where Paul was writing this letter (cp. Acts 18:1-3). • gather in their home: Aquila and Priscilla later continued this practice in Rome (see Rom 16:3-5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Corinthians 16:20](/en/kjv/1-corinthians/16#v20)</div>
<p>16:20 a sacred kiss: A kiss on the cheek was a common greeting.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Corinthians 16:21](/en/kjv/1-corinthians/16#v21)</div>
<p>16:21 Paul takes up the pen and signs the letter in his own handwriting. As was common, Paul used a secretary to write this letter and others (see Rom 16:22; Gal 6:11; Col 4:18; 2 Thes 3:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Corinthians 16:22](/en/kjv/1-corinthians/16#v22)</div>
<p>16:22 To be cursed is to be condemned by God (see also Rom 9:3; Gal 1:8-9). • Our Lord, come! This phrase was sometimes used in early Christian prayers (see also Rev 22:20).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Corinthians 16:23](/en/kjv/1-corinthians/16#v23)</div>
<p>16:23-24 All of Paul’s letters close with a benediction similar to this one. • Paul invokes grace at both the beginning and the end of this letter (see 1:3).</p>
</div>

</div>
