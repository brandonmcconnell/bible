<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Deuteronomy 30:1](/en/kjv/deuteronomy/30#v1)</div>
<p>30:1-10 Repentance was the only solution to the threat of judgment. The possibility of blessing and the prevention of judgment both depended on a proper relationship with the Lord.30:1 These blessings and curses: See 27:1–28:68.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Deuteronomy 30:2](/en/kjv/deuteronomy/30#v2)</div>
<p>30:2-3 with all your heart and all your soul: The expectation of these verses was clearly articulated in the Shema, Israel’s great statement of covenant faith and commitment (cp. 6:4). Obedience to the covenant was a prerequisite necessary for Israel to receive God’s forgiveness and return to its land.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Deuteronomy 30:6](/en/kjv/deuteronomy/30#v6)</div>
<p>30:6 change your heart: This work of God, initiated by his grace, would result in Israel’s regeneration and identify the nation as the Lord’s true people (see 10:16; Jer 4:4; Rom 4:1-12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Deuteronomy 30:11](/en/kjv/deuteronomy/30#v11)</div>
<p>30:11-20 Adherence to the covenant terms that Moses had outlined would give life; disobedience would surely bring death. Life and death refer to physical, mortal life in the land—of individuals and of the community of Israel.30:11 This command refers to the whole covenant text, all of Deuteronomy (see 4:2; 5:29; 7:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Deuteronomy 30:12](/en/kjv/deuteronomy/30#v12)</div>
<p>30:12 not kept in heaven: The revelation of God is not known only to him but is accessible to human beings (see Rom 10:6-8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Deuteronomy 30:13](/en/kjv/deuteronomy/30#v13)</div>
<p>30:13 not kept beyond the sea: God’s purposes are not in the custody of faraway nations but are available to his people (see Rom 10:6-8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Deuteronomy 30:14](/en/kjv/deuteronomy/30#v14)</div>
<p>30:14 The message of salvation is objectively close (on your lips) and subjectively near (in your heart). It is capable of being taught and learned (see 6:6-7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Deuteronomy 30:16](/en/kjv/deuteronomy/30#v16)</div>
<p>30:16 love the Lord your God: This paraphrases the Shema, Israel’s central covenant affirmation (see 6:5). To love God means to obey him in every respect, which results in divine blessing.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Deuteronomy 30:19](/en/kjv/deuteronomy/30#v19)</div>
<p>30:19 life and death . . . blessings and curses: This grammatical structure suggests an equivalence between life and blessing and between death and curses. The essence of a blessing is life, and the essence of a curse is death.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Deuteronomy 30:20](/en/kjv/deuteronomy/30#v20)</div>
<p>30:20 the key to your life: In its most meaningful sense, life consists of absolute commitment to the Lord.</p>
</div>

</div>
