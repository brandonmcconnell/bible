<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 12:1](/en/kjv/isaiah/12#v1)</div>
<p>12:1-6 The prophet’s personal song of praise for God’s salvation closes the meditations on trust in chs 7–12. God will prove himself trustworthy in his promises to rescue his people even though they initially refused to trust him. The song anticipates chs 40–66 with its themes of salvation, forgiveness, praise, joy, and the nations.12:1 God’s comfort is the main subject of chs 40–66.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 12:2](/en/kjv/isaiah/12#v2)</div>
<p>12:2 God’s promise to save includes reconciliation (1:18), participation (11:10), cleansing and protection (4:3-6), peace and confidence (32:16-17), and restoration (11:6-9). • Lord God (Hebrew Yah Yahweh): The first word is an abbreviation of the second. • The Lord God is my strength and my song: See Exod 15:2; Ps 118:14.</p>
</div>
