As a complement to his words, Isaiah walked about exposed and barefoot, a symbolic action for what God would do.20:1 Sargon of Assyria is Sargon II (721–705 BC). • Ashdod was one of the five Philistine cities that rebelled against Assyria in 713 BC.</p>
