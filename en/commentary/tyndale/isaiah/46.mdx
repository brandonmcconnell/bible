<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 46:1](/en/kjv/isaiah/46#v1)</div>
<p>46:1-2 This taunt was against the self-contradiction of Babylonian religion.46:1 Marduk-bel was the most important god in the Babylonian pantheon. Bel (similar to “Baal”) designated lordship. • Nebo (Nabu, Nebu), the patron deity of Babylonian scribes, was thought to be the son of Marduk-bel. • Instead of being paraded in glory at a Babylonian festival, these gods would be hauled away on ox carts and disposed of, rejected by the Babylonian people.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 46:3](/en/kjv/isaiah/46#v3)</div>
<p>46:3 Those who remain in Israel refers to the remnant that would be left alive after the destruction of Jerusalem in 586 BC. • The Lord, the Creator of humanity, cares for and carries his people (contrast the Babylonian idols that must be carried instead). • since you were born . . . before you were born: The Lord chose Israel even before they were a nation (see also 49:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 46:8](/en/kjv/isaiah/46#v8)</div>
<p>46:8-13 Many of the exiles would be completely discouraged and not believe the prediction of God’s rescue. God challenged this unbelief.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 46:10](/en/kjv/isaiah/46#v10)</div>
<p>46:10 God knows the future before it even happens; he has comprehensive control over all events, and all his acts match his words (41:4, 26). He is the First and the Last (44:6; 48:12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 46:11](/en/kjv/isaiah/46#v11)</div>
<p>46:11 The swift bird of prey from the east refers to Cyrus, the Persian king (see study note on 41:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 46:12](/en/kjv/isaiah/46#v12)</div>
<p>46:12 Formerly, Israel cared little for doing right (literally righteousness; see also 58:2; 59:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 46:13](/en/kjv/isaiah/46#v13)</div>
<p>46:13 The good news for Israel was that God was coming to set things right (literally I will bring my righteousness near; see 40:9; 51:5). • show my glory to Israel: Unlike a craftsman who bestows material splendor on an idol (44:13), the Lord bestows true dignity on his people.</p>
</div>
