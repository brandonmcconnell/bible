<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 59:1](/en/kjv/isaiah/59#v1)</div>
<p>59:1-20 The Lord alone can and will usher in his salvation. Like a warrior, he will break into the world to avenge himself on his enemies and to vindicate his holy people.59:1-8 The people complained that the Lord could not rescue them, but the real problem was that they had not repented (59:20) and were still living in sin.59:1 not too weak: The delay in their rescue could not be attributed to an inherent weakness in the Lord.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 59:2](/en/kjv/isaiah/59#v2)</div>
<p>59:2 your sins . . . cut you off: The Israelites, like all people (Rom 3:15-17), had sinned, which resulted in alienation from God (he has turned away).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 59:4](/en/kjv/isaiah/59#v4)</div>
<p>59:4 being fair and honest: Israel did not practice these qualities that are part of God’s nature and that he expects from people (1:21; see 25:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 59:5](/en/kjv/isaiah/59#v5)</div>
<p>59:5-6 The images of deadly snakes and spiders’ webs represent plans designed to destroy others’ lives and relationships.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 59:7](/en/kjv/isaiah/59#v7)</div>
<p>59:7-8 Paul quotes from these verses in his description of the depravity of all people (Rom 3:15-17). • Misery and destruction: The people lamented being victims of oppression (Isa 51:19), yet they were oppressors themselves.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 59:9](/en/kjv/isaiah/59#v9)</div>
<p>59:9-15 Israel’s history of rebellion was well established. The prophet here confesses the nation’s sins and laments that they could not apprehend the light of God’s salvation.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 59:10](/en/kjv/isaiah/59#v10)</div>
<p>59:10 people without eyes: See 6:10; Deut 28:29.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 59:11](/en/kjv/isaiah/59#v11)</div>
<p>59:11-14 The people growl and moan with longing for redemption, finally coming to terms with their sinfulness.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 59:15](/en/kjv/isaiah/59#v15)</div>
<p>59:15-17 The Lord goes out as a warrior intent on achieving victory against the wicked (cp. Eph 6:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 59:18](/en/kjv/isaiah/59#v18)</div>
<p>59:18 repay his enemies: The Lord will dole out righteous retribution on the whole earth. His justice requires people to be judged for the evil they have done (see also 3:9-11; 13:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 59:19](/en/kjv/isaiah/59#v19)</div>
<p>59:19 In the west . . . in the east: I.e., in all parts of the world.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Isaiah 59:21](/en/kjv/isaiah/59#v21)</div>
<p>59:21 The members of the new community have the Spirit, and they know and practice God’s word (see also Ezek 36:24-28). The Lord promises to regenerate the children by his Spirit and bring them into the covenant of obedience and love for the Lord (Joel 2:28; Acts 2:39).</p>
</div>

</div>
