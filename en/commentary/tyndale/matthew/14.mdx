<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Matthew 14:1](/en/kjv/matthew/14#v1)</div>
<p>14:1 Herod Antipas’s official title was tetrarch (ruler of a fourth part of a kingdom) of Galilee and Perea (4 BC—AD 39; see 2:19-22). Jesus referred to Antipas as “that fox” (Luke 13:32), and John the Baptist justly condemned him for adultery (Matt 14:3-12; Luke 3:19-20).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Matthew 14:2](/en/kjv/matthew/14#v2)</div>
<p>14:2 There was a rumor that John had been resurrected (see 16:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Matthew 14:3](/en/kjv/matthew/14#v3)</div>
<p>14:3-12 The popular appeal of John’s message posed a political threat (see Josephus, Antiquities 18.5.2). Years after John’s execution, the Jews viewed Herod’s military defeat at the hands of Aretas (AD 36) as a just recompense for putting an innocent man to death.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Matthew 14:4](/en/kjv/matthew/14#v4)</div>
<p>14:4 John had continually warned Herod of his sin (see Lev 18:6; 20:21).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Matthew 14:6](/en/kjv/matthew/14#v6)</div>
<p>14:6 Herodias’s daughter was probably Salome, a daughter from Herodias’s previous marriage to Philip. Sexually provocative dances such as this were a regular occurrence for depraved royalty.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Matthew 14:7](/en/kjv/matthew/14#v7)</div>
<p>14:7 Such an extravagant, foolish oath was consistent with Herod Antipas’s character.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Matthew 14:8](/en/kjv/matthew/14#v8)</div>
<p>14:8 The practice of bringing a head . . . on a tray was not unknown in the ancient world (see Josephus, Antiquities 13.4.8; 14.15.13; 18.5.1; 20.5.1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Matthew 14:13](/en/kjv/matthew/14#v13)</div>
<p>14:13-21 Jesus’ actions consistently fulfilled Old Testament promises regarding the Kingdom and the Messiah (11:5-6; 12:28). Here Jesus evoked the memory of God’s provision of manna for the Israelites (Exod 16; see John 6:32), demonstrating that he is the promised end-time prophet (see Deut 18:15-16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Matthew 14:21](/en/kjv/matthew/14#v21)</div>
<p>14:21 5,000 men . . . in addition to all the women and children: The count was of families, denoted by heads of households, rather than individuals.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Matthew 14:22](/en/kjv/matthew/14#v22)</div>
<p>14:22-33 Jesus’ power over nature presented the disciples with the opportunity to trust in him as the Son of God (14:33; see also 8:23-27; Ps 8:6; Heb 2:8-9).14:22 After the miraculous feeding, the crowds sought to make Jesus king (John 6:15), prompting his withdrawal because it was not the appropriate time.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Matthew 14:23](/en/kjv/matthew/14#v23)</div>
<p>14:23 Jesus often sought solitude in order to pray, particularly in conjunction with important events in his life and ministry (26:36-46; Mark 1:35; Luke 5:16; 6:12; 9:28).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Matthew 14:25](/en/kjv/matthew/14#v25)</div>
<p>14:25 About three o’clock in the morning: Literally In the fourth watch of the night. This was approximately 3:00–6:00 am by Roman reckoning. • The fact that Jesus came . . . walking on the water demonstrates God’s sovereign reign over the stormy waters (see Job 9:8; Ps 77:19; Isa 43:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Matthew 14:27](/en/kjv/matthew/14#v27)</div>
<p>14:27 I am here: Literally I am. This declaration is associated with the appearance of God himself (see also John 8:24; 18:5-6; cp. Exod 3:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v34" data-v="34">
<div className="cmv-head">[Matthew 14:34](/en/kjv/matthew/14#v34)</div>
<p>14:34-36 On the purpose of Jesus’ miracles, see study notes on Matt 11:5; 12:28.</p>
</div>
