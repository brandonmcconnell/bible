<p>24:2 3,000 elite troops: David’s force was outnumbered five to one (23:13).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Samuel 24:4](/en/kjv/1-samuel/24#v4)</div>
<p>24:4 David’s men believed, as Saul did, that if the circumstances seem right, the Lord is telling you through those circumstances what to do (cp. 23:7). • Perhaps David was able to cut off a piece of the hem of Saul’s robe without being apprehended because Saul had taken off the robe and laid it aside, or perhaps he fell asleep in the cave.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Samuel 24:5](/en/kjv/1-samuel/24#v5)</div>
<p>24:5 David’s conscience began bothering him because he had dishonored God’s anointed king.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Samuel 24:6](/en/kjv/1-samuel/24#v6)</div>
<p>24:6 The Lord’s anointed one was not to be violated (26:9, 11, 16, 23; 2 Sam 1:14, 16; 19:21). David would not harm Saul even though God had anointed him to be the next king (1 Sam 16:13).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Samuel 24:11](/en/kjv/1-samuel/24#v11)</div>
<p>24:11 my father: Saul was, in fact, David’s father-in-law (18:27). David was demonstrating his respect for the king and recalling a day when their relationship had been much friendlier.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Samuel 24:12](/en/kjv/1-samuel/24#v12)</div>
<p>24:12 May the Lord judge: There was no human authority to adjudicate between Saul and David (cp. Gen 16:5; 31:53; Exod 5:21; Judg 11:27). • Perhaps the Lord will punish you: David rested in God’s will rather than trying to force God’s hand.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Samuel 24:13](/en/kjv/1-samuel/24#v13)</div>
<p>24:13 The proverb vindicates David and indicts Saul. David refrained from evil deeds, such as killing Saul. Saul, however, repeatedly tried to kill David.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Samuel 24:20](/en/kjv/1-samuel/24#v20)</div>
<p>24:20 I realize that you are surely going to be king: This was Saul’s first open admission of the truth (cp. 23:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Samuel 24:21](/en/kjv/1-samuel/24#v21)</div>
<p>24:21-22 Now swear to me . . . you will not kill my family: Having descendants was a way that Saul’s name would endure among the living. David had already sworn such an oath with Jonathan (20:14-17).</p>
</div>
