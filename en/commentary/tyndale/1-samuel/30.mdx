<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">

</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">

</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Samuel 30:13](/en/kjv/1-samuel/30#v13)</div>
<p>30:13 because I was sick: Only healthy and vigorous slaves were valuable to the Amalekites; the rest were expendable.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv-head">[1 Samuel 30:14](/en/kjv/1-samuel/30#v14)</div>
