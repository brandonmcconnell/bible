<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Corinthians 9:1](/en/kjv/2-corinthians/9#v1)</div>
<p>9:1-5 Two motives reinforce the spirit of love in giving: (1) to emulate the Macedonians, who in their poverty had been forthcoming in their generosity (9:2); and (2) not to shame Paul or themselves, since he had boasted about the Corinthians’ generosity to the Macedonians (9:3-5). To ensure the timely gathering of the collection at Corinth, Paul would send the delegates (8:16-24) in advance to make sure the gift you promised is ready.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Corinthians 9:6](/en/kjv/2-corinthians/9#v6)</div>
<p>9:6 Paul turns to agriculture for an illustration of the benefits of generosity. A farmer who expects a rich harvest must sow many seeds.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Corinthians 9:7](/en/kjv/2-corinthians/9#v7)</div>
<p>9:7 It is the spirit of giving that counts, “For God loves a person who gives cheerfully.” Cheerfulness in giving is contrasted with giving reluctantly (literally out of sorrow) or in response to pressure.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Corinthians 9:8](/en/kjv/2-corinthians/9#v8)</div>
<p>9:8-9 As the Scriptures say: The quotation from Ps 112:9 supports the assurance that God, who provides for all human needs, will reward generosity.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Corinthians 9:10](/en/kjv/2-corinthians/9#v10)</div>
<p>9:10-14 The Corinthians’ generosity would be matched by God’s generosity (9:10-11). It would also meet the needs of Jewish Christians (9:12), honor God (9:13), show believers in Jerusalem that their Gentile brothers and sisters were genuine believers (9:13), and result in the Jewish believers’ intercessory prayer and affection for the Gentile believers (9:14). Paul envisioned a united, worldwide Christian church, composed of believing Jews and Gentiles who are all one in Christ Jesus (Gal 3:28; Eph 2:11-22), which would become a powerful witness to the Lord’s work of reconciliation.</p>
</div>
