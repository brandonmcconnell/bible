<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Corinthians 3:1](/en/kjv/2-corinthians/3#v1)</div>
<p>3:1-3 Paul’s ministry was validated by the lives of those who were changed by the Good News rather than by a letter of recommendation (cp. Acts 18:27). Christ, the author of this transformation, used Paul to lead believers to him. The marks of genuineness are not in letters written . . . with pen and ink on parchment, but in the fruit of the Spirit (Gal 5:22-23) in human lives and carved . . . on human hearts.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Corinthians 3:4](/en/kjv/2-corinthians/3#v4)</div>
<p>3:4-18 The contrast between Paul’s ministry and the others’ ministries leads Paul to contrast the old covenant of written laws (Exod 31:18) with the new covenant written on human hearts (Jer 31:33).3:4-6 The old written covenant is the Mosaic law as interpreted by the Jewish rabbis. It ends in death for adherents who see it as a way of salvation. The law is good (Rom 7:12-14), but those who use it to gain merit fail. It leads either to transgression or to pride; either way, the law brings condemnation (2 Cor 3:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Corinthians 3:7](/en/kjv/2-corinthians/3#v7)</div>
<p>3:7-11 The old covenant had its moments of glory, such as when Moses’ face . . . shone from his communion with the Lord (Exod 34:29-35). • The old way has been replaced by the new way, which is eternal (Heb 8:8-13).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Corinthians 3:12](/en/kjv/2-corinthians/3#v12)</div>
<p>3:12-15 The old covenant, represented by Moses’ veil, led to fear and did not remove spiritual blindness.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Corinthians 3:16](/en/kjv/2-corinthians/3#v16)</div>
<p>3:16-18 The believer who turns to the Lord has freedom in the Spirit. We receive something Moses never knew as we become more and more like Christ (4:4; John 1:1-14; Col 1:15; Heb 1:1-4) and reflect the glory of the Lord. Divine glory in this present life leads to our being like Christ in the next life (Rom 8:29; Gal 4:19; Phil 3:21; 1 Jn 3:2).</p>
</div>
