<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Chronicles 20:1](/en/kjv/2-chronicles/20#v1)</div>
<p>20:1 Meunites (see 1 Chr 4:41): The Meunites were a nomadic group living on the southern borders of Judah; their name probably survives as the Arab town of Ma’an, twelve miles southeast of Petra.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Chronicles 20:2](/en/kjv/2-chronicles/20#v2)</div>
<p>20:2 Translating the word as Edom instead of Aram is consistent with the Chronicler’s later description of the armies (20:10, 22-23) and with the geographical description of the attack from the southeast through En-gedi. (Aram was northeast of Judah.)</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Chronicles 20:3](/en/kjv/2-chronicles/20#v3)</div>
<p>20:3-12 Jehoshaphat responded to the news of war with neighboring nations with a prayer of lament, keeping with the ideals of Solomon’s prayer (6:24-40).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Chronicles 20:10](/en/kjv/2-chronicles/20#v10)</div>
<p>20:10 Mount Seir was another name for Edom (see Gen 32:3 and the study note on Gen 25:25).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Chronicles 20:14](/en/kjv/2-chronicles/20#v14)</div>
<p>20:14-17 Following Jehoshaphat’s lament, Jahaziel’s proclamation of rescue fulfilled the requirements for the speech that a priest was to give before battle (Deut 20:2-4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Chronicles 20:20](/en/kjv/2-chronicles/20#v20)</div>
<p>20:20-21 The song of the appointed singers replaced a battle cry. The prophets were Jahaziel (20:14-17) and the Levitical musicians (20:21), whose musical praise for the battle march was a prophecy, a declaration that God was at the head of the army.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v32" data-v="32">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Chronicles 20:32](/en/kjv/2-chronicles/20#v32)</div>
<p>20:32-33 The comparison of Jehoshaphat with Asa is adopted from 1 Kgs 22:43 without alteration.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v35" data-v="35">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Chronicles 20:35](/en/kjv/2-chronicles/20#v35)</div>
<p>20:35-37 The Lord destroyed the fleet to prevent Jehoshaphat’s alliance with King Ahaziah of Israel from prospering.</p>
</div>
