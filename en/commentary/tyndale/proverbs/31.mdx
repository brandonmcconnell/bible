<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 31:1](/en/kjv/proverbs/31#v1)</div>
<p>31:1-9 Lemuel, like Agur, might have been from Massa (see study note on 30:1). Lemuel’s mother’s teaching encourages him to control his lusts (particularly for women and alcohol) so that he might reign justly. • Apart from this passage, Lemuel is unknown.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 31:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/31#v2)</div>
<p>31:2-3 The admonition not to waste energy on lust for women echoes earlier warnings (ch 5; 6:20-35; 7:1-27).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 31:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/31#v4)</div>
<p>31:4-7 Too much wine numbs the senses. A king needs his wits about him and should not overindulge in alcohol.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 31:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/31#v8)</div>
<p>31:8-9 Rather than opening his mouth to drink wine (31:4-7), the king should speak up on behalf of the defenseless.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 31:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/31#v10)</div>
<p>31:10-31 Proverbs ends with a powerful poem celebrating the virtuous wife. The poem’s acrostic arrangement gives an impression of completeness and provides the student with an aid for learning.31:10-12 No one can find a virtuous and capable wife without God’s help (18:22). Such a woman, like wisdom, is more precious than rubies (cp. 3:15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 31:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/31#v13)</div>
<p>31:13-15 This wife works to provide food and clothing for her family.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 31:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/31#v16)</div>
<p>31:16-18 An excellent wife is engaged in commercial enterprises outside her home.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 31:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/31#v19)</div>
<p>31:19-21 A virtuous wife is generous to the poor, another characteristic of wise people (11:24-26; 21:13; 22:9, 16, 22-23; 28:27).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 31:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/31#v22)</div>
<p>31:22-24 A capable wife’s industriousness enables her husband to distinguish himself among community leaders. In ancient Israel, civic leaders met at the city gates to conduct trials and make decisions.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 31:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/31#v25)</div>
<p>31:25-27 The noble wife’s character exemplifies wisdom. She is coolheaded as she faces future uncertainties. She gives advice in a winsome way.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 31:28](/en/kjv/proverbs/31#v28)</div>
<p>31:28-29 Those most important to the woman testify to her character, abilities, and achievements.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v30" data-v="30">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 31:30](/en/kjv/proverbs/31#v30)</div>
<p>31:30-31 The book concludes where it began, by affirming the ultimate importance of the fear of the Lord (see 1:7). A woman’s character is more important than her physical beauty.</p>
</div>
