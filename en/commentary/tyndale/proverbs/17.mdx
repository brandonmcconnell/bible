<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">

<p>17:3 The Lord’s refining fire (see Ps 66:10; Jer 9:7; Mal 3:2-3) separates what is precious from the impurities in a person’s heart (cp. Prov 16:2; 21:2; 27:21).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v4)</div>
<p>17:4 Lies (6:16-19; 14:5, 25; 25:18), gossip (11:13; 18:8), and slander (10:18) distort reality for malicious purposes.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v5)</div>
<p>17:5 To make fun of what God created is to mock God.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v6)</div>
<p>17:6 Only those who have been blessed with a long life live to become grandparents, and the continuation of the family line is a sign of God’s blessing (see Gen 12:1-3). • parents: Literally fathers. This proverb applies to both fathers and mothers.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v9)</div>
<p>17:9 Maintaining a good relationship with another person means forgiving rather than dwelling on faults.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v10)</div>
<p>17:10 The wise will listen to a mild rebuke and act on good advice. Even harsh correction (a hundred lashes) will not change a fool.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v11)</div>
<p>17:11 Rebellion flows from the inner character of evil people who defy the punishment that will follow.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v12)</div>
<p>17:12 This saying cautions those who try to teach fools. A fool caught in foolishness will react with anger and violence.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v14)</div>
<p>17:14 opening a floodgate: Once a quarrel starts, it is very difficult to stop it; a dispute should be avoided if at all possible (see 10:12; 15:18; 16:28; 20:3; 22:10; 26:21).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v18)</div>
<p>17:18 The essence of this message is repeated in 6:1-5; 11:15; 20:16; 22:26; 27:13.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v19)</div>
<p>17:19 high walls: Foolish confidence in the ability to win a fight emboldens people to engage in disastrous arguments.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v24)</div>
<p>17:24 Wisdom is not a natural quality but must be pursued. Sensible people persistently cultivate wisdom, but a fool’s eyes cannot stay focused.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 17:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/17#v25)</div>
<p>17:25 Foolish children: This proverb is equally true of sons and daughters.</p>
</div>
