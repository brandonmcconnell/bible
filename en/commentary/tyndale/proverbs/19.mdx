<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:1](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v1)</div>
<p>19:1 It is better to be . . . honest, though poor, because wealth gained by dishonest means is short-lived (10:2) and will not preserve one from harm (11:4; see also 15:16-17; 16:8, 16; 17:1; 22:1; 28:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v2)</div>
<p>19:2 Rushing into things without understanding is dangerous.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v3)</div>
<p>19:3 Rather than acknowledge wrong choices and actions, fools blame the Lord.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v5)</div>
<p>19:5 God will make sure that liars are punished (see 12:17, 19; 14:5, 25; 19:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v8)</div>
<p>19:8 It is in our self-interest to acquire wisdom because understanding helps us prosper.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v9)</div>
<p>19:9 The reference to a false witness (see also 19:5) reminds us of the ninth commandment (Exod 20:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v12)</div>
<p>19:12 A lion’s roar is a frightening prelude to life-threatening violence (cp. 20:2). Dew on the grass is soothing and contributes to growth (see 16:14-15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v13)</div>
<p>19:13-14 No choice for a son is more important than a good wife (cp. 31:10-31); a poor choice brings agony (see 10:1; 11:22; 12:4; 21:9, 19; 25:24; 27:15-17). The wise man will look to the Lord to give an understanding wife.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v16)</div>
<p>19:16 Keeping God’s commandments (i.e., the law of Moses) protects one’s life (see Deut 28:15-68).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v18)</div>
<p>19:18 Growing in wisdom requires discipline for everyone, including children (see 10:17; 13:1, 10; 15:31-32; 17:10). The temporary grief that accompanies discipline is worth the protection from ruin.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v24)</div>
<p>19:24 // 26:15 This proverb is a humorous comment on the foolishness of being lazy (see also 10:4-6, 26; 12:11, 27; 13:4; 15:19; 18:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v25)</div>
<p>19:25 A mocker is beyond help (see study notes on 1:22; 21:11). A leader should still punish him because the simpleminded will learn a lesson.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 19:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/19#v26)</div>

</div>
