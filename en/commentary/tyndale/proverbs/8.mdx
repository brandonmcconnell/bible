<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 8:1](/en/kjv/proverbs/8#v1)</div>
<p>8:1–9:18 The first part of Proverbs climaxes when the son encounters two women. Both invite the young man, and the reader with him, into an intimate relationship. The young man and the reader must decide between the woman named Wisdom, personifying the true wisdom of God (8:1–9:6), and the woman named Folly (9:13-18), representing the wisdom of the world (1 Cor 1:18–2:16). Both women’s houses are on “the heights overlooking the city” (Prov 9:3, 14), suggesting ancient temples; the choice is thus between the true God and false gods.8:1-2 Wisdom calls out openly and publicly, appealing to all and offering them her gift of wisdom. Like a prophet, she wants all to respond to her words.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 8:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/8#v2)</div>
<p>8:2 The hilltop and crossroads provide maximum public access to all who pass by.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 8:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/8#v3)</div>
<p>8:3 In ancient Near Eastern cities, the city gates had built-in chambers for conducting legal proceedings and business transactions. It was an appropriate place for Wisdom to make her appeal to the most people.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 8:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/8#v6)</div>
<p>8:6-9 Wisdom is associated with truth, understanding, and knowledge, which are right, wholesome, plain, and clear. Wisdom detests deception, which is devious and crooked (cp. 1:2-7; 6:16-19).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 8:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/8#v10)</div>
<p>8:10-11 Choose: An ethical choice must be made between seeking money or valuables and seeking wisdom, which is far more valuable.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 8:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/8#v12)</div>
<p>8:12 Good judgment, knowledge, and discernment are only available to those who have Wisdom.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 8:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/8#v13)</div>
<p>8:13 Those who choose wisdom, i.e., those who fear the Lord (see 1:7; 9:10), will not speak in ways that harm others or bend the truth.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 8:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/8#v15)</div>
<p>8:15-16 The best rulers choose wisdom to guide them (e.g., 1 Kgs 3:16-28).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 8:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/8#v22)</div>
<p>8:22-25 formed me from the beginning . . . I was appointed in ages past: God’s wisdom has always existed. His wisdom is here personified, but wisdom itself is not a person. Wisdom does not exist outside of God; wisdom is an expression of his character and nature. Unlike pagan gods, God needs no outside counselor to give him instructions (see Isa 40:13-14). Jesus is the apex of God’s wisdom (see Col 1:15-17; 2:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 8:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/8#v27)</div>
<p>8:27-29 God used his wisdom to establish the created order, so wisdom can tell us how the world works and testifies to God’s greatness.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v30" data-v="30">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 8:30](/en/kjv/proverbs/8#v30)</div>
<p>8:30 architect: God’s wisdom guided the shaping of creation, bringing order out of chaos.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v32" data-v="32">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 8:32](/en/kjv/proverbs/8#v32)</div>
<p>8:32-36 Wisdom offers great rewards to those who listen to and follow wisdom.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v35" data-v="35">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 8:35](/en/kjv/proverbs/8#v35)</div>
<p>8:35-36 Life is the ultimate reward for the wise, while rejecting wisdom results in death. These verses point to eternal life and death (see Luke 23:43; 1 Cor 15).</p>
</div>
