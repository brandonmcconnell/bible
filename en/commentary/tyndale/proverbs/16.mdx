<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:1](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v1)</div>
<p>16:1 Our own plans will come to fruition only if God allows (see also 16:9, 33; Jas 4:13-16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v2)</div>
<p>16:2 God’s moral evaluation is what counts (see Gen 8:21; 1 Sam 13:14; 16:6-7; Luke 16:15). This proverb is repeated almost verbatim in Prov 21:2.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v3)</div>
<p>16:3 God must affirm our plans (16:1), so it makes sense to ask his help in accomplishing our goals (see Jas 4:13-16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v4)</div>
<p>16:4 The wicked will have trouble in this life and in death. Nothing is outside of God’s control (see also Rom 9:10-24; 2 Pet 2:9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v5)</div>
<p>16:5 God detests the proud, who refuse to commit their ways to the Lord (16:3) and mistakenly believe that they can accomplish their plans on their own (16:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v6)</div>
<p>16:6 God’s unfailing love and faithfulness are foundational in his covenant relationship with Israel (see Exod 15:13; 34:6-7; Num 14:18-19; Deut 7:9-11).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v8)</div>
<p>16:8 Better to have little: See also 15:16.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v10)</div>
<p>16:10-15 This series of sayings reflects on the king, who represents God’s power on earth.16:10 Divine wisdom refers to divinely inspired guidance that helps the king make judgments (e.g., 1 Kgs 3:28; see also Ps 72:2).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v11)</div>
<p>16:11 The message of this proverb is stated four times in the collection (cp. 11:1; 20:10, 23).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v12)</div>
<p>16:12-13 These proverbs presuppose a wise, godly king (see Isa 9:6-7). Many of Israel’s kings loved wrongdoing and hated truth (e.g., Ahab in 1 Kgs 21).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v14)</div>
<p>16:14-15 The king is powerful, so people are wise to avoid his anger and gain his favor.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v16)</div>
<p>16:16 Wisdom and good judgment may lead to wealth, but wealth cannot buy wisdom.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v18)</div>
<p>16:18 The pride of those who resist God and stubbornly rely on their own strength (16:5) is self-destructive and foolish because, apart from God’s help, no one has the resources required for life.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v25)</div>
<p>16:25 This proverb repeats 14:12.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v27)</div>
<p>16:27-29 Scoundrels, a troublemaker, and violent people all disrupt relationships, creating trouble with their harmful actions.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v31" data-v="31">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:31](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v31)</div>
<p>16:31 Godly people grow in wisdom as they grow older, so their gray hair is a crown (cp. 4:9; 12:4; 17:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v32" data-v="32">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:32](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v32)</div>
<p>16:32 Patience and self-control are far more valuable than brute force.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v33" data-v="33">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 16:33](/en/kjv/proverbs/16#v33)</div>
<p>16:33 We may throw the dice: These dice were not for gambling but were a means commissioned by God to determine his will (Exod 28:30; see also Josh 14:2; 1 Sam 10:17-27; 14:40-43).</p>
</div>

</div>
