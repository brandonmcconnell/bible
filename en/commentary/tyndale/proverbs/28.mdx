<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v4)</div>

</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v5)</div>
<p>28:5 Evil people seek gain by breaking the law. Justice ensures that they are punished and the righteous are rewarded.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v6)</div>
<p>28:6 Virtue is more valuable than material wealth. This proverb is repeated with a slightly different conclusion in 19:1.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v7)</div>
<p>28:7 their parents: This proverb applies to both fathers and mothers.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v8)</div>
<p>28:8 The law prohibited charging needy Israelites interest on loans (Exod 22:25; Lev 25:36; Deut 23:19). The rich were instructed to lend generously to the poor (Prov 3:27-28; 11:24; 28:27; 29:7, 14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v9)</div>
<p>28:9 A person who ignores the law shows no fear of the Lord (1:7), the lawgiver. That person’s prayers are hypocritical—why should God listen to them?</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v12)</div>
<p>28:12 When the wicked take charge: See also 28:28.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v13)</div>
<p>28:13 The wise admit their mistakes and sins and pursue correction (see Ps 32:1-5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v15)</div>
<p>28:15 A wicked ruler will not help the poor because his rule is not just. Like a roaring lion or an attacking bear, he destroys their lives.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v17)</div>
<p>28:17 tormented conscience: See 1 Sam 25:31; Rom 2:15; 13:5; cp. Heb 10:22. • Don’t protect him! Murder is such a serious offense that a fine could not replace the death penalty (see Num 35:32).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v19)</div>
<p>28:19 This proverb is repeated but with a different conclusion in 12:11.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:20](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v20)</div>
<p>28:20 quick riches: Wealth must be earned ethically, or it will cause harm and will not last (see 13:11, cp. 10:2; 11:18; 13:22; 21:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v21)</div>
<p>28:21 Rendering a wrong verdict based on a bribe is evil (17:23; 22:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:23](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v23)</div>
<p>28:23 The wise desire honest criticism of their faults so they can improve (10:17; 13:1, 10; 15:24, 31-32).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v24)</div>
<p>28:24 Callous children who exploit their parents are wicked (see Exod 20:12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v25)</div>
<p>28:25 Those who are greedy fight to control circumstances so as to gain riches. Trusting the Lord allows the wise to face life calmly and, ironically, to experience prosperity.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 28:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/28#v26)</div>
<p>28:26 The one who walks in wisdom listens to the advice of others; those who trust their own insight show themselves foolish.</p>
</div>
