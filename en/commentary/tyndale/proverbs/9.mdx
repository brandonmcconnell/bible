<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:1](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v1)</div>
<p>9:1-6 The invitation to join Wisdom for a lavish meal is met by a competing invitation in 9:13-18.9:1 The number seven can denote grandeur or fullness, so the seven columns represent the grandeur of Wisdom’s house, the place where it dwells.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v2)</div>
<p>9:2 In the ancient Near East, a great banquet would accompany a marriage, a military victory, or the making of a treaty. Wisdom’s celebration marks the beginning of a committed relationship with the hearer.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v3)</div>
<p>9:3 Throughout the ancient Near East, temples were built on the heights overlooking the city. A commitment to wisdom involves a commitment to the Lord (contrast 9:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v4)</div>
<p>9:4 Wisdom makes her appeal especially to the simple and those who lack good judgment because they have not yet decided between wisdom and folly. See 9:16, where Folly mimics and then perverts Wisdom’s words.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v6)</div>
<p>9:6 Wisdom gives people the ability to navigate life successfully.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v7)</div>
<p>9:7-9 These verses seem to create an awkward break between the invitations of Wisdom and Folly, but perhaps the purpose is to caution that, in contrast with inviting the simple (9:4), inviting mockers and the wicked is futile at best. Such people have already chosen their path and respond to Wisdom’s advice with violence. By contrast, the wise appreciate corrective advice; they welcome it and benefit from it.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v10)</div>
<p>9:10-12 Fear of the Lord: See study note on 1:7.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v13)</div>
<p>9:13-18 The woman named Folly also invites the simple and those who lack good judgment to join her for the banquet she has prepared. Her invitation and advice are antithetical to Wisdom’s (9:1-6).9:13 The woman named Folly is brash: Fools talk boldly and without shame but have nothing intelligent to say.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v14)</div>
<p>9:14 On the heights overlooking the city was the traditional location for a temple. Folly personifies false gods and goddesses that compete for the affection and loyalty of God’s people (contrast 9:2-3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v17)</div>
<p>9:17 Like Wisdom, Folly has prepared a banquet for her guests (cp. 9:2-5). However, this meal is dishonest and perverse.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 9:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/9#v18)</div>
<p>9:18 Folly’s dinner guests end up in the depths of the grave, in contrast to the reward for Wisdom’s guests (see 9:6).</p>
</div>

</div>
