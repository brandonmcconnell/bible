<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:1](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v1)</div>
<p>25:1–29:27 During the reign of King Hezekiah (728–686 BC), wise men (advisers) collected these proverbs of Solomon and added them to the collection.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:2](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v2)</div>
<p>25:2-3 This warning reminds young men entering royal service that some things cannot be understood, including the king’s sometimes mysterious reasoning (e.g., 2 Sam 11:14-25; 24:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v4)</div>
<p>25:4-5 As silver is ready for use once it is refined of impurities, the king’s court can do justice when wicked people are removed. A little bit of evil can spoil much good (Eccl 10:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v6)</div>
<p>25:6-7 To gain an audience with the king, a wise person will practice humility instead of pride (cp. Matt 20:20-28).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v7)</div>
<p>25:7 Rash actions often result in shame.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v9)</div>
<p>25:9-10 See 11:13; 20:19.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v11)</div>
<p>25:11-12 Timely advice and valid criticism are precious, beautiful, and rare (see also 15:23; 26:7, 9), and should be welcomed (see 10:17; 13:1, 10; 15:24, 31-32; 17:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v13)</div>
<p>25:13 Snow would be a welcome relief in Israel’s hot, dry summers (cp. 26:1); trustworthy messengers refresh their employer by enabling him or her to plan for the future.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v14)</div>
<p>25:14 In Israel, rain is rare. When rain does not follow the appearance of storm clouds, it is like the disappointment caused by someone who gives empty promises. Cp. Jude’s description of false teachers (Jude 1:12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v16)</div>
<p>25:16-17 Honey in moderation is tasty and healthy (24:13-14); too much will cause vomiting. This principle can be applied to other areas of life; for example, it is good to visit your neighbors, but not too often.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v18)</div>
<p>25:18 Lying can have deadly consequences (see 6:16-19; 14:5, 25).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:21](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v21)</div>
<p>25:21-22 Contrary to expectation, compassion toward an enemy is more effective than anger (see Rom 12:20).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v24)</div>
<p>25:24 This proverb repeats 21:9.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:26](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v26)</div>
<p>25:26 A spring or fountain that yields fresh water gives life. When godly people allow the wicked to dominate them, what was life-giving becomes deadly.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 25:27](/en/kjv/proverbs/25#v27)</div>
<p>25:27-28 The wise have self-control and cannot easily be dominated.</p>
</div>

</div>
