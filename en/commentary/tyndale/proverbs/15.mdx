</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:3](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v3)</div>
<p>15:3 Knowing that thethe eyes of the Lord is watching everywhere is motivation for wise behavior.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:5](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v5)</div>
<p>15:5 Wisdom requires learning from correction.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:6](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v6)</div>
<p>15:6 God blesses the godly with wealth (10:22; cp. 10:15 and 14:24). The wicked may also gain wealth, but it will bring trouble rather than blessing (11:4, 18; 13:11; 21:6; 22:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:8](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v8)</div>
<p>15:8 God looks beyond the outward actions of sacrifice and prayers to see if they reflect the heart’s attitude (see also 21:27; Ps 40:6-8; Mic 6:6-8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v11)</div>
<p>15:11 Death and Destruction (Hebrew Sheol and Abaddon) personify the destiny of the dead. Sheol is the abode of the dead; Abaddon is a synonym for Sheol that has an added implication of punishment. • God knows all secrets (see Ps 139:1-12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v12)</div>
<p>15:12 Mockers cannot become wise because they keep criticism at a distance (cp. 10:17; 13:1, 10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v13)</div>
<p>15:13 What people feel affects how they present themselves (cp. 15:30).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:14](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v14)</div>
<p>15:14 Whether we enjoy knowledge or trash reveals whether we are on the path of wisdom or of folly.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v15)</div>
<p>15:15 Personality affects our outlook on life.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:16](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v16)</div>
<p>15:16 A healthy fear for the Lord counteracts inner turmoil and brings inner peace. • Better to have little: See also 16:8.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v17)</div>
<p>15:17 This better than proverb shows the relative value of wealth. A substantial meal (steak) is good, but love is better.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:18](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v18)</div>
<p>15:18 Controlling one’s anger is a sign of wisdom (see also Jas 1:19-20).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:19](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v19)</div>
<p>15:19 A lazy person can find all kinds of obstacles (e.g., a path blocked with briers) to avoid work (see 10:4, 26; 12:11, 14; 13:4; 14:4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v22)</div>
<p>15:22 An individual might not think of all the angles of a problem; a wise person invites advice from many advisers.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:24](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v24)</div>
<p>15:24 A wise person avoids death by choosing the path of life.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v25" data-v="25">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:25](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v25)</div>
<p>15:25 The proud think that they are self-sufficient. Widows in the ancient Near East were just the opposite—completely vulnerable and without means of support or protection.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v29" data-v="29">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:29](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v29)</div>
<p>15:29 The heart behind prayers determines whether God hears them.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v30" data-v="30">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:30](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v30)</div>
<p>15:30 Just as how we feel affects our demeanor (see 15:13), what we experience (such as another person’s cheerful look or good news) can affect how we feel.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v33" data-v="33">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 15:33](/en/kjv/proverbs/15#v33)</div>
<p>15:33 Fear of the Lord fosters humility because proper regard for God counteracts our delusions of self-sufficiency.</p>
</div>

</div>
