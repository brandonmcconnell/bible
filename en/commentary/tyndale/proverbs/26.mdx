</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">

<p>26:3 A fool is like a dumb animal that can only be guided by brute force (see 10:13; 14:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:4](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v4)</div>
<p>26:4-5 Proverbs are often context sensitive. Whether or not to answer the foolish arguments of fools depends on what kind of fool and what kind of situation.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:7](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v7)</div>
<p>26:7 A fool might know a proverb but not be able to use it correctly. Cp. 26:9.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:9](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v9)</div>
<p>26:9 A proverb misapplied by a fool might actually cause harm. Cp. 26:7.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:10](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v10)</div>
<p>26:10 A fool or a bystander might easily be lazy (see 10:4-6) or incompetent (see 26:6-7). An employer should beware!</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:11](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v11)</div>
<p>26:11 Even when foolishness brings terrible consequences, a fool persists in it (17:10; see also 2 Pet 2:22).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:12](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v12)</div>
<p>26:12 Fools have hope because they might recognize their folly and seek advice. However, those who think they are wise will remain ignorant.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:13](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v13)</div>

</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:15](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v15)</div>

</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:17](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v17)</div>
<p>26:17 The wise avoid getting involved in other people’s arguments (cp. 26:21).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">

</div>

<div className="cmv-head">[Proverbs 26:22](/en/kjv/proverbs/26#v22)</div>

</div>

<p>26:23-26 People sometimes hide evil attitudes and wicked intentions through flattery. In the end, the true hatred of smooth talkers will be exposed.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v27" data-v="27">

</div>
