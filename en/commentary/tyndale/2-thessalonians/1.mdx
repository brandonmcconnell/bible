<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Thessalonians 1:1](/en/kjv/2-thessalonians/1#v1)</div>
<p>1:1-2 Paul follows ancient letter writing customs, but instead of the normal greeting (Greek chairein) he blesses them with grace (Greek charis) and peace.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Thessalonians 1:2](/en/kjv/2-thessalonians/1#v2)</div>
<p>1:2 God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ are coequal as the source of grace and peace. The Thessalonians’ hope was bound to God’s grace (2:16), and even in persecution they could experience God-given peace (3:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Thessalonians 1:3](/en/kjv/2-thessalonians/1#v3)</div>
<p>1:3-10 After thanking God for the Thessalonian church (1:3-4), Paul writes about persecution (1:4), which had intensified since his first letter (1 Thes 1:6; 2:14; 3:3-4). God will relieve their suffering (2 Thes 1:5-10) and judge their persecutors.1:3 Dear brothers and sisters: Greek adelphoi, a generic term that refers to members of the same family, both male and female. • thank God: Paul begins with thanksgiving for the Thessalonians’ faith, love, and “endurance” (1:4). These fundamental Christian virtues (see 1 Cor 13:13; Col 1:4-5; Heb 10:21-24; 1 Pet 2:1-22) were maturing in the church despite the persecution they endured (2 Thes 1:5).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Thessalonians 1:4](/en/kjv/2-thessalonians/1#v4)</div>
<p>1:4 Paul’s boasting to other churches about the Thessalonians’ endurance and faithfulness would stimulate the church’s resolve to endure and remain faithful in the face of great hostility (1 Thes 2:14; 3:3-4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Thessalonians 1:5](/en/kjv/2-thessalonians/1#v5)</div>
<p>1:5 Those called by God to enter his Kingdom will suffer for it (Acts 14:22; 1 Thes 2:12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Thessalonians 1:7](/en/kjv/2-thessalonians/1#v7)</div>
<p>1:7 rest: Relief from suffering. • Elsewhere, Paul speaks of Christ’s “coming” (2:1; 1 Thes 2:19; 3:13; 4:15; 5:23); here, he speaks of the event as the “appearing” or “unveiling” of the Lord Jesus, who cannot currently be seen physically (cp. 1 Pet 1:7-8; 4:13).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Thessalonians 1:9](/en/kjv/2-thessalonians/1#v9)</div>
<p>1:9 They will be punished for rejecting the message about Christ (1:8). • Eternal destruction (see 1 Thes 5:3; 1 Tim 6:9) is irrevocable. • Separated from the Lord refers to the source of the judgment to come (Isa 2:10, 19, 21).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Thessalonians 1:10](/en/kjv/2-thessalonians/1#v10)</div>
<p>1:10 that day: The day of the Lord (2:2; 1 Thes 5:2-4; see “The Day of the Lord” Theme Note).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Thessalonians 1:11](/en/kjv/2-thessalonians/1#v11)</div>
<p>1:11-12 Paul assures the Thessalonian believers of God’s promised justice, both for them and their persecutors (1:5-10). Because future deliverance means carrying out present responsibilities, Paul prays that they will be found worthy at Christ’s return.1:11 enable you to live a life worthy of his call: Paul had previously reminded the Thessalonians that God called them to conduct themselves “in a way that God would consider worthy” (1 Thes 2:12; see also Eph 4:1; Phil 1:27; Col 1:10). • Paul prayed that God would give the Thessalonian Christians power to live and work in a manner that would please God.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[2 Thessalonians 1:12](/en/kjv/2-thessalonians/1#v12)</div>
<p>1:12 Though the name of our Lord Jesus was rejected by the Thessalonians’ persecutors (1:8), Jesus will ultimately be glorified because of the lives of the believers. Further, they will be honored along with him (2:14; 1 Thes 2:12; cp. Rom 8:17-18; Col 3:4). Cp. Isa 66:5.</p>
</div>

</div>
