</div>

</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Job 37:20](/en/kjv/job/37#v20)</div>
<p>37:20 speak when they are confused? Or speak without being swallowed up? Punishment as severe as death might result from contesting God’s judgment (cp. 13:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Job 37:22](/en/kjv/job/37#v22)</div>
<p>37:22 the mountain of God (Hebrew from Zaphon): In the literature of the ancient city of Ugarit, Zaphon was the northern mountainous abode of Baal; here it figuratively refers to God’s lofty home in the heavens (see Isa 14:13-14).</p>
</div>
