<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Exodus 22:22](/en/kjv/exodus/22#v22)</div>
<p>22:22-23 The widow, the orphan, and the foreigner were in a helpless and economically disadvantaged position. God, by his protective stance toward these persons, demonstrated that he does not value people on the basis of their power or wealth, the standards accepted by most humans. Instead, God values people because they share his image (Gen 1:27).</p>
</div>

</div>
