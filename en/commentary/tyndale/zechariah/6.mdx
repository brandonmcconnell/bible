<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Zechariah 6:1](/en/kjv/zechariah/6#v1)</div>
<p>6:1-8 In Zechariah’s first and last visions, God sends horses to patrol the earth. Both visions show God’s sovereignty and his concern for the nations, a vital component of Zechariah’s message of comfort and encouragement to postexilic Judah.6:1 Chariots symbolize the swift and decisive power of God’s intervention in human affairs. • The two bronze mountains are enhanced images of the two bronze pillars that once flanked the entrance to Solomon’s Temple (1 Kgs 7:13-22). Bronze symbolizes the impregnable strength of God’s dwelling.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Zechariah 6:2](/en/kjv/zechariah/6#v2)</div>
<p>6:2-3 red . . . black . . . white . . . dappled-gray horses: Zechariah attaches no particular significance to the colors of the horses in his vision (cp. Rev 6:1-8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Zechariah 6:5](/en/kjv/zechariah/6#v5)</div>
<p>6:5 The four spirits (or the four winds) of heaven are divine council members who report to God on their reconnaissance missions to the four compass points (see 2:6). The whole world is under God’s dominion. The teams of chariot horses are agents of God’s judgment.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Zechariah 6:7](/en/kjv/zechariah/6#v7)</div>
<p>6:7 The teams of horses are portrayed as powerful and eager to do the Lord’s work (6:5). They move only at the Lord’s command.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Zechariah 6:8](/en/kjv/zechariah/6#v8)</div>
<p>6:8 the Lord summoned me: The series of visions closes with a direct word from the Lord to Zechariah. In bypassing the interpreting angel, God emphasizes the sure and effective implementation of his word to Israel. • have vented the anger of my Spirit (literally have given my Spirit rest): “Spirit” can mean “anger,” as it does here (see Judg 8:3; Isa 33:11).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Zechariah 6:9](/en/kjv/zechariah/6#v9)</div>
<p>6:9-15 This authoritative message accompanies Zechariah’s eighth vision (6:1-8). Jeshua’s symbolic coronation as both king and priest was not an actual political arrangement for Judah; it probably symbolizes the coming of the Messiah, the Branch (6:12; cp. 3:8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Zechariah 6:10](/en/kjv/zechariah/6#v10)</div>
<p>6:10 Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah were apparently couriers designated by Jews in Babylon to carry donations to the Temple building fund.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Zechariah 6:11](/en/kjv/zechariah/6#v11)</div>
<p>6:11 This crown was probably made of two bands of metal, one gold and one silver. Each band represented one of the offices (king and priest) to which Jeshua was appointed.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Zechariah 6:12](/en/kjv/zechariah/6#v12)</div>
<p>6:12-13 The Branch is a title for the Messiah (see 3:8), whom Jeshua represents. • he will build the Temple of the Lord: Just as Jeshua the high priest helped to build the Temple in Jerusalem (Ezra 3:1-2, 8-9; 5:2), Jesus the Messiah would build the eternal heavenly Temple through his death, burial, and resurrection (John 2:19-22; 4:23-24; Eph 2:19-22; Heb 8:1-2). • The Messiah would rule as king, a role associated with David and the tribe of Judah (2 Sam 7:12-16). • He will also serve as priest from his throne: The Messiah’s priestly role is associated with Aaron and the tribe of Levi (Exod 29:44). • Jeshua’s crown represents his two roles. Melchizedek also fulfilled the double functions of priest and king (Gen 14:17-20), as does the Messiah (Ps 110:4; Heb 7:1-3, 15-17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Zechariah 6:14](/en/kjv/zechariah/6#v14)</div>
<p>6:14 The symbolic crown that united kingship and priesthood was placed in the Temple as a memorial to the donors of gold and silver. It was also a permanent reminder of Jeshua’s coronation as priest-king and a visual aid for priests in teaching this new development. • Josiah was honored for his role as broker for the meeting between Zechariah and the three former exiles.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Zechariah 6:15](/en/kjv/zechariah/6#v15)</div>
<p>6:15 Exiled Jews in Babylon and other distant lands helped to rebuild the Temple; all Israel could identify with the structure.</p>
</div>
