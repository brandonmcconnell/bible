<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 23:1](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/23#v1)</div>
<p>23:1-2 When David . . . appointed his son Solomon as Israel’s king (cp. 1 Kgs 1), David outlined the organizational structure of the kingdom and made all the necessary provisions for the Temple’s functioning. • The account of Solomon’s coronation continues in 1 Chr 28–29; the intervening chapters (23:3–27:24) record in detail the organization of the kingdom.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 23:3](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/23#v3)</div>
<p>23:3-5 The Levites are recorded according to their roles rather than their families.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 23:6](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/23#v6)</div>
<p>23:6-23 David divided the Levites into divisions according to their ancestral families. The number of available clerics far exceeded the requirements for a single Temple; the divisions provided a necessary time-sharing mechanism that enabled all the priests and Levites to serve in the Temple periodically (cp. Luke 1:5, 8).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 23:24](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/23#v24)</div>
<p>23:24-27 In the census of 23:3, individuals were counted at age thirty, but the actual registration of the divisions included those twenty years old or older (cp. Num 1:3). The age of entry into Levitical service apparently varied over time according to need.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 23:28](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/23#v28)</div>
<p>23:28-32 The work of the Levites was to assist the priests, which they accomplished in various ways: maintaining the Temple, setting out the sacred bread and other offerings, singing, and helping the priests with the sacrifices. These duties were allotted to different family divisions of Levites (23:4-6).</p>
</div>
