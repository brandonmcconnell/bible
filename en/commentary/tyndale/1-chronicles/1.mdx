<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 1:1](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/1#v1)</div>
<p>1:1–2:2 The Chronicler begins by showing Israel’s place among the nations, tracing their line of descent from Adam to Jacob, using material from Genesis.1:1-4 An overview of the generations between Adam and the sons of Noah (cp. Gen 5:3-32).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 1:5](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/1#v5)</div>
<p>1:5-23 The record of Noah’s descendants establishes Abraham’s place in history. The ethnic backdrop of the seventy nations of the world provides the context for Israel’s history (cp. Gen 10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 1:13](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/1#v13)</div>
<p>1:13 ancestor of the Hittites: Hebrew ancestor of Heth.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 1:24](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/1#v24)</div>
<p>1:24-27 The summary of Shem’s descendants ends with Abraham (cp. Gen 11:10-26).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 1:28](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/1#v28)</div>
<p>1:28-33 Using the same pattern as the author of Genesis, the Chronicler traces separately the descendants of Abraham apart from Isaac, listing the sons of Ishmael (1:29-31; cp. Gen 25:12-15) and the sons of Keturah (1 Chr 1:32-33; cp. Gen 25:1-3) before detailing the descendants in the chosen family line.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v34" data-v="34">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 1:34](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/1#v34)</div>
<p>1:34–2:2 Isaac’s genealogy first lists the descendants of Esau (1:35-54) and continues with Israel. The history of Edom is given in three components: the sons of Esau (1:35-37), the descendants of Seir (1:38-42), and the kings who ruled in the land of Edom (1:43-54). This arrangement follows Gen 36.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v51" data-v="51">
<div className="cmv-head">[1 Chronicles 1:51](/en/kjv/1-chronicles/1#v51)</div>
<p>1:51-54 The Chronicler lists the clan leaders of Edom as far as his own sources allowed (see Gen 36:40-43).</p>
</div>
