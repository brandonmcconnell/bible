<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 17:1](/en/kjv/genesis/17#v1)</div>
<p>17:1-27 God now gave the family signs that the promises would be fulfilled. He changed Abram’s name to Abraham (17:1-8), instituted the rite of circumcision as the sign of the covenant (17:9-14, 23-27), and changed Sarai’s name to Sarah (17:15-22).17:1 El-Shaddai: This name for God emphasizes his power (see also 28:3; 35:11; 43:14; 48:3; 49:25). • Serve me faithfully and live a blameless life: Being a blessing to the nations required obedience from Abram; his conduct would be guided by Almighty God.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 17:4](/en/kjv/genesis/17#v4)</div>
<p>17:4-5 God guaranteed his promise by changing Abram’s name. Abram referred to his noble lineage, as Terah was the “exalted father” (11:27). His new name, Abraham (Hebrew ’ab hamon) was a wordplay on the promise of his own progeny (see also John 8:31-59; Rom 4:16-17; Gal 3:7, 15-19, 29). Whenever the new name was used, he and his household would remember that a multitude of nations would issue from him.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 17:6](/en/kjv/genesis/17#v6)</div>
<p>17:6 kings will be among them! This is the first indication that Israel would become a monarchy (see also 35:11; 36:31; Num 24:7; Deut 17:14-18; 28:36).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 17:7](/en/kjv/genesis/17#v7)</div>
<p>17:7-8 The land of Canaan was to be an everlasting possession for the descendants of Abraham; the Lord would be their God forever (see Jer 31:31-40; Zech 8:8; Luke 1:68-79; Rev 21:1-4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 17:9](/en/kjv/genesis/17#v9)</div>
<p>17:9-14 God gave circumcision as a confirming sign that reminded all households of loyalty to the covenant.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 17:14](/en/kjv/genesis/17#v14)</div>
<p>17:14 will be cut off: This punishment seems to have several applications. A person could be exiled from society or put to death by the community; most often it warned that a person might die prematurely as God cut him off from the land of the living (see Exod 31:14; Lev 7:20-27; 17:3-4; 20:17-18; 23:28-29; Num 15:30-31; see also Ps 31:22; Ezek 21:4; Rom 9:3; 11:22). Failure to be circumcised was a serious violation (see Exod 4:24-26; cp. Gal 5:2-4).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 17:15](/en/kjv/genesis/17#v15)</div>
<p>17:15-16 Sarah: The new name, fitting for one who would be the mother of kings, was a milestone in Sarah’s calling and brought attention to the promise.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 17:17](/en/kjv/genesis/17#v17)</div>
<p>17:17-18 Abraham laughed (Hebrew yitskhaq) because the promise seemed unbelievable; he had begun to believe that his line would come through Ishmael. But Abraham and Sarah would have a son of their own.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 17:19](/en/kjv/genesis/17#v19)</div>
<p>17:19 The name Isaac (Hebrew yitskhaq, “he laughs”) would constantly recall Abraham’s disbelieving laughter when he heard the promise. It was also a reminder of God’s favor and his pleasure in the birth (cp. 21:6).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 17:20](/en/kjv/genesis/17#v20)</div>
<p>17:20-21 Ishmael would not be abandoned; his family would prosper (see 25:13-16), but the covenant promises were for Isaac.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 17:23](/en/kjv/genesis/17#v23)</div>
<p>17:23-27 Having received God’s word about Isaac, Abraham immediately complied with God’s instructions. He implemented the rite of circumcision as an act of faith; it signified their participation in the covenant (cp. Rom 4:11-12; Gal 5:2-6, 11; 6:15; Phil 3:2-3; Col 2:11-12; 1 Pet 3:21).</p>
</div>

</div>
