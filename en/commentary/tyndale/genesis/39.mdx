<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 39:1](/en/kjv/genesis/39#v1)</div>
<p>39:1–47:31 Joseph began as a slave, alienated from his brothers and separated from his father; he ended as Pharaoh’s viceroy. Through the trips to Egypt, the covenant family went from the brink of apostasy, divided by jealousy and deception, to being reconciled and united by Judah’s intercession and Joseph’s forgiveness.39:1-23 Joseph’s integrity in Potiphar’s service contrasts with Judah’s moral failure (ch 38). God was with Joseph (39:2-3, 21, 23) and enabled him to prosper and be a blessing (see study notes on 12:1-9; 28:16-22) despite his slavery and imprisonment.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 39:2](/en/kjv/genesis/39#v2)</div>
<p>39:2 God is mentioned for the first time (apart from 38:7-10) since Jacob built his altar in Bethel (35:1-15); his covenant name, “the Lord,” is used for the first time since Jacob left Laban (31:49).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 39:5](/en/kjv/genesis/39#v5)</div>
<p>39:5 God began to bless Egypt through Joseph (see 22:18).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v6" data-v="6">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 39:6](/en/kjv/genesis/39#v6)</div>
<p>39:6-10 One of the major motifs of wisdom literature (see study note on 37:2–50:26) is to warn young people that immorality will lead them to disaster (cp. Prov 5–7). Joseph was able to resist temptation because he had godly wisdom—he was guided by the fear of the Lord (Prov 1:7; 9:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 39:14](/en/kjv/genesis/39#v14)</div>
<p>39:14 Though Potiphar’s wife was addressing slaves, she appealed to them as fellow Egyptians (us) to enlist them as witnesses against the despised Hebrew (see 43:32) who had won Potiphar’s trust.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 39:19](/en/kjv/genesis/39#v19)</div>
<p>39:19-20 This was the second time that Joseph, while faithfully doing the right thing, was thrown into bondage with his clothing used deceptively as evidence (cp. 37:23-24, 31-33).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v21" data-v="21">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 39:21](/en/kjv/genesis/39#v21)</div>
<p>39:21-23 Joseph thrived in prison because God was with him. Each time Joseph prospered, he was put in charge of something.</p>
</div>
