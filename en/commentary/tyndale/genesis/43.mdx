<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 43:1](/en/kjv/genesis/43#v1)</div>
<p>43:1-7 As the famine continued, Jacob’s family needed more grain, but they could not return to Egypt without Benjamin (42:16, 20). Jacob realized that he was in a bind; he needed more food but was loath to lose Benjamin.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 43:8](/en/kjv/genesis/43#v8)</div>
<p>43:8-10 Judah broke the deadlock by taking responsibility for Benjamin’s well-being, thus succeeding where Reuben failed. His action was fitting, since it had been his idea to sell Joseph to the Ishmaelites (37:26-27) instead of killing him. Now he would secure Benjamin’s safety with his own life (see 44:18-34).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 43:11](/en/kjv/genesis/43#v11)</div>
<p>43:11-13 Jacob provided everything he could to ensure a favorable reception from the Egyptian governor (the man; cp. 32:13-21).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 43:13](/en/kjv/genesis/43#v13)</div>
<p>43:13-14 Jacob finally entrusted his family’s future to God Almighty, the divine title that stresses God’s power (see also 17:1; 28:3; 35:11; 48:3; 1 Kgs 19:10, 14; Rev 21:22).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 43:16](/en/kjv/genesis/43#v16)</div>
<p>43:16 When Joseph saw Benjamin with them: Joseph now knew that Benjamin was well (see study note on 42:1–44:34). The feast was both a celebration and a test.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 43:18](/en/kjv/genesis/43#v18)</div>
<p>43:18 The brothers were terrified: Cp. 32:6-12. Their guilt would not let them see that something good might happen.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 43:19](/en/kjv/genesis/43#v19)</div>
<p>43:19-22 The brothers, completely vulnerable and feeling the weight of God’s judgment on their consciences, approached the manager in desperation.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v24" data-v="24">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 43:24](/en/kjv/genesis/43#v24)</div>
<p>43:24 Joseph no longer treated his brothers harshly, but provided kind hospitality.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 43:26](/en/kjv/genesis/43#v26)</div>
<p>43:26 For the second time, the brothers bowed . . . before Joseph in fulfillment of his first dream (37:7; see 42:6-7; cp. 33:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v29" data-v="29">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 43:29](/en/kjv/genesis/43#v29)</div>
<p>43:29 May God be gracious to you, my son: Joseph’s blessing to his full brother fulfilled Jacob’s prayer (43:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v30" data-v="30">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 43:30](/en/kjv/genesis/43#v30)</div>
<p>43:30 he was overcome with emotion for his brother: Cp. 42:24. Joseph’s tears were of painful memories and years of lost fellowship, as well as of joy and thanksgiving at seeing his brother again. • He went into his private room: He did not yet plan to reveal his identity.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v33" data-v="33">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 43:33](/en/kjv/genesis/43#v33)</div>
<p>43:33 to their amazement: This ruler knew more about the brothers than seemed possible (cp. 44:15).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v34" data-v="34">
<div className="cmv-head">[Genesis 43:34](/en/kjv/genesis/43#v34)</div>
<p>43:34 The brothers were confronted with generous and gracious dealings from God through Joseph, who tested their tolerance by reenacting the favoritism toward Rachel’s son that had galvanized their earlier hostility.</p>
</div>

</div>
