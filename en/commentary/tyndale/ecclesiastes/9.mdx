<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 9:1](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/9#v1)</div>
<p>9:1 No one knows whether God will show them favor: There is no guarantee that righteousness will be rewarded in this life (cp. 3:17-22; 8:10-15; 12:14).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 9:2](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/9#v2)</div>
<p>9:2 The destiny all people share is death.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 9:3](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/9#v3)</div>
<p>9:3 When punishments, including death, are delayed, people continue their own mad course, perhaps intensifying their sin and foolishness.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 9:4](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/9#v4)</div>
<p>9:4-6 The hope for enjoyment is only as alive as the one who is hoping. In this sense, it is better to be alive (cp. 6:3; 7:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 9:7](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/9#v7)</div>
<p>9:7-10 Though death is inevitable, enjoying life is beneficial.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 9:10](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/9#v10)</div>
<p>9:10 to the grave: Hebrew to Sheol. In the Old Testament, Sheol is the abode of the dead. It is not necessarily associated with punishment.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 9:11](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/9#v11)</div>
<p>9:11 decided by chance: We cannot control the outcome (cp. 7:13).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 9:13](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/9#v13)</div>
<p>9:13-18 This poor man’s particular wise action was effective. Wisdom is better than power, strength, or weapons (see 4:13; 7:9, 19; 10:4), yet the worthy are not always honored, and even the wise are forgotten (see 2:16; 4:16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 9:18](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/9#v18)</div>
<p>9:18–10:1 one sinner can destroy . . . a little foolishness spoils: Sin and foolishness can undo a lot of good in a very short time.</p>
</div>

</div>
