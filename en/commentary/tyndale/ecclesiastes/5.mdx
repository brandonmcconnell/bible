<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 5:4](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/5#v4)</div>
<p>5:4-5 Keep all the promises you make to him: This is virtually a direct quote from the law (Deut 23:21-23; see also Prov 12:22; 20:25). Vows to God were voluntary, but once they were made, it was obligatory to keep them.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 5:7](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/5#v7)</div>
<p>5:7 Fear God: Reverence for God is the foundation for worthwhile words and useful activities (see 12:1; Prov 1:7).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 5:8](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/5#v8)</div>
<p>5:8-9 For every official is under orders from higher up, and matters of justice get lost in red tape and bureaucracy. Even the king milks the land for his own profit! Or For one official watches over another, and higher officials are over them. The land benefits from a king who cultivates the field. The NLT understands these verses to mean that bureaucracy brings oppression rather than justice, all the way up to the king (see also 1 Sam 8:11-18). This should not be surprising because that is how bureaucracies tend to function. Others interpret the verses along the lines of the alternate translation above: The king and his officials provide oversight to restrain injustice and oppression. The former view is more in keeping with the realistic perspective of the Teacher.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 5:10](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/5#v10)</div>
<p>5:10 Because we are created as spiritual and not just physical beings, possessions and wealth can never completely satisfy us.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 5:12](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/5#v12)</div>
<p>5:12 People who work hard sleep well: Hard work and moderation yield the formula for a peaceful, productive life. Pursuing wealth leads to unnecessary anxieties (5:3).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 5:13](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/5#v13)</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">

<p>5:16-17 Working simply to gain possessions is like working for the wind; it leads to being frustrated, discouraged, and angry.</p>
</div>
