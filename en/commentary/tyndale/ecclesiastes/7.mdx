<div className="cmv" id="v1" data-v="1">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:1](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v1)</div>
<p>7:1-4 These proverbs are similar to the sayings in the book of Proverbs.7:1 The effort to pursue luxuries such as costly perfume is better spent seeking a good reputation for wisdom and righteousness (Prov 22:1; 28:6). • the day you die is better than the day you are born: There is a sense of relief when the troubles of life are over. The difficulties of life can make one look forward to the peace of death (Eccl 1:18; 2:22; 4:2-3; 12:1).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v2" data-v="2">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:2](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v2)</div>
<p>7:2-6 Frivolous merry-making is foolish when wisdom demands sobriety about death (see also 2:12-13).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v3" data-v="3">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:3](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v3)</div>
<p>7:3-4 refining influence: Thinking soberly about death leads us to see the severity of God’s curse on sin and convinces us of the need to enjoy life wisely (9:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:5](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v5)</div>
<p>7:5-6 Being praised by a fool and enjoying a fool’s laughter are short-lived and worthless. Being criticized by a wise person can lead to real gain (Prov 17:10).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:8](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v8)</div>
<p>7:8 Finishing a project by exercising patience is better than starting a project with the pride of boastful words.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v9" data-v="9">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:9](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v9)</div>
<p>7:9 anger labels you a fool: Cp. Prov 14:29; 29:11; see “Anger” Theme Note.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:11](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v11)</div>
<p>7:11-12 Wisdom and money are powerful means to bring us benefit. • but only wisdom can save your life: See 7:17; Prov 10:2; 11:4.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v13" data-v="13">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:13](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v13)</div>
<p>7:13 God’s sovereign acts are irresistible; it is useless to counter or avoid his purposes.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:14](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v14)</div>
<p>7:14 Enjoying prosperity is beneficial, but such gifts are fleeting. The wise person accepts God’s sovereign hand in everything (cp. Phil 4:11-13).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:16](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v16)</div>
<p>7:16 However hard we work, we could always do more, and we can destroy ourselves in the process.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v17" data-v="17">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:17](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v17)</div>
<p>7:17 don’t be too wicked either: Wicked foolishness can lead to an early death (e.g., 1 Sam 25).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:19](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v19)</div>
<p>7:19 One wise person: E.g., see 9:14-15; 2 Sam 20:15-22.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:20](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v20)</div>
<p>7:20 Not a single person . . . always good and never sins: Cp. 1 Kgs 8:46; Prov 20:9; Rom 3:23.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v22" data-v="22">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:22](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v22)</div>
<p>7:22 It is right to forgive and be gracious toward others’ indiscretions or slips of the tongue.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v23" data-v="23">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:23](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v23)</div>
<p>7:23-25 it didn’t work: The Teacher was not able to find the wisdom that he sought or the reason for things. Such reasons are hidden in the mind of God (3:11; 8:17).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v26" data-v="26">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:26](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v26)</div>
<p>7:26 a seductive woman: Cp. Prov 5; 6:20–7:27.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v28" data-v="28">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:28](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v28)</div>
<p>7:28 The NLT adds the phrase is virtuous (based on 7:29) to give the sense of the verse. Virtue is extremely rare: In his own experience, the Teacher found only a few men, and no women, with that quality.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v29" data-v="29">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 7:29](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/7#v29)</div>
<p>7:29 After much searching, the Teacher did find that humanity’s downward path from God’s created order (Gen 1:27-28; 2:23-25; 3:1-19) was repeated by Adam and Eve’s descendants (cp. Rom 3:10-18, 23).</p>
</div>
