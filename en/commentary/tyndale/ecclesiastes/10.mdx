<div className="cmv" id="v4" data-v="4">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 10:4](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/10#v4)</div>
<p>10:4 A boss can be anyone in authority.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v5" data-v="5">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 10:5](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/10#v5)</div>
<p>10:5-7 The unjust and destructive delegation of authority to those incapable of using it wisely is a grave mistake. Favoritism, nepotism, extortion, and bribery can place the most reckless fools in the most powerful positions.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v7" data-v="7">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 10:7](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/10#v7)</div>
<p>10:7 servants riding . . . princes walking: Cp. Prov 19:10.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v8" data-v="8">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 10:8](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/10#v8)</div>
<p>10:8-9 Every activity requires wisdom. Wisdom is evident in the safety precautions taken by those engaged in every profession, yet there is always a degree of risk.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v10" data-v="10">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 10:10](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/10#v10)</div>
<p>10:10 wisdom . . . helps you succeed: In all occupations, there are tools that must be kept in good order to be most efficient. Simply sharpening the blade of a dull ax can be a significant act of wisdom for those whose livelihood depends on it.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v11" data-v="11">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 10:11](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/10#v11)</div>
<p>10:11 One purpose of charming a snake is to keep it from biting, but a snake charmer risks being bitten before it is charmed. Some occupations are so hazardous that the wise do not engage in them at all.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v12" data-v="12">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 10:12](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/10#v12)</div>
<p>10:12 Fools are destroyed by such things as foolish promises, getting caught in false testimony, and making the wrong people angry (see 5:6; Prov 17:20; 18:6-7; 19:5, 9).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v14" data-v="14">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 10:14](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/10#v14)</div>
<p>10:14 they chatter on and on: Fools are always talking about nothing (see 5:3; 6:11; Prov 10:19; 17:28). • no one can predict the future: The wise will not waste time in foolish talking nor suffer its consequences (see Jas 4:13-16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v15" data-v="15">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 10:15](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/10#v15)</div>
<p>10:15-20 The audience for this speech might have been an assembly of government workers. How should one rule when power and wealth invite a lack of accountability?10:15 Fools are so unaccustomed to work that when they actually try to work, they become exhausted.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v16" data-v="16">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 10:16](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/10#v16)</div>
<p>10:16-17 A servant lacks leadership skills. • feast at the proper time: Wise leaders take their leisure only after the work is finished, not in the morning before work has even started. Lazy leaders weaken a society or organization.</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v18" data-v="18">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 10:18](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/10#v18)</div>
<p>10:18 Delay in repairing a roof leads to drooping rafters and eventually to the ruin of the home (see Prov 12:27; 20:4). Corruption in government has a similar effect (Eccl 10:15-16).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v19" data-v="19">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 10:19](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/10#v19)</div>
<p>10:19 money gives everything: It is the way to get what you want or need in this world (cp. 7:11-12).</p>
</div>

<div className="cmv" id="v20" data-v="20">
<div className="cmv-head">[Ecclesiastes 10:20](/en/kjv/ecclesiastes/10#v20)</div>
<p>10:20 It is often a mystery how someone knows what we have said. The best way to control our reputation is to control our thoughts.</p>
</div>

</div>
