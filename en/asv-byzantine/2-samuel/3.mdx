<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Kings 14:30; Proverbs 4:18–19; Job 8:7"> Now there was long war between the house of Saul and the house of David: and David waxed stronger and stronger, but the house of Saul waxed weaker and weaker.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:1–4; Genesis 49:3–4; 2 Samuel 13:1–29"> And unto David were sons born in Hebron: and his firstborn was Amnon, of Ahinoam the Jezreelitess;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 27:8; 1 Chronicles 3:1; Deuteronomy 3:14"> and his second, Chileab, of Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite; and the third, Absalom the son of Maacah the daughter of Talmai king of Geshur;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 2:13–25; 1 Kings 1:5–18"> and the fourth, Adonijah the son of Haggith; and the fifth, Shephatiah the son of Abital;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and the sixth, Ithream, of Eglah, David’s wife. These were born to David in Hebron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Samuel 2:8–9; 2 Chronicles 25:8; Isaiah 8:9–10"> And it came to pass, while there was war between the house of Saul and the house of David, that Abner made himself strong in the house of Saul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Samuel 21:8–11; 1 Kings 2:21–22; 2 Samuel 12:8"> Now Saul had a concubine, whose name was Rizpah, the daughter of Aiah: and Ish-bosheth said to Abner, Wherefore hast thou gone in unto my father’s concubine?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Samuel 9:8; 2 Samuel 16:9; Mark 6:18–19"> Then was Abner very wroth for the words of Ish-bosheth, and said, Am I a dog’s head that belongeth to Judah? This day do I show kindness unto the house of Saul thy father, to his brethren, and to his friends, and have not delivered thee into the hand of David; and yet thou chargest me this day with a fault concerning this woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; 1 Kings 19:2; Ruth 1:17"> God do so to Abner, and more also, if, as Jehovah hath sworn to David, I do not even so to him;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Judges 20:1; 1 Samuel 3:20; 1 Kings 4:25"> to transfer the kingdom from the house of Saul, and to set up the throne of David over Israel and over Judah, from Dan even to Beer-sheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And he could not answer Abner another word, because he feared him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:38–40; 2 Samuel 3:21; Luke 16:5–8"> And Abner sent messengers to David on his behalf, saying, Whose is the land? saying also, Make thy league with me, and, behold, my hand shall be with thee, to bring about all Israel unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 43:3; 1 Samuel 19:11–17; Genesis 44:23"> And he said, Well; I will make a league with thee; but one thing I require of thee: that is, thou shalt not see my face, except thou first bring Michal, Saul’s daughter, when thou comest to see my face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Samuel 18:27; 1 Samuel 18:25; 2 Samuel 2:10"> And David sent messengers to Ish-bosheth, Saul’s son, saying, Deliver me my wife Michal, whom I betrothed to me for a hundred foreskins of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:44"> And Ish-bosheth sent, and took her from her husband, even from Paltiel the son of Laish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Samuel 16:5; 2 Samuel 19:16; 1 Kings 2:8"> And her husband went with her, weeping as he went, and followed her to Bahurim. Then said Abner unto him, Go, return: and he returned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Abner had communication with the elders of Israel, saying, In times past ye sought for David to be king over you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Samuel 15:28; Psalms 89:19–23; 1 Samuel 9:16"> now then do it; for Jehovah hath spoken of David, saying, By the hand of my servant David I will save my people Israel out of the hand of the Philistines, and out of the hand of all their enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Chronicles 12:29; 1 Samuel 10:20–21; Psalms 68:27"> And Abner also spake in the ears of Benjamin: and Abner went also to speak in the ears of David in Hebron all that seemed good to Israel, and to the whole house of Benjamin.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 31:54; Genesis 26:30; Esther 1:3"> So Abner came to David to Hebron, and twenty men with him. And David made Abner and the men that were with him a feast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:12; 1 Kings 11:37; 2 Samuel 3:10"> And Abner said unto David, I will arise and go, and will gather all Israel unto my lord the king, that they may make a covenant with thee, and that thou mayest reign over all that thy soul desireth. And David sent Abner away; and he went in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And, behold, the servants of David and Joab came from a foray, and brought in a great spoil with them: but Abner was not with David in Hebron; for he had sent him away, and he was gone in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> When Joab and all the host that was with him were come, they told Joab, saying, Abner the son of Ner came to the king, and he hath sent him away, and he is gone in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Samuel 3:8; 2 Samuel 19:5–7; John 18:35"> Then Joab came to the king, and said, What hast thou done? behold, Abner came unto thee; why is it that thou hast sent him away, and he is quite gone?</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:6; Isaiah 37:28; Numbers 27:17"> Thou knowest Abner the son of Ner, that he came to deceive thee, and to know thy going out and thy coming in, and to know all that thou doest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Proverbs 26:23–26; Proverbs 27:4–6"> And when Joab was come out from David, he sent messengers after Abner, and they brought him back from the well of Sirah: but David knew it not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Kings 2:5; 2 Samuel 20:9–10; 1 Kings 2:32"> And when Abner was returned to Hebron, Joab took him aside into the midst of the gate to speak with him quietly, and smote him there in the body, so that he died, for the blood of Asahel his brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 35:33; Exodus 21:12; Genesis 9:6"> And afterward, when David heard it, he said, I and my kingdom are guiltless before Jehovah forever of the blood of Abner the son of Ner:</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:16; Psalms 109:8–19; Leviticus 13:44–46"> let it fall upon the head of Joab, and upon all his father’s house; and let there not fail from the house of Joab one that hath an issue, or that is a leper, or that leaneth on a staff, or that falleth by the sword, or that lacketh bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Proverbs 28:17; Acts 28:4; 2 Samuel 2:19–23"> So Joab and Abishai his brother slew Abner, because he had killed their brother Asahel at Gibeon in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Genesis 37:34; 2 Samuel 1:11; 2 Samuel 1:2"> And David said to Joab, and to all the people that were with him, Rend your clothes, and gird you with sackcloth, and mourn before Abner. And king David followed the bier.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Proverbs 24:17; Job 31:28; 1 Samuel 30:4"> And they buried Abner in Hebron: and the king lifted up his voice, and wept at the grave of Abner; and all the people wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Samuel 1:17; 2 Chronicles 35:25; Luke 12:19–20"> And the king lamented for Abner, and said,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Should Abner die as a fool dieth?</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Judges 16:21; 2 Samuel 1:12; Job 24:14"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thy hands were not bound, nor thy feet put into fetters:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">As a man falleth before the children of iniquity, so didst thou fall.</span>And all the people wept again over him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:17; 2 Samuel 1:12; Ruth 1:17"> And all the people came to cause David to eat bread while it was yet day; but David sware, saying, God do so to me, and more also, if I taste bread, or aught else, till the sun be down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Mark 7:37; Psalms 62:9; Mark 15:11–13"> And all the people took notice of it, and it pleased them; as whatsoever the king did pleased all the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> So all the people and all Israel understood that day that it was not of the king to slay Abner the son of Ner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Job 32:9; 2 Samuel 2:8; 2 Samuel 3:12"> And the king said unto his servants, Know ye not that there is a prince and a great man fallen this day in Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="1 Kings 2:33–34; Psalms 101:8; Psalms 28:4"> And I am this day weak, though anointed king; and these men the sons of Zeruiah are too hard for me: Jehovah reward the evildoer according to his wickedness.</span>
