<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Mark 9:33–37; Luke 9:46–48; Matthew 20:20–28"> In that hour came the disciples unto Jesus, saying, Who then is greatest in the kingdom of heaven?</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Matthew 19:13–14; Mark 9:36–37; 1 Kings 3:7"> And Jesus called to him a little child, and set him in the midst of them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Mark 10:14–15; 1 Peter 2:2; Matthew 19:14"> and said, Verily I say unto you, Except ye turn, and become as little children, ye shall in no wise enter into the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="James 4:10; Luke 14:11; Mark 10:43"> Whosoever therefore shall humble himself as this little child, the same is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Matthew 25:40; Mark 9:37; John 13:20"> And whoso shall receive one such little child in my name receiveth me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Mark 9:42; Luke 17:1–2; Matthew 18:10"> but whoso shall cause one of these little ones that believe on me to stumble, it is profitable for him that a great millstone should be hung upon his neck, and that he should be sunk in the depth of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Luke 17:1; 1 Corinthians 11:19; Matthew 26:24"> Woe unto the world because of occasions of stumbling! for it must needs be that the occasions come; but woe to that man through whom the occasion cometh!</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 5:29–30; Philippians 3:8–9; Romans 13:12"> And if thy hand or thy foot causeth thee to stumble, cut them off, and cast them from thee: it is good for thee to enter into life halt or maimed, rather than having two hands or two feet to be cast into the eternal fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Matthew 18:8; Matthew 5:29"> And if thine eye causeth thee to stumble, pluck it out, and cast it from thee: it is good for thee to enter into life with one eye, rather than having two eyes to be cast into the hell of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 91:11; Psalms 34:7; Hebrews 1:14"> See that ye despise not one of these little ones: for I say unto you, that in heaven their angels do always behold the face of my Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For the Son of man came to save that which was lost.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:12; Luke 15:4–7; Ezekiel 34:16"> How think ye? if any man have a hundred sheep, and one of them be gone astray, doth he not leave the ninety and nine, and go seek on the mountains that which goeth astray?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:17; Luke 15:5–10; James 2:13"> And if so be that he find it, verily I say unto you, he rejoiceth over it more than over the ninety and nine which have not gone astray.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 17:12; John 6:39–40; 2 Peter 3:9"> Even so it is not the will of your Father who is in heaven, that one of these little ones should perish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Leviticus 19:17; Luke 17:3–4; Galatians 6:1"> And if thy brother sin against thee, go and show him his fault between thee and him alone: if he hear thee, thou hast gained thy brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Deuteronomy 19:15; 2 Corinthians 13:1; John 8:17"> But if he hear thee not, take with thee one or two more, that at the mouth of two witnesses or three every word may be established.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:6; 1 Corinthians 5:9–7; 2 Thessalonians 3:14–15"> And if he refuse to hear them, tell it unto the church: and if he refuse to hear the church also, let him be unto thee as the Gentile and the publican.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Matthew 16:19; 2 Corinthians 2:10; John 20:23"> Verily I say unto you, What things soever ye shall bind on earth shall be bound in heaven; and what things soever ye shall loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; 1 John 5:14–16; Mark 11:24"> Again, verily I say unto you, that if two of you shall agree on earth as touching anything that they shall ask, it shall be done for them of my Father who is in heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Corinthians 5:4; Revelation 21:3; Matthew 28:20"> For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 17:3–4; Matthew 18:15"> Then came Peter to him and said, Lord, how oft shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him? until seven times?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Colossians 3:13; Mark 11:25; Matthew 6:14–15"> Jesus saith unto him, I say not unto thee, Until seven times; but, Until seventy times seven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 13:24; Romans 14:12; Matthew 3:2"> Therefore is the kingdom of heaven likened unto a certain king, who would make a reckoning with his servants.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 38:4; Psalms 130:3–4; Luke 16:5"> And when he had begun to reckon, one was brought unto him, that owed him ten thousand talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Kings 4:1; Nehemiah 5:5; Luke 7:42"> But forasmuch as he had not wherewith to pay, his lord commanded him to be sold, and his wife, and children, and all that he had, and payment to be made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 18:29; Luke 7:43; Matthew 8:2"> The servant therefore fell down and worshiped him, saying, Lord, have patience with me, and I will pay thee all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Psalms 145:8; Nehemiah 9:17; Psalms 86:15"> And the lord of that servant, being moved with compassion, released him, and forgave him the debt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 20:2; Ezekiel 45:9; Deuteronomy 15:2"> But that servant went out, and found one of his fellow servants, who owed him a hundred denarii: and he laid hold on him, and took him by the throat, saying, Pay me what thou owest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Matthew 18:26; Matthew 6:12; Philemon 1:18–19"> So his fellow servant fell down at his feet, and besought him, saying, Have patience with me, and I will pay thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 22:27; 1 Kings 21:27–29"> And he would not: but went and cast him into prison, till he should pay that which was due.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 12:15; Hebrews 13:3; Mark 3:5"> But when his fellow servants saw what was done, they were exceeding sorry, and came and told unto their lord all that was done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 19:22; Matthew 25:26; Romans 3:19"> Then his lord called him unto him, and saith to him, Thou wicked servant, I forgave thee all that debt, because thou besoughtest me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Colossians 3:13; Matthew 6:12; Ephesians 4:32–2"> shouldest not thou also have had mercy on thy fellow servant, even as I had mercy on thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Matthew 5:25–26; James 2:13; Luke 12:58–59"> And his lord was wroth, and delivered him to the tormentors, till he should pay all that was due unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Matthew 6:14–15; James 2:13; Luke 6:37–38"> So shall also my heavenly Father do unto you, if ye from your hearts forgive not every one his brother their trespasses.</span>
