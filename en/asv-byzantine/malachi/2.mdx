<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hosea 5:1; Malachi 1:6; Jeremiah 13:13"> And now, O ye priests, this commandment is for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 17:18; Revelation 14:7; Psalms 69:22"> If ye will not hear, and if ye will not lay it to heart, to give glory unto my name, saith Jehovah of hosts, then will I send the curse upon you, and I will curse your blessings; yea, I have cursed them already, because ye do not lay it to heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nahum 3:6; Malachi 2:9; Job 20:7"> Behold, I will rebuke your seed, and will spread dung upon your faces, even the dung of your feasts; and ye shall be taken away with it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Numbers 3:12; Ezekiel 38:23; Matthew 3:12"> And ye shall know that I have sent this commandment unto you, that my covenant may be with Levi, saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 25:12–13; Ezekiel 34:25; Ezekiel 37:26"> My covenant was with him of life and peace; and I gave them to him that he might fear; and he feared me, and stood in awe of my name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 12:3; 2 Timothy 2:15–16; Revelation 14:5"> The law of truth was in his mouth, and unrighteousness was not found in his lips: he walked with me in peace and uprightness, and turned many away from iniquity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Leviticus 10:11; Deuteronomy 21:5"> For the priest’s lips should keep knowledge, and they should seek the law at his mouth; for he is the messenger of Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Nehemiah 13:29; Jeremiah 18:15; Isaiah 30:11"> But ye are turned aside out of the way; ye have caused many to stumble in the law; ye have corrupted the covenant of Levi, saith Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:30; Deuteronomy 1:17; Matthew 19:17–18"> Therefore have I also made you contemptible and base before all the people, according as ye have not kept my ways, but have had respect of persons in the law.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 100:3; Malachi 2:11; 1 Corinthians 8:6"> Have we not all one father? hath not one God created us? why do we deal treacherously every man against his brother, profaning the covenant of our fathers?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–18; Ezra 9:1–2; Nehemiah 13:23–29"> Judah hath dealt treacherously, and an abomination is committed in Israel and in Jerusalem; for Judah hath profaned the holiness of Jehovah which he loveth, and hath married the daughter of a foreign god.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 18:29; Leviticus 20:3; Ezekiel 24:21"> Jehovah will cut off, to the man that doeth this, him that waketh and him that answereth, out of the tents of Jacob, and him that offereth an offering unto Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:11–15; Jeremiah 14:12; Psalms 78:34–37"> And this again ye do: ye cover the altar of Jehovah with tears, with weeping, and with sighing, insomuch that he regardeth not the offering anymore, neither receiveth it with good will at your hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 2:15; Ecclesiastes 9:9; Isaiah 54:6"> Yet ye say, Wherefore? Because Jehovah hath been witness between thee and the wife of thy youth, against whom thou hast dealt treacherously, though she is thy companion, and the wife of thy covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 19:4–6; Malachi 2:14; Mark 10:6–8"> And did he not make one, although he had the residue of the Spirit? And wherefore one? He sought a godly seed. Therefore take heed to your spirit, and let none deal treacherously against the wife of his youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 19:3–9; Matthew 5:31–32; Mark 10:2–12"> For I hate putting away, saith Jehovah, the God of Israel, and him that covereth his garment with violence, saith Jehovah of hosts: therefore take heed to your spirit, that ye deal not treacherously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 43:24; 2 Peter 3:3–4; Ecclesiastes 8:11"> Ye have wearied Jehovah with your words. Yet ye say, Wherein have we wearied him? In that ye say, Everyone that doeth evil is good in the sight of Jehovah, and he delighteth in them; or where is the God of justice?</span>
