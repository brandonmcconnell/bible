<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:1–3; Habakkuk 2:12; Hosea 4:2"> Woe to the bloody city! it is all full of lies and rapine; the prey departeth not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jeremiah 47:3; Nahum 2:3–4; Job 39:22–25"> The noise of the whip, and the noise of the rattling of wheels, and prancing horses, and bounding chariots,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Habakkuk 3:11; Ezekiel 39:4; Isaiah 34:3"> the horseman mounting, and the flashing sword, and the glittering spear, and a multitude of slain, and a great heap of corpses, and there is no end of the bodies; they stumble upon their bodies—</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 47:9; Isaiah 47:12–13; Revelation 17:1–5"> because of the multitude of the whoredoms of the well-favored harlot, the mistress of witchcrafts, that selleth nations through her whoredoms, and families through her witchcrafts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:26; Ezekiel 23:29; Jeremiah 13:22"> Behold, I am against thee, saith Jehovah of hosts, and I will uncover thy skirts upon thy face; and I will show the nations thy nakedness, and the kingdoms thy shame.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Malachi 2:9; Jeremiah 51:37; Job 9:31"> And I will cast abominable filth upon thee, and make thee vile, and will set thee as a gazingstock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:5; Isaiah 51:19; Revelation 18:10"> And it shall come to pass, that all they that look upon thee shall flee from thee, and say, Nineveh is laid waste: who will bemoan her? whence shall I seek comforters for thee?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Amos 6:2; Ezekiel 30:14–16; Jeremiah 46:25–26"> Art thou better than No-amon, that was situate among the rivers, that had the waters round about her; whose rampart was the sea, and her wall was of the sea?</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:5; Ezekiel 27:10; Isaiah 20:5"> Ethiopia and Egypt were her strength, and it was infinite; Put and Libyans were thy helpers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Joel 3:3; Hosea 13:16"> Yet was she carried away, she went into captivity; her young children also were dashed in pieces at the head of all the streets; and they cast lots for her honorable men, and all her great men were bound in chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 49:26; Isaiah 2:10; Psalms 75:8"> Thou also shalt be drunken; thou shalt be hidden; thou also shalt seek a stronghold because of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Revelation 6:13; Isaiah 28:4; Habakkuk 1:10"> All thy fortresses shall be like fig trees with the first-ripe figs: if they be shaken, they fall into the mouth of the eater.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Isaiah 19:16; Jeremiah 50:37"> Behold, thy people in the midst of thee are women; the gates of thy land are set wide open unto thine enemies: the fire hath devoured thy bars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Chronicles 32:3–4; Nahum 2:1; 2 Chronicles 32:11"> Draw thee water for the siege; strengthen thy fortresses; go into the clay, and tread the mortar; make strong the brickkiln.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 1:4; Nahum 2:13; Nahum 3:13"> There shall the fire devour thee; the sword shall cut thee off; it shall devour thee like the cankerworm: make thyself many as the cankerworm; make thyself many as the locust.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Nehemiah 9:23; Jeremiah 33:22"> Thou hast multiplied thy merchants above the stars of heaven: the cankerworm ravageth, and fleeth away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:27; Revelation 9:7"> Thy princes are as the locusts, and thy marshals as the swarms of grasshoppers, which encamp in the hedges in the cold day, but when the sun ariseth they flee away, and their place is not known where they are.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 22:17; Psalms 76:5–6; Jeremiah 50:18"> Thy shepherds slumber, O king of Assyria; thy nobles are at rest; thy people are scattered upon the mountains, and there is none to gather them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Micah 1:9; Lamentations 2:15; Job 27:23"> There is no assuaging of thy hurt; thy wound is grievous: all that hear the report of thee clap their hands over thee; for upon whom hath not thy wickedness passed continually?</span>
