<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Galatians 6:3; Philippians 2:3–8; Proverbs 26:12"> For I say, through the grace that was given me, to every man that is among you, not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think; but so to think as to think soberly, according as God hath dealt to each man a measure of faith.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:27; 1 Corinthians 12:12–14; Ephesians 4:15–16"> For even as we have many members in one body, and all the members have not the same office:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:20; 1 Corinthians 10:17; 1 Corinthians 12:27–28"> so we, who are many, are one body in Christ, and severally members one of another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="1 Peter 4:10–11; 1 Corinthians 7:7; 1 Corinthians 12:28–31"> And having gifts differing according to the grace that was given to us, whether prophecy, let us prophesy according to the proportion of our faith;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Ephesians 4:11; 2 Timothy 4:2; Acts 13:1"> or ministry, let us give ourselves to our ministry; or he that teacheth, to his teaching;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:7; 1 Timothy 5:17; 1 Corinthians 12:28"> or he that exhorteth, to his exhorting: he that giveth, let him do it with liberality; he that ruleth, with diligence; he that showeth mercy, with cheerfulness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 34:14; 1 Peter 4:8; 1 Timothy 1:5"> Let love be without hypocrisy. Abhor that which is evil; cleave to that which is good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Philippians 2:3; John 13:34–35; Hebrews 13:1"> In love of the brethren be tenderly affectioned one to another; in honor preferring one another;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Hebrews 6:10–11; Acts 18:25; Revelation 3:15–16"> in diligence not slothful; fervent in spirit; serving the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:13; Hebrews 10:36; Psalms 62:8"> rejoicing in hope; patient in tribulation; continuing steadfastly in prayer;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Hebrews 13:16; 2 Corinthians 9:12; 1 John 3:17"> communicating to the necessities of the saints; given to hospitality.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Matthew 5:44; 1 Thessalonians 5:15"> Bless them that persecute you; bless, and curse not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; Hebrews 13:3; Psalms 35:13–14"> Rejoice with them that rejoice; and weep with them that weep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Peter 3:8; Proverbs 3:7; James 3:13–17"> Be of the same mind one toward another. Set not your mind on high things, but condescend to things that are lowly. Be not wise in your own conceits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 20:22; 1 Thessalonians 5:15; Matthew 5:39"> Render to no man evil for evil. Take thought for things honorable in the sight of all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Hebrews 12:14; Matthew 5:9; Romans 14:19"> If it be possible, as much as in you lieth, be at peace with all men.</span>

<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Peter 3:9; Luke 6:27–30; Proverbs 16:32"> Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good.</span>
