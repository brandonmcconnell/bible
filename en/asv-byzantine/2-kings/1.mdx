<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Samuel 8:2; Numbers 24:7; 2 Kings 3:4–5"> And Moab rebelled against Israel after the death of Ahab.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Kings 1:16; Matthew 10:25; Mark 3:22"> And Ahaziah fell down through the lattice in his upper chamber that was in Samaria, and was sick: and he sent messengers, and said unto them, Go, inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron, whether I shall recover of this sickness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 17:1; Jonah 2:8; Acts 12:7–11"> But the angel of Jehovah said to Elijah the Tishbite, Arise, go up to meet the messengers of the king of Samaria, and say unto them, Is it because there is no God in Israel, that ye go to inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 3:4; Genesis 2:17; Ezekiel 18:4"> Now therefore thus saith Jehovah, Thou shalt not come down from the bed whither thou art gone up, but shalt surely die. And Elijah departed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the messengers returned unto him, and he said unto them, Why is it that ye are returned?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 16:4; 1 Chronicles 10:13–14; Isaiah 41:22–23"> And they said unto him, There came up a man to meet us, and said unto us, Go, turn again unto the king that sent you, and say unto him, Thus saith Jehovah, Is it because there is no God in Israel, that thou sendest to inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron? therefore thou shalt not come down from the bed whither thou art gone up, but shalt surely die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:14; Judges 8:18"> And he said unto them, What manner of man was he that came up to meet you, and told you these words?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 3:4; Zechariah 13:4; Mark 1:6"> And they answered him, He was a hairy man, and girt with a girdle of leather about his loins. And he said, It is Elijah the Tishbite.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Kings 6:13–14; Luke 6:11–12; Hebrews 11:36"> Then the king sent unto him a captain of fifty with his fifty. And he went up to him: and, behold, he was sitting on the top of the hill. And he spake unto him, O man of God, the king hath said, Come down.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Luke 9:54; Daniel 3:25; 1 Kings 18:36–38"> And Elijah answered and said to the captain of fifty, If I be a man of God, let fire come down from heaven, and consume thee and thy fifty. And there came down fire from heaven, and consumed him and his fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 2:16; Luke 22:63–64; Jeremiah 5:3"> And again he sent unto him another captain of fifty with his fifty. And he answered and said unto him, O man of God, thus hath the king said, Come down quickly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Kings 1:9–10"> And Elijah answered and said unto them, If I be a man of God, let fire come down from heaven, and consume thee and thy fifty. And the fire of God came down from heaven, and consumed him and his fifty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 1:5; 1 Samuel 26:21; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> And again he sent the captain of a third fifty with his fifty. And the third captain of fifty went up, and came and fell on his knees before Elijah, and besought him, and said unto him, O man of God, I pray thee, let my life, and the life of these fifty thy servants, be precious in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 20:24; Psalms 49:8; Proverbs 6:26"> Behold, there came fire down from heaven, and consumed the two former captains of fifty with their fifties; but now let my life be precious in thy sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 51:12; Ezekiel 2:6; Jeremiah 1:17"> And the angel of Jehovah said unto Elijah, Go down with him: be not afraid of him. And he arose, and went down with him unto the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Kings 1:2–4; 1 Kings 22:28; Exodus 4:22–23"> And he said unto him, Thus saith Jehovah, Forasmuch as thou hast sent messengers to inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron, is it because there is no God in Israel to inquire of his word? therefore thou shalt not come down from the bed whither thou art gone up, but shalt surely die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 3:1; 1 Kings 22:51; 2 Kings 8:16–17"> So he died according to the word of Jehovah which Elijah had spoken. And Jehoram began to reign in his stead in the second year of Jehoram the son of Jehoshaphat king of Judah; because he had no son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 22:39; 1 Kings 14:19"> Now the rest of the acts of Ahaziah which he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?</span>
