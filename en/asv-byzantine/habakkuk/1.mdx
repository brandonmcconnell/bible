<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nahum 1:1; Isaiah 22:1"> The burden which Habakkuk the prophet did see.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Psalms 22:1–2; Jeremiah 14:9; Psalms 13:1–2"> O Jehovah, how long shall I cry, and thou wilt not hear? I cry out unto thee of violence, and thou wilt not save.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 55:9–11; Jeremiah 9:2–6; Jeremiah 20:8"> Why dost thou show me iniquity, and look upon perverseness? for destruction and violence are before me; and there is strife, and contention riseth up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 119:126; Isaiah 1:21–23; Job 21:7"> Therefore the law is slacked, and justice doth never go forth; for the wicked doth compass about the righteous; therefore justice goeth forth perverted.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 13:40–41; Lamentations 4:12; Isaiah 29:14"> Behold ye among the nations, and look, and wonder marvelously; for I am working a work in your days, which ye will not believe though it be told you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 24:2; Jeremiah 5:15; Isaiah 23:13"> For, lo, I raise up the Chaldeans, that bitter and hasty nation, that march through the breadth of the earth, to possess dwelling places that are not theirs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:5–9; Jeremiah 52:9–11; Deuteronomy 5:27"> They are terrible and dreadful; their judgment and their dignity proceed from themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:13; Zephaniah 3:3; Hosea 8:1"> Their horses also are swifter than leopards, and are more fierce than the evening wolves; and their horsemen press proudly on: yea, their horsemen come from far; they fly as an eagle that hasteth to devour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Hosea 13:15; Hosea 1:10"> They come all of them for violence; the set of their faces is forward; and they gather captives as the sand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:6; Jeremiah 32:24; Isaiah 14:16"> Yea, he scoffeth at kings, and princes are a derision unto him; he derideth every stronghold; for he heapeth up dust, and taketh it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 5:20; Daniel 4:30–34; Jeremiah 4:11–12"> Then shall he sweep by as a wind, and shall pass over, and be guilty, even he whose might is his god.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:4; Jeremiah 30:11; Jeremiah 46:28"> Art not thou from everlasting, O Jehovah my God, my Holy One? we shall not die. O Jehovah, thou hast ordained him for judgment; and thou, O Rock, hast established him for correction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 5:4–5; Psalms 34:15–16; Jeremiah 12:1–2"> Thou that art of purer eyes than to behold evil, and that canst not look on perverseness, wherefore lookest thou upon them that deal treacherously, and holdest thy peace when the wicked swalloweth up the man that is more righteous than he;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 6:7"> and makest men as the fish of the sea, as the creeping things, that have no ruler over them?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Amos 4:2; Jeremiah 16:16; Psalms 10:9"> He taketh up all of them with the angle, he catcheth them in his net, and gathereth them in his drag: therefore he rejoiceth and is glad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Habakkuk 1:11; Deuteronomy 8:17; Daniel 4:30"> Therefore he sacrificeth unto his net, and burneth incense unto his drag; because by them his portion is fat, and his food plenteous.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:5–8; Ezekiel 25:1–17; Jeremiah 25:9–26"> Shall he therefore empty his net, and spare not to slay the nations continually?</span>
</div>
