<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 1:2; 2 Chronicles 34:1–33; 2 Peter 1:19"> The word of Jehovah which came unto Zephaniah the son of Cushi, the son of Gedaliah, the son of Amariah, the son of Hezekiah, in the days of Josiah the son of Amon, king of Judah.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Micah 7:13; 2 Kings 22:16–17; Ezekiel 33:27–29"> I will utterly consume all things from off the face of the ground, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Hosea 4:3; Ezekiel 7:19; Jeremiah 12:4"> I will consume man and beast; I will consume the birds of the heavens, and the fish of the sea, and the stumbling blocks with the wicked; and I will cut off man from off the face of the ground, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Hosea 10:5; Micah 5:13; 2 Kings 23:4–5"> And I will stretch out my hand upon Judah, and upon all the inhabitants of Jerusalem; and I will cut off the remnant of Baal from this place, and the name of the Chemarim with the priests;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 19:13; 1 Kings 11:33; 2 Kings 17:33"> and them that worship the host of heaven upon the housetops; and them that worship, that swear to Jehovah and swear by Malcam;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 1:4; Jeremiah 2:13; Jeremiah 15:6"> and them that are turned back from following Jehovah; and those that have not sought Jehovah, nor inquired after him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:20; Zechariah 2:13; Zephaniah 1:14"> Hold thy peace at the presence of the Lord Jehovah; for the day of Jehovah is at hand: for Jehovah hath prepared a sacrifice, he hath consecrated his guests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 24:21; Isaiah 3:18–24; Jeremiah 22:24–30"> And it shall come to pass in the day of Jehovah’s sacrifice, that I will punish the princes, and the king’s sons, and all such as are clothed with foreign apparel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 5:5; Amos 3:10; 1 Samuel 2:15–16"> And in that day I will punish all those that leap over the threshold, that fill their master’s house with violence and deceit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Chronicles 33:14; Amos 8:3; 2 Kings 22:14"> And in that day, saith Jehovah, there shall be the noise of a cry from the fish gate, and a wailing from the second quarter, and a great crashing from the hills.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="James 5:1; Jeremiah 25:34; Hosea 12:7–8"> Wail, ye inhabitants of Maktesh; for all the people of Canaan are undone; all they that were laden with silver are cut off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Amos 6:1; Jeremiah 48:11; Ezekiel 8:12"> And it shall come to pass at that time, that I will search Jerusalem with lamps; and I will punish the men that are settled on their lees, that say in their heart, Jehovah will not do good, neither will he do evil.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Amos 5:11; Micah 6:15; Deuteronomy 28:30"> And their wealth shall become a spoil, and their houses a desolation: yea, they shall build houses, but shall not inhabit them; and they shall plant vineyards, but shall not drink the wine thereof.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Malachi 4:5; Ezekiel 30:3; Zephaniah 1:7"> The great day of Jehovah is near, it is near and hasteth greatly, even the voice of the day of Jehovah; the mighty man crieth there bitterly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joel 2:2; Isaiah 22:5; Revelation 6:17"> That day is a day of wrath, a day of trouble and distress, a day of wasteness and desolation, a day of darkness and gloominess, a day of clouds and thick darkness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:1; Amos 3:6; Isaiah 32:14"> a day of the trumpet and alarm, against the fortified cities, and against the high battlements.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 3:17; Psalms 83:10; Jeremiah 2:17"> And I will bring distress upon men, that they shall walk like blind men, because they have sinned against Jehovah; and their blood shall be poured out as dust, and their flesh as dung.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:8; Ezekiel 7:19; Proverbs 11:4"> Neither their silver nor their gold shall be able to deliver them in the day of Jehovah’s wrath; but the whole land shall be devoured by the fire of his jealousy: for he will make an end, yea, a terrible end, of all them that dwell in the land.</span>
</div>
