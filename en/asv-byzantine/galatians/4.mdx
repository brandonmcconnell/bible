<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Galatians 4:29; Genesis 24:2–3"> But I say that so long as the heir is a child, he differeth nothing from a bondservant though he is lord of all;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> but is under guardians and stewards until the day appointed of the father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:20; Colossians 2:8; Galatians 4:9"> So we also, when we were children, were held in bondage under the rudiments of the world:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; John 1:14; Romans 1:3"> but when the fullness of the time came, God sent forth his Son, born of a woman, born under the law,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 9:15; Ephesians 1:5; 1 Peter 1:18–20"> that he might redeem them that were under the law, that we might receive the adoption of sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Romans 8:15–17; Romans 5:5; Romans 8:9"> And because ye are sons, God sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, Abba, Father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:16–17; Galatians 3:29–2; Galatians 3:26"> So that thou art no longer a bondservant, but a son; and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:4; Ephesians 2:11–12; 2 Thessalonians 1:8"> Howbeit at that time, not knowing God, ye were in bondage to them that by nature are no gods:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:3; Galatians 3:3; Romans 8:3"> but now that ye have come to know God, or rather to be known by God, how turn ye back again to the weak and beggarly rudiments, whereunto ye desire to be in bondage over again?</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Colossians 2:16–17; Romans 14:5; Leviticus 23:1–44"> Ye observe days, and months, and seasons, and years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 3:5; Philippians 2:16; Galatians 2:2"> I am afraid of you, lest by any means I have bestowed labor upon you in vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:5; 1 Kings 22:4; Genesis 34:15"> I beseech you, brethren, become as I am, for I also am become as ye are. Ye did me no wrong:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 12:7–10; 2 Corinthians 11:30"> but ye know that because of an infirmity of the flesh I preached the gospel unto you the first time:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Matthew 10:40; 1 Thessalonians 2:13; Hebrews 13:2"> and my trial that was in my flesh ye despised not, nor rejected; but ye received me as an angel of God, even as Christ Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 5:2; 1 John 3:16–18; Colossians 4:13"> What then was the gratulation of yourselves? for I bear you witness, that, if possible, ye would have plucked out your eyes and given them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Amos 5:10; Proverbs 9:8; John 8:45"> So then am I become your enemy, by telling you the truth?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Romans 16:18; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15; 2 Peter 2:3"> They zealously seek you in no good way; nay, they desire to shut us out, that ye may seek them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Philippians 2:12; Revelation 3:19; Philippians 1:27"> But it is good to be zealously sought in a good matter at all times, and not only when I am present with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ephesians 4:13; Romans 8:29; Romans 13:14"> My little children, of whom I am again in travail until Christ be formed in you—</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:17–18; 1 Corinthians 4:19–21; 1 Thessalonians 3:9"> but I could wish to be present with you now, and to change my tone; for I am perplexed about you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Galatians 3:10; Galatians 3:23–24; Romans 7:5–6"> Tell me, ye that desire to be under the law, do ye not hear the law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 16:15; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–2"> For it is written, that Abraham had two sons, one by the handmaid, and one by the freewoman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Hebrews 11:11; Romans 9:7–8; Galatians 4:28–29"> Howbeit the son by the handmaid is born after the flesh; but the son by the freewoman is born through the promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 21:9–13; Hebrews 10:15–18; Genesis 16:8"> Which things contain an allegory: for these women are two covenants; one from mount Sinai, bearing children unto bondage, which is Hagar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 19:44; Acts 1:11; Galatians 1:17"> For this Hagar is mount Sinai in Arabia and answereth to the Jerusalem that now is: and she is in bondage with her children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Hebrews 12:22; Philippians 3:20; Micah 4:1–2"> But the Jerusalem that is above is free, which is the mother of us all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 54:1–5; Psalms 113:9; Ruth 4:14–16"> For it is written,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Rejoice, thou barren that bearest not;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Break forth and cry, thou that travailest not:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">For more are the children of the desolate than of her that hath the husband.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Galatians 4:23; Romans 9:8–9; Romans 4:13–18"> Now we, brethren, as Isaac was, are children of promise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 21:9; Romans 8:13; Galatians 5:11"> But as then he that was born after the flesh persecuted him that was born after the Spirit, so also it is now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 21:10–12; John 8:35; Romans 8:15–17"> Howbeit what saith the scripture? Cast out the handmaid and her son: for the son of the handmaid shall not inherit with the son of the freewoman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="John 8:36; John 1:12–13; Galatians 5:1"> So then, brethren, we are not children of a handmaid, but of the freewoman.</span>
