<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> After these things Tobias also went his way, blessing God because he had prospered his journey; and he blessed Raguel and Edna his wife. And he went on his way till they drew near unto Nineveh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And Raphael said to Tobias, Knowest thou not, brother, how thou didst leave thy father?</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Let us run forward before thy wife, and prepare the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> But take in thy hand the gall of the fish. And they went their way, and the dog went after them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Anna sat looking about toward the way for her son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And she espied him coming, and said to his father, Behold, thy son cometh, and the man that went with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Raphael said, I know, Tobias, that thy father will open his eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Do thou therefore anoint his eyes with the gall, and being pricked therewith, he shall rub, and shall make the white films to fall away, and he shall see thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And Anna ran unto him, and fell upon the neck of her son, and said unto him, I have seen thee, my child; from henceforth I will die. And they wept both.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And Tobit went forth toward the door, and stumbled: but his son ran unto him,</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and took hold of his father: and he sprinkled the gall on his father’s eyes, saying, Be of good cheer, my father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> But when his eyes began to smart, he rubbed them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and the white films scaled away from the corners of his eyes; and he saw his son, and fell upon his neck.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And he wept, and said, Blessed art thou, O God, and blessed is thy name forever, and blessed are all thy holy angels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> for thou didst scourge, and didst have mercy on me: behold, I see my son Tobias. And his son went in rejoicing, and told his father the great things that had happened to him in Media.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Tobit went out to meet his daughter-in-law at the gate of Nineveh, rejoicing, and blessing God: and they that saw him go marveled, because he had received his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And Tobit gave thanks before them, because God had shown mercy on him. And when Tobit came near to Sarah his daughter-in-law, he blessed her, saying, Welcome, daughter: blessed is God who hath brought thee unto us, and blessed are thy father and thy mother. And there was joy to all his brethren who were at Nineveh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And Ahikar, and Nadab his brother’s son, came:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and Tobias’ wedding feast was kept seven days with great gladness.</span>
