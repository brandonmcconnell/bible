<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> O thou mother, who together with seven children didst destroy the violence of the tyrant, and render void his wicked intentions, and exhibit the nobleness of faith!</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For thou, as a house bravely built upon the pillar of thy children, didst bear without swaying, the shock of tortures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Be of good cheer, therefore, O holy-minded mother, holding the firm substance of the hope of your steadfastness with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Not so gracious doth the moon appear with the stars in heaven, as thou art established honorable before God, and fixed in the firmament with thy sons whom thou didst illuminate with religion to the stars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> For thy bearing of children was after the fashion of a child of Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And, were it lawful for us to paint as on a tablet the religion of thy story, the spectators would not shudder at beholding the mother of seven children enduring for the sake of religion various tortures even unto death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And it had been a worthy thing to have inscribed upon the tomb itself these words as a memorial to those of the nation,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Here an aged priest, and an aged woman, and seven sons, are buried through the violence of a tyrant, who wished to destroy the polity of the Hebrews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> These also avenged their nation, looking unto God, and enduring torments unto death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For it was a truly divine contest which was carried through by them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For at that time virtue presided over the contest, approving the victory through endurance, namely, immortality, eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> Eleazar was the first to contend: and the mother of the seven children entered the contest; and the brethren contended.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> The tyrant was the opposite; and the world and living men were the spectators.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And reverence for God conquered, and crowned its own athletes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Who did not admire those champions of true legislation? who were not astonished?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> The tyrant himself, and all their council, admired their endurance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> through which, also, they now stand beside the divine throne, and live a blessed life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For Moses saith, And all the saints are under thy hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> These, therefore, having been sanctified through God, have been honored not only with this honor, but that also by their means the enemy did not overcome our nation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and that the tyrant was punished, and their country purified.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> For they became the ransom for the sin of the nation; and the divine providence saved Israel, aforetime afflicted, by the blood of those pious ones, and their propitiatory death.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For the tyrant Antiochus, looking to their manly virtue, and to their endurance in torture, proclaimed that endurance as an example to his soldiers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And they proved to be to him noble and brave for land battles and sieges; and he conquered and stormed the towns of all his enemies.</span>
</div>
