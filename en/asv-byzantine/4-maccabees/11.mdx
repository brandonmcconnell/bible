<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And when he had died, disfigured in his torments, the fifth leaped forward, and said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> I intend not, O tyrant, to get excused from the torment which is in behalf of virtue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> But I have come of mine own accord, that by the death of me, you may owe heavenly vengeance a punishment for more crimes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> O thou hater of virtue and of men, what have we done that thou thus revelest in our blood?</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Doth it seem evil to thee that we worship the Founder of all things, and live according to his surpassing law?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> But this is worthy of honors, not of torments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> hadst thou been capable of the higher feelings of men, and possessed the hope of salvation from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Behold, now, being alien from God, thou makest war against those who are religious toward God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> As he said this, the spear-bearers bound him, and drew him to the catapult:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> to which binding him at his knees, and fastening them with iron fetters, they bent down his loins upon the wedge of the wheel; and his body was then dismembered, scorpion-fashion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> With his breath thus confined, and his body strangled, he said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> A great favor thou bestowest upon us, O tyrant, by enabling us to manifest our adherence to the law by means of nobler sufferings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> He also being dead, the sixth, quite a youth, was brought out; and on the tyrant asking him whether he would eat and be delivered, he said,</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> I am indeed younger than my brethren, but in understanding I am as old;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> for having been born and reared unto the same end, we are bound to die also in behalf of the same cause.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> So that if thou think proper to torment us for not eating the unclean—torment!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> As he said this, they brought him to the wheel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Extended upon which, with limbs racked and dislocated, he was gradually roasted from beneath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And having heated sharp spits, they approached them to his back; and having transfixed his sides, they burned away his entrails.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And he, while tormented, said, O period good and holy, in which, for the sake of religion, we brethren have been called to the contest of pain, and have not been conquered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For religious understanding, O tyrant, is unconquered.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Armed with upright virtue, I also shall depart with my brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> I, too, bearing with me a great avenger, O deviser of tortures, and enemy of the truly pious.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> We six youths have destroyed thy tyranny.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> For is not your inability to overrule our reasoning, and to compel us to eat the unclean, thy destruction?</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> Your fire is cold to us, your catapults are painless, and your violence harmless.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> For the guards not of a tyrant but of a divine law are our defenders: through this we keep our reasoning unconquered.</span>
</div>
