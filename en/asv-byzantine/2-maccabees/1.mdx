<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> The brethren, the Jews that are in Jerusalem and they that are in the country of Judea, send greeting to the brethren, the Jews that are throughout Egypt, and wish them good peace:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and may God do good unto you, and remember his covenant with Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, his faithful servants;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and give you all a heart to worship him and do his pleasure with a great heart and a willing soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and open your heart in his law and in his statutes, and make peace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and hearken to your supplications, and be reconciled with you, and not forsake you in an evil time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And now we here are praying for you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> In the reign of Demetrius, in the hundred threescore and ninth year, we the Jews have already written unto you in the tribulation and in the extremity that hath come upon us in these years, from the time that Jason and his company revolted from the holy land and the kingdom,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> and set the gate on fire, and shed innocent blood: and we besought the Lord, and were heard; and we offered sacrifice and meal offering, and we lighted the lamps, and we set forth the showbread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And now see that ye keep the days of the feast of tabernacles of the month Chislev.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> Written in the hundred fourscore and eighth year.<br />They that are in Jerusalem and they that are in Judea and the senate and Judas, unto Aristobulus, king Ptolemy’s teacher, who is also of the stock of the anointed priests, and unto the Jews that are in Egypt, send greeting and health.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Having been saved by God out of great perils, as men arrayed against a king, we thank him greatly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> For himself cast forth into Persia them that arrayed themselves against us in the holy city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> For when the prince was come there, and the army with him that seemed irresistible, they were cut to pieces in the temple of Nanea by the treachery of Nanea’s priests.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> For Antiochus, on the pretense that he would marry her, came into the place, he and his Friends that were with him, that they might take a great part of the treasures in name of a dowry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And when the priests of Nanea’s temple had set the treasures forth, and he was come there with a small company within the wall of the precincts, they shut the temple when Antiochus was come in:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> and opening the secret door of the paneled ceiling, they threw stones and struck down the prince, and they hewed him and his company in pieces, and smote off their heads, and cast them to those that were without.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Blessed be our God in all things, who gave for a prey them that had committed impiety.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Whereas we are now about to keep the purification of the temple in the month Chislev, on the five and twentieth day, we thought it necessary to certify you thereof, that ye also may keep a feast of tabernacles, and a memorial of the fire which was given when Nehemiah offered sacrifices, after that he had built both the temple and the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> For indeed when our fathers were about to be led into the land of Persia, the godly priests of that time took of the fire of the altar, and hid it privily in the hollow of a well that was without water, wherein they made it sure, so that the place was unknown to all men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Now after many years, when it pleased God, Nehemiah, having received a charge from the king of Persia, sent in quest of the fire the descendants of the priests that hid it. When they declared to us that they had found no fire, but thick water,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> he commanded them to draw out thereof and bring to him: and when the sacrifices had been offered on the altar, Nehemiah commanded the priests to sprinkle with the water both the wood and the things laid thereupon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And when it was done, and some time had passed, and the sun shone out, which before was hidden with clouds, there was kindled a great blaze, so that all men marveled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And the priests made a prayer while the sacrifice was consuming, both the priests and all others, Jonathan leading and the rest answering, as Nehemiah did.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And the prayer was after this manner: O Lord, Lord God, the Creator of all things, who art terrible and strong and righteous and merciful, who alone art King and gracious,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> who alone suppliest every need, who alone art righteous and almighty and eternal, thou that savest Israel out of all evil, who madest the fathers thy chosen, and didst sanctify them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> accept the sacrifice for all thy people Israel, and guard thine own portion, and consecrate it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Gather together our Dispersion, set at liberty them that are in bondage among the heathen, look upon them that are despised and abhorred, and let the heathen know that thou art our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Torment them that oppress us and in arrogancy shamefully treat us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> Plant thy people in thy holy place, even as Moses said.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And thereupon the priests sang the hymns.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And as soon as the sacrifice was consumed, then Nehemiah commanded to pour on great stones the water that was left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And when this was done, a flame was kindled; but when the light from the altar shone over against it, all was consumed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And when the matter became known, and it was told the king of the Persians, that, in the place where the priests that were led away had hidden the fire, there appeared the water, wherewith also Nehemiah and they that were with him purified the sacrifice,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> then the king, enclosing the place, made it sacred, after he had proved the matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And when the king would show favor to any, he would take from them many presents and give them some of this water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And Nehemiah and they that were with him called this thing Nephthar, which is by interpretation, Cleansing; but most men call it Naphtha.</span>
