<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:6; 2 Peter 1:12–15; 1 Peter 1:22"> This is now, beloved, the second epistle that I write unto you; and in both of them I stir up your sincere mind by putting you in remembrance;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Jude 1:17; 2 Peter 2:21; Luke 1:70"> that ye should remember the words which were spoken before by the holy prophets, and the commandment of the Lord and Savior through your apostles:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jude 1:18; 1 Timothy 4:1–2; 2 Peter 2:10"> knowing this first, that in the end of days mockers shall come, walking after their own lusts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 17:15; Malachi 2:17; Jeremiah 5:12–13"> and saying, Where is the promise of his coming? for, from the day that the fathers fell asleep, all things continue as they were from the beginning of the creation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:3; Genesis 1:9; Psalms 24:2"> For this they willfully forget, that there were heavens from of old, and an earth compacted out of water and amidst water, by the word of God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 24:38–39; 2 Peter 2:5; Luke 17:27"> by which means the world that then was, being overflowed with water, perished:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 51:6; 2 Peter 3:12"> but the heavens that now are, and the earth, by his word have been stored up, reserved for fire against the day of judgment and destruction of ungodly men.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 90:4; Romans 11:25; 1 Corinthians 12:1"> But forget not this one thing, beloved, that one day is with the Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:4; Romans 2:4; Habakkuk 2:3"> The Lord is not slack concerning his promise, as some count slackness; but is long-suffering to us-ward, not wishing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:1; Matthew 24:35; 2 Peter 3:7"> But the day of the Lord will come as a thief in the night; in which the heavens shall pass away with a great noise, and the elements shall be dissolved with fervent heat, and the earth and the works that are therein shall be burned up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 1:15; Philippians 1:27; 1 Peter 2:12"> Seeing then that these things are all to be dissolved, what manner of persons ought ye to be in all holy living and godliness,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 3:10; Isaiah 34:4; Micah 1:4"> looking for and earnestly desiring the coming of the day of God, by reason of which the heavens being on fire shall be dissolved, and the elements shall melt with fervent heat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 65:17; Revelation 21:1; Isaiah 66:22"> But, according to his promise, we look for new heavens and a new earth, wherein dwelleth righteousness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:23; 1 Thessalonians 3:13; Philippians 1:10"> Wherefore, beloved, seeing that ye look for these things, give diligence that ye may be found in peace, without spot and blameless in his sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 2:4; 2 Peter 3:9; 1 Timothy 1:16"> And account that the long-suffering of our Lord is salvation; even as our beloved brother Paul also, according to the wisdom given to him, wrote unto you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 15:6; Hebrews 5:11; James 1:8"> as also in all his epistles, speaking in them of these things; wherein are some things hard to be understood, which the ignorant and unsteadfast wrest, as they do also the other scriptures, unto their own destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Hebrews 3:14; Colossians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 11:13–15"> Ye therefore, beloved, knowing these things beforehand, beware lest, being carried away with the error of the wicked, ye fall from your own steadfastness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Colossians 1:10; Ephesians 4:15; 1 Peter 2:2"> But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To him be the glory both now and forever. Amen.</span>
</div>
