<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Peter 1:20–21; Joel 2:28; Numbers 12:6–8"> God, having of old time spoken unto the fathers in the prophets by diverse portions and in diverse manners, hath at the end of these days spoken unto us in his Son,</span>

<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 1:15–17; Revelation 4:11; John 14:9–10"> who being the effulgence of his glory, and the very image of his substance, and upholding all things by the word of his power, when he had by himself made purification of our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 1:21; Philippians 2:9–11; 1 Peter 3:22"> having become by so much better than the angels, as he hath inherited a more excellent name than they.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 2:7; Acts 13:33; Hebrews 5:5"> For unto which of the angels said he at any time,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thou art my Son,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">This day have I begotten thee?</span>and again,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">I will be to him a Father,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And he shall be to me a Son?</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 97:7; Colossians 1:18; Deuteronomy 32:43"> And when he again bringeth in the firstborn into the world he saith, And let all the angels of God worship him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 104:4; Hebrews 1:14; 2 Kings 6:17"> And of the angels he saith,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Who maketh his angels winds,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And his ministers a flame of fire:</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Psalms 45:6–7; Isaiah 9:6–7; Jeremiah 23:5–6"> but of the Son he saith,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thy throne, O God, is forever and ever;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">A scepter of uprightness is the scepter of thy kingdom.</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 45:7; Psalms 23:5; Isaiah 61:3"> <span className="pl pl-1">Thou hast loved righteousness, and hated iniquity;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Therefore God, thy God, hath anointed thee</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">With the oil of gladness above thy fellows.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 102:25–27; Genesis 1:1; John 1:1–3"> And,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Thou, Lord, in the beginning didst lay the foundation of the earth,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And the heavens are the works of thy hands:</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 51:6; Mark 13:31; Luke 21:33"> <span className="pl pl-1">They shall perish; but thou continuest:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And they all shall wax old as doth a garment;</span><br /></span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Psalms 102:26–27; Hebrews 13:8; John 8:58"> <span className="pl pl-1">And as a mantle shalt thou roll them up,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And they shall be changed:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">But thou art the same,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And thy years shall not fail.</span></span>
