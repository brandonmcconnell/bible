<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 12:15; 1 Peter 1:1; John 7:35"> James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, to the twelve tribes which are of the Dispersion, greeting.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Peter 1:6–8; James 1:12; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> Count it all joy, my brethren, when ye fall into manifold temptations;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Romans 5:3–4; Hebrews 10:36; Colossians 1:11"> knowing that the proving of your faith worketh patience.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 21:19; 1 Peter 5:10; Matthew 5:48"> And let patience have its perfect work, that ye may be perfect and entire, lacking in nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Proverbs 2:3–6; John 15:7; Proverbs 3:5–7"> But if any of you lacketh wisdom, let him ask of God, who giveth to all liberally and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 21:21–22; Hebrews 11:6; Mark 11:22–24"> But let him ask in faith, nothing doubting: for he that doubteth is like the surge of the sea driven by the wind and tossed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="James 4:3; Isaiah 58:3–4; Proverbs 21:27"> For let not that man think that he shall receive anything of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 18:21; Matthew 6:24; James 4:8"> a double-minded man, unstable in all his ways.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:23–24; James 2:5–6; Proverbs 19:1"> But let the brother of low degree glory in his high estate:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Peter 1:24; 1 John 2:17; Job 14:2"> and the rich, in that he is made low: because as the flower of the grass he shall pass away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 40:7–8; Psalms 37:35–36; Psalms 49:6–14"> For the sun ariseth with the scorching wind, and withereth the grass; and the flower thereof falleth, and the grace of the fashion of it perisheth: so also shall the rich man fade away in his goings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 5:10; 1 Peter 1:6–8; Revelation 2:10"> Blessed is the man that endureth temptation; for when he hath been approved, he shall receive the crown of life, which the Lord promised to them that love him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Habakkuk 2:12–13; James 1:12; Romans 9:19–20"> Let no man say when he is tempted, I am tempted of God; for God cannot be tempted with evil, and he himself tempteth no man:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 7:21–22; Ephesians 4:22; Matthew 15:18"> but each man is tempted, when he is drawn away by his own lust, and enticed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Psalms 7:14; Job 15:35; Romans 5:12–21"> Then the lust, when it hath conceived, beareth sin: and the sin, when it is full-grown, bringeth forth death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 6:7; James 1:19; Philippians 2:12"> Be not deceived, my beloved brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 3:27; Matthew 7:11; Hebrews 13:8"> Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of lights, with whom can be no variation, neither shadow that is cast by turning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 8:29–31; 1 Peter 1:23; John 3:3–5"> Of his own will he brought us forth by the word of truth, that we should be a kind of firstfruits of his creatures.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Proverbs 17:27; Proverbs 21:23; Proverbs 14:29"> Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:24–25; Ephesians 4:26; James 3:17–18"> for the wrath of man worketh not the righteousness of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Ephesians 4:22; Ephesians 1:13; 1 Peter 2:11"> Wherefore putting away all filthiness and overflowing of wickedness, receive with meekness the implanted word, which is able to save your souls.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Romans 2:13; Luke 6:46–48; John 13:17"> But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deluding your own selves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Matthew 7:26–27; Ezekiel 33:31–32; James 2:14–26"> For if anyone is a hearer of the word and not a doer, he is like unto a man beholding his natural face in a mirror:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Peter 3:11; Luke 1:66; Luke 7:39"> for he beholdeth himself, and goeth away, and straightaway forgetteth what manner of man he was.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Luke 11:28; John 13:17; 2 Corinthians 3:17–18"> But he that looketh into the perfect law, the law of liberty, and so continueth, he being not a hearer that forgetteth but a doer that worketh, this man shall be blessed in his doing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Ephesians 4:29; Psalms 141:3; 1 Peter 3:10"> If any man among you thinketh himself to be religious, while he bridleth not his tongue but deceiveth his heart, this man’s religion is vain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 1:16–17; 1 John 3:17–19; Galatians 6:9–10"> Pure religion and undefiled before God, even the Father, is this, to visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, and to keep oneself unspotted from the world.</span>
</div>
