<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 20:5; 1 Kings 13:33–34; 1 Samuel 31:2"> At that time Abijah the son of Jeroboam fell sick.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:8; 1 Kings 11:29–38; 2 Samuel 14:2"> And Jeroboam said to his wife, Arise, I pray thee, and disguise thyself, that thou be not known to be the wife of Jeroboam; and get thee to Shiloh: behold, there is Ahijah the prophet, who spake concerning me that I should be king over this people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:7–8; 1 Kings 13:7; 2 Kings 4:42"> And take with thee ten loaves, and cakes, and a cruse of honey, and go to him: he will tell thee what shall become of the child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Kings 11:29; 1 Samuel 3:2; 1 Samuel 4:15"> And Jeroboam’s wife did so, and arose, and went to Shiloh, and came to the house of Ahijah. Now Ahijah could not see; for his eyes were set by reason of his age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Kings 6:8–12; Proverbs 21:30; 2 Kings 4:27"> And Jehovah said unto Ahijah, Behold, the wife of Jeroboam cometh to inquire of thee concerning her son; for he is sick: thus and thus shalt thou say unto her; for it will be, when she cometh in, that she will feign herself to be another woman.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 5:17–28; Acts 5:9–10; Luke 20:20–23"> And it was so, when Ahijah heard the sound of her feet, as she came in at the door, that he said, Come in, thou wife of Jeroboam; why feignest thou thyself to be another? for I am sent to thee with heavy tidings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 16:2; 2 Samuel 12:7–8; 1 Samuel 15:16"> Go, tell Jeroboam, Thus saith Jehovah, the God of Israel: Forasmuch as I exalted thee from among the people, and made thee prince over my people Israel,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Kings 15:5; 1 Kings 11:33–38; 2 Chronicles 28:1"> and rent the kingdom away from the house of David, and gave it thee; and yet thou hast not been as my servant David, who kept my commandments, and who followed me with all his heart, to do that only which was right in mine eyes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 23:35; Nehemiah 9:26; Psalms 50:17"> but hast done evil above all that were before thee, and hast gone and made thee other gods, and molten images, to provoke me to anger, and hast cast me behind thy back:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:36; 1 Kings 21:21; 2 Kings 14:26"> therefore, behold, I will bring evil upon the house of Jeroboam, and will cut off from Jeroboam every man-child, him that is shut up and him that is left at large in Israel, and will utterly sweep away the house of Jeroboam, as a man sweepeth away dung, till it be all gone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Kings 16:4; Jeremiah 15:3; 1 Kings 21:23–24"> Him that dieth of Jeroboam in the city shall the dogs eat; and him that dieth in the field shall the birds of the heavens eat: for Jehovah hath spoken it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 14:3; 1 Kings 14:16–17; 2 Kings 1:6"> Arise thou therefore, get thee to thy house: and when thy feet enter into the city, the child shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Chronicles 19:3; 2 Chronicles 12:12; Job 19:28"> And all Israel shall mourn for him, and bury him; for he only of Jeroboam shall come to the grave, because in him there is found some good thing toward Jehovah, the God of Israel, in the house of Jeroboam.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Kings 15:27–29; Ezekiel 7:2–7; Ezekiel 12:22–28"> Moreover Jehovah will raise him up a king over Israel, who shall cut off the house of Jeroboam that day: but what? even now.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Joshua 23:15–16; Psalms 52:5; 2 Kings 15:29"> For Jehovah will smite Israel, as a reed is shaken in the water; and he will root up Israel out of this good land which he gave to their fathers, and will scatter them beyond the River, because they have made their Asherim, provoking Jehovah to anger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:34; 1 Kings 16:2; 1 Kings 13:34"> And he will give Israel up because of the sins of Jeroboam, which he hath sinned, and wherewith he hath made Israel to sin.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 15:33; 1 Kings 16:15; 1 Kings 16:23"> And Jeroboam’s wife arose, and departed, and came to Tirzah: and as she came to the threshold of the house, the child died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 14:13"> And all Israel buried him, and mourned for him, according to the word of Jehovah, which he spake by his servant Ahijah the prophet.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="2 Chronicles 13:2–20; 1 Chronicles 27:24; 1 Kings 16:20"> And the rest of the acts of Jeroboam, how he warred, and how he reigned, behold, they are written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Job 14:12; Psalms 3:5; 1 Kings 11:43"> And the days which Jeroboam reigned were two and twenty years: and he slept with his fathers, and Nadab his son reigned in his stead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:13; 1 Kings 11:36; 1 Kings 14:31"> And Rehoboam the son of Solomon reigned in Judah. Rehoboam was forty and one years old when he began to reign, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city which Jehovah had chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, to put his name there: and his mother’s name was Naamah the Ammonitess.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:1; 1 Corinthians 10:22; Psalms 78:58"> And Judah did that which was evil in the sight of Jehovah, and they provoked him to jealousy with their sins which they committed, above all that their fathers had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:2; Isaiah 57:5; 2 Kings 17:9–10"> For they also built them high places, and pillars, and Asherim, on every high hill, and under every green tree;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Deuteronomy 23:17; 1 Corinthians 6:9; 1 Kings 15:12"> and there were also sodomites in the land: they did according to all the abominations of the nations which Jehovah drove out before the children of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Kings 11:40; 2 Chronicles 12:2–4; 2 Chronicles 12:9"> And it came to pass in the fifth year of king Rehoboam, that Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Kings 15:18; 2 Chronicles 9:15–16; 1 Kings 7:51"> and he took away the treasures of the house of Jehovah, and the treasures of the king’s house; he even took away all: and he took away all the shields of gold which Solomon had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="1 Samuel 8:11; 1 Samuel 22:17; 1 Kings 18:46"> And king Rehoboam made in their stead shields of brass, and committed them to the hands of the captains of the guard, who kept the door of the king’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:11"> And it was so, that, as oft as the king went into the house of Jehovah, the guard bare them, and brought them back into the guard chamber.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Kings 22:45; 1 Kings 15:23; 1 Kings 14:19"> Now the rest of the acts of Rehoboam, and all that he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="1 Kings 12:24; 1 Kings 12:21; 1 Kings 15:6–7"> And there was war between Rehoboam and Jeroboam continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="2 Chronicles 12:16; Matthew 1:7; 1 Kings 15:3"> And Rehoboam slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David: and his mother’s name was Naamah the Ammonitess. And Abijam his son reigned in his stead.</span>
</div>
