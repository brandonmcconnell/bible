<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="John 20:1–2; Mark 16:1–8; Matthew 28:1–8"> But on the first day of the week, at early dawn, they came unto the tomb, bringing the spices which they had prepared, and certain others with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Mark 16:3–4; Matthew 27:60–66; Mark 15:46–47"> And they found the stone rolled away from the tomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 24:23; John 20:6–7; Matthew 16:5"> And they entered in, and found not the body of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Mark 16:5; John 20:11–12; Acts 1:10"> And it came to pass, while they were much perplexed thereabout, behold, two men stood by them in dazzling garments:</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Mark 16:5–6; Matthew 28:3–5; Revelation 1:18"> and as they were affrighted and bowed down their face to the earth, they said unto them, Why seek ye the living among the dead?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Matthew 17:22–23; Luke 9:22; Mark 8:31"> He is not here, but is risen: remember how he spake unto you when he was yet in Galilee,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 16:21"> saying, The Son of man must be delivered up into the hands of sinful men, and be crucified, and the third day rise again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 12:16; John 14:26; John 2:19–22"> And they remembered his words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Luke 24:22–24; Mark 16:7–8; Matthew 28:7–8"> and returned from the tomb, and told all these things to the eleven, and to all the rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 27:56; John 20:11–18; Luke 8:2–3"> Now it was Mary Magdalene, and Joanna, and Mary the mother of James, and the other women with them, who told these things unto the apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Job 9:16; Mark 16:11; Genesis 19:14"> And their words appeared in their sight as idle talk; and they disbelieved them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="John 20:3–10"> But Peter arose, and ran unto the tomb; and stooping and looking in, he seeth the linen cloths lying by themselves; and he departed to his own home, wondering at that which was come to pass.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Luke 24:18; Mark 16:12–13"> And behold, two of them were going that very day to a village named Emmaus, which was threescore furlongs from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 6:45; Deuteronomy 6:7; Malachi 3:6"> And they communed with each other of all these things which had happened.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:20; John 14:18–19; Luke 24:36"> And it came to pass, while they communed and questioned together, that Jesus himself drew near, and went with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 20:14; John 21:4; Luke 24:31"> But their eyes were holden that they should not know him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 16:20–22; John 16:6; Ezekiel 9:4–6"> And he said unto them, What communications are these that ye have one with another, as ye walk and are looking sad?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="John 19:25"> And the one, whose name was Cleopas, answered and said unto him, Dost thou alone sojourn in Jerusalem and not know the things which are come to pass there in these days?</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 21:11; Acts 7:22; John 4:19"> And he said unto them, What things? And they said unto him, The things concerning Jesus of Nazareth, who was a prophet mighty in deed and word before God and all the people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Acts 3:13–15; Acts 4:8–10; Luke 23:13"> and how the chief priests and our rulers delivered him up to be condemned to death, and crucified him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Luke 1:68; 1 Peter 1:18–19; Luke 2:38"> But we hoped that it was he who should redeem Israel: and besides all this, today is now the third day since these things came to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Mark 16:9–10; Matthew 28:7–8; John 20:1–2"> Moreover certain women of our company amazed us, having been early at the tomb;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and when they found not his body, they came, saying, that they had also seen a vision of angels, who said that he was alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 24:12; John 20:1–10"> And certain of them that were with us went to the tomb, and found it even so as the women also had said: but him they saw not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Mark 7:18; Mark 9:19; Hebrews 5:11–12"> And he said unto them, O foolish men, and slow of heart to believe in all that the prophets have spoken!</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Peter 1:11; Isaiah 53:1–12; Zechariah 13:7"> Behooved it not the Christ to suffer these things, and to enter into his glory?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Isaiah 7:14; Luke 24:44; Deuteronomy 18:15"> And beginning from Moses and from all the prophets, he interpreted to them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Mark 6:48; Genesis 19:2; Genesis 32:26"> And they drew nigh unto the village, whither they were going: and he made as though he would go further.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="2 Kings 4:8; Luke 14:23; Genesis 19:3"> And they constrained him, saying, Abide with us; for it is toward evening, and the day is far spent. And he went in to abide with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Matthew 14:19; Luke 24:35; Matthew 15:36"> And it came to pass, when he had sat down with them to meat, he took the bread and blessed; and breaking it he gave to them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:16; John 8:59; Luke 4:30"> And their eyes were opened, and they knew him; and he vanished out of their sight.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Luke 24:45; Jeremiah 23:29; Hebrews 4:12"> And they said one to another, Was not our heart burning within us, while he spake to us in the way, and while he opened to us the scriptures?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Mark 16:13; John 20:19–26; Acts 1:14"> And they rose up that very hour, and returned to Jerusalem, and found the eleven gathered together, and them that were with them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:5; Mark 16:7; Luke 22:54–62"> saying, The Lord is risen indeed, and hath appeared to Simon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Mark 16:12–13; Acts 2:42; Luke 24:30–31"> And they rehearsed the things that happened in the way, and how he was known of them in the breaking of the bread.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:16; Isaiah 57:18; Mark 16:14"> And as they spake these things, Jesus himself stood in the midst of them, and saith unto them, Peace be unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Samuel 28:13; Matthew 14:26–27; Job 4:14–16"> But they were terrified and affrighted, and supposed that they beheld a spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:14; Hebrews 4:13; Daniel 4:19"> And he said unto them, Why are ye troubled? and wherefore do questionings arise in your hearts?</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="John 20:27; 1 John 1:1; Ecclesiastes 12:7"> See my hands and my feet, that it is I myself: handle me, and see; for a spirit hath not flesh and bones, as ye behold me having.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And when he had said this, he showed them his hands and his feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="John 21:5; Psalms 126:1–2; Genesis 45:26–28"> And while they still disbelieved for joy, and wondered, he said unto them, Have ye here anything to eat?</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And they gave him a piece of a broiled fish, and of a honeycomb.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Acts 10:41"> And he took them, and ate before them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Zechariah 12:10; Luke 24:26–27; Zechariah 9:9"> And he said unto them, These are the words which I spake unto you, while I was yet with you, that all things must needs be fulfilled, which are written in the law of Moses, and the prophets, and the psalms, concerning me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Acts 16:14; 1 John 5:20; Psalms 119:18"> Then opened he their mind, that they might understand the scriptures;</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Isaiah 53:2–12; Isaiah 50:6; Luke 24:44"> and he said unto them, Thus it is written, and thus it behooved the Christ to suffer, and rise again from the dead the third day;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Acts 5:31; Isaiah 49:6; Acts 2:38"> and that repentance and remission of sins should be preached in his name unto all the nations, beginning from Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 2:32; Acts 13:31"> And ye are witnesses of these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Acts 1:8; Acts 1:4; Joel 2:28–32"> And behold, I send the promise of my Father upon you: but tarry ye in the city of Jerusalem, until ye be clothed with power from on high.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Acts 1:12; Matthew 21:17; Mark 11:1"> And he led them out as far as to Bethany: and he lifted up his hands, and blessed them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Acts 1:9; Mark 16:19; 2 Kings 2:11"> And it came to pass, while he blessed them, he parted from them, and was carried up into heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Matthew 28:9; John 16:22; Psalms 30:11"> And they worshiped him, and returned to Jerusalem with great joy:</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Mark 16:20; Acts 2:46–47; Acts 5:41–42"> and were continually in the temple, praising and blessing God. Amen.</span>
</div>
