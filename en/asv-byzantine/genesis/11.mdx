<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Acts 2:6; Zephaniah 3:9; Isaiah 19:18"> And the whole earth was of one language and of one speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Daniel 1:2; Genesis 14:1"> And it came to pass, as they journeyed east, that they found a plain in the land of Shinar; and they dwelt there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 14:10; Exodus 1:14; Exodus 2:3"> And they said one to another, Come, let us make brick, and burn them thoroughly. And they had brick for stone, and slime had they for mortar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Deuteronomy 1:28; John 5:44; Luke 1:51"> And they said, Come, let us build us a city, and a tower, whose top may reach unto heaven, and let us make us a name; lest we be scattered abroad upon the face of the whole earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 18:21; Psalms 33:13–14; John 3:13"> And Jehovah came down to see the city and the tower, which the children of men built.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Psalms 2:1–4; Genesis 3:22; Genesis 11:1"> And Jehovah said, Behold, they are one people, and they have all one language; and this is what they begin to do: and now nothing will be withholden from them, which they purpose to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 33:10; Psalms 55:9; Job 5:12–13"> Come, let us go down, and there confound their language, that they may not understand one another’s speech.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Luke 1:51; Deuteronomy 32:8; Genesis 10:25"> So Jehovah scattered them abroad from thence upon the face of all the earth: and they left off building the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 10:10; Genesis 10:5; Genesis 10:20"> Therefore was the name of it called Babel; because Jehovah did there confound the language of all the earth: and from thence did Jehovah scatter them abroad upon the face of all the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Chronicles 1:17–27; Genesis 10:21–22; Luke 3:34–36"> These are the generations of Shem. Shem was a hundred years old, and begat Arpachshad two years after the flood:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 9:7; Psalms 144:12; Genesis 1:28"> and Shem lived after he begat Arpachshad five hundred years, and begat sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Luke 3:36"> And Arpachshad lived five and thirty years, and begat Shelah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and Arpachshad lived after he begat Shelah four hundred and three years, and begat sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And Shelah lived thirty years, and begat Eber:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and Shelah lived after he begat Eber four hundred and three years, and begat sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 10:21; 1 Chronicles 1:19; Numbers 24:24"> And Eber lived four and thirty years, and begat Peleg:</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and Eber lived after he begat Peleg four hundred and thirty years, and begat sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Luke 3:35"> And Peleg lived thirty years, and begat Reu:</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> and Peleg lived after he begat Reu two hundred and nine years, and begat sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 3:35"> And Reu lived two and thirty years, and begat Serug:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and Reu lived after he begat Serug two hundred and seven years, and begat sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Joshua 24:2"> And Serug lived thirty years, and begat Nahor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and Serug lived after he begat Nahor two hundred years, and begat sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Luke 3:34; Joshua 24:2"> And Nahor lived nine and twenty years, and begat Terah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and Nahor lived after he begat Terah a hundred and nineteen years, and begat sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Joshua 24:2; 1 Chronicles 1:26–27; Genesis 22:20–24"> And Terah lived seventy years, and begat Abram, Nahor, and Haran.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 12:4; Genesis 14:12; Genesis 11:31"> Now these are the generations of Terah. Terah begat Abram, Nahor, and Haran; and Haran begat Lot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 15:7; Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–4"> And Haran died before his father Terah in the land of his nativity, in Ur of the Chaldeans.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 17:15; Genesis 22:20; Genesis 24:15"> And Abram and Nahor took them wives: the name of Abram’s wife was Sarai; and the name of Nahor’s wife, Milcah, the daughter of Haran, the father of Milcah, and the father of Iscah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Genesis 29:31; Genesis 21:1–2; Luke 1:7"> And Sarai was barren; she had no child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:7; Acts 7:2–4; Genesis 24:10"> And Terah took Abram his son, and Lot the son of Haran, his son’s son, and Sarai his daughter-in-law, his son Abram’s wife; and they went forth with them from Ur of the Chaldeans, to go into the land of Canaan; and they came unto Haran, and dwelt there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And the days of Terah were two hundred and five years: and Terah died in Haran.</span>
