<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Daniel 3:19; 1 Samuel 18:9–11; Genesis 4:5"> And Jacob beheld the countenance of Laban, and, behold, it was not toward him as beforetime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Genesis 28:15; Genesis 32:9; Hebrews 13:5"> And Jehovah said unto Jacob, Return unto the land of thy fathers, and to thy kindred; and I will be with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And Jacob sent and called Rachel and Leah to the field unto his flock,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Genesis 31:2–3; Genesis 31:53; Genesis 48:15"> and said unto them, I see your father’s countenance, that it is not toward me as beforetime; but the God of my father hath been with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Genesis 30:29; Ephesians 6:5–8; Titus 2:9–10"> And ye know that with all my power I have served your father.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 37:28; Genesis 31:29; Numbers 14:22"> And your father hath deceived me, and changed my wages ten times; but God suffered him not to hurt me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Genesis 30:32"> If he said thus, The speckled shall be thy wages; then all the flock bare speckled: and if he said thus, The ringstreaked shall be thy wages; then bare all the flock ringstreaked.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 31:1; Genesis 31:16; Proverbs 13:22"> Thus God hath taken away the cattle of your father, and given them to me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 13:1; Genesis 30:39; Genesis 20:6"> And it came to pass at the time that the flock conceive, that I lifted up mine eyes, and saw in a dream, and, behold, the he-goats which leaped upon the flock were ringstreaked, speckled, and grizzled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 58:9; Genesis 31:13; 1 Samuel 3:6"> And the angel of God said unto me in the dream, Jacob: and I said, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 3:7; Deuteronomy 24:15; Genesis 31:42"> And he said, Lift up now thine eyes, and see: all the he-goats which leap upon the flock are ringstreaked, speckled, and grizzled: for I have seen all that Laban doeth unto thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 31:3; Genesis 32:9; Genesis 28:12–22"> I am the God of Beth-el, where thou anointedst a pillar, where thou vowedst a vow unto me: now arise, get thee out from this land, and return unto the land of thy nativity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ruth 4:11; Genesis 2:24; Genesis 29:24"> And Rachel and Leah answered and said unto him, Is there yet any portion or inheritance for us in our father’s house?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 29:15–20; Genesis 31:41; Genesis 29:27–30"> Are we not accounted by him as foreigners? for he hath sold us, and hath also quite devoured our money.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 30:35–1; Genesis 31:9; Psalms 45:10"> For all the riches which God hath taken away from our father, that is ours and our children’s: now then, whatsoever God hath said unto thee, do.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:17; Genesis 24:61; Genesis 24:10"> Then Jacob rose up, and set his sons and his wives upon the camels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Genesis 27:1–2; Genesis 27:41; Genesis 35:27–29"> and he carried away all his cattle, and all his substance which he had gathered, the cattle of his getting, which he had gathered in Paddan-aram, to go to Isaac his father unto the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 19:13; Genesis 31:30; Hosea 3:4"> Now Laban was gone to shear his sheep: and Rachel stole the teraphim that were her father’s.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Jacob stole away unawares to Laban the Syrian, in that he told him not that he fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Kings 12:17; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 46:28"> So he fled with all that he had; and he rose up, and passed over the River, and set his face toward the mountain of Gilead.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Job 5:12–13; Genesis 30:36; Exodus 14:5–31"> And it was told Laban on the third day that Jacob was fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 13:8; Genesis 24:27; Exodus 2:11"> And he took his brethren with him, and pursued after him seven days’ journey; and he overtook him in the mountain of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 24:50; Genesis 31:29; Genesis 20:3"> And God came to Laban the Syrian in a dream of the night, and said unto him, Take heed to thyself that thou speak not to Jacob either good or bad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 12:8; Genesis 33:18; Hebrews 11:9"> And Laban came up with Jacob. Now Jacob had pitched his tent in the mountain: and Laban with his brethren encamped in the mountain of Gilead.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="1 Samuel 30:2; Genesis 12:18; Genesis 20:9–10"> And Laban said to Jacob, What hast thou done, that thou hast stolen away unawares to me, and carried away my daughters as captives of the sword?</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Exodus 15:20; Proverbs 26:23–26; Genesis 31:3–5"> Wherefore didst thou flee secretly, and steal away from me, and didst not tell me, that I might have sent thee away with mirth and with songs, with tabret and with harp;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 31:55; Ruth 1:14; Acts 20:37"> and didst not suffer me to kiss my sons and my daughters? now hast thou done foolishly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 31:53; Genesis 31:42; Genesis 31:24"> It is in the power of my hand to do you hurt: but the God of your father spake unto me yesternight, saying, Take heed to thyself that thou speak not to Jacob either good or bad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:11; Genesis 31:19; Judges 18:24"> And now, though thou wouldest needs be gone, because thou sore longedst after thy father’s house, yet wherefore hast thou stolen my gods?</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Proverbs 29:25; Genesis 31:26–27; Genesis 20:11"> And Jacob answered and said to Laban, Because I was afraid: for I said, Lest thou shouldest take thy daughters from me by force.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="1 Samuel 12:3–5; Genesis 31:30; Genesis 31:19"> With whomsoever thou findest thy gods, he shall not live: before our brethren discern thou what is thine with me, and take it to thee. For Jacob knew not that Rachel had stolen them.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 24:28"> And Laban went into Jacob’s tent, and into Leah’s tent, and into the tent of the two maidservants; but he found them not. And he went out of Leah’s tent, and entered into Rachel’s tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Genesis 31:19; Genesis 31:17"> Now Rachel had taken the teraphim, and put them in the camel’s saddle, and sat upon them. And Laban felt about all the tent, but found them not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Leviticus 19:32; Exodus 20:12; 1 Peter 2:18"> And she said to her father, Let not my lord be angry that I cannot rise up before thee; for the manner of women is upon me. And he searched, but found not the teraphim.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ephesians 4:26; Genesis 49:7; Numbers 16:15"> And Jacob was wroth, and chode with Laban: and Jacob answered and said to Laban, What is my trespass? what is my sin, that thou hast hotly pursued after me?</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="1 Peter 2:12; 1 Corinthians 6:4–5; 1 Samuel 12:3–4"> Whereas thou hast felt about all my stuff, what hast thou found of all thy household stuff? Set it here before my brethren and thy brethren, that they may judge betwixt us two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 30:27; Deuteronomy 28:4; Ezekiel 34:2–4"> These twenty years have I been with thee; thy ewes and thy she-goats have not cast their young, and the rams of thy flocks have I not eaten.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Exodus 22:31; Exodus 22:10–13; Leviticus 22:8"> That which was torn of beasts I brought not unto thee; I bare the loss of it; of my hand didst thou require it, whether stolen by day or stolen by night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Luke 2:8; Exodus 2:19–22; 1 Peter 5:2–4"> Thus I was; in the day the drought consumed me, and the frost by night; and my sleep fled from mine eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 31:7; 1 Corinthians 15:10; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> These twenty years have I been in thy house; I served thee fourteen years for thy two daughters, and six years for thy flock: and thou hast changed my wages ten times.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 31:7; Genesis 31:53; Isaiah 8:13"> Except the God of my father, the God of Abraham, and the Fear of Isaac, had been with me, surely now hadst thou sent me away empty. God hath seen mine affliction and the labor of my hands, and rebuked thee yesternight.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And Laban answered and said unto Jacob, The daughters are my daughters, and the children are my children, and the flocks are my flocks, and all that thou seest is mine: and what can I do this day unto these my daughters, or unto their children whom they have borne?</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 31:52; Joshua 24:25–27; Joshua 22:27"> And now come, let us make a covenant, I and thou; and let it be for a witness between me and thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Genesis 28:18–22"> And Jacob took a stone, and set it up for a pillar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 3:5; Genesis 31:23; Genesis 31:54"> And Jacob said unto his brethren, Gather stones; and they took stones, and made a heap: and they did eat there by the heap.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Hebrews 12:1"> And Laban called it Jegar-saha-dutha: but Jacob called it Galeed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Joshua 24:27; Joshua 13:8–9; Genesis 31:23"> And Laban said, This heap is witness between me and thee this day. Therefore was the name of it called Galeed:</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Judges 11:29; Judges 10:17; 1 Samuel 7:5–6"> and Mizpah, for he said, Jehovah watch between me and thee, when we are absent one from another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:5; Jeremiah 29:23; Micah 1:2"> If thou shalt afflict my daughters, and if thou shalt take wives besides my daughters, no man is with us; see, God is witness betwixt me and thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And Laban said to Jacob, Behold this heap, and behold the pillar, which I have set betwixt me and thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 31:48; Genesis 31:44–45"> This heap be witness, and the pillar be witness, that I will not pass over this heap to thee, and that thou shalt not pass over this heap and this pillar unto me, for harm.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 31:42; Genesis 16:5; Joshua 24:2"> The God of Abraham, and the God of Nahor, the God of their father, judge betwixt us. And Jacob sware by the Fear of his father Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Exodus 18:12; Genesis 37:25; Genesis 26:30"> And Jacob offered a sacrifice in the mountain, and called his brethren to eat bread: and they did eat bread, and tarried all night in the mountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Genesis 31:28; Deuteronomy 23:5; Genesis 18:33"> And early in the morning Laban rose up, and kissed his sons and his daughters, and blessed them: and Laban departed, and returned unto his place.</span>
