<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Isaiah 56:4–5; Psalms 25:10; Psalms 103:18"> And God said unto Abraham, And as for thee, thou shalt keep my covenant, thou, and thy seed after thee throughout their generations.</span>

<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Acts 7:8; Deuteronomy 10:16"> And ye shall be circumcised in the flesh of your foreskin; and it shall be a token of a covenant betwixt me and you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 12:3; Luke 2:21; Exodus 12:48–49"> And he that is eight days old shall be circumcised among you, every male throughout your generations, he that is born in the house, or bought with money of any foreigner that is not of thy seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 12:44; Genesis 37:36; Nehemiah 5:8"> He that is born in thy house, and he that is bought with thy money, must needs be circumcised: and my covenant shall be in your flesh for an everlasting covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 4:24–26; Numbers 15:30–31; Exodus 12:19"> And the uncircumcised male who is not circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin, that soul shall be cut off from his people; he hath broken my covenant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Samuel 12:25; Genesis 32:28; Genesis 17:5"> And God said unto Abraham, As for Sarai thy wife, thou shalt not call her name Sarai, but Sarah shall her name be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 35:11; Genesis 17:6; Isaiah 49:23"> And I will bless her, and moreover I will give thee a son of her: yea, I will bless her, and she shall be a mother of nations; kings of peoples shall be of her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Revelation 11:16; Genesis 21:6; Genesis 18:12"> Then Abraham fell upon his face, and laughed, and said in his heart, Shall a child be born unto him that is a hundred years old? and shall Sarah, that is ninety years old, bear?</span>

<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 21:18; Genesis 21:13; Genesis 25:12–18"> And as for Ishmael, I have heard thee: behold, I have blessed him, and will make him fruitful, and will multiply him exceedingly; twelve princes shall he beget, and I will make him a great nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 21:2–3; Genesis 26:2–5; Galatians 3:29"> But my covenant will I establish with Isaac, whom Sarah shall bear unto thee at this set time in the next year.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Genesis 18:33; Genesis 35:9–15; Judges 6:21"> And he left off talking with him, and God went up from Abraham.</span>

<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Romans 4:11; Genesis 17:17; Romans 4:19–20"> And Abraham was ninety years old and nine, when he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin.</span>
