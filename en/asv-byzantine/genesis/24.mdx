<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 112:1–3; Galatians 3:9; Matthew 6:33"> And Abraham was old, and well stricken in age: and Jehovah had blessed Abraham in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Genesis 39:4–6; Genesis 15:2"> And Abraham said unto his servant, the elder of his house, that ruled over all that he had, Put, I pray thee, thy hand under my thigh:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:14–17; Genesis 26:34–35; Genesis 28:8"> and I will make thee swear by Jehovah, the God of heaven and the God of the earth, that thou wilt not take a wife for my son of the daughters of the Canaanites, among whom I dwell:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Genesis 28:2; Genesis 22:20–23; Genesis 12:7"> but thou shalt go unto my country, and to my kindred, and take a wife for my son Isaac.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 4:2; Exodus 9:2; Exodus 20:7"> And the servant said unto him, Peradventure the woman will not be willing to follow me unto this land: must I needs bring thy son again unto the land from whence thou camest?</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Galatians 5:1; 2 Peter 2:20–22; Hebrews 10:39"> And Abraham said unto him, Beware thou that thou bring not my son thither again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 33:2; Genesis 13:15; Hebrews 1:14"> Jehovah, the God of heaven, who took me from my father’s house, and from the land of my nativity, and who spake unto me, and who sware unto me, saying, Unto thy seed will I give this land; he will send his angel before thee, and thou shalt take a wife for my son from thence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 2:17–20; Numbers 30:5; Numbers 30:8"> And if the woman be not willing to follow thee, then thou shalt be clear from this my oath; only thou shalt not bring my son thither again.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 24:2"> And the servant put his hand under the thigh of Abraham his master, and sware to him concerning this matter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 11:31; Deuteronomy 23:4; Genesis 27:43"> And the servant took ten camels, of the camels of his master, and departed, having all goodly things of his master’s in his hand: and he arose, and went to Mesopotamia, unto the city of Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Samuel 9:11; Exodus 2:16; John 4:7"> And he made the camels to kneel down without the city by the well of water at the time of evening, the time that women go out to draw water.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Genesis 24:27; Exodus 3:6; Genesis 26:24"> And he said, O Jehovah, the God of my master Abraham, send me, I pray thee, good speed this day, and show kindness unto my master Abraham.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Genesis 24:43; Psalms 37:5; Exodus 2:16"> Behold, I am standing by the fountain of water; and the daughters of the men of the city are coming out to draw water:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Judges 6:37; Judges 6:17; Genesis 15:8"> and let it come to pass, that the damsel to whom I shall say, Let down thy pitcher, I pray thee, that I may drink; and she shall say, Drink, and I will give thy camels drink also: let the same be she that thou hast appointed for thy servant Isaac; and thereby shall I know that thou hast shown kindness unto my master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 24:45; Isaiah 65:24"> And it came to pass, before he had done speaking, that, behold, Rebekah came out, who was born to Bethuel the son of Milcah, the wife of Nahor, Abraham’s brother, with her pitcher upon her shoulder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Genesis 26:7; Numbers 31:17–18; Genesis 39:6"> And the damsel was very fair to look upon, a virgin, neither had any man known her: and she went down to the fountain, and filled her pitcher, and came up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="John 4:7; Isaiah 21:14; Genesis 26:1–35"> And the servant ran to meet her, and said, Give me to drink, I pray thee, a little water from thy pitcher.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 31:26; 1 Peter 3:8; Genesis 24:14"> And she said, Drink, my lord: and she hasted, and let down her pitcher upon her hand, and gave him drink.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Peter 4:9; Genesis 24:45–46"> And when she had done giving him drink, she said, I will draw for thy camels also, until they have done drinking.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And she hasted, and emptied her pitcher into the trough, and ran again unto the well to draw, and drew for all his camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Psalms 107:43; Genesis 24:12; 2 Samuel 7:18–20"> And the man looked steadfastly on her, holding his peace, to know whether Jehovah had made his journey prosperous or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11–12; Exodus 32:2–3; Genesis 23:15–16"> And it came to pass, as the camels had done drinking, that the man took a golden ring of half a shekel weight, and two bracelets for her hands of ten shekels weight of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> and said, Whose daughter art thou? tell me, I pray thee. Is there room in thy father’s house for us to lodge in?</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 24:15; Genesis 22:23; Genesis 22:20"> And she said unto him, I am the daughter of Bethuel the son of Milcah, whom she bare unto Nahor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 32:8; 1 Peter 4:9; Judges 19:19–21"> She said moreover unto him, We have both straw and provender enough, and room to lodge in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Genesis 24:48; Exodus 4:31; Genesis 24:52"> And the man bowed his head, and worshiped Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Genesis 24:48; Genesis 24:12; Psalms 98:3"> And he said, Blessed be Jehovah, the God of my master Abraham, who hath not forsaken his lovingkindness and his truth toward my master: as for me, Jehovah hath led me in the way to the house of my master’s brethren.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Genesis 24:67; Genesis 24:55; Genesis 31:33"> And the damsel ran, and told her mother’s house according to these words.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Genesis 29:5; Genesis 29:13; Genesis 24:55"> And Rebekah had a brother, and his name was Laban: and Laban ran out unto the man, unto the fountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And it came to pass, when he saw the ring, and the bracelets upon his sister’s hands, and when he heard the words of Rebekah his sister, saying, Thus spake the man unto me; that he came unto the man; and, behold, he was standing by the camels at the fountain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Ruth 3:10; Genesis 26:29; Psalms 115:15"> And he said, Come in, thou blessed of Jehovah; wherefore standest thou without? for I have prepared the house, and room for the camels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Judges 19:21; Genesis 43:24; Genesis 18:4"> And the man came into the house, and he ungirded the camels; and he gave straw and provender for the camels, and water to wash his feet and the feet of the men that were with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Psalms 132:3–5; 1 Timothy 6:2; Ephesians 6:5–8"> And there was set food before him to eat: but he said, I will not eat, until I have told mine errand. And he said, Speak on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And he said, I am Abraham’s servant.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Genesis 24:1; Genesis 13:2; Proverbs 10:22"> And Jehovah hath blessed my master greatly; and he is become great: and he hath given him flocks and herds, and silver and gold, and menservants and maidservants, and camels and asses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Genesis 25:5; Genesis 21:10; Genesis 21:1–7"> And Sarah my master’s wife bare a son to my master when she was old: and unto him hath he given all that he hath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Genesis 27:46; Ezra 9:1–3; Genesis 24:2–9"> And my master made me swear, saying, Thou shalt not take a wife for my son of the daughters of the Canaanites, in whose land I dwell:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Genesis 24:4; Genesis 31:19; Genesis 12:1"> but thou shalt go unto my father’s house, and to my kindred, and take a wife for my son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Genesis 24:5"> And I said unto my master, Peradventure the woman will not follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 23:20; Psalms 16:8; Genesis 5:24"> And he said unto me, Jehovah, before whom I walk, will send his angel with thee, and prosper thy way; and thou shalt take a wife for my son of my kindred, and of my father’s house:</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Genesis 24:8; Deuteronomy 29:12"> then shalt thou be clear from my oath, when thou comest to my kindred; and if they give her not to thee, thou shalt be clear from my oath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:11; Genesis 24:12–14; Ezra 8:21"> And I came this day unto the fountain, and said, O Jehovah, the God of my master Abraham, if now thou do prosper my way which I go:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Genesis 24:13–14"> behold, I am standing by the fountain of water; and let it come to pass, that the maiden that cometh forth to draw, to whom I shall say, Give me, I pray thee, a little water from thy pitcher to drink;</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Genesis 24:14; 1 Peter 3:8; Genesis 2:22"> and she shall say to me, Both drink thou, and I will also draw for thy camels: let the same be the woman whom Jehovah hath appointed for my master’s son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Matthew 7:7; Acts 12:12–17; Genesis 24:15–20"> And before I had done speaking in my heart, behold, Rebekah came forth with her pitcher on her shoulder; and she went down unto the fountain, and drew: and I said unto her, Let me drink, I pray thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Genesis 24:18–19"> And she made haste, and let down her pitcher from her shoulder, and said, Drink, and I will give thy camels drink also: so I drank, and she made the camels drink also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47" data-xref="Psalms 45:13–14; Genesis 24:22–24; Isaiah 62:3–5"> And I asked her, and said, Whose daughter art thou? And she said, The daughter of Bethuel, Nahor’s son, whom Milcah bare unto him: and I put the ring upon her nose, and the bracelets upon her hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Genesis 24:26–27; Psalms 48:14"> And I bowed my head, and worshiped Jehovah, and blessed Jehovah, the God of my master Abraham, who had led me in the right way to take my master’s brother’s daughter for his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49" data-xref="Genesis 47:29; Joshua 2:14; Proverbs 3:3"> And now if ye will deal kindly and truly with my master, tell me: and if not, tell me; that I may turn to the right hand, or to the left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50" data-xref="Psalms 118:23; Genesis 31:24; Genesis 31:29"> Then Laban and Bethuel answered and said, The thing proceedeth from Jehovah: we cannot speak unto thee bad or good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51" data-xref="Genesis 20:15; Genesis 24:15; 2 Samuel 16:10"> Behold, Rebekah is before thee, take her, and go, and let her be thy master’s son’s wife, as Jehovah hath spoken.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52" data-xref="Genesis 24:26; Genesis 24:48; Psalms 116:1–2"> And it came to pass, that, when Abraham’s servant heard their words, he bowed himself down to the earth unto Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53" data-xref="Genesis 24:22; Genesis 24:10; Exodus 12:35"> And the servant brought forth jewels of silver, and jewels of gold, and raiment, and gave them to Rebekah: he gave also to her brother and to her mother precious things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54" data-xref="Genesis 24:56; Genesis 24:59; Genesis 28:5–6"> And they did eat and drink, he and the men that were with him, and tarried all night; and they rose up in the morning, and he said, Send me away unto my master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55" data-xref="Genesis 4:3; Leviticus 25:29; Judges 14:8"> And her brother and her mother said, Let the damsel abide with us a few days, at the least ten; after that she shall go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56" data-xref="Proverbs 25:25; Joshua 1:8; Isaiah 48:15"> And he said unto them, Hinder me not, seeing Jehovah hath prospered my way; send me away that I may go to my master.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> And they said, We will call the damsel, and inquire at her mouth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58" data-xref="Luke 1:38; Psalms 45:10–11"> And they called Rebekah, and said unto her, Wilt thou go with this man? And she said, I will go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59" data-xref="Genesis 35:8; Numbers 11:12; Genesis 24:50"> And they sent away Rebekah their sister, and her nurse, and Abraham’s servant, and his men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60" data-xref="Genesis 22:17; Genesis 17:16; Ruth 4:11–12"> And they blessed Rebekah, and said unto her, Our sister, be thou the mother of thousands of ten thousands, and let thy seed possess the gate of those that hate them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61" data-xref="Psalms 45:10; Genesis 2:24; Esther 8:14"> And Rebekah arose, and her damsels, and they rode upon the camels, and followed the man: and the servant took Rebekah, and went his way.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62" data-xref="Genesis 25:11; Genesis 16:14; Genesis 12:9"> And Isaac came from the way of Beer-lahai-roi; for he dwelt in the land of the South.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63" data-xref="Psalms 119:15; Psalms 1:2; Psalms 104:34"> And Isaac went out to meditate in the field at the eventide: and he lifted up his eyes, and saw, and, behold, there were camels coming.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64" data-xref="Judges 1:14; Joshua 15:18"> And Rebekah lifted up her eyes, and when she saw Isaac, she alighted from the camel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v65" data-v="65" data-xref="1 Timothy 2:9; 1 Corinthians 11:5–6; 1 Corinthians 11:10"> And she said unto the servant, What man is this that walketh in the field to meet us? And the servant said, It is my master: and she took her veil, and covered herself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v66" data-v="66" data-xref="Mark 6:30"> And the servant told Isaac all the things that he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v67" data-v="67" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:2; Ephesians 5:22–33; Genesis 23:1–2"> And Isaac brought her into his mother Sarah’s tent, and took Rebekah, and she became his wife; and he loved her. And Isaac was comforted after his mother’s death.</span>
