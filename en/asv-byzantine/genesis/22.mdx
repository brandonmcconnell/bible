<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Hebrews 11:17; James 2:21; Deuteronomy 8:2"> And it came to pass after these things, that God did prove Abraham, and said unto him, Abraham; and he said, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="John 3:16; Romans 8:32; Hebrews 11:17"> And he said, Take now thy son, thine only son, whom thou lovest, even Isaac, and get thee into the land of Moriah; and offer him there for a burnt offering upon one of the mountains which I will tell thee of.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 119:60; Matthew 10:37; Luke 14:26"> And Abraham rose early in the morning, and saddled his ass, and took two of his young men with him, and Isaac his son; and he clave the wood for the burnt offering, and rose up, and went unto the place of which God had told him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 13:32; Matthew 17:23; Hosea 6:2"> On the third day Abraham lifted up his eyes, and saw the place afar off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Hebrews 11:19; Hebrews 12:1"> And Abraham said unto his young men, Abide ye here with the ass, and I and the lad will go yonder; and we will worship, and come again to you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:17; 1 Peter 2:24; Isaiah 53:6"> And Abraham took the wood of the burnt offering, and laid it upon Isaac his son; and he took in his hand the fire and the knife; and they went both of them together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:15; Matthew 26:39; Genesis 8:20"> And Isaac spake unto Abraham his father, and said, My father: and he said, Here am I, my son. And he said, Behold, the fire and the wood: but where is the lamb for a burnt offering?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="John 1:36; John 1:29; Revelation 5:12"> And Abraham said, God will provide himself the lamb for a burnt offering, my son: so they went both of them together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="James 2:21; Hebrews 11:17–19; Philippians 2:7–8"> And they came to the place which God had told him of; and Abraham built the altar there, and laid the wood in order, and bound Isaac his son, and laid him on the altar, upon the wood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 2:21–23; Hebrews 11:17–19; Isaiah 53:6–12"> And Abraham stretched forth his hand, and took the knife to slay his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Genesis 21:17; Exodus 3:4; Genesis 16:9–10"> And the angel of Jehovah called unto him out of heaven, and said, Abraham, Abraham: and he said, Here am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="James 2:21–22; James 2:18; 1 Samuel 15:22"> And he said, Lay not thy hand upon the lad, neither do thou anything unto him; for now I know that thou fearest God, seeing thou hast not withheld thy son, thine only son, from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:13; Psalms 40:6–8; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10"> And Abraham lifted up his eyes, and looked, and, behold, behind him a ram caught in the thicket by his horns: and Abraham went and took the ram, and offered him up for a burnt offering in the stead of his son.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Genesis 22:13; Exodus 17:15; Genesis 22:8"> And Abraham called the name of that place Jehovah-jireh: as it is said to this day, In the mount of Jehovah it shall be provided.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And the angel of Jehovah called unto Abraham a second time out of heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 6:13–14; Luke 1:73; Psalms 105:9"> and said, By myself have I sworn, saith Jehovah, because thou hast done this thing, and hast not withheld thy son, thine only son,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Genesis 15:5; Genesis 24:60; Jeremiah 33:22"> that in blessing I will bless thee, and in multiplying I will multiply thy seed as the stars of the heavens, and as the sand which is upon the seashore; and thy seed shall possess the gate of his enemies;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 3:25; Genesis 12:3; Galatians 3:16"> and in thy seed shall all the nations of the earth be blessed; because thou hast obeyed my voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 21:31; Judges 20:1; Joshua 15:28"> So Abraham returned unto his young men, and they rose up and went together to Beer-sheba; and Abraham dwelt at Beer-sheba.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Genesis 11:29; Genesis 24:24; Proverbs 25:25"> And it came to pass after these things, that it was told Abraham, saying, Behold, Milcah, she also hath borne children unto thy brother Nahor:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Job 1:1; Job 32:2; Psalms 60:1–12"> Uz his firstborn, and Buz his brother, and Kemuel the father of Aram,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and Chesed, and Hazo, and Pildash, and Jidlaph, and Bethuel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Genesis 24:15; Genesis 28:5; Genesis 24:51"> And Bethuel begat Rebekah: these eight did Milcah bear to Nahor, Abraham’s brother.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Genesis 16:3; Genesis 25:6; Proverbs 15:25"> And his concubine, whose name was Reumah, she also bare Tebah, and Gaham, and Tahash, and Maacah.</span>
