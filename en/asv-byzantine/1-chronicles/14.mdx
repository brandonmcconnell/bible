<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 24:7; Psalms 89:20–37; 2 Samuel 7:16"> And David perceived that Jehovah had established him king over Israel; for his kingdom was exalted on high, for his people Israel’s sake.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 5:18–19; 2 Samuel 5:13; Ecclesiastes 7:26–29"> And David took more wives at Jerusalem; and David begat more sons and daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:5–9; 2 Samuel 5:14; Luke 3:31"> And these are the names of the children whom he had in Jerusalem: Shammua, and Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon,</span>

<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and Nogah, and Nepheg, and Japhia,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Chronicles 3:8; 2 Samuel 5:16"> and Elishama, and Beeliada, and Eliphelet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Samuel 21:11; Revelation 11:15–18; Psalms 2:1–6"> And when the Philistines heard that David was anointed king over all Israel, all the Philistines went up to seek David: and David heard of it, and went out against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 11:15; 1 Chronicles 14:13; 2 Samuel 23:13"> Now the Philistines had come and made a raid in the valley of Rephaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Proverbs 3:6; 1 Chronicles 14:14; 1 Chronicles 13:3"> And David inquired of God, saying, Shall I go up against the Philistines? and wilt thou deliver them into my hand? And Jehovah said unto him, Go up; for I will deliver them into thy hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 28:21; Exodus 14:28; 2 Samuel 5:20"> So they came up to Baal-perazim, and David smote them there; and David said, God hath broken mine enemies by my hand, like the breach of waters. Therefore they called the name of that place Baal-perazim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 32:20; 1 Samuel 5:2–6; 2 Kings 19:18"> And they left their gods there; and David gave commandment, and they were burned with fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Chronicles 14:9; 1 Kings 20:22; 2 Samuel 5:22–25"> And the Philistines yet again made a raid in the valley.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="John 9:6–7; 1 Chronicles 14:10; Psalms 27:4"> And David inquired again of God; and God said unto him, Thou shalt not go up after them: turn away from them, and come upon them over against the mulberry trees.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 13:4; Acts 2:2; Philippians 2:12–13"> And it shall be, when thou hearest the sound of marching in the tops of the mulberry trees, that then thou shalt go out to battle; for God is gone out before thee to smite the host of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="John 15:14; John 13:17; Genesis 6:22"> And David did as God commanded him: and they smote the host of the Philistines from Gibeon even to Gezer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Deuteronomy 2:25; Joshua 6:27; Exodus 15:14–16"> And the fame of David went out into all lands; and Jehovah brought the fear of him upon all nations.</span>
