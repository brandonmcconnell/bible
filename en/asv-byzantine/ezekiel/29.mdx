<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:17; Ezekiel 26:1; Ezekiel 20:1"> In the tenth year, in the tenth month, in the twelfth day of the month, the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Ezekiel 6:2; Jeremiah 44:30; Jeremiah 46:2–16"> Son of man, set thy face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and prophesy against him, and against all Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:2; Isaiah 27:1; Jeremiah 44:30"> speak, and say, Thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold, I am against thee, Pharaoh king of Egypt, the great monster that lieth in the midst of his rivers, that hath said, My river is mine own, and I have made it for myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Kings 19:28; Ezekiel 38:4; Amos 4:2"> And I will put hooks in thy jaws, and I will cause the fish of thy rivers to stick unto thy scales; and I will bring thee up out of the midst of thy rivers, with all the fish of thy rivers which stick unto thy scales.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 34:20; Ezekiel 32:4–6; Jeremiah 8:2"> And I will cast thee forth into the wilderness, thee and all the fish of thy rivers: thou shalt fall upon the open field; thou shalt not be brought together, nor gathered; I have given thee for food to the beasts of the earth and to the birds of the heavens.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Kings 18:21; Isaiah 36:6; Exodus 9:14"> And all the inhabitants of Egypt shall know that I am Jehovah, because they have been a staff of reed to the house of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 36:6; Ezekiel 17:15–17; Jeremiah 17:5–6"> When they took hold of thee by thy hand, thou didst break, and didst rend all their shoulders; and when they leaned upon thee, thou brakest, and madest all their loins to be at a stand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:17; Ezekiel 30:10; Ezekiel 29:19–20"> Therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold, I will bring a sword upon thee, and will cut off from thee man and beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:3; Proverbs 16:18; Ezekiel 29:10–12"> And the land of Egypt shall be a desolation and a waste; and they shall know that I am Jehovah. Because he hath said, The river is mine, and I have made it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:12; Ezekiel 30:6–9; Exodus 14:2"> therefore, behold, I am against thee, and against thy rivers, and I will make the land of Egypt an utter waste and desolation, from the tower of Syene even unto the border of Ethiopia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ezekiel 32:13; Jeremiah 43:11–12; Ezekiel 30:10–13"> No foot of man shall pass through it, nor foot of beast shall pass through it, neither shall it be inhabited forty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:19; Ezekiel 30:7; Ezekiel 30:23"> And I will make the land of Egypt a desolation in the midst of the countries that are desolate; and her cities among the cities that are laid waste shall be a desolation forty years; and I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations, and will disperse them through the countries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Jeremiah 46:26; Isaiah 19:22"> For thus saith the Lord Jehovah: At the end of forty years will I gather the Egyptians from the peoples whither they were scattered;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:14; Isaiah 11:11; Jeremiah 44:1"> and I will bring back the captivity of Egypt, and will cause them to return into the land of Pathros, into the land of their birth; and they shall be there a base kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Zechariah 10:11; Ezekiel 30:13; Ezekiel 17:14"> It shall be the basest of the kingdoms; neither shall it anymore lift itself up above the nations: and I will diminish them, that they shall no more rule over the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hosea 8:13; Isaiah 20:5; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> And it shall be no more the confidence of the house of Israel, bringing iniquity to remembrance, when they turn to look after them: and they shall know that I am the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 29:1; Ezekiel 1:2; Ezekiel 24:1"> And it came to pass in the seven and twentieth year, in the first month, in the first day of the month, the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 27:6; Jeremiah 25:9; Ezekiel 26:7–12"> Son of man, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon caused his army to serve a great service against Tyre: every head was made bald, and every shoulder was worn; yet had he no wages, nor his army, from Tyre, for the service that he had served against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:24–25; Jeremiah 43:10–13; Ezekiel 32:11"> Therefore thus saith the Lord Jehovah: Behold, I will give the land of Egypt unto Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon; and he shall carry off her multitude, and take her spoil, and take her prey; and it shall be the wages for his army.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Jeremiah 25:9; Isaiah 10:6–7; Isaiah 45:1–3"> I have given him the land of Egypt as his recompense for which he served, because they wrought for me, saith the Lord Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 Samuel 2:10; Luke 21:15; Psalms 132:17"> In that day will I cause a horn to bud forth unto the house of Israel, and I will give thee the opening of the mouth in the midst of them; and they shall know that I am Jehovah.</span>
