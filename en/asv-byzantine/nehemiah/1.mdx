<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Nehemiah 10:1; Zechariah 7:1; Esther 1:2"> The words of Nehemiah the son of Hacaliah.<br />Now it came to pass in the month Chislev, in the twentieth year, as I was in Shushan the palace,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:2; Ezekiel 6:9; Jeremiah 44:14"> that Hanani, one of my brethren, came, he and certain men out of Judah; and I asked them concerning the Jews that had escaped, that were left of the captivity, and concerning Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:17; 2 Kings 25:10; Nehemiah 7:6"> And they said unto me, The remnant that are left of the captivity there in the province are in great affliction and reproach: the wall of Jerusalem also is broken down, and the gates thereof are burned with fire.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 9:3; Psalms 137:1; Ezra 10:1"> And it came to pass, when I heard these words, that I sat down and wept, and mourned certain days; and I fasted and prayed before the God of heaven,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 20:6; Daniel 9:4–19; Deuteronomy 7:21"> and said, I beseech thee, O Jehovah, the God of heaven, the great and terrible God, that keepeth covenant and lovingkindness with them that love him and keep his commandments:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Daniel 9:20; Daniel 9:4; Psalms 34:15"> let thine ear now be attentive, and thine eyes open, that thou mayest hearken unto the prayer of thy servant, which I pray before thee at this time, day and night, for the children of Israel thy servants, while I confess the sins of the children of Israel, which we have sinned against thee. Yea, I and my father’s house have sinned:</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Daniel 9:5–6; Daniel 9:11; Daniel 9:13"> we have dealt very corruptly against thee, and have not kept the commandments, nor the statutes, nor the ordinances, which thou commandedst thy servant Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:64; Deuteronomy 4:25–27; Leviticus 26:33–46"> Remember, I beseech thee, the word that thou commandedst thy servant Moses, saying, If ye trespass, I will scatter you abroad among the peoples:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:11–14; Deuteronomy 30:2–5; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> but if ye return unto me, and keep my commandments and do them, though your outcasts were in the uttermost part of the heavens, yet will I gather them from thence, and will bring them unto the place that I have chosen, to cause my name to dwell there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 9:29; Daniel 9:15–27; Exodus 32:11"> Now these are thy servants and thy people, whom thou hast redeemed by thy great power, and by thy strong hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Nehemiah 1:6; Nehemiah 2:8; Proverbs 21:1"> O Lord, I beseech thee, let now thine ear be attentive to the prayer of thy servant, and to the prayer of thy servants, who delight to fear thy name; and prosper, I pray thee, thy servant this day, and grant him mercy in the sight of this man.<br />Now I was cupbearer to the king.</span>
</div>
