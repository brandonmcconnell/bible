<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Psalms 55:17; Acts 10:3; Acts 4:13"> Now Peter and John were going up together into the temple at the hour of prayer, being the ninth hour.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 14:8; John 9:8; Luke 16:20"> And a certain man that was lame from his mother’s womb was carried, whom they laid daily at the door of the temple which is called Beautiful, to ask alms of them that entered into the temple;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> who seeing Peter and John about to go into the temple, asked an alms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 14:9–10; John 11:40; Luke 4:20"> And Peter, fastening his eyes upon him, with John, said, Look on us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And he gave heed unto them, expecting to receive something from them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 3:16; Acts 10:38; Acts 4:10"> But Peter said, Silver and gold have I none; but what I have, that give I thee. In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, arise and walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Mark 1:31; Mark 9:27; Acts 9:41"> And he took him by the right hand, and raised him up: and immediately his feet and his anklebones received strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 14:10; Isaiah 35:6; Psalms 107:20–22"> And leaping up, he stood, and began to walk; and he entered with them into the temple, walking, and leaping, and praising God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:21; Acts 4:16; Luke 13:17"> And all the people saw him walking and praising God:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 3:2; John 5:20; John 9:3"> and they took knowledge of him, that it was he that sat for alms at the Beautiful Gate of the temple; and they were filled with wonder and amazement at that which had happened unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:12; John 10:23; Luke 8:38"> And as the lame man who was healed held Peter and John, all the people ran together unto them in the porch that is called Solomon’s, greatly wondering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; John 3:27–28; Daniel 2:28–30"> And when Peter saw it, he answered unto the people, Ye men of Israel, why marvel ye at this man? or why fasten ye your eyes on us, as though by our own power or godliness we had made him to walk?</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Matthew 22:32; Acts 7:32; Matthew 27:17–25"> The God of Abraham, and of Isaac, and of Jacob, the God of our fathers, hath glorified his Servant Jesus; whom ye indeed delivered up, and denied him before the face of Pilate, when he had determined to release him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Mark 1:24; Acts 7:52; 1 Peter 3:18"> But ye denied the Holy and Righteous One, and asked for a murderer to be granted unto you,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Acts 2:24; Revelation 21:6; Acts 2:32"> and killed the Prince of life; whom God raised from the dead; whereof we are witnesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Matthew 21:21–22; Acts 4:10; Acts 14:9"> And by faith in his name hath his name made this man strong, whom ye behold and know: yea, the faith which is through him hath given him this perfect soundness in the presence of you all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Luke 23:34; Acts 13:27; John 16:3"> And now, brethren, I know that in ignorance ye did it, as did also your rulers.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Isaiah 53:1–12; Acts 26:22–23; Acts 2:23"> But the things which God foreshowed by the mouth of all his prophets, that the Christ should suffer, he thus fulfilled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 2:38; Isaiah 44:22; Isaiah 43:25"> Repent ye therefore, and turn again, that your sins may be blotted out, that so there may come seasons of refreshing from the presence of the Lord;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Matthew 24:30–36; Revelation 19:11–16; 2 Thessalonians 2:2"> and that he may send the Christ who hath been appointed for you, even Jesus:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Romans 8:21; Isaiah 1:26; Acts 1:11"> whom the heaven must receive until the times of restoration of all things, whereof God spake by the mouth of all his holy prophets that have been from of old.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:15–19; Acts 7:37; Galatians 4:4"> For Moses indeed said unto the fathers, A prophet shall the Lord our God raise up unto you from among your brethren, like unto me; to him shall ye hearken in all things whatsoever he shall speak unto you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Deuteronomy 18:19; John 12:48; Hebrews 10:28–30"> And it shall be, that every soul that shall not hearken to that prophet, shall be utterly destroyed from among the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 3:21; Luke 24:27; 1 Samuel 3:20"> Yea and all the prophets from Samuel and them that followed after, as many as have spoken, they also told of these days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Genesis 22:18; Galatians 3:8; Acts 2:39"> Ye are sons of the prophets, and of the covenant which God made with our fathers, saying unto Abraham, And in thy seed shall all the families of the earth be blessed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Matthew 1:21; Acts 3:22; Jude 1:24"> Unto you first God, having raised up his Servant Jesus, sent him to bless you, in turning away every one of you from your iniquities.</span>
