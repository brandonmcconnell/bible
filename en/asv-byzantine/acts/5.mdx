<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–3; 2 Timothy 2:20; Matthew 13:47–48"> But a certain man named Ananias, with Sapphira his wife, sold a possession,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 4:37; 2 Peter 2:14–15; Matthew 23:5"> and kept back part of the price, his wife also being privy to it, and brought a certain part, and laid it at the apostles’ feet.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 23:24; Isaiah 29:15; John 13:2"> But Peter said, Ananias, why hath Satan filled thy heart to lie to the Holy Spirit, and to keep back for thyself part of the price of the land?</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Acts 5:3; Joshua 7:25–26; 2 Kings 5:25–27"> While it remained, did it not remain thine own? and after it was sold, was it not in thy power? How is it that thou hast conceived this thing in thy heart? thou hast not lied unto men, but unto God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Acts 5:10–11; Acts 2:43; Acts 5:13"> And Ananias hearing these words fell down and gave up the ghost: and great fear came upon all that heard these things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="John 19:40; 2 Samuel 18:17; Deuteronomy 21:23"> And the young men arose and wrapped him round, and they carried him out and buried him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And it was about the space of three hours after, when his wife, not knowing what was done, came in.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 5:2"> And Peter answered unto her, Tell me whether ye sold the land for so much. And she said, Yea, for so much.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:9; Acts 5:3–4; Psalms 95:8–11"> But Peter said unto her, How is it that ye have agreed together to try the Spirit of the Lord? behold, the feet of them that have buried thy husband are at the door, and they shall carry thee out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:5"> And she fell down immediately at his feet, and gave up the ghost: and the young men came in and found her dead, and they carried her out and buried her by her husband.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Acts 5:5; 1 Corinthians 10:11–12; Acts 19:17"> And great fear came upon the whole church, and upon all that heard these things.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 19:11; 2 Corinthians 12:12; Hebrews 2:4"> And by the hands of the apostles were many signs and wonders wrought among the people: and they were all with one accord in Solomon’s porch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 2:47; Acts 4:21; John 12:42"> But of the rest durst no man join himself to them: howbeit the people magnified them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Acts 2:47; Acts 4:4; Isaiah 55:11–13"> and believers were the more added to the Lord, multitudes both of men and women:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 14:36; Acts 19:11–12; John 14:12"> insomuch that they carried out the sick into the streets, and laid them on beds and couches, that, as Peter came by, at the least his shadow might overshadow some one of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Mark 16:17–18; Mark 2:3–4; Matthew 15:30–31"> And there also came together the multitude from the cities round about unto Jerusalem, bringing sick folk, and them that were vexed with unclean spirits: and they were healed every one.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 13:45; Acts 7:9; Acts 17:5"> But the high priest rose up, and all they that were with him (which is the sect of the Sadducees), and they were filled with jealousy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 4:3; Luke 21:12; 2 Corinthians 11:23"> and laid their hands on the apostles, and put them in public ward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 16:26; Psalms 34:7; Acts 12:7–11"> But an angel of the Lord by night opened the prison doors, and brought them out, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="John 6:63; John 6:68; 1 John 5:11–12"> Go ye, and stand and speak in the temple to the people all the words of this Life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="John 8:2; Acts 22:15; Acts 12:18–19"> And when they heard this, they entered into the temple about daybreak, and taught. But the high priest came, and they that were with him, and called the council together, and all the senate of the children of Israel, and sent to the prison house to have them brought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> But the officers that came found them not in the prison; and they returned, and told,</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 2:4; Psalms 33:10; Daniel 6:22–24"> saying, The prison house truly we found shut in all safety, and the keepers standing before the doors: but when we had opened, we found no man within.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 4:1; Acts 5:26; John 12:19"> Now when the priest and the captain of the temple and the chief priests heard these words, they were much perplexed concerning them whereunto this would grow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And there came one and told them, Behold, the men whom ye put in the prison are in the temple standing and teaching the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 5:13; Acts 4:21; Matthew 26:5"> Then went the captain with the officers, and brought them, but without violence; for they feared the people, lest they should be stoned.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Matthew 5:22; Acts 4:7; Luke 22:66"> And when they had brought them, they set them before the council. And the high priest asked them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Matthew 27:25; Acts 7:52; Acts 4:10–11"> saying, Did we not strictly charge you not to teach in this name? and behold, ye have filled Jerusalem with your teaching, and intend to bring this man’s blood upon us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Acts 4:19; 1 Samuel 15:24; Mark 7:7–9"> But Peter and the apostles answered and said, We must obey God rather than men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Galatians 3:13; 1 Peter 2:24; Acts 10:39"> The God of our fathers raised up Jesus, whom ye slew, hanging him on a tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Luke 24:47; Acts 2:33; Hebrews 2:10"> Him did God exalt with his right hand to be a Prince and a Savior, to give repentance to Israel, and remission of sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="John 15:26–27; Hebrews 2:3–4; Acts 1:8"> And we are his witnesses of these things; and so is also the Holy Spirit, whom God hath given to them that obey him.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 7:54; Acts 2:37; Luke 4:28–29"> But they, when they heard this, were cut to the heart, and took counsel to slay them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Acts 22:3; Luke 2:46; Luke 5:17"> But there stood up one in the council, a Pharisee, named Gamaliel, a doctor of the law, had in honor of all the people, and commanded to put the apostles forth a little while.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Acts 22:26; Jeremiah 26:19; Matthew 27:19"> And he said unto them, Ye men of Israel, take heed to yourselves as touching these men, what ye are about to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Acts 8:9; 2 Peter 2:2; Galatians 6:3"> For before these days rose up Theudas, giving himself out to be somebody; to whom were called a number of men, about four hundred: who was slain; and all, as many as obeyed him, were dispersed, and came to nought.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Luke 2:1–2; Psalms 7:14–15; Matthew 26:52"> After this man rose up Judas of Galilee in the days of the enrollment, and drew away much people after him: he also perished; and all, as many as obeyed him, were scattered abroad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Matthew 15:13; Lamentations 3:37; Isaiah 8:9–10"> And now I say unto you, Refrain from these men, and let them alone: for if this counsel or this work be of men, it will be overthrown:</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Proverbs 21:30; Isaiah 46:10; Matthew 16:18"> but if it is of God, ye cannot overthrow it; lest haply ye be found even to be fighting against God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Matthew 10:17; Acts 5:28; Acts 4:17–21"> And to him they agreed: and when they had called the apostles unto them, they beat them and charged them not to speak in the name of Jesus, and let them go.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="James 1:2; 1 Peter 4:13–16; Luke 6:22"> They therefore departed from the presence of the council, rejoicing that they were counted worthy to suffer dishonor for the Name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Acts 2:46; Acts 20:20; Luke 21:37"> And every day, in the temple and at home, they ceased not to teach and to preach Jesus as the Christ.</span>
</div>
