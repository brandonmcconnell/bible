<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 24:9; John 15:20; Matthew 10:17–18"> Now about that time Herod the king put forth his hands to afflict certain of the church.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 11:37; Matthew 20:23; Matthew 4:21–22"> And he killed James the brother of John with the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 23:15; John 12:43; Acts 24:27"> And when he saw that it pleased the Jews, he proceeded to seize Peter also. And those were the days of unleavened bread.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 19:21; Luke 22:33; Luke 21:12"> And when he had taken him, he put him in prison, and delivered him to four quaternions of soldiers to guard him; intending after the Passover to bring him forth to the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Corinthians 12:26; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; James 5:16"> Peter therefore was kept in the prison: but earnest prayer was made of the church unto God for him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Acts 21:33; Hebrews 13:6; Philippians 4:6–7"> And when Herod was about to bring him forth, the same night Peter was sleeping between two soldiers, bound with two chains: and guards before the door kept the prison.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Acts 16:26; Psalms 146:7"> And behold, an angel of the Lord stood by him, and a light shined in the cell: and he smote Peter on the side, and awoke him, saying, Rise up quickly. And his chains fell off from his hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the angel said unto him, Gird thyself, and bind on thy sandals. And he did so. And he saith unto him, Cast thy garment about thee, and follow me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Psalms 126:1; Hebrews 11:8; Genesis 45:26"> And he went out, and followed him; and he knew not that it was true which was done by the angel, but thought he saw a vision.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Acts 5:19; Acts 16:26; Isaiah 45:1–2"> And when they were past the first and the second guard, they came unto the iron gate that leadeth into the city; which opened to them of its own accord: and they went out, and passed on through one street; and straightaway the angel departed from him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Daniel 6:22; Daniel 3:28; 2 Peter 2:9"> And when Peter was come to himself, he said, Now I know of a truth, that the Lord hath sent forth his angel and delivered me out of the hand of Herod, and from all the expectation of the people of the Jews.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 John 5:14–15; Acts 12:5; Matthew 18:19–20"> And when he had considered the thing, he came to the house of Mary the mother of John whose surname was Mark; where many were gathered together and were praying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Acts 12:16; Luke 13:25; John 18:16–17"> And when Peter knocked at the door of the gate, a maid came to answer, named Rhoda.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Luke 24:41; Matthew 28:8"> And when she knew Peter’s voice, she opened not the gate for joy, but ran in, and told that Peter stood before the gate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 18:10; Luke 24:11; Mark 16:14"> And they said unto her, Thou art mad. But she confidently affirmed that it was even so. And they said, It is his angel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> But Peter continued knocking: and when they had opened, they saw him, and were amazed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Acts 21:40; Acts 13:16; Acts 19:33"> But he, beckoning unto them with the hand to hold their peace, declared unto them how the Lord had brought him forth out of the prison. And he said, Tell these things unto James, and to the brethren. And he departed, and went to another place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Acts 5:22–25; Acts 16:27; Acts 19:23"> Now as soon as it was day, there was no small stir among the soldiers, what was become of Peter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Acts 16:27; Psalms 37:32–33; John 12:10–11"> And when Herod had sought for him, and found him not, he examined the guards, and commanded that they should be put to death. And he went down from Judea to Caesarea, and tarried there.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezra 3:7; Matthew 11:21–22; Ezekiel 27:17"> Now Herod was highly displeased with them of Tyre and Sidon: and they came with one accord to him, and, having made Blastus the king’s chamberlain their friend, they asked for peace, because their country was fed from the king’s country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And upon a set day Herod arrayed himself in royal apparel, and sat on the throne, and made an oration unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Acts 14:10–13; Revelation 13:4; Jude 1:16"> And the people shouted, saying, The voice of a god, and not of a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Psalms 115:1; Acts 10:25–26; 2 Thessalonians 2:4"> And immediately an angel of the Lord smote him, because he gave not glory to God: and he was eaten of worms, and gave up the ghost.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Acts 6:7; Acts 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 3:1"> But the word of God grew and multiplied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 12:12; 1 Peter 5:13; Acts 15:37"> And Barnabas and Saul returned to Jerusalem, when they had fulfilled their ministration, taking also with them John whose surname was Mark.</span>
