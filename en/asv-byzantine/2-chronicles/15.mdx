<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 20:14; Numbers 24:2"> And the Spirit of God came upon Azariah the son of Oded:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="James 4:8; 2 Chronicles 24:20; 2 Chronicles 15:4"> and he went out to meet Asa, and said unto him, Hear ye me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin: Jehovah is with you, while ye are with him; and if ye seek him, he will be found of you; but if ye forsake him, he will forsake you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Nehemiah 8:9; Leviticus 10:11; 2 Chronicles 17:8–9"> Now for a long season Israel was without the true God, and without a teaching priest, and without law:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 55:6; Deuteronomy 4:29–30; 2 Chronicles 15:15"> but when in their distress they turned unto Jehovah, the God of Israel, and sought him, he was found of them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Judges 5:6; Psalms 121:8; 1 Samuel 13:6"> And in those times there was no peace to him that went out, nor to him that came in; but great vexations were upon all the inhabitants of the lands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Luke 21:22–24; Matthew 24:7; 2 Chronicles 33:11"> And they were broken in pieces, nation against nation, and city against city; for God did vex them with all adversity.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:58; Joshua 1:7; Joshua 1:9"> But be ye strong, and let not your hands be slack; for your work shall be rewarded.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Chronicles 8:12; 2 Chronicles 13:19; 2 Chronicles 4:1"> And when Asa heard these words, and the prophecy of Oded the prophet, he took courage, and put away the abominations out of all the land of Judah and Benjamin, and out of the cities which he had taken from the hill country of Ephraim; and he renewed the altar of Jehovah, that was before the porch of Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Chronicles 11:16; Acts 9:31; Zechariah 8:21–23"> And he gathered all Judah and Benjamin, and them that sojourned with them out of Ephraim and Manasseh, and out of Simeon: for they fell to him out of Israel in abundance, when they saw that Jehovah his God was with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Esther 8:9"> So they gathered themselves together at Jerusalem in the third month, in the fifteenth year of the reign of Asa.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:13–15; Numbers 31:28–29; Numbers 31:50"> And they sacrificed unto Jehovah in that day, of the spoil which they had brought, seven hundred oxen and seven thousand sheep.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Acts 24:14; 2 Chronicles 23:16; Deuteronomy 10:12"> And they entered into the covenant to seek Jehovah, the God of their fathers, with all their heart and with all their soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 22:20; Psalms 115:13; 1 Kings 18:40"> and that whosoever would not seek Jehovah, the God of Israel, should be put to death, whether small or great, whether man or woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Nehemiah 5:13; Psalms 81:1–4; Nehemiah 10:29"> And they sware unto Jehovah with a loud voice, and with shouting, and with trumpets, and with cornets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Chronicles 14:7; 2 Chronicles 15:12; Isaiah 26:8"> And all Judah rejoiced at the oath; for they had sworn with all their heart, and sought him with their whole desire; and he was found of them: and Jehovah gave them rest round about.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 15:13–24; 1 Kings 15:10; 2 Kings 23:15"> And also Maacah, the mother of Asa the king, he removed from being queen, because she had made an abominable image for an Asherah; and Asa cut down her image, and made dust of it, and burnt it at the brook Kidron.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Kings 14:4; 2 Kings 12:3; 1 Kings 11:4"> But the high places were not taken away out of Israel: nevertheless the heart of Asa was perfect all his days.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Kings 7:51; 1 Chronicles 26:20–26; 1 Kings 15:14–15"> And he brought into the house of God the things that his father had dedicated, and that he himself had dedicated, silver, and gold, and vessels.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Kings 15:31; 1 Kings 15:33; 1 Kings 15:16–17"> And there was no more war unto the five and thirtieth year of the reign of Asa.</span>
</div>
