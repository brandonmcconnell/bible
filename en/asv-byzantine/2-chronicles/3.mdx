<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Kings 6:1"> And he began to build in the second day of the second month, in the fourth year of his reign.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Kings 6:2–3; 1 Chronicles 28:11–19"> Now these are the foundations which Solomon laid for the building of the house of God. The length by cubits after the first measure was threescore cubits, and the breadth twenty cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="John 10:23; Acts 5:12; Acts 3:11"> And the porch that was before the house, the length of it, according to the breadth of the house, was twenty cubits, and the height a hundred and twenty; and he overlaid it within with pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="1 Kings 6:15–17; 1 Kings 6:21–22"> And the greater house he ceiled with fir wood, which he overlaid with fine gold, and wrought thereon palm trees and chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 54:11–12; 1 Chronicles 29:8; Revelation 21:18–21"> And he garnished the house with precious stones for beauty: and the gold was gold of Parvaim.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="1 Kings 6:20–22; Exodus 26:29; Ezekiel 7:20"> He overlaid also the house, the beams, the thresholds, and the walls thereof, and the doors thereof, with gold; and graved cherubim on the walls.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 26:33; 1 Kings 6:19–20; 1 Kings 6:16"> And he made the most holy house: the length thereof, according to the breadth of the house, was twenty cubits, and the breadth thereof twenty cubits; and he overlaid it with fine gold, amounting to six hundred talents.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Chronicles 28:11"> And the weight of the nails was fifty shekels of gold. And he overlaid the upper chambers with gold.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Kings 6:23–28; Exodus 25:18"> And in the most holy house he made two cherubim of image work; and they overlaid them with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And the wings of the cherubim were twenty cubits long: the wing of the one cherub was five cubits, reaching to the wall of the house; and the other wing was likewise five cubits, reaching to the wing of the other cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the wing of the other cherub was five cubits, reaching to the wall of the house; and the other wing was five cubits also, joining to the wing of the other cherub.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:20"> The wings of these cherubim spread themselves forth twenty cubits: and they stood on their feet, and their faces were toward the house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:3; Hebrews 10:20; Matthew 27:51"> And he made the veil of blue, and purple, and crimson, and fine linen, and wrought cherubim thereon.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Kings 7:15–24; Jeremiah 52:20–23"> Also he made before the house two pillars of thirty and five cubits high, and the capital that was on the top of each of them was five cubits.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Kings 7:20; 1 Kings 6:21"> And he made chains in the oracle, and put them on the tops of the pillars; and he made a hundred pomegranates, and put them on the chains.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Kings 7:21"> And he set up the pillars before the temple, one on the right hand, and the other on the left; and called the name of that on the right hand Jachin, and the name of that on the left Boaz.</span>
