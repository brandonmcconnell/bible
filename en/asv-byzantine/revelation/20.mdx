<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 1:18; 2 Peter 2:4; Jude 1:6"> And I saw an angel coming down out of heaven, having the key of the abyss and a great chain in his hand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Revelation 12:9; Isaiah 27:1"> And he laid hold on the dragon, the old serpent, which is the Devil and Satan, who deceiveth the whole world, and bound him for a thousand years,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Revelation 20:1; Revelation 17:8; 2 Thessalonians 2:9–11"> and cast him into the abyss, and shut it, and sealed it over him, that he should deceive the nations no more, until the thousand years should be finished: and after this he must be loosed for a little time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Daniel 7:27; Daniel 7:18; Matthew 19:28"> And I saw thrones, and they sat upon them, and judgment was given unto them: and I saw the souls of them that had been beheaded for the testimony of Jesus, and for the word of God, and such as worshiped not the beast, neither his image, and received not the mark upon their forehead and upon their hand; and they lived, and reigned with Christ for the thousand years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Philippians 3:11; Revelation 11:15; Luke 14:14"> And the rest of the dead lived not until the thousand years should be finished. This is the first resurrection.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 2:11; Revelation 1:6; 2 Timothy 2:12"> Blessed and holy is he that hath part in the first resurrection: over these the second death hath no power; but they shall be priests of God and of Christ, and shall reign with him a thousand years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Revelation 20:2"> And when the thousand years are finished, Satan shall be loosed out of his prison,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Revelation 16:14; Ezekiel 38:1; Revelation 20:10"> and shall come forth to deceive the nations which are in the four corners of the earth, Gog and Magog, to gather them together to the war: the number of whom is as the sand of the sea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 38:16; Ezekiel 39:6; Ezekiel 38:9"> And they went up over the breadth of the earth, and compassed the camp of the saints about, and the beloved city: and fire came down out of heaven from God, and devoured them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Matthew 25:41; Revelation 19:20; Matthew 25:46"> And the devil that deceived them was cast into the lake of fire and brimstone, where are also the beast and the false prophet; and they shall be tormented day and night forever and ever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 21:1; 2 Peter 3:7; Matthew 25:31"> And I saw a great white throne, and him that sat upon it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled away; and there was found no place for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 5:10; Matthew 16:27; Daniel 7:10"> And I saw the dead, the great and the small, standing before the throne; and books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life: and the dead were judged out of the things which were written in the books, according to their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="John 5:28–29; Revelation 6:8; Hosea 13:14"> And the sea gave up the dead that were in it; and death and Hades gave up the dead that were in them: and they were judged every man according to their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="1 Corinthians 15:26; Revelation 20:6; Revelation 21:8"> And death and Hades were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death, even the lake of fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="John 3:36; Matthew 13:50; Matthew 25:41"> And if any was not found written in the book of life, he was cast into the lake of fire.</span>
