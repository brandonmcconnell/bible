<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Revelation 22:16; Revelation 22:6; Galatians 1:12"> The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave him to show unto his servants, even the things which must shortly come to pass: and he sent and signified it by his angel unto his servant John;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:6; John 19:35; 1 John 1:1"> who bare witness of the word of God, and of the testimony of Jesus Christ, even of all things that he saw.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Luke 11:28; Revelation 22:10; Romans 13:11"> Blessed is he that readeth, and they that hear the words of the prophecy, and keep the things that are written therein: for the time is at hand.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 11:2; John 1:1; Hebrews 13:8"> John to the seven churches that are in Asia: Grace to you and peace from God, who is and who was and who is to come; and from the seven Spirits that are before his throne;</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Psalms 89:27; Hebrews 9:14; 1 John 1:7"> and from Jesus Christ, who is the faithful witness, the firstborn of the dead, and the ruler of the kings of the earth. Unto him that loveth us, and washed us from our sins by his blood;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Revelation 5:10; Revelation 20:6; Exodus 19:6"> and he made us to be a kingdom, to be priests unto his God and Father; to him be the glory and the dominion forever and ever. Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Matthew 24:30; Zechariah 12:10; Daniel 7:13"> Behold, he cometh with the clouds; and every eye shall see him, and they that pierced him; and all the tribes of the earth shall mourn over him. Even so, Amen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Revelation 22:13; Isaiah 43:10"> I am the Alpha and the Omega, saith the Lord God, who is and who was and who is to come, the Almighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 14:22; Revelation 3:10; John 16:33"> I John, your brother and partaker in the tribulation and kingdom and patience which are in Christ Jesus, was in the isle that is called Patmos, for the word of God, and for the testimony of Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Revelation 21:10; 2 Corinthians 12:2–4; Revelation 4:1–2"> I was in the Spirit on the Lord’s day, and I heard behind me a great voice, as of a trumpet</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Revelation 3:1; Jeremiah 30:2; Habakkuk 2:2"> saying, What thou seest, write in a book and send it to the seven churches: unto Ephesus, and unto Smyrna, and unto Pergamum, and unto Thyatira, and unto Sardis, and unto Philadelphia, and unto Laodicea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Zechariah 4:2; Exodus 25:37; Revelation 1:20–1"> And there I turned to see the voice that spake with me. And having turned I saw seven golden candlesticks;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Daniel 7:13; Daniel 10:5–6; Daniel 10:16"> and in the midst of the seven candlesticks one like unto a son of man, clothed with a garment down to the foot, and girt about at the breasts with a golden girdle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Daniel 10:6; Daniel 7:9; Revelation 19:12"> And his head and his hair were white as white wool, white as snow; and his eyes were as a flame of fire;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ezekiel 43:2; Daniel 10:6; Revelation 14:2"> and his feet like unto burnished brass, as if they had been refined in a furnace; and his voice as the voice of many waters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 4:12; Revelation 19:15; Revelation 2:12"> And he had in his right hand seven stars: and out of his mouth proceeded a sharp two-edged sword: and his countenance was as the sun shineth in his strength.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 44:6; Revelation 22:13; Daniel 10:8–10"> And when I saw him, I fell at his feet as one dead. And he laid his right hand upon me, saying, Fear not; I am the first and the last,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Romans 6:9; Hebrews 7:25; Romans 14:8–9"> and the Living one; and I was dead, and behold, I am alive forevermore, Amen; and I have the keys of death and of Hades.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Revelation 4:1–11; Revelation 1:11–3"> Write therefore the things which thou sawest, and the things which are, and the things which shall come to pass hereafter;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Revelation 2:1; Revelation 3:1; Revelation 1:16"> the mystery of the seven stars which thou sawest in my right hand, and the seven golden candlesticks. The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches: and the seven candlesticks are seven churches.</span>
</div>
