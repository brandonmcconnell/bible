<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Joshua 15:10; Joshua 19:43; 1 John 2:16"> And Samson went down to Timnah, and saw a woman in Timnah of the daughters of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Genesis 34:4; Genesis 21:21; Genesis 24:2–3"> And he came up, and told his father and his mother, and said, I have seen a woman in Timnah of the daughters of the Philistines: now therefore get her for me to wife.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 15:18; 1 Samuel 17:36; 1 Samuel 14:6"> Then his father and his mother said unto him, Is there never a woman among the daughters of thy brethren, or among all my people, that thou goest to take a wife of the uncircumcised Philistines? And Samson said unto his father, Get her for me; for she pleaseth me well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Joshua 11:20; Psalms 115:3; Judges 13:1"> But his father and his mother knew not that it was of Jehovah; for he sought an occasion against the Philistines. Now at that time the Philistines had rule over Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Then went Samson down, and his father and his mother, to Timnah, and came to the vineyards of Timnah: and, behold, a young lion roared against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25; 1 Samuel 11:6"> And the Spirit of Jehovah came mightily upon him, and he rent him as he would have rent a kid; and he had nothing in his hand: but he told not his father or his mother what he had done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And he went down, and talked with the woman, and she pleased Samson well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Matthew 1:20; Genesis 29:21"> And after a while he returned to take her; and he turned aside to see the carcass of the lion: and, behold, there was a swarm of bees in the body of the lion, and honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="1 Samuel 14:25–30; Proverbs 25:15"> And he took it into his hands, and went on, eating as he went; and he came to his father and mother, and gave unto them, and they did eat: but he told them not that he had taken the honey out of the body of the lion.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Genesis 29:22; Revelation 19:9; Esther 1:7–22"> And his father went down unto the woman: and Samson made there a feast; for so used the young men to do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Matthew 9:15; 1 Samuel 16:6; 1 Samuel 10:23"> And it came to pass, when they saw him, that they brought thirty companions to be with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Kings 10:1; Ezekiel 17:2; Genesis 45:22"> And Samson said unto them, Let me now put forth a riddle unto you: if ye can declare it unto me within the seven days of the feast, and find it out, then I will give you thirty linen garments and thirty changes of raiment;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> but if ye cannot declare it unto me, then shall ye give me thirty linen garments and thirty changes of raiment. And they said unto him, Put forth thy riddle, that we may hear it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="James 1:2–4; 1 Kings 17:6; Romans 8:37"> And he said unto them,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Out of the eater came forth food,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And out of the strong came forth sweetness.</span><br />And they could not in three days declare the riddle.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp poetry">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Judges 16:5; Judges 15:6; Proverbs 6:26"> And it came to pass on the seventh day, that they said unto Samson’s wife, Entice thy husband, that he may declare unto us the riddle, lest we burn thee and thy father’s house with fire: have ye called us to impoverish us? is it not so?</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Judges 16:15; Genesis 2:24"> And Samson’s wife wept before him, and said, Thou dost but hate me, and lovest me not: thou hast put forth a riddle unto the children of my people, and hast not told it me. And he said unto her, Behold, I have not told it my father nor my mother, and shall I tell thee?</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Judges 16:16; Job 2:9; Judges 16:13"> And she wept before him the seven days, while their feast lasted: and it came to pass on the seventh day, that he told her, because she pressed him sore; and she told the riddle to the children of her people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the men of the city said unto him on the seventh day before the sun went down, What is sweeter than honey? and what is stronger than a lion? And he said unto them,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">If ye had not plowed with my heifer,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">Ye had not found out my riddle.</span></span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Samuel 11:6; Judges 3:10; Judges 13:25"> And the Spirit of Jehovah came mightily upon him, and he went down to Ashkelon, and smote thirty men of them, and took their spoil, and gave the changes of raiment unto them that declared the riddle. And his anger was kindled, and he went up to his father’s house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Judges 15:2; John 3:29; Psalms 55:12–13"> But Samson’s wife was given to his companion, whom he had used as his friend.</span>
