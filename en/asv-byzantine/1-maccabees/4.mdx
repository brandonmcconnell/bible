<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And Gorgias took five thousand footmen, and a thousand chosen horse, and the army removed by night,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> that it might fall upon the army of the Jews and smite them suddenly: and the men of the citadel were his guides.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And Judas heard thereof, and removed, he and the valiant men, that he might smite the king’s host which was at Emmaus,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> while as yet the forces were dispersed from the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And Gorgias came into the camp of Judas by night, and found no man; and he sought them in the mountains; for he said, These men flee from us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And as soon as it was day, Judas appeared in the plain with three thousand men: howbeit they had not armor nor swords as they desired.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And they saw the camp of the Gentiles strong and fortified, and horsemen compassing it round about; and these were expert in war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And Judas said to the men that were with him, Fear ye not their multitude, neither be ye afraid of their onset.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Remember how our fathers were saved in the Red Sea, when Pharaoh pursued them with a host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And now let us cry unto heaven, if he will have us, and will remember the covenant of our fathers, and destroy this army before our face today:</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and all the Gentiles shall know that there is one who redeemeth and saveth Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the strangers lifted up their eyes, and saw them coming over against them:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and they went out of the camp to battle. And they that were with Judas sounded their trumpets,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and joined battle, and the Gentiles were discomfited, and fled into the plain.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> But all the hindmost fell by the sword: and they pursued them unto Gazara, and unto the plains of Idumea and Azotus and Jamnia, and there fell of them about three thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And Judas and his host returned from pursuing after them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> and he said unto the people, Be not greedy of the spoils, inasmuch as there is a battle before us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and Gorgias and his host are nigh unto us in the mountain. But stand ye now against our enemies, and fight against them, and afterward take the spoils with boldness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> While Judas was yet making an end of these words, there appeared a part of them looking out from the mountain:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and they saw that their host had been put to flight, and that the Jews were burning the camp; for the smoke that was seen declared what was done.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> But when they perceived these things, they were sore afraid; and perceiving also the army of Judas in the plain ready for battle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> they fled all of them into the land of the Philistines.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Judas returned to spoil the camp, and they gat much gold, and silver, and blue, and sea purple, and great riches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And they returned home, and sang a song of thanksgiving, and gave praise unto heaven; because his mercy is good, because his mercy endureth forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And Israel had a great deliverance that day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> But the strangers, as many as had escaped, came and told Lysias all the things that had happened:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> but when he heard thereof, he was confounded and discouraged, because neither had such things as he would been done unto Israel, nor had such things as the king commanded him come to pass.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And in the next year he gathered together threescore thousand chosen footmen, and five thousand horse, that he might subdue them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And they came into Idumea, and encamped at Beth-zur; and Judas met them with ten thousand men.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And he saw that the army was strong, and he prayed and said, Blessed art thou, O Savior of Israel, who didst quell the onset of the mighty man by the hand of thy servant David, and didst deliver the army of the Philistines into the hands of Jonathan the son of Saul, and of his armor-bearer:</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> shut up this army in the hand of thy people Israel, and let them be ashamed for their host and their horsemen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> give them faintness of heart, and cause the boldness of their strength to melt away, and let them quake at their destruction:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> cast them down with the sword of them that love thee, and let all that know thy name praise thee with thanksgiving.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And they joined battle; and there fell of the army of Lysias about five thousand men, and they fell down over against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> But when Lysias saw that his array was put to flight, and the boldness that had come upon them that were with Judas, and how they were ready either to live or to die nobly, he removed to Antioch, and gathered together hired soldiers, that he might come again into Judea with even a greater company.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> But Judas and his brethren said, Behold, our enemies are discomfited: let us go up to cleanse the holy place, and to dedicate it afresh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And all the army was gathered together, and they went up unto mount Zion.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And they saw the sanctuary laid desolate, and the altar profaned, and the gates burned up, and shrubs growing in the courts as in a forest or as on one of the mountains, and the priests’ chambers pulled down;</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> and they rent their clothes, and made great lamentation, and put ashes upon their heads,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and fell on their faces to the ground, and blew with the solemn trumpets, and cried toward heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Then Judas appointed certain men to fight against those that were in the citadel, until he should have cleansed the holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And he chose blameless priests, such as had pleasure in the law:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> and they cleansed the holy place, and bare out the stones of defilement into an unclean place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And they took counsel concerning the altar of burnt offerings, which had been profaned, what they should do with it:</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> and there came into their mind a good counsel, that they should pull it down, lest it should be a reproach to them, because the Gentiles had defiled it: and they pulled down the altar,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and laid up the stones in the mountain of the house in a convenient place, until there should come a prophet to give an answer concerning them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And they took whole stones according to the law, and built a new altar after the fashion of the former;</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> and they built the holy place, and the inner parts of the house; and they hallowed the courts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And they made the holy vessels new, and they brought the candlestick, and the altar of burnt offerings and of incense, and the table, into the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And they burned incense upon the altar, and they lighted the lamps that were upon the candlestick, and they gave light in the temple.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> And they set loaves upon the table, and spread out the veils, and finished all the works which they made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And they rose up early in the morning, on the five and twentieth day of the ninth month, which is the month Chislev, in the hundred and forty and eighth year,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> and offered sacrifice according to the law upon the new altar of burnt offerings which they had made.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> At what time and on what day the Gentiles had profaned it, even on that day was it dedicated afresh, with songs and harps and lutes, and with cymbals.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> And all the people fell upon their faces, and worshiped, and gave praise unto heaven, which had given them good success.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And they kept the dedication of the altar eight days, and offered burnt offerings with gladness, and sacrificed a sacrifice of deliverance and praise.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> And they decked the forefront of the temple with crowns of gold and small shields, and dedicated afresh the gates and the priests’ chambers, and made doors for them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> And there was exceeding great gladness among the people, and the reproach of the Gentiles was turned away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And Judas and his brethren and the whole congregation of Israel ordained, that the days of the dedication of the altar should be kept in their seasons from year to year by the space of eight days, from the five and twentieth day of the month Chislev, with gladness and joy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> And at that season they built up the mount Zion with high walls and strong towers round about, lest haply the Gentiles should come and tread them down, as they had done aforetime.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And he set there a force to keep it, and they fortified Beth-zur to keep it; that the people might have a stronghold over against Idumea.</span>
