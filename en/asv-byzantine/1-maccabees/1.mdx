<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And it came to pass, after that Alexander the Macedonian, the son of Philip, who came out of the land of Kittim, and smote Darius king of the Persians and Medes, it came to pass, after he had smitten him, that he reigned in his stead, in former time, over Greece.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And he fought many battles, and won many strongholds, and slew the kings of the earth,</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and went through to the ends of the earth, and took spoils of a multitude of nations. And the earth was quiet before him, and he was exalted, and his heart was lifted up,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> and he gathered together an exceeding strong host, and ruled over countries and nations and principalities, and they became tributary unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And after these things he fell sick, and perceived that he should die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And he called his servants, who were honorable, who had been brought up with him from his youth, and he divided unto them his kingdom, while he was yet alive.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And Alexander reigned twelve years, and he died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And his servants bare rule, each one in his place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And they did all put diadems upon themselves after that he was dead, and so did their sons after them many years: and they multiplied evils in the earth.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And there came forth out of them a sinful root, Antiochus Epiphanes, son of Antiochus the king, who had been a hostage at Rome, and he reigned in the hundred and thirty and seventh year of the kingdom of the Greeks.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> In those days came there forth out of Israel transgressors of the law, and persuaded many, saying, let us go and make a covenant with the Gentiles that are round about us; for since we were parted from them many evils have befallen us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the saying was good in their eyes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And certain of the people were forward herein and went to the king, and he gave them license to do after the ordinances of the Gentiles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And they built a place of exercise in Jerusalem according to the laws of the Gentiles;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and they made themselves uncircumcised, and forsook the holy covenant, and joined themselves to the Gentiles, and sold themselves to do evil.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And the kingdom was well ordered in the sight of Antiochus, and he thought to reign over Egypt, that he might reign over the two kingdoms.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he entered into Egypt with a great multitude, with chariots, and with elephants, and with horsemen, and with a great navy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> and he made war against Ptolemy king of Egypt; and Ptolemy was put to shame before him, and fled; and many fell wounded to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And they gat possession of the strong cities in the land of Egypt; and he took the spoils of Egypt.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And Antiochus, after that he had smitten Egypt, returned in the hundred and forty and third year, and went up against Israel and Jerusalem with a great multitude,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and entered presumptuously into the sanctuary, and took the golden altar, and the candlestick of the light, and all that pertained thereto,</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> and the table of the showbread, and the cups to pour withal, and the bowls, and the golden censers, and the veil, and the crowns, and the adorning of gold which was on the face of the temple, and he scaled it all off.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And he took the silver and the gold and the precious vessels; and he took the hidden treasures which he found.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And when he had taken all, he went away into his own land, and he made a great slaughter, and spake very presumptuously.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And there came great mourning upon Israel, in every place where they were;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> and the rulers and elders groaned, the virgins and young men were made feeble, and the beauty of the women was changed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Every bridegroom took up lamentation, she that sat in the marriage chamber was in heaviness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the land was moved for the inhabitants thereof, and all the house of Jacob was clothed with shame.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And after two full years the king sent a chief collector of tribute unto the cities of Judah, and he came unto Jerusalem with a great multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And he spake words of peace unto them in subtlety, and they gave him credence: and he fell upon the city suddenly, and smote it very sore, and destroyed much people out of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And he took the spoils of the city, and set it on fire, and pulled down the houses thereof and the walls thereof on every side.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And they led captive the women and the children, and the cattle they took in possession.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And they built the city of David with a great and strong wall, with strong towers, and it became unto them a citadel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And they put there a sinful nation, transgressors of the law, and they strengthened themselves therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And they stored up arms and victuals, and gathering together the spoils of Jerusalem, they laid them up there, and they became a sore snare:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> and it became a place to lie in wait in against the sanctuary, and an evil adversary to Israel continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> And they shed innocent blood on every side of the sanctuary, and defiled the sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And the inhabitants of Jerusalem fled because of them; and she became a habitation of strangers, and she became strange to them that were born in her, and her children forsook her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Her sanctuary was laid waste like a wilderness, her feasts were turned into mourning, her Sabbaths into reproach, her honor into contempt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> According to her glory, so was her dishonor multiplied, and her high estate was turned into mourning.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And king Antiochus wrote to his whole kingdom, that all should be one people,</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> and that each should forsake his own laws. And all the nations agreed according to the word of the king;</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> and many of Israel consented to his worship, and sacrificed to the idols, and profaned the Sabbath.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And the king sent letters by the hand of messengers unto Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, that they should follow laws strange to the land,</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> and should forbid whole burnt offerings and sacrifice and drink offerings in the sanctuary; and should profane the Sabbaths and feasts,</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and pollute the sanctuary and them that were holy;</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> that they should build altars, and temples, and shrines for idols, and should sacrifice swine’s flesh and unclean beasts:</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> and that they should leave their sons uncircumcised, that they should make their souls abominable with all manner of uncleanness and profanation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> so that they might forget the law, and change all the ordinances.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And whosoever shall not do according to the word of the king, he shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> According to all these words wrote he to his whole kingdom; and he appointed overseers over all the people, and he commanded the cities of Judah to sacrifice, city by city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> And from the people were gathered together unto them many, everyone that had forsaken the law; and they did evil things in the land;</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> and they made Israel to hide themselves in every place of refuge which they had.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> And on the fifteenth day of Chislev, in the hundred and forty and fifth year, they built an abomination of desolation upon the altar, and in the cities of Judah on every side they built idol altars.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> And at the doors of the houses and in the streets they burnt incense.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> And they rent in pieces the books of the law which they found, and set them on fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> And wheresoever was found with any a book of the covenant, and if any consented to the law, the king’s sentence delivered him to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> Thus did they in their might unto Israel, to those that were found month by month in the cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> And on the five and twentieth day of the month they sacrificed upon the idol altar of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> And the women that had circumcised their children they put to death according to the commandment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And they hung their babes about their necks, and destroyed their houses, and them that had circumcised them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> And many in Israel were fully resolved and confirmed in themselves not to eat unclean things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> And they chose to die, that they might not be defiled with the meats, and that they might not profane the holy covenant: and they died.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> And there came exceeding great wrath upon Israel.</span>
</div>
</div>
