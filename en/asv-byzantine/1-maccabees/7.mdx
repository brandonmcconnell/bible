<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In the hundred and one and fiftieth year Demetrius the son of Seleucus came forth from Rome, and went up with a few men unto a city by the sea, and reigned there.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> And it came to pass, when he would go into the house of the kingdom of his fathers, that the army laid hands on Antiochus and Lysias, to bring them unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And the thing was known to him, and he said, show me not their faces.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the army slew them. And Demetrius sat upon the throne of his kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And there came unto him all the lawless and ungodly men of Israel; and Alcimus was their leader, desiring to be high priest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> and they accused the people to the king, saying, Judas and his brethren have destroyed all thy friends, and have scattered us from our own land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Now therefore send a man whom thou trustest, and let him go and see all the havoc which he hath made of us, and of the king’s country, and how he hath punished them and all that helped them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the king chose Bacchides, one of the king’s Friends, who was ruler in the country beyond the river, and was a great man in the kingdom, and faithful to the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And he sent him, and that ungodly Alcimus, and made sure to him the high priesthood, and he commanded him to take vengeance upon the children of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And they removed, and came with a great host into the land of Judah, and he sent messengers to Judas and his brethren with words of peace deceitfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And they gave no heed to their words; for they saw that they were come with a great host.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And there were gathered together unto Alcimus and Bacchides a company of scribes, to seek for justice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the Hasideans were the first among the children of Israel that sought peace of them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> for they said, One that is a priest of the seed of Aaron is come with the forces, and he will do us no wrong.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And he spake with them words of peace, and sware unto them, saying, We will seek the hurt neither of you nor your friends.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And they gave him credence: and he laid hands on threescore men of them, and slew them in one day, according to the word which the psalmist wrote,</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> <span className="pl pl-1">The flesh of thy saints did they cast out,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And their blood did they shed round about Jerusalem;</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And there was no man to bury them.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And the fear and the dread of them fell upon all the people, for they said, There is neither truth nor judgment in them; for they have broken the covenant and the oath which they sware.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And Bacchides removed from Jerusalem, and encamped in Beth-zaith; and he sent and took away many of the deserters that were with him, and certain of the people, and he slew them, and cast them into the great pit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And he made sure the country to Alcimus, and left with him a force to aid him; and Bacchides went away unto the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And Alcimus strove for his high priesthood.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And there were gathered unto him all they that troubled their people, and they gat the mastery of the land of Judah, and did great hurt in Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And Judas saw all the mischief that Alcimus and his company had done among the children of Israel, even above the Gentiles,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> and he went out into all the borders of Judea round about, and took vengeance on the men that had deserted from him, and they were restrained from going forth into the country.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> But when Alcimus saw that Judas and his company waxed strong, and knew that he was not able to withstand them, he returned to the king, and brought evil accusations against them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And the king sent Nicanor, one of his honorable princes, a man that hated Israel and was their enemy, and commanded him to destroy the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And Nicanor came to Jerusalem with a great host; and he sent unto Judas and his brethren deceitfully with words of peace, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> Let there be no battle between me and you; I will come with a few men, that I may see your faces in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> And he came to Judas, and they saluted one another peaceably. And the enemies were ready to take away Judas by violence.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> And the thing was known to Judas, to wit, that he came unto him with deceit, and he was sore afraid of him, and would see his face no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> And Nicanor knew that his counsel was discovered; and he went out to meet Judas in battle beside Capharsalama;</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> and there fell of Nicanor’s side about five hundred men, and they fled into the city of David.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And after these things Nicanor went up to mount Zion: and there came some of the priests out of the sanctuary, and some of the elders of the people, to salute him peaceably, and to show him the whole burnt sacrifice that was being offered for the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And he mocked them, and laughed at them, and treated them shamefully, and spake haughtily,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> and sware in a rage, saying, Unless Judas and his army be now delivered into my hands, it shall be that, if I come again in peace, I will burn up this house: and he went out in a great rage.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the priests entered in, and stood before the altar and the temple; and they wept, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Thou didst choose this house to be called by thy name, to be a house of prayer and supplication for thy people:</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> take vengeance on this man and his army, and let them fall by the sword: remember their blasphemies, and suffer them not to live any longer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And Nicanor went forth from Jerusalem, and encamped in Beth-horon, and there met him the host of Syria.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> And Judas encamped in Adasa with three thousand men: and Judas prayed and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> When they that came from the king blasphemed, thine angel went out, and smote among them a hundred and fourscore and five thousand.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Even so discomfit thou this army before us today, and let all the rest know that he hath spoken wickedly against thy sanctuary, and judge thou him according to his wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> And on the thirteenth day of the month Adar the armies joined battle: and Nicanor’s army was discomfited, and he himself was the first to fall in the battle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> Now when his army saw that Nicanor was fallen, they cast away their arms, and fled.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And they pursued after them a day’s journey from Adasa until thou comest to Gazara, and they sounded an alarm after them with the solemn trumpets.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And they came forth out of all the villages of Judea round about, and closed them in; and these turned them back on those, and they all fell by the sword, and there was not one of them left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> And they took the spoils, and the booty, and they smote off Nicanor’s head, and his right hand, which he stretched out so haughtily, and brought them, and hung them up beside Jerusalem.</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> And the people were exceeding glad, and they kept that day as a day of great gladness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> And they ordained to keep this day year by year, to wit, the thirteenth day of Adar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> And the land of Judah had rest a little while.</span>
