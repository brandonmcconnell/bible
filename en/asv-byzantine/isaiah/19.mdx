<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 12:12; Revelation 1:7; Joshua 2:11"> The burden of Egypt. Behold, Jehovah rideth upon a swift cloud, and cometh unto Egypt: and the idols of Egypt shall tremble at his presence; and the heart of Egypt shall melt in the midst of it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Judges 7:22; 1 Samuel 14:20; Matthew 10:36"> And I will stir up the Egyptians against the Egyptians: and they shall fight every one against his brother, and every one against his neighbor; city against city, and kingdom against kingdom.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 8:19; Daniel 2:2; 1 Chronicles 10:13"> And the spirit of Egypt shall fail in the midst of it; and I will destroy the counsel thereof: and they shall seek unto the idols, and to the charmers, and to them that have familiar spirits, and to the wizards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Isaiah 20:4; Jeremiah 46:26; Ezekiel 29:19"> And I will give over the Egyptians into the hand of a cruel lord; and a fierce king shall rule over them, saith the Lord, Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:36; Ezekiel 30:12; Zechariah 14:18"> And the waters shall fail from the sea, and the river shall be wasted and become dry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 37:25; Exodus 7:18; 2 Kings 19:24"> And the rivers shall become foul; the streams of Egypt shall be diminished and dried up; the reeds and flags shall wither away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 32:20; Joel 1:17–18; Isaiah 23:3"> The meadows by the Nile, by the brink of the Nile, and all the sown fields of the Nile, shall become dry, be driven away, and be no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 47:10; Habakkuk 1:15; Numbers 11:5"> And the fishers shall lament, and all they that cast angle into the Nile shall mourn, and they that spread nets upon the waters shall languish.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:7; Proverbs 7:16; 1 Kings 10:28"> Moreover they that work in combed flax, and they that weave white cloth, shall be confounded.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Deuteronomy 11:10; Exodus 7:19; Exodus 8:5"> And the pillars of Egypt shall be broken in pieces; all they that work for hire shall be grieved in soul.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Numbers 13:22; Acts 7:22; 1 Kings 4:30"> The princes of Zoan are utterly foolish; the counsel of the wisest counselors of Pharaoh is become brutish: how say ye unto Pharaoh, I am the son of the wise, the son of ancient kings?</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:20; Isaiah 14:24; Judges 9:38"> Where then are thy wise men? and let them tell thee now; and let them know what Jehovah of hosts hath purposed concerning Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Jeremiah 2:16; Jeremiah 46:14"> The princes of Zoan are become fools, the princes of Memphis are deceived; they have caused Egypt to go astray, that are the cornerstone of her tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 2:11; 1 Kings 22:20–23; Ezekiel 14:7–9"> Jehovah hath mingled a spirit of perverseness in the midst of her; and they have caused Egypt to go astray in every work thereof, as a drunken man staggereth in his vomit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 1 Thessalonians 4:11–12; Proverbs 14:23"> Neither shall there be for Egypt any work, which head or tail, palm branch or rush, may do.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Jeremiah 51:30; Nahum 3:13; Isaiah 11:15"> In that day shall the Egyptians be like unto women; and they shall tremble and fear because of the shaking of the hand of Jehovah of hosts, which he shaketh over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 14:24; Daniel 4:35; Ezekiel 29:6–7"> And the land of Judah shall become a terror unto Egypt; everyone to whom mention is made thereof shall be afraid, because of the purpose of Jehovah of hosts, which he purposeth against it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:9; Zechariah 2:11; Isaiah 19:21"> In that day there shall be five cities in the land of Egypt that speak the language of Canaan, and swear to Jehovah of hosts; one shall be called The city of destruction.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Genesis 28:18; Joshua 22:10; Joshua 22:26"> In that day shall there be an altar to Jehovah in the midst of the land of Egypt, and a pillar at the border thereof to Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 50:15; Titus 2:13; Isaiah 45:21–22"> And it shall be for a sign and for a witness unto Jehovah of hosts in the land of Egypt; for they shall cry unto Jehovah because of oppressors, and he will send them a savior, and a defender, and he will deliver them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 11:9; Malachi 1:11; John 17:3"> And Jehovah shall be known to Egypt, and the Egyptians shall know Jehovah in that day; yea, they shall worship with sacrifice and oblation, and shall vow a vow unto Jehovah, and shall perform it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Deuteronomy 32:39; Hebrews 12:11; Hosea 14:1"> And Jehovah will smite Egypt, smiting and healing; and they shall return unto Jehovah, and he will be entreated of them, and will heal them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 11:16; Ephesians 2:18–22; Isaiah 35:8–10"> In that day shall there be a highway out of Egypt to Assyria, and the Assyrian shall come into Egypt, and the Egyptian into Assyria; and the Egyptians shall worship with the Assyrians.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 65:8; Genesis 12:2; Galatians 3:14"> In that day shall Israel be the third with Egypt and with Assyria, a blessing in the midst of the earth;</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 29:23; Hosea 2:23; Romans 9:24–25"> for that Jehovah of hosts hath blessed them, saying, Blessed be Egypt my people, and Assyria the work of my hands, and Israel mine inheritance.</span>
