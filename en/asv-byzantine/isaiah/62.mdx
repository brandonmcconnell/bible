<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Isaiah 62:6–7; Proverbs 4:18; Isaiah 32:15–17"> For Zion’s sake will I not hold my peace, and for Jerusalem’s sake I will not rest, until her righteousness go forth as brightness, and her salvation as a lamp that burneth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 62:12; Genesis 17:5; Isaiah 66:12"> And the nations shall see thy righteousness, and all kings thy glory, and thou shalt be called by a new name, which the mouth of Jehovah shall name.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 28:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:19; Zechariah 9:16"> Thou shalt also be a crown of beauty in the hand of Jehovah, and a royal diadem in the hand of thy God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zephaniah 3:17; Hosea 2:19–20; Isaiah 62:12"> Thou shalt no more be termed Forsaken; neither shall thy land anymore be termed Desolate: but thou shalt be called Hephzi-bah, and thy land Beulah; for Jehovah delighteth in thee, and thy land shall be married.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Isaiah 62:4; Isaiah 65:19; Hebrews 12:2"> For as a young man marrieth a virgin, so shall thy sons marry thee; and as the bridegroom rejoiceth over the bride, so shall thy God rejoice over thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 52:8; Ezekiel 3:17–21; Psalms 134:1–2"> I have set watchmen upon thy walls, O Jerusalem; they shall never hold their peace day nor night: ye that are Jehovah’s remembrancers, take ye no rest,</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:9; Zephaniah 3:19–20; Isaiah 60:18"> and give him no rest, till he establish, and till he make Jerusalem a praise in the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 65:21–23; Judges 6:3–6; Deuteronomy 28:33"> Jehovah hath sworn by his right hand, and by the arm of his strength, Surely I will no more give thy grain to be food for thine enemies; and foreigners shall not drink thy new wine, for which thou hast labored:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Deuteronomy 12:12; Deuteronomy 16:11; Deuteronomy 12:7"> but they that have garnered it shall eat it, and praise Jehovah; and they that have gathered it shall drink it in the courts of my sanctuary.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 57:14; Isaiah 40:3; Hebrews 12:13"> Go through, go through the gates; prepare ye the way of the people; cast up, cast up the highway; gather out the stones; lift up an ensign for the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Zechariah 9:9; Matthew 21:5; John 12:15"> Behold, Jehovah hath proclaimed unto the end of the earth, Say ye to the daughter of Zion, Behold, thy salvation cometh; behold, his reward is with him, and his recompense before him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 2:9; Deuteronomy 26:19; Isaiah 62:4"> And they shall call them The holy people, The redeemed of Jehovah: and thou shalt be called Sought out, A city not forsaken.</span>
