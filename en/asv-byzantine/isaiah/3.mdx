<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 26:26; Ezekiel 14:13; Isaiah 1:24"> For, behold, the Lord, Jehovah of hosts, doth take away from Jerusalem and from Judah stay and staff, the whole stay of bread, and the whole stay of water;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 9:14–15; 2 Kings 24:14–16; Lamentations 5:12–14"> the mighty man, and the man of war; the judge, and the prophet, and the diviner, and the elder;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Judges 8:18; Deuteronomy 1:15; Exodus 18:21"> the captain of fifty, and the honorable man, and the counselor, and the expert artificer, and the skillful enchanter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 10:16; 2 Chronicles 33:1; 1 Kings 3:7–9"> And I will give children to be their princes, and babes shall rule over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:3–8; Malachi 3:5; Matthew 27:28–30"> And the people shall be oppressed, every one by another, and every one by his neighbor: the child shall behave himself proudly against the old man, and the base against the honorable.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 4:1; John 6:15; Judges 11:6–8"> When a man shall take hold of his brother in the house of his father, saying, Thou hast clothing, be thou our ruler, and let this ruin be under thy hand;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hosea 5:13; Lamentations 2:13; Ezekiel 34:4"> in that day shall he lift up his voice, saying, I will not be a healer; for in my house is neither bread nor clothing: ye shall not make me ruler of the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:22; Ezekiel 9:9; Malachi 3:13–15"> For Jerusalem is ruined, and Judah is fallen; because their tongue and their doings are against Jehovah, to provoke the eyes of his glory.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 13:13; Genesis 18:20–21; 2 Kings 9:30"> The show of their countenance doth witness against them; and they declare their sin as Sodom, they hide it not. Woe unto their soul! for they have done evil unto themselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Psalms 128:1–2; Hebrews 6:10; Ecclesiastes 8:12"> Say ye of the righteous, that it shall be well with him; for they shall eat the fruit of their doings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 8:13; James 2:13; Psalms 62:12"> Woe unto the wicked! it shall be ill with him; for what his hands have done shall be done unto him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 3:4; Nahum 3:13; 2 Kings 11:1"> As for my people, children are their oppressors, and women rule over them. O my people, they that lead thee cause thee to err, and destroy the way of thy paths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Micah 6:2; Hosea 4:1–2; Proverbs 22:22–23"> Jehovah standeth up to contend, and standeth to judge the peoples.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Job 22:4; Psalms 143:2; Isaiah 3:2–3"> Jehovah will enter into judgment with the elders of his people, and the princes thereof: It is ye that have eaten up the vineyard; the spoil of the poor is in your houses:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Micah 3:2–3; Psalms 94:5; Amos 2:6–7"> what mean ye that ye crush my people, and grind the face of the poor? saith the Lord, Jehovah of hosts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 32:9–11; Ezekiel 16:49–50; Isaiah 4:4"> Moreover Jehovah said, Because the daughters of Zion are haughty, and walk with outstretched necks and wanton eyes, walking and mincing as they go, and making a tinkling with their feet;</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Jeremiah 13:22; Micah 1:11; Revelation 16:2"> therefore the Lord will smite with a scab the crown of the head of the daughters of Zion, and Jehovah will lay bare their secret parts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Judges 8:21; Judges 8:26"> In that day the Lord will take away the beauty of their anklets, and the cauls, and the crescents;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 16:11; Numbers 31:50; Exodus 35:22"> the pendants, and the bracelets, and the mufflers;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Exodus 39:28; Genesis 35:4; Ezekiel 16:12"> the headtires, and the ankle chains, and the sashes, and the perfume boxes, and the amulets;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Genesis 24:47; 1 Peter 3:3–4; Luke 15:22"> the rings, and the nose jewels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> the festival robes, and the mantles, and the shawls, and the satchels;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 38:8; Ruth 3:15; Ezekiel 16:10"> the hand mirrors, and the fine linen, and the turbans, and the veils.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Ezekiel 27:31; Isaiah 22:12"> And it shall come to pass, that instead of sweet spices there shall be rottenness; and instead of a girdle, a rope; and instead of well set hair, baldness; and instead of a robe, a girding of sackcloth; branding instead of beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:9; Isaiah 1:20; Amos 9:10"> Thy men shall fall by the sword, and thy mighty in the war.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Lamentations 2:10; Jeremiah 14:2; Lamentations 1:4"> And her gates shall lament and mourn; and she shall be desolate and sit upon the ground.</span>
