<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Ephesians 4:31; James 1:21; James 4:11"> Putting away therefore all wickedness, and all guile, and hypocrisies, and envies, and all evil speakings,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hebrews 5:12–13; Ephesians 4:15; 2 Peter 3:18"> as newborn babes, long for the spiritual milk which is without guile, that ye may grow thereby;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 34:8; Hebrews 6:5–6; Psalms 63:5"> if so be that ye have tasted that the Lord is gracious:</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Acts 4:11–12; 1 Peter 2:7"> unto whom coming, a living stone, rejected indeed of men, but with God elect, precious,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ephesians 2:20–22; 1 Peter 2:9; 1 Corinthians 3:9"> ye also, as living stones, are built up a spiritual house, a holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices, acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 28:16; Romans 9:32–33; 1 Peter 2:4"> Because it is contained in the scripture,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">Behold, I lay in Zion a chief cornerstone, elect, precious:</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">And he that believeth on him shall not be put to shame.</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 118:22–23; Mark 12:10–11; Acts 4:11–12"> For you therefore that believe is the preciousness: but unto them that are disobedient,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">The stone which the builders rejected,</span><br /><span className="pl pl-1">The same was made the head of the corner;</span></span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Isaiah 8:14; 1 Corinthians 1:23; Romans 9:32–33"> and,<br /><span className="pl pl-1">A stone of stumbling, and a rock of offense;</span>for they stumble at the word, being disobedient: whereunto also they were appointed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Exodus 19:5–6; Deuteronomy 7:6; Revelation 1:6"> But ye are an elect race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for God’s own possession, that ye may show forth the excellencies of him who called you out of darkness into his marvelous light:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Hebrews 4:16; Hosea 1:9–10"> who in time past were no people, but now are the people of God: who had not obtained mercy, but now have obtained mercy.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 John 2:15–17; Galatians 5:24; 1 Peter 4:2"> Beloved, I beseech you as sojourners and pilgrims, to abstain from fleshly lusts, which war against the soul;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Peter 3:16; Matthew 5:16; Titus 2:7–8"> having your behavior seemly among the Gentiles; that, wherein they speak against you as evildoers, they may by your good works, which they shall behold, glorify God in the day of visitation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Titus 3:1; Romans 13:1–7; 1 Timothy 2:1–2"> Be subject therefore to every ordinance of man for the Lord’s sake: whether to the king, as supreme;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Romans 13:3–4"> or unto governors, as sent by him for vengeance on evildoers and for praise to them that do well.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Peter 3:17; Titus 2:8; Matthew 7:26"> For so is the will of God, that by well-doing ye should put to silence the ignorance of foolish men:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Galatians 5:13; Romans 6:22; Romans 6:18"> as free, and not using your freedom for a cloak of wickedness, but as bondservants of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 24:21; Romans 12:10; Romans 13:7"> Honor all men. Love the brotherhood. Fear God. Honor the king.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Titus 2:9–10; Ephesians 6:5–7; Colossians 3:22–25"> Servants, be in subjection to your masters with all fear; not only to the good and gentle, but also to the froward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Matthew 5:10–12; 1 Peter 3:14–17; 1 Peter 2:20"> For this is acceptable, if for conscience toward God a man endureth griefs, suffering wrongfully.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 Peter 2:19; 1 Peter 3:17; Matthew 5:10–12"> For what glory is it, if, when ye sin, and are buffeted for it, ye shall take it patiently? but if, when ye do well, and suffer for it, ye shall take it patiently, this is acceptable with God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:6; Matthew 16:24; John 13:15"> For hereunto were ye called: because Christ also suffered for us, leaving you an example, that ye should follow his steps:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Isaiah 53:9; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Hebrews 4:15"> who did no sin, neither was guile found in his mouth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Isaiah 53:7; 1 Peter 4:19; Hebrews 12:3"> who, when he was reviled, reviled not again; when he suffered, threatened not; but committed himself to him that judgeth righteously:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Isaiah 53:4–6; Matthew 8:17; Romans 6:11"> who his own self bare our sins in his body upon the tree, that we, having died unto sins, might live unto righteousness; by whose stripes ye were healed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; Ezekiel 34:6; Luke 15:4–6"> For ye were like sheep going astray; but are now returned unto the Shepherd and Bishop of your souls.</span>
