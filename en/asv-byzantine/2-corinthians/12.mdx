<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Galatians 1:12; 2 Corinthians 12:7; 1 John 5:20"> Surely it is not expedient for me to glory; for I will come to visions and revelations of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Acts 22:17; Ezekiel 8:1–3; 1 Thessalonians 4:17"> I know a man in Christ, fourteen years ago (whether in the body, I know not; or whether out of the body, I know not; God knoweth), such a one caught up even to the third heaven.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And I know such a man (whether in the body, or out of the body, I know not; God knoweth),</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Luke 23:43; Revelation 2:7; Ezekiel 31:9"> how that he was caught up into Paradise, and heard unspeakable words, which it is not lawful for a man to utter.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:30; 1 Corinthians 2:3; 2 Corinthians 12:9–10"> On behalf of such a one will I glory: but on mine own behalf I will not glory, save in my weaknesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:16; 2 Corinthians 11:31; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> For if I should desire to glory, I shall not be foolish; for I shall speak the truth: but I forbear, lest any man should account of me above that which he seeth me to be, or anything he heareth from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Numbers 33:55; Judges 2:3; 2 Chronicles 32:25–26"> And by reason of the exceeding greatness of the revelations, that I should not be exalted overmuch, there was given to me a thorn in the flesh, a messenger of Satan to buffet me, that I should not be exalted overmuch.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 5:7; Matthew 26:39–44; 1 Samuel 15:11"> Concerning this thing I besought the Lord thrice, that it might depart from me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Philippians 4:13; 2 Corinthians 12:10; Isaiah 40:29–31"> And he hath said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my power is made perfect in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in my weaknesses, that the power of Christ may rest upon me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:9; Ephesians 6:10; Romans 5:3"> Wherefore I take pleasure in weaknesses, in injuries, in necessities, in persecutions, in distresses, for Christ’s sake: for when I am weak, then am I strong.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:5; 2 Corinthians 11:1; 1 Corinthians 15:8–10"> I am become foolish in glorying: ye compelled me; for I ought to have been commended of you: for in nothing was I behind the very chiefest apostles, though I am nothing.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Romans 15:18–19; 2 Corinthians 11:4; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> Truly the signs of an apostle were wrought among you in all patience, in signs and wonders and mighty works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 9:6; 2 Corinthians 11:7–9"> For what is there wherein ye were made inferior to the rest of the churches, except it be that I myself was not a burden to you? forgive me this wrong.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 19:14; Genesis 24:35–36; 1 Corinthians 10:33"> Behold, a third time I am ready to come to you; and I will not be a burden to you: for I seek not yours, but you: for the children ought not to lay up for the parents, but the parents for the children.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:8; 2 Corinthians 1:6; Philippians 2:17"> And I will most gladly spend and be spent for your souls, though the more abundantly I love you, the less I am loved.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 2:5; 2 Corinthians 10:2–3; 2 Corinthians 12:13"> But be it so, I did not myself burden you; but, being crafty, I caught you with guile.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Corinthians 9:5; 1 Corinthians 4:17; 2 Kings 5:20–27"> Did I take advantage of you by any one of them whom I have sent unto you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:6; Romans 4:12; Acts 20:33–35"> I exhorted Titus, and I sent the brother with him. Did Titus take any advantage of you? walked we not in the same spirit? walked we not in the same steps?</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Romans 9:1; 1 Corinthians 14:26; 2 Corinthians 10:8"> Again, think ye that we are excusing ourselves unto you? In the sight of God speak we in Christ. But all things, beloved, are for your edifying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 5:19–21; 1 Peter 2:1; Romans 1:29"> For I fear, lest by any means, when I come, I should find you not such as I would, and should myself be found of you such as ye would not; lest by any means there should be strifes, jealousies, wraths, factions, backbitings, whisperings, swellings, tumults;</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:2; 1 Peter 4:2–3; Colossians 3:5"> lest again when I come my God will humble me before you, and I should mourn for many of them that have sinned heretofore, and repented not of the uncleanness and fornication and lasciviousness which they committed.</span>
</div>
