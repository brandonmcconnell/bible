<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:4; 1 Corinthians 1:21; 2 Corinthians 11:19"> Would that ye could bear with me a little in my foolishness: but indeed ye do bear with me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Hosea 2:19–20; Ephesians 5:26–27; Isaiah 54:5"> For I am jealous over you with a godly jealousy: for I espoused you to one husband, that I might present you as a pure virgin to Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Colossians 2:8; Galatians 1:6; Ephesians 4:14"> But I fear, lest by any means, as the serpent beguiled Eve in his craftiness, so your minds should be corrupted from the simplicity that is in Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Galatians 1:6–8; 1 Corinthians 3:11; 1 Timothy 2:5"> For if he that cometh preacheth another Jesus, whom we did not preach, or if ye receive a different spirit, which ye did not receive, or a different gospel, which ye did not accept, ye might well bear with him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:11–12; 1 Corinthians 15:10; Galatians 2:6–9"> For I reckon that I am not a whit behind the very chiefest apostles.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Ephesians 3:4; 1 Corinthians 1:17; 2 Corinthians 4:2"> But though I be rude in speech, yet am I not in knowledge; nay, in every way have we been made manifest unto you in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:13; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Corinthians 10:1"> Or did I commit a sin in abasing myself that ye might be exalted, because I preached to you the gospel of God for nought?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Philippians 4:18; 2 Corinthians 11:9; Philippians 4:14–16"> I robbed other churches, taking wages of them that I might minister unto you;</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Thessalonians 3:8–9; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 1 Thessalonians 2:6"> and when I was present with you and was in want, I was not a burden on any man; for the brethren, when they came from Macedonia, supplied the measure of my want; and in everything I kept myself from being burdensome unto you, and so will I keep myself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Romans 9:1; Romans 1:9; Acts 18:12"> As the truth of Christ is in me, no man shall stop me of this glorying in the regions of Achaia.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:15; 2 Corinthians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 7:3"> Wherefore? because I love you not? God knoweth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="1 Corinthians 9:12; 1 Corinthians 5:6; 2 Corinthians 5:12"> But what I do, that I will do, that I may cut off occasion from them that desire an occasion; that wherein they glory, they may be found even as we.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Galatians 1:7; Revelation 2:2; 1 John 4:1"> For such men are false apostles, deceitful workers, fashioning themselves into apostles of Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Galatians 1:8; 2 Corinthians 2:11; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> And no marvel; for even Satan fashioneth himself into an angel of light.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Philippians 3:19; Revelation 9:11; Isaiah 9:14–15"> It is no great thing therefore if his ministers also fashion themselves as ministers of righteousness; whose end shall be according to their works.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Corinthians 11:1; 2 Corinthians 12:6; 2 Corinthians 12:11"> I say again, Let no man think me foolish; but if ye do, yet as foolish receive me, that I also may glory a little.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:12; Philippians 3:3–6; 2 Corinthians 9:4"> That which I speak, I speak not after the Lord, but as in foolishness, in this confidence of glorying.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:10; 2 Corinthians 11:21–23; 2 Corinthians 10:12–18"> Seeing that many glory after the flesh, I will glory also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:10; 1 Corinthians 10:15; 1 Corinthians 8:1"> For ye bear with the foolish gladly, being wise yourselves.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Galatians 2:4; Galatians 4:3; Galatians 5:1"> For ye bear with a man, if he bringeth you into bondage, if he devoureth you, if he taketh you captive, if he exalteth himself, if he smiteth you on the face.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:10; 2 Corinthians 11:17; 2 Corinthians 10:1–2"> I speak by way of disparagement, as though we were weak. Yet whereinsoever any is bold (I speak in foolishness), I am bold also.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Philippians 3:5; Romans 11:1; Romans 9:4"> Are they Hebrews? so am I. Are they Israelites? so am I. Are they the seed of Abraham? so am I.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:4–5; 2 Corinthians 1:9–10; 2 Corinthians 6:9"> Are they ministers of Christ? (I speak as one beside himself) I more; in labors more abundantly, in stripes above measure, in prisons more abundantly, in deaths oft.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Mark 13:9; Deuteronomy 25:2–3; Matthew 10:17"> Of the Jews five times received I forty stripes save one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Acts 14:19; Acts 16:37; Hebrews 11:37"> Thrice was I beaten with rods, once was I stoned, thrice I suffered shipwreck, a night and a day have I been in the deep;</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Acts 14:5; Acts 17:5; Acts 13:50"> in journeyings often, in perils of rivers, in perils of robbers, in perils from my countrymen, in perils from the Gentiles, in perils in the city, in perils in the wilderness, in perils in the sea, in perils among false brethren;</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Philippians 4:12; 2 Corinthians 6:5; 1 Thessalonians 2:9"> in labor and travail, in watchings often, in hunger and thirst, in fastings often, in cold and nakedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="1 Corinthians 7:17; Romans 15:16; Acts 20:18–35"> Besides those things that are without, there is that which presseth upon me daily, anxiety for all the churches.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="1 Corinthians 8:13; 1 Corinthians 9:22; Galatians 4:8–20"> Who is weak, and I am not weak? who is caused to stumble, and I burn not?</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Corinthians 12:5–11; Proverbs 25:27; 1 Corinthians 2:3"> If I must needs glory, I will glory of the things that concern my weakness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Romans 9:5; Ephesians 1:3; John 10:30"> The God and Father of the Lord Jesus Christ, he who is blessed forevermore knoweth that I lie not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Acts 9:24–25; 2 Corinthians 11:26"> In Damascus the governor under Aretas the king guarded the city of the Damascenes, desiring to take me:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Acts 9:25; 1 Samuel 19:12; Joshua 2:18"> and through a window was I let down in a basket by the wall, and escaped his hands.</span>
