<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jude 1:22–23; Galatians 6:1–2; Galatians 6:10"> Wherefore I beseech you to confirm your love toward him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 10:6; Philemon 1:21; Philippians 2:22"> For to this end also did I write, that I might know the proof of you, whether ye are obedient in all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="John 20:23; Matthew 18:18; 2 Corinthians 5:20"> But to whom ye forgive anything, I forgive also: for I also, if I have forgiven anything, to whom I have forgiven it, for your sakes have I forgiven it in the presence of Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="1 Peter 5:8; Ephesians 6:11–12; 2 Corinthians 11:3"> that no advantage may be gained over us by Satan: for we are not ignorant of his devices.</span>

</div>

<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Ephesians 5:2; 1 Corinthians 1:18; Ezekiel 20:41"> For we are a sweet savor of Christ unto God, in them that are saved, and in them that perish;</span>
