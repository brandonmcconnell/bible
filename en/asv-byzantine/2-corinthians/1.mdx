<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="2 Timothy 1:1; Philippians 1:1; Ephesians 1:1"> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ through the will of God, and Timothy our brother, unto the church of God which is at Corinth, with all the saints that are in the whole of Achaia:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Romans 1:7; Ephesians 6:23; Philippians 1:2"> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 1:3; 1 Peter 1:3; Romans 15:5–6"> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and God of all comfort;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Thessalonians 5:11; Isaiah 51:12; 2 Thessalonians 2:16–17"> who comforteth us in all our affliction, that we may be able to comfort them that are in any affliction, through the comfort wherewith we ourselves are comforted of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Colossians 1:24; Philippians 3:10; 2 Corinthians 4:10–11"> For as the sufferings of Christ abound unto us, even so our comfort also aboundeth through Christ.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:10; 2 Corinthians 1:4; 2 Corinthians 4:15–18"> But whether we are afflicted, it is for your comfort and salvation, which worketh in the patient enduring of the same sufferings which we also suffer; and our hope for you is steadfast: or whether we are comforted, it is for your comfort and salvation;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 8:17–18; 2 Thessalonians 1:4–7; James 1:12"> knowing that, as ye are partakers of the sufferings, so also are ye of the comfort.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:7–12; 1 Corinthians 15:32; 1 Corinthians 4:8"> For we would not have you ignorant, brethren, of our affliction which befell us in Asia, that we were weighed down exceedingly, beyond our power, insomuch that we despaired even of life:</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:5; Hebrews 11:19; 2 Corinthians 4:7"> yea, we ourselves have had the sentence of death within ourselves, that we should not trust in ourselves, but in God who raiseth the dead:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:10; Psalms 34:19; 2 Peter 2:9"> who delivered us out of so great a death, and doth deliver: on whom we have set our hope that he will also still deliver us;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Philippians 1:19; 2 Corinthians 4:15; Acts 12:5"> ye also helping together on our behalf by your supplication; that, for the gift bestowed upon us by means of many, thanks may be given by many persons on your behalf.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 2:17; 1 Corinthians 2:13; James 3:13–18"> For our glorying is this, the testimony of our conscience, that in simplicity and sincerity of God, not in fleshly wisdom but in the grace of God, we behaved ourselves in the world, and more abundantly to you-ward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Corinthians 4:2; Philemon 1:6; 2 Corinthians 13:6"> For we write no other things unto you, than what ye read or even acknowledge, and I hope ye will acknowledge even unto the end:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Philippians 2:16; 1 Corinthians 1:8; 1 Thessalonians 2:19–20"> as also ye did acknowledge us in part, that we are your glorying, even as ye also are ours, in the day of the Lord Jesus.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="1 Corinthians 4:19; Romans 1:11; Romans 15:29"> And in this confidence I was minded to come unto you before, that ye might have a second benefit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="1 Corinthians 16:5–7; Acts 21:5; Acts 19:21–22"> and by you to pass into Macedonia, and again from Macedonia to come unto you, and of you to be set forward on my journey unto Judea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Matthew 5:37; James 5:12; 2 Corinthians 10:2–3"> When I therefore purposed thus, did I show fickleness? or the things that I purpose, do I purpose according to the flesh, that with me there should be the yea yea and the nay nay?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="1 Corinthians 1:9; Revelation 3:7; John 7:28"> But as God is faithful, our word toward you was not yea and nay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Hebrews 13:8; Revelation 1:17; 2 John 1:9"> For the Son of God, Jesus Christ, who was preached among you by us, even by me and Silvanus and Timothy, was not yea and nay, but in him is yea.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="1 John 2:24–25; Galatians 3:22; Revelation 7:12"> For how many soever be the promises of God, in him is the yea, and in him is the Amen, unto the glory of God through us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 2:27; 1 Peter 5:10; 1 John 2:20"> Now he that establisheth us with you in Christ, and anointed us, is God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ephesians 4:30; 2 Corinthians 5:5; Ephesians 1:13–14"> who also sealed us, and gave us the earnest of the Spirit in our hearts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Galatians 1:20; 1 Corinthians 4:21; Romans 1:9"> But I call God for a witness upon my soul, that to spare you I forbare to come unto Corinth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="1 Peter 5:3; 1 Corinthians 15:1; Philippians 1:25–26"> Not that we have lordship over your faith, but are helpers of your joy: for in faith ye stand fast.</span>
