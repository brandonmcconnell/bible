<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Matthew 5:13–16; Galatians 2:12–14; Nehemiah 13:26"> Dead flies cause the oil of the perfumer to send forth an evil odor; so doth a little folly outweigh wisdom and honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Luke 12:18–20; Ecclesiastes 10:14; Ecclesiastes 10:10"> A wise man’s heart is at his right hand; but a fool’s heart at his left.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Proverbs 18:2; Proverbs 13:16; Proverbs 18:6"> Yea also, when the fool walketh by the way, his understanding faileth him, and he saith to everyone that he is a fool.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Proverbs 25:15; Ecclesiastes 8:3; 1 Samuel 25:24–44"> If the spirit of the ruler rise up against thee, leave not thy place; for gentleness allayeth great offenses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Ecclesiastes 5:13; Ecclesiastes 4:7; Ecclesiastes 9:3"> There is an evil which I have seen under the sun, as it were an error which proceedeth from the ruler:</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Proverbs 28:12; Proverbs 29:2; Esther 3:1"> folly is set in great dignity, and the rich sit in a low place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Proverbs 19:10; Esther 6:8; Proverbs 30:22"> I have seen servants upon horses, and princes walking like servants upon the earth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Proverbs 26:27; Psalms 7:15–16; Esther 7:10"> He that diggeth a pit shall fall into it; and whoso breaketh through a wall, a serpent shall bite him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Whoso heweth out stones shall be hurt therewith; and he that cleaveth wood is endangered thereby.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="James 1:5; Colossians 4:5; Ecclesiastes 10:15"> If the iron be blunt, and one do not whet the edge, then must he put to more strength: but wisdom is profitable to direct.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 8:17; Psalms 58:4–5; Psalms 64:3"> If the serpent bite before it is charmed, then is there no advantage in the charmer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Colossians 4:6; Luke 4:22; Proverbs 22:11"> The words of a wise man’s mouth are gracious; but the lips of a fool will swallow up himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="1 Samuel 25:10–11; Proverbs 29:9; Acts 6:9–11"> The beginning of the words of his mouth is foolishness; and the end of his talk is mischievous madness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Proverbs 15:2; Ecclesiastes 6:12; Ecclesiastes 5:3"> A fool also multiplieth words: yet man knoweth not what shall be; and that which shall be after him, who can tell him?</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Isaiah 57:1; Isaiah 35:8–10"> The labor of fools wearieth every one of them; for he knoweth not how to go to the city.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Isaiah 3:12; Isaiah 5:11–12; Isaiah 3:4–5"> Woe to thee, O land, when thy king is a child, and thy princes eat in the morning!</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Proverbs 31:4–5; Proverbs 28:2–3; Isaiah 5:11"> Happy art thou, O land, when thy king is the son of nobles, and thy princes eat in due season, for strength, and not for drunkenness!</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Proverbs 14:1; Proverbs 24:30–34; Proverbs 20:4"> By slothfulness the roof sinketh in; and through idleness of the hands the house leaketh.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Psalms 104:15; Ecclesiastes 2:1–2; 1 Peter 4:3"> A feast is made for laughter, and wine maketh glad the life; and money answereth all things.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Luke 12:2–3; Acts 23:5; Ecclesiastes 7:21–22"> Revile not the king, no, not in thy thought; and revile not the rich in thy bedchamber: for a bird of the heavens shall carry the voice, and that which hath wings shall tell the matter.</span>
