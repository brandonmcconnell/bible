<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> There dwelt a man in Babylon, and his name was Joakim:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> and he took a wife, whose name was Susanna, the daughter of Hilkiah, a very fair woman, and one that feared the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Her parents also were righteous, and taught their daughter according to the law of Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> Now Joakim was a great rich man, and had a fair garden joining unto his house: and to him resorted the Jews; because he was more honorable than all others.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> And the same year there were appointed two of the ancients of the people to be judges, such as the Lord spake of, that wickedness came from Babylon from ancient judges, who were accounted to govern the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> These kept much at Joakim’s house: and all that had any suits in law came unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> Now when the people departed away at noon, Susanna went into her husband’s garden to walk.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And the two elders beheld her going in every day, and walking; and they were inflamed with love for her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And they perverted their own mind, and turned away their eyes, that they might not look unto heaven, nor remember just judgments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And albeit they both were wounded with her love, yet durst not one show another his grief.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For they were ashamed to declare their lust, that they desired to have to do with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Yet they watched jealously from day to day to see her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And the one said to the other, Let us now go home: for it is dinner time.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> So when they were gone out, they parted the one from the other, and turning back again they came to the same place; and after that they had asked one another the cause, they acknowledged their lust: and then appointed they a time both together, when they might find her alone.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> And it fell out, as they watched a fit day, she went in as aforetime with two maids only, and she was desirous to wash herself in the garden: for it was hot.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And there was no one there save the two elders, that had hidden themselves, and watched her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> Then she said to her maids, Bring me oil and washing balls, and shut the garden doors, that I may wash me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And they did as she bade them, and shut the garden doors, and went out themselves at the side doors to fetch the things that she had commanded them: and they saw not the elders, because they were hidden.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Now when the maids were gone forth, the two elders rose up, and ran unto her, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> Behold, the garden doors are shut, that no man can see us, and we are in love with thee; therefore consent unto us, and lie with us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> If thou wilt not, we will bear witness against thee, that a young man was with thee: and therefore thou didst send away thy maids from thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Then Susanna sighed, and said, I am straitened on every side: for if I do this thing, it is death unto me: and if I do it not, I cannot escape your hands.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> It is better for me to fall into your hands, and not do it, than to sin in the sight of the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> With that Susanna cried with a loud voice: and the two elders cried out against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> Then ran the one, and opened the garden doors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So when the servants of the house heard the cry in the garden, they rushed in at the side door, to see what had befallen her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> But when the elders had told their tale, the servants were greatly ashamed: for there was never such a report made of Susanna.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And it came to pass on the morrow, when the people assembled to her husband Joakim, the two elders came full of their wicked intent against Susanna to put her to death;</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> and said before the people, Send for Susanna, the daughter of Hilkiah, Joakim’s wife. So they sent;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and she came with her father and mother, her children, and all her kindred.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Now Susanna was a very delicate woman, and beauteous to behold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> And these wicked men commanded her to be unveiled, (for she was veiled) that they might be filled with her beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> Therefore her friends and all that saw her wept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Then the two elders stood up in the midst of the people, and laid their hands upon her head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> And she weeping looked up toward heaven: for her heart trusted in the Lord.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> And the elders said, As we walked in the garden alone, this woman came in with two maids, and shut the garden doors, and sent the maids away.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Then a young man, who there was hidden, came unto her, and lay with her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And we, being in a corner of the garden, saw this wickedness, and ran unto them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> And when we saw them together, the man we could not hold: for he was stronger than we, and opened the doors, and leaped out.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> But having taken this woman, we asked who the young man was, but she would not tell us: these things do we testify.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> Then the assembly believed them, as those that were elders of the people and judges: so they condemned her to death.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> Then Susanna cried out with a loud voice, and said, O everlasting God, that knowest the secrets, that knowest all things before they come to be:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> thou knowest that they have borne false witness against me, and, behold, I must die; whereas I never did such things as these men have maliciously invented against me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And the Lord heard her voice.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> Therefore when she was led away to be put to death, God raised up the holy spirit of a young youth, whose name was Daniel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> and he cried with a loud voice, I am clear from the blood of this woman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> Then all the people turned them toward him, and said, What mean these words that thou hast spoken?</span>
<span className="v" id="v48" data-v="48"> So he standing in the midst of them said, Are ye such fools, ye sons of Israel, that without examination or knowledge of the truth ye have condemned a daughter of Israel?</span>
<span className="v" id="v49" data-v="49"> Return again to the place of judgment: for these have borne false witness against her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v50" data-v="50"> Wherefore all the people turned again in haste, and the elders said unto him, Come, sit down among us, and show it us, seeing God hath given thee the honor of an elder.</span>
<span className="v" id="v51" data-v="51"> Then said Daniel unto them, Put them asunder one far from another, and I will examine them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v52" data-v="52"> So when they were put asunder one from another, he called one of them, and said unto him, O thou that art waxen old in wickedness, now are thy sins come home to thee which thou hast committed aforetime,</span>
<span className="v" id="v53" data-v="53"> in pronouncing unjust judgment, and condemning the innocent, and letting the guilty go free; albeit the Lord saith, The innocent and righteous shalt thou not slay.</span>
<span className="v" id="v54" data-v="54"> Now then, if thou sawest her, tell me, Under what tree sawest thou them companying together? Who answered, Under a mastic tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v55" data-v="55"> And Daniel said, Right well hast thou lied against thine own head; for even now the angel of God hath received the sentence of God and shall cut thee in two.</span>
<span className="v" id="v56" data-v="56"> So he put him aside, and commanded to bring the other, and said unto him, O thou seed of Canaan, and not of Judah, beauty hath deceived thee, and lust hath perverted thy heart.</span>
<span className="v" id="v57" data-v="57"> Thus have ye dealt with the daughters of Israel, and they for fear companied with you: but the daughter of Judah would not abide your wickedness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v58" data-v="58"> Now therefore tell me, Under what tree didst thou take them companying together? Who answered, Under a holm tree.</span>
<span className="v" id="v59" data-v="59"> Then said Daniel unto him, Right well hast thou also lied against thine own head: for the angel of God waiteth with the sword to cut thee in two, that he may destroy you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v60" data-v="60"> With that all the assembly cried out with a loud voice, and blessed God, who saveth them that hope in him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v61" data-v="61"> And they arose against the two elders, for Daniel had convicted them of false witness out of their own mouth:</span>
<span className="v" id="v62" data-v="62"> and according to the law of Moses they did unto them in such sort as they maliciously intended to do to their neighbor: and they put them to death, and the innocent blood was saved the same day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v63" data-v="63"> Therefore Hilkiah and his wife praised God for their daughter Susanna, with Joakim her husband, and all the kindred, because there was no dishonesty found in her.</span>
<span className="v" id="v64" data-v="64"> And from that day forth was Daniel had in great reputation in the sight of the people.</span>
