<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> In the thirtieth year after the ruin of the city, I Salathiel (the same is Ezra) was in Babylon, and lay troubled upon my bed, and my thoughts came up over my heart:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> for I saw the desolation of Zion, and the wealth of them that dwelt at Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And my spirit was sore moved, so that I began to speak words full of fear to the Most High, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> O Lord that bearest rule, didst thou not speak at the beginning, when thou didst fashion the earth, and that thyself alone, and commandest the dust,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> and it gave thee Adam, a body without a soul? yet it was the workmanship of thy hands, and thou didst breathe into him the breath of life, and he was made living before thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> And thou leddest him into paradise, which thy right hand did plant, before ever the earth came forward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And unto him thou gavest thy one commandment: which he transgressed, and immediately thou appointedst death for him and in his generations; and there were born of him nations and tribes, peoples and kindreds, without number.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> And every nation walked after their own will, and did ungodly things before thee, and despised thy commandments, and thou didst not forbid them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Nevertheless again in process of time thou broughtest the flood upon those that dwelt in the world, and destroyedst them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And it came to pass that the same hap befell them; like as death was to Adam, so was the flood to these.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> Nevertheless one of them thou leftest, Noah with his household, even all the righteous men that came of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And it came to pass, that when they that dwelt upon the earth began to multiply, they multiplied also children, and peoples, and many nations, and began again to be more ungodly than the first.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And it came to pass, when they did wickedly before thee, thou didst choose thee one from among them, whose name was Abraham;</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> and him thou lovedst, and unto him only thou showedst the end of the times secretly by night:</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> and madest an everlasting covenant with him, promising him that thou wouldest never forsake his seed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And unto him thou gavest Isaac, and unto Isaac thou gavest Jacob and Esau. And thou didst set apart Jacob for thyself, but didst put by Esau: and Jacob became a great multitude.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And it came to pass, that when thou leddest his seed out of Egypt, thou broughtest them up to the mount Sinai.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> Thou bowedst the heavens also, and didst shake the earth, and movedst the whole world, and madest the depths to tremble, and troubledst the course of that age.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> And thy glory went through four gates, of fire, and of earthquake, and of wind, and of cold; that thou mightest give the law unto the seed of Jacob, and the commandment unto the generation of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> And yet tookest thou not away from them their wicked heart, that thy law might bring forth fruit in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> For the first Adam bearing a wicked heart transgressed, and was overcome; and not he only, but all they also that are born of him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Thus disease was made permanent; and the law was in the heart of the people along with the wickedness of the root; so the good departed away, and that which was wicked abode still.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> So the times passed away, and the years were brought to an end: then didst thou raise thee up a servant, called David,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> whom thou commandedest to build a city unto thy name, and to offer oblations unto thee therein of thine own.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> When this was done many years, then they that inhabited the city did evil,</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> in all things doing even as Adam and all his generations had done: for they also bare a wicked heart:</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and so thou gavest thy city over into the hands of thine enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And I said then in my heart, are their deeds any better that inhabit Babylon? and hath she therefore dominion over Zion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For it came to pass when I came hither, that I saw also impieties without number, and my soul saw many evildoers in this thirtieth year, so that my heart failed me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> For I have seen how thou sufferest them sinning, and hast spared the ungodly doers, and hast destroyed thy people, and hast preserved thine enemies; and thou hast not signified</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> unto any how thy way may be comprehended. Are the deeds of Babylon better than those of Zion?</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Or is there any other nation that knoweth thee beside Israel? or what tribes have so believed thy covenants as these tribes of Jacob?</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> And yet their reward appeareth not, and their labor hath no fruit: for I have gone hither and thither through the nations, and I see that they abound in wealth, and think not upon thy commandments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> Weigh thou therefore our iniquities now in the balance, and theirs also that dwell in the world; and so shall it be found which way the scale inclineth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> Or when was it that they that dwell upon the earth have not sinned in thy sight? or what nation hath so kept thy commandments?</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> Thou shalt find that men who may be reckoned by name have kept thy precepts; but nations thou shalt not find.</span>
