<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> And he answered me, and said, Measure thou diligently within thyself: and when thou seest that a certain part of the signs are past, which have been told thee beforehand,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> then shalt thou understand, that it is the very time, wherein the Most High will visit the world which was made by him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And when there shall be seen in the world earthquakes, disquietude of peoples, devices of nations, wavering of leaders, disquietude of princes,</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> then shalt thou understand, that the Most High spake of these things from the days that were aforetime from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> For like as of all that is made in the world, the beginning is evident, and the end manifest;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> so also are the times of the Most High: the beginnings are manifest in wonders and mighty works, and the end in effects and signs.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And every one that shall be saved, and shall be able to escape by his works, or by faith, whereby he hath believed,</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> shall be preserved from the said perils, and shall see my salvation in my land, and within my borders, which I have sanctified for me from the beginning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> Then shall they be amazed, who now have abused my ways: and they that have cast them away despitefully shall dwell in torments.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> For as many as in their life have received benefits, and yet have not known me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> and as many as have scorned my law, while they had yet liberty, and, when as yet place of repentance was open unto them, understood not, but despised it;</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> the same must know it after death by torment.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And therefore be thou no longer curious how the ungodly shall be punished; but inquire how the righteous shall be saved, they whose the world is, and for whom the world was created.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> And I answered and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> I have said before, and now do speak, and will speak it also hereafter, that there are more of them that perish, than of them that will be saved:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> like as a wave is greater than a drop.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And he answered me, saying, Like as the field is, so also the seed; and as the flowers are, such are the colors also; and such as the work is, such also is the judgment thereon; and as is the husbandman, so is his threshing floor also. For there was a time in the world,</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> even then when I was preparing for them that now live, before the world was made for them to dwell in; and then no man spake against me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> for there was not any: but now they that are created in this world that is prepared, both with a table that faileth not, and a law which is unsearchable, are corrupted in their manners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> So I considered my world, and, lo, it was destroyed, and my earth, and, lo, it was in peril, because of the devices that were come into it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> And I saw, and spared them, but not greatly, and saved me a grape out of a cluster, and a plant out of a great forest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> Let the multitude perish then, which was born in vain; and let my grape be saved, and my plant; for with great labor have I made them perfect.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> Nevertheless if thou wilt cease yet seven days more, (howbeit thou shalt not fast in them,</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> but shalt go into a field of flowers, where no house is built, and eat only of the flowers of the field; and thou shalt taste no flesh, and shalt drink no wine, but shalt eat flowers only;)</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> and pray unto the Most High continually, then will I come and talk with thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> So I went my way, like as he commanded me, into the field which is called Ardat; and there I sat among the flowers, and did eat of the herbs of the field, and the meat of the same satisfied me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And it came to pass after seven days that I lay upon the grass, and my heart was vexed again, like as before:</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> and my mouth was opened, and I began to speak before the Lord Most High, and said,</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> O Lord, thou didst show thyself among us, unto our fathers in the wilderness, when they went forth out of Egypt, and when they came into the wilderness, where no man treadeth and that beareth no fruit;</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> and thou didst say, Hear me, thou Israel; and mark my words, O seed of Jacob.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> For, behold, I sow my law in you, and it shall bring forth fruit in you, and ye shall be glorified in it forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> But our fathers, who received the law, kept it not, and observed not the statutes: and the fruit of the law did not perish, neither could it, for it was thine;</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> yet they that received it perished, because they kept not the thing that was sown in them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And, lo, it is a custom, that when the ground hath received seed, or the sea a ship, or any vessel meat or drink, and when it cometh to pass that that which is sown, or that which is launched,</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> or the things which have been received, should come to an end, these come to an end, but the receptacles remain: yet with us it hath not happened so.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> For we that have received the law shall perish by sin, and our heart also which received it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Notwithstanding the law perisheth not, but remaineth in its honor.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38"> And when I spake these things in my heart, I looked about me with mine eyes, and upon the right side I saw a woman, and, behold, she mourned and wept with a loud voice, and was much grieved in mind, and her clothes were rent, and she had ashes upon her head.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39"> Then let I my thoughts go wherein I was occupied, and turned me unto her,</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40"> and said unto her, Wherefore weepest thou? and why art thou grieved in thy mind?</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41"> And she said unto me, Let me alone, my Lord, that I may bewail myself, and add unto my sorrow, for I am sore vexed in my mind, and brought very low.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42"> And I said unto her, What aileth thee? Tell me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43"> She said unto me, I thy servant was barren, and had no child, though I had a husband thirty years.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44"> And every hour and every day these thirty years did I make my prayer to the Most High day and night.</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45"> And it came to pass after thirty years that God heard me thy handmaid, and looked upon my low estate, and considered my trouble, and gave me a son: and I rejoiced in him greatly, I and my husband, and all my neighbors: and we gave great honor unto the Mighty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46"> And I nourished him with great travail.</span>
<span className="v" id="v47" data-v="47"> So when he grew up, and I came to take him a wife, I made him a feast day.</span>
