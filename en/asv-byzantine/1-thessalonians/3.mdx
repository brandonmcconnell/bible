<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Corinthians 13:9; 1 Thessalonians 3:11; Philippians 1:25"> night and day praying exceedingly that we may see your face, and may perfect that which is lacking in your faith?</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Proverbs 3:5–6; Matthew 6:32; 2 Thessalonians 2:16"> Now may our God and Father himself, and our Lord Jesus Christ, direct our way unto you:</span>
