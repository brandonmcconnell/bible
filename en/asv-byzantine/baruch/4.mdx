<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1"> This is the book of the commandments of God, and the law that endureth forever: all they that hold it fast are appointed to life; but such as leave it shall die.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2"> Turn thee, O Jacob, and take hold of it: walk toward her shining in the presence of the light thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Give not thy glory to another, nor the things that are profitable unto thee to a strange nation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> O Israel, happy are we: for the things that are pleasing to God are made known unto us.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Be of good cheer, my people, the memorial of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6"> Ye were sold to the nations, but not for destruction: because ye moved God to wrath, ye were delivered unto your adversaries.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> For ye provoked him that made you by sacrificing unto demons, and not to God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8"> Ye forgat the everlasting God, that brought you up; ye grieved also Jerusalem, that nursed you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> For she saw the wrath that is come upon you from God, and said, Hearken, ye women that dwell about Zion: for God hath brought upon me great mourning;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> for I have seen the captivity of my sons and daughters, which the Everlasting hath brought upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> For with joy did I nourish them; but sent them away with weeping and mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> Let no man rejoice over me, a widow, and forsaken of many: for the sins of my children am I left desolate; because they turned aside from the law of God,</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> and had no regard to his statutes, neither walked they in the ways of God’s commandments, nor trod in the paths of discipline in his righteousness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14"> Let them that dwell about Zion come, and remember ye the captivity of my sons and daughters, which the Everlasting hath brought upon them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15"> For he hath brought a nation upon them from far, a shameless nation, and of a strange language, who neither reverenced old man, nor pitied child.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> And they have carried away the dear beloved sons of the widow, and left her that was alone desolate of her daughters.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> But I, what can I help you?</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> For he that brought these plagues upon you will deliver you from the hand of your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19"> Go your way, O my children, go your way: for I am left desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> I have put off the garment of peace, and put upon me the sackcloth of my petition: I will cry unto the Everlasting as long as I live.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> Be of good cheer, O my children, cry unto God, and he shall deliver you from the power and hand of the enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> For I have trusted in the Everlasting, that he will save you; and joy is come unto me from the Holy One, because of the mercy which shall soon come unto you from the Everlasting your Savior.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> For I sent you out with mourning and weeping: but God will give you to me again with joy and gladness forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> For like as now they that dwell about Zion have seen your captivity: so shall they see shortly your salvation from our God, which shall come upon you with great glory, and brightness of the Everlasting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> My children, suffer patiently the wrath that is come upon you from God: for thine enemy hath persecuted thee; but shortly thou shalt see his destruction, and shalt tread upon their necks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> My delicate ones have gone rough ways; they were taken away as a flock carried off by the enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> Be of good cheer, O my children, and cry unto God: for ye shall be remembered of him that hath brought these things upon you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> For as it was your mind to go astray from God: so, return and seek him ten times more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29"> For he that brought these plagues upon you shall bring you everlasting joy again with your salvation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30"> Be of good cheer, O Jerusalem: for he that called thee by name will comfort thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31"> Miserable are they that afflicted thee, and rejoiced at thy fall.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32"> Miserable are the cities which thy children served: miserable is she that received thy sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33"> For as she rejoiced at thy fall, and was glad of thy ruin: so shall she be grieved for her own desolation.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34"> And I will take away her exultation in her great multitude, and her boasting shall be turned into mourning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35"> For fire shall come upon her from the Everlasting, long to endure; and she shall be inhabited of demons for a great time.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36"> O Jerusalem, look about thee toward the east, and behold the joy that cometh unto thee from God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37"> Lo, thy sons come, whom thou sentest away, they come gathered together from the east to the west at the word of the Holy One, rejoicing in the glory of God.</span>
</div>
