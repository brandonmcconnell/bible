<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 19:4; Deuteronomy 27:15; Numbers 33:52"> Ye shall make you no idols, neither shall ye rear you up a graven image, or a pillar, neither shall ye place any figured stone in your land, to bow down unto it: for I am Jehovah your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 19:30"> Ye shall keep my sabbaths, and reverence my sanctuary: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Isaiah 1:19; Deuteronomy 11:13–15; Deuteronomy 28:1–14"> If ye walk in my statutes, and keep my commandments, and do them;</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Zechariah 8:12; Psalms 67:6; Leviticus 25:21"> then I will give your rains in their season, and the land shall yield its increase, and the trees of the field shall yield their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 9:13; Joel 2:26; Joel 2:19"> And your threshing shall reach unto the vintage, and the vintage shall reach unto the sowing time; and ye shall eat your bread to the full, and dwell in your land safely.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Isaiah 35:9; Zephaniah 3:13; Psalms 147:14"> And I will give peace in the land, and ye shall lie down, and none shall make you afraid: and I will cause evil beasts to cease out of the land, neither shall the sword go through your land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7"> And ye shall chase your enemies, and they shall fall before you by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Joshua 23:10; Deuteronomy 28:7; Deuteronomy 32:30"> And five of you shall chase a hundred, and a hundred of you shall chase ten thousand; and your enemies shall fall before you by the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Genesis 17:6–7; Nehemiah 9:23; Ezekiel 16:62"> And I will have respect unto you, and make you fruitful, and multiply you, and will establish my covenant with you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Leviticus 25:22; 2 Kings 19:29; Luke 12:17"> And ye shall eat old store long kept, and ye shall bring forth the old because of the new.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 25:8; Revelation 21:3; Exodus 29:45"> And I will set my tabernacle among you: and my soul shall not abhor you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Corinthians 6:16; Jeremiah 30:22; Jeremiah 7:23"> And I will walk among you, and will be your God, and ye shall be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:27; Leviticus 25:38; Psalms 116:16"> I am Jehovah your God, who brought you forth out of the land of Egypt, that ye should not be their bondmen; and I have broken the bars of your yoke, and made you go upright.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:15–68; Leviticus 26:18; Jeremiah 17:27"> But if ye will not hearken unto me, and will not do all these commandments;</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="2 Kings 17:15; Deuteronomy 31:16; Zechariah 7:11–13"> and if ye shall reject my statutes, and if your soul abhor mine ordinances, so that ye will not do all my commandments, but break my covenant;</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Job 31:8; Jeremiah 5:17; Micah 6:15"> I also will do this unto you: I will appoint terror over you, even consumption and fever, that shall consume the eyes, and make the soul to pine away; and ye shall sow your seed in vain, for your enemies shall eat it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Leviticus 17:10; Proverbs 28:1; Psalms 53:5"> And I will set my face against you, and ye shall be smitten before your enemies: they that hate you shall rule over you; and ye shall flee when none pursueth you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 26:21; Leviticus 26:28; Leviticus 26:24"> And if ye will not yet for these things hearken unto me, then I will chastise you seven times more for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:23; 1 Kings 17:1; Daniel 4:37"> And I will break the pride of your power: and I will make your heaven as iron, and your earth as brass;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Psalms 127:1; Deuteronomy 11:17; Isaiah 49:4"> and your strength shall be spent in vain; for your land shall not yield its increase, neither shall the trees of the land yield their fruit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Leviticus 26:18; Leviticus 26:27"> And if ye walk contrary unto me, and will not hearken unto me, I will bring seven times more plagues upon you according to your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Ezekiel 14:15; Deuteronomy 32:24; Judges 5:6"> And I will send the beast of the field among you, which shall rob you of your children, and destroy your cattle, and make you few in number; and your ways shall become desolate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Jeremiah 5:3; Jeremiah 2:30; Amos 4:6–12"> And if by these things ye will not be reformed unto me, but will walk contrary unto me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Psalms 18:26; 2 Samuel 22:27; Job 9:4"> then will I also walk contrary unto you; and I will smite you, even I, seven times for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Numbers 14:12; Ezekiel 5:17; Jeremiah 29:17–18"> And I will bring a sword upon you, that shall execute the vengeance of the covenant; and ye shall be gathered together within your cities: and I will send the pestilence among you; and ye shall be delivered into the hand of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Micah 6:14; Isaiah 3:1; Psalms 105:16"> When I break your staff of bread, ten women shall bake your bread in one oven, and they shall deliver your bread again by weight: and ye shall eat, and not be satisfied.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 26:24; Leviticus 26:21"> And if ye will not for all this hearken unto me, but walk contrary unto me;</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Isaiah 59:18; Jeremiah 21:5; Isaiah 66:15"> then I will walk contrary unto you in wrath; and I also will chastise you seven times for your sins.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Ezekiel 5:10; Lamentations 4:10; Matthew 24:19"> And ye shall eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters shall ye eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="2 Kings 23:20; Isaiah 27:9; Ezekiel 6:13"> And I will destroy your high places, and cut down your sun images, and cast your dead bodies upon the bodies of your idols; and my soul shall abhor you.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Nehemiah 2:3; Lamentations 1:10; Hebrews 10:26"> And I will make your cities a waste, and will bring your sanctuaries unto desolation, and I will not smell the savor of your sweet odors.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Jeremiah 9:11; Jeremiah 19:8; Jeremiah 18:16"> And I will bring the land into desolation; and your enemies that dwell therein shall be astonished at it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Zechariah 7:14; Ezekiel 20:23; Deuteronomy 28:64–66"> And you will I scatter among the nations, and I will draw out the sword after you: and your land shall be a desolation, and your cities shall be a waste.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:21; Leviticus 26:43; Leviticus 25:2–4"> Then shall the land enjoy its sabbaths, as long as it lieth desolate, and ye are in your enemies’ land; even then shall the land rest, and enjoy its sabbaths.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Isaiah 24:5–6; Romans 8:22"> As long as it lieth desolate it shall have rest, even the rest which it had not in your sabbaths, when ye dwelt upon it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Ezekiel 21:7; Isaiah 30:17; Leviticus 26:17"> And as for them that are left of you, I will send a faintness into their heart in the lands of their enemies: and the sound of a driven leaf shall chase them; and they shall flee, as one fleeth from the sword; and they shall fall when none pursueth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Joshua 7:12–13; Judges 2:14; Jeremiah 37:10"> And they shall stumble one upon another, as it were before the sword, when none pursueth: and ye shall have no power to stand before your enemies.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Deuteronomy 28:48; Isaiah 27:13; Jeremiah 42:17–18"> And ye shall perish among the nations, and the land of your enemies shall eat you up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Ezekiel 4:17; Ezekiel 24:23; Ezekiel 33:10"> And they that are left of you shall pine away in their iniquity in your enemies’ lands; and also in the iniquities of their fathers shall they pine away with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Proverbs 28:13; Nehemiah 9:2–5; 1 John 1:8–10"> And they shall confess their iniquity, and the iniquity of their fathers, in their trespass which they trespassed against me, and also that, because they walked contrary unto me,</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:7; 2 Chronicles 32:26; 2 Chronicles 12:6–7"> I also walked contrary unto them, and brought them into the land of their enemies: if then their uncircumcised heart be humbled, and they then accept of the punishment of their iniquity;</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Psalms 106:45; Exodus 6:5; Ezekiel 16:60"> then will I remember my covenant with Jacob; and also my covenant with Isaac, and also my covenant with Abraham will I remember; and I will remember the land.</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Romans 8:7; Leviticus 26:15; John 7:7"> The land also shall be left by them, and shall enjoy its sabbaths, while it lieth desolate without them: and they shall accept of the punishment of their iniquity; because, even because they rejected mine ordinances, and their soul abhorred my statutes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v44" data-v="44" data-xref="Romans 11:2; Nehemiah 9:31; Deuteronomy 4:29–31"> And yet for all that, when they are in the land of their enemies, I will not reject them, neither will I abhor them, to destroy them utterly, and to break my covenant with them; for I am Jehovah their God;</span>
<span className="v" id="v45" data-v="45" data-xref="Leviticus 25:38; Ezekiel 20:22; Ezekiel 20:9"> but I will for their sakes remember the covenant of their ancestors, whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt in the sight of the nations, that I might be their God: I am Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v46" data-v="46" data-xref="Leviticus 27:34; Deuteronomy 12:1; Deuteronomy 6:1"> These are the statutes and ordinances and laws, which Jehovah made between him and the children of Israel in mount Sinai by Moses.</span>
