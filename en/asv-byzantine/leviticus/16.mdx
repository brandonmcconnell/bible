<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1–2"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses, after the death of the two sons of Aaron, when they drew near before Jehovah, and died;</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Exodus 30:10; 1 Kings 8:10–12; Hebrews 10:19–20"> and Jehovah said unto Moses, Speak unto Aaron thy brother, that he come not at all times into the holy place within the veil, before the mercy seat which is upon the ark; that he die not: for I will appear in the cloud upon the mercy seat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Leviticus 4:3; Hebrews 9:24–25; Hebrews 9:7"> Herewith shall Aaron come into the holy place: with a young bullock for a sin offering, and a ram for a burnt offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 28:39–43; Ezekiel 44:17–18; Exodus 30:20"> He shall put on the holy linen coat, and he shall have the linen breeches upon his flesh, and shall be girded with the linen girdle, and with the linen miter shall he be attired: they are the holy garments; and he shall bathe his flesh in water, and put them on.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="2 Chronicles 29:21; Leviticus 8:2; Ezra 6:17"> And he shall take of the congregation of the children of Israel two he-goats for a sin offering, and one ram for a burnt offering.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Leviticus 9:7; Hebrews 9:7; Hebrews 7:27"> And Aaron shall present the bullock of the sin offering, which is for himself, and make atonement for himself, and for his house.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Romans 12:1; Leviticus 4:4; Matthew 16:21"> And he shall take the two goats, and set them before Jehovah at the door of the tent of meeting.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Acts 1:23–26; Jonah 1:7; Proverbs 16:33"> And Aaron shall cast lots upon the two goats; one lot for Jehovah, and the other lot for Azazel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Acts 4:27–28; Acts 2:23"> And Aaron shall present the goat upon which the lot fell for Jehovah, and offer him for a sin offering.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="1 John 2:2; Leviticus 16:21–22; Hebrews 9:23–24"> But the goat, on which the lot fell for Azazel, shall be set alive before Jehovah, to make atonement for him, to send him away for Azazel into the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Leviticus 16:6; Hebrews 7:27; Hebrews 9:7"> And Aaron shall present the bullock of the sin offering, which is for himself, and shall make atonement for himself, and for his house, and shall kill the bullock of the sin offering which is for himself.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Leviticus 10:1; Exodus 30:34–38; Revelation 8:3–4"> And he shall take a censer full of coals of fire from off the altar before Jehovah, and his hands full of sweet incense beaten small, and bring it within the veil:</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 25:21; Exodus 30:1; Exodus 30:7–8"> and he shall put the incense upon the fire before Jehovah, that the cloud of the incense may cover the mercy seat that is upon the testimony, that he die not:</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Hebrews 9:25; Leviticus 4:5–6; Hebrews 9:13"> and he shall take of the blood of the bullock, and sprinkle it with his finger upon the mercy seat on the east; and before the mercy seat shall he sprinkle of the blood with his finger seven times.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Hebrews 9:7; Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 9:3"> Then shall he kill the goat of the sin offering, that is for the people, and bring his blood within the veil, and do with his blood as he did with the blood of the bullock, and sprinkle it upon the mercy seat, and before the mercy seat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Hebrews 9:22–23; Leviticus 16:18; Ezekiel 45:18–19"> and he shall make atonement for the holy place, because of the uncleannesses of the children of Israel, and because of their transgressions, even all their sins: and so shall he do for the tent of meeting, that dwelleth with them in the midst of their uncleannesses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; Luke 1:10; Exodus 34:3"> And there shall be no man in the tent of meeting when he goeth in to make atonement in the holy place, until he come out, and have made atonement for himself, and for his household, and for all the assembly of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Leviticus 4:7; Hebrews 5:7–8; Leviticus 16:16"> And he shall go out unto the altar that is before Jehovah, and make atonement for it, and shall take of the blood of the bullock, and of the blood of the goat, and put it upon the horns of the altar round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Zechariah 13:1; Ezekiel 43:18–22"> And he shall sprinkle of the blood upon it with his finger seven times, and cleanse it, and hallow it from the uncleannesses of the children of Israel.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Leviticus 16:16; Ezekiel 45:20; Colossians 1:20"> And when he hath made an end of atoning for the holy place, and the tent of meeting, and the altar, he shall present the live goat:</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Isaiah 53:6; 2 Corinthians 5:21; Ezra 10:1"> and Aaron shall lay both his hands upon the head of the live goat, and confess over him all the iniquities of the children of Israel, and all their transgressions, even all their sins; and he shall put them upon the head of the goat, and shall send him away by the hand of a man that is in readiness into the wilderness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Hebrews 9:28; Isaiah 53:11–12; John 1:29"> and the goat shall bear upon him all their iniquities unto a solitary land: and he shall let go the goat in the wilderness.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:19; Ezekiel 42:14; Leviticus 16:4"> And Aaron shall come into the tent of meeting, and shall put off the linen garments, which he put on when he went into the holy place, and shall leave them there:</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Leviticus 16:3–5; Hebrews 10:19–22; Exodus 28:4–14"> and he shall bathe his flesh in water in a holy place, and put on his garments, and come forth, and offer his burnt offering and the burnt offering of the people, and make atonement for himself and for the people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 29:13; Leviticus 4:8–10; Leviticus 16:6"> And the fat of the sin offering shall he burn upon the altar.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Leviticus 16:10; Hebrews 7:19; Numbers 19:21"> And he that letteth go the goat for Azazel shall wash his clothes, and bathe his flesh in water, and afterward he shall come into the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Leviticus 4:21; Leviticus 6:30; Leviticus 4:11–12"> And the bullock of the sin offering, and the goat of the sin offering, whose blood was brought in to make atonement in the holy place, shall be carried forth without the camp; and they shall burn in the fire their skins, and their flesh, and their dung.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And he that burneth them shall wash his clothes, and bathe his flesh in water, and afterward he shall come into the camp.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Isaiah 58:3; Numbers 29:7; Exodus 20:10"> And it shall be a statute forever unto you: in the seventh month, on the tenth day of the month, ye shall afflict your souls, and shall do no manner of work, the homeborn, or the stranger that sojourneth among you:</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:8; Psalms 51:2; Ephesians 5:26"> for on this day shall atonement be made for you, to cleanse you; from all your sins shall ye be clean before Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 23:32; Isaiah 58:5; Leviticus 25:4"> It is a sabbath of solemn rest unto you, and ye shall afflict your souls; it is a statute forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Leviticus 16:4; Exodus 29:29–30; Leviticus 4:5"> And the priest, who shall be anointed and who shall be consecrated to be priest in his father’s stead, shall make the atonement, and shall put on the linen garments, even the holy garments:</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Leviticus 16:16; Leviticus 16:24; Leviticus 16:6"> and he shall make atonement for the holy sanctuary; and he shall make atonement for the tent of meeting and for the altar; and he shall make atonement for the priests and for all the people of the assembly.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Hebrews 9:7; Hebrews 9:25; Exodus 30:10"> And this shall be an everlasting statute unto you, to make atonement for the children of Israel because of all their sins once in the year. And he did as Jehovah commanded Moses.</span>
</div>
