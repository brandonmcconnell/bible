<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 26:8; Jeremiah 51:63; Jeremiah 42:22"> And it came to pass that, when Jeremiah had made an end of speaking unto all the people all the words of Jehovah their God, wherewith Jehovah their God had sent him to them, even all these words,</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Chronicles 36:13; Isaiah 7:9; Jeremiah 40:8"> then spake Azariah the son of Hoshaiah, and Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the proud men, saying unto Jeremiah, Thou speakest falsely: Jehovah our God hath not sent thee to say, Ye shall not go into Egypt to sojourn there;</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Jeremiah 45:1–3; Jeremiah 36:4; Jeremiah 36:26"> but Baruch the son of Neriah setteth thee on against us, to deliver us into the hand of the Chaldeans, that they may put us to death, and carry us away captive to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:5–6; Psalms 37:3; 2 Chronicles 25:16"> So Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the captains of the forces, and all the people, obeyed not the voice of Jehovah, to dwell in the land of Judah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Jeremiah 40:11–12; Jeremiah 41:15–16; 1 Samuel 26:19"> But Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the captains of the forces, took all the remnant of Judah, that were returned from all the nations whither they had been driven, to sojourn in the land of Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 39:10; Jeremiah 40:7; Jeremiah 41:10"> the men, and the women, and the children, and the king’s daughters, and every person that Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard had left with Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, the son of Shaphan; and Jeremiah the prophet, and Baruch the son of Neriah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:16; Jeremiah 44:1; Isaiah 30:4"> And they came into the land of Egypt; for they obeyed not the voice of Jehovah: and they came unto Tahpanhes.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 2:16; Psalms 139:7; 2 Timothy 2:9"> Then came the word of Jehovah unto Jeremiah in Tahpanhes, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 18:2–12; Jeremiah 19:1–15; Jeremiah 51:63–64"> Take great stones in thy hand, and hide them in mortar in the brickwork, which is at the entry of Pharaoh’s house in Tahpanhes, in the sight of the men of Judah;</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 44:28–1; Jeremiah 1:15; Psalms 31:20"> and say unto them, Thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the God of Israel: Behold, I will send and take Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, my servant, and will set his throne upon these stones that I have hidden; and he shall spread his royal pavilion over them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:2; Jeremiah 44:13; Ezekiel 29:19–20"> And he shall come, and shall smite the land of Egypt; such as are for death shall be given to death, and such as are for captivity to captivity, and such as are for the sword to the sword.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Ezekiel 30:13; Psalms 109:18–19; Isaiah 19:1"> And I will kindle a fire in the houses of the gods of Egypt; and he shall burn them, and carry them away captive: and he shall array himself with the land of Egypt, as a shepherd putteth on his garment; and he shall go forth from thence in peace.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Isaiah 19:18"> He shall also break the pillars of Beth-shemesh, that is in the land of Egypt; and the houses of the gods of Egypt shall he burn with fire.</span>
