<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 29:2; 2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Kings 24:12–16"> Jehovah showed me, and, behold, two baskets of figs set before the temple of Jehovah, after that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had carried away captive Jeconiah the son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, and the princes of Judah, with the craftsmen and smiths, from Jerusalem, and had brought them to Babylon.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Isaiah 5:7; Isaiah 5:4; Micah 7:1"> One basket had very good figs, like the figs that are first-ripe; and the other basket had very bad figs, which could not be eaten, they were so bad.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Amos 8:2; Zechariah 5:5–11; Matthew 25:32–33"> Then said Jehovah unto me, What seest thou, Jeremiah? And I said, Figs; the good figs, very good; and the bad, very bad, that cannot be eaten, they are so bad.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4"> And the word of Jehovah came unto me, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Zechariah 13:9; Nahum 1:7; Galatians 4:9"> Thus saith Jehovah, the God of Israel: Like these good figs, so will I regard the captives of Judah, whom I have sent out of this place into the land of the Chaldeans, for good.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 42:10; Jeremiah 29:10; Jeremiah 33:7"> For I will set mine eyes upon them for good, and I will bring them again to this land: and I will build them, and not pull them down; and I will plant them, and not pluck them up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Hebrews 8:10; Jeremiah 30:22; Ezekiel 36:24–28"> And I will give them a heart to know me, that I am Jehovah: and they shall be my people, and I will be their God; for they shall return unto me with their whole heart.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Jeremiah 43:1; Jeremiah 39:2–9; Jeremiah 24:2"> And as the bad figs, which cannot be eaten, they are so bad, surely thus saith Jehovah, So will I give up Zedekiah the king of Judah, and his princes, and the residue of Jerusalem, that remain in this land, and them that dwell in the land of Egypt,</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Jeremiah 15:4; Jeremiah 29:18; Deuteronomy 28:37"> I will even give them up to be tossed to and fro among all the kingdoms of the earth for evil; to be a reproach and a proverb, a taunt and a curse, in all places whither I shall drive them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 51:19; Ezekiel 5:12–17; Jeremiah 16:4"> And I will send the sword, the famine, and the pestilence, among them, till they be consumed from off the land that I gave unto them and to their fathers.</span>
</div>
