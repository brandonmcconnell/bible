<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Jeremiah 30:22; Jeremiah 30:24; Zechariah 13:9"> At that time, saith Jehovah, will I be the God of all the families of Israel, and they shall be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Numbers 10:33; Psalms 95:11; Matthew 11:28"> Thus saith Jehovah, The people that were left of the sword found favor in the wilderness; even Israel, when I went to cause him to rest.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Psalms 103:17; Isaiah 54:8–9; Hosea 11:4"> Jehovah appeared of old unto me, saying, Yea, I have loved thee with an everlasting love: therefore with lovingkindness have I drawn thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:7; Jeremiah 31:13; Amos 5:2"> Again will I build thee, and thou shalt be built, O virgin of Israel: again shalt thou be adorned with thy tabrets, and shalt go forth in the dances of them that make merry.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Amos 9:14; Leviticus 19:23–25; Deuteronomy 28:30"> Again shalt thou plant vineyards upon the mountains of Samaria; the planters shall plant, and shall enjoy the fruit thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:4–5; Micah 4:1–3; Jeremiah 50:19"> For there shall be a day, that the watchmen upon the hills of Ephraim shall cry, Arise ye, and let us go up to Zion unto Jehovah our God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Psalms 14:7; Psalms 28:9; Isaiah 37:31"> For thus saith Jehovah, Sing with gladness for Jacob, and shout for the chief of the nations: publish ye, praise ye, and say, O Jehovah, save thy people, the remnant of Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Ezekiel 34:16; Isaiah 42:16; Jeremiah 23:8"> Behold, I will bring them from the north country, and gather them from the uttermost parts of the earth, and with them the blind and the lame, the woman with child and her that travaileth with child together: a great company shall they return hither.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Matthew 5:4; Jeremiah 3:4; Exodus 4:22"> They shall come with weeping; and with supplications will I lead them: I will cause them to walk by rivers of waters, in a straight way wherein they shall not stumble; for I am a father to Israel, and Ephraim is my firstborn.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Isaiah 40:11; Ezekiel 34:12; Deuteronomy 30:4"> Hear the word of Jehovah, O ye nations, and declare it in the isles afar off; and say, He that scattered Israel will gather him, and keep him, as a shepherd doth his flock.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Isaiah 48:20; Titus 2:14; Isaiah 44:23"> For Jehovah hath ransomed Jacob, and redeemed him from the hand of him that was stronger than he.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Isaiah 35:10; Isaiah 58:11; Joel 3:18"> And they shall come and sing in the height of Zion, and shall flow unto the goodness of Jehovah, to the grain, and to the new wine, and to the oil, and to the young of the flock and of the herd: and their soul shall be as a watered garden; and they shall not sorrow anymore at all.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Psalms 30:11; Isaiah 61:3; Isaiah 51:11"> Then shall the virgin rejoice in the dance, and the young men and the old together; for I will turn their mourning into joy, and will comfort them, and make them rejoice from their sorrow.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:25; 1 Peter 2:9; Jeremiah 33:9"> And I will satiate the soul of the priests with fatness, and my people shall be satisfied with my goodness, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Matthew 2:16–18; Jeremiah 40:1; Psalms 77:2"> Thus saith Jehovah: A voice is heard in Ramah, lamentation, and bitter weeping, Rachel weeping for her children; she refuseth to be comforted for her children, because they are not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Ruth 2:12; Jeremiah 30:3; Hebrews 6:10"> Thus saith Jehovah: Refrain thy voice from weeping, and thine eyes from tears; for thy work shall be rewarded, saith Jehovah; and they shall come again from the land of the enemy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Lamentations 3:21; Jeremiah 29:11–16; Lamentations 3:18"> And there is hope for thy latter end, saith Jehovah; and thy children shall come again to their own border.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Jeremiah 31:9; Psalms 80:3; Job 5:17"> I have surely heard Ephraim bemoaning himself thus, Thou hast chastised me, and I was chastised, as a calf unaccustomed to the yoke: turn thou me, and I shall be turned; for thou art Jehovah my God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Ezekiel 36:31; Jeremiah 3:25; Ezekiel 21:12"> Surely after that I was turned, I repented; and after that I was instructed, I smote upon my thigh: I was ashamed, yea, even confounded, because I did bear the reproach of my youth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Isaiah 55:7; Micah 7:18–19; Isaiah 63:15"> Is Ephraim my dear son? is he a darling child? for as often as I speak against him, I do earnestly remember him still: therefore my heart yearneth for him; I will surely have mercy upon him, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Jeremiah 50:5; Isaiah 62:10; Psalms 84:5"> Set thee up waymarks, make thee guideposts; set thy heart toward the highway, even the way by which thou wentest: turn again, O virgin of Israel, turn again to these thy cities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Jeremiah 49:4; Jeremiah 7:24; Matthew 1:21"> How long wilt thou go hither and thither, O thou backsliding daughter? for Jehovah hath created a new thing in the earth: A woman shall encompass a man.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Zechariah 8:3; Isaiah 1:26; Jeremiah 50:7"> Thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the God of Israel, Yet again shall they use this speech in the land of Judah and in the cities thereof, when I shall bring again their captivity: Jehovah bless thee, O habitation of righteousness, O mountain of holiness.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Zechariah 8:4–8; Ezekiel 36:10; Zechariah 2:4"> And Judah and all the cities thereof shall dwell therein together, the husbandmen, and they that go about with flocks.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Matthew 5:6; Matthew 11:28; John 4:14"> For I have satiated the weary soul, and every sorrowful soul have I replenished.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Psalms 127:2; Zechariah 4:1–2"> Upon this I awoke, and beheld; and my sleep was sweet unto me.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Hosea 2:23; Zechariah 10:9; Ezekiel 36:9"> Behold, the days come, saith Jehovah, that I will sow the house of Israel and the house of Judah with the seed of man, and with the seed of beast.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Jeremiah 24:6; Jeremiah 1:10; Jeremiah 18:7–9"> And it shall come to pass that, like as I have watched over them to pluck up and to break down and to overthrow and to destroy and to afflict, so will I watch over them to build and to plant, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Lamentations 5:7; Ezekiel 18:2–3; Jeremiah 31:30"> In those days they shall say no more, The fathers have eaten sour grapes, and the children’s teeth are set on edge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Ezekiel 18:20; Isaiah 3:11; Deuteronomy 24:16"> But everyone shall die for his own iniquity: every man that eateth the sour grapes, his teeth shall be set on edge.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Hebrews 10:16–17; Ezekiel 37:26; Jeremiah 31:31–34"> Behold, the days come, saith Jehovah, that I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel, and with the house of Judah:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 24:6–8; Deuteronomy 5:3; Exodus 19:5"> not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers in the day that I took them by the hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt; which my covenant they brake, although I was a husband unto them, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="2 Corinthians 3:3; Hebrews 10:16; Hebrews 8:10"> But this is the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel after those days, saith Jehovah: I will put my law in their inward parts, and in their heart will I write it; and I will be their God, and they shall be my people.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Isaiah 43:25; Hebrews 8:12; 1 John 2:27"> And they shall teach no more every man his neighbor, and every man his brother, saying, Know Jehovah; for they shall all know me, from the least of them unto the greatest of them, saith Jehovah: for I will forgive their iniquity, and their sin will I remember no more.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Jeremiah 10:16; Isaiah 51:15; Psalms 136:7–9"> Thus saith Jehovah, who giveth the sun for a light by day, and the ordinances of the moon and of the stars for a light by night, who stirreth up the sea, so that the waves thereof roar; Jehovah of hosts is his name:</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Isaiah 54:9–10; Jeremiah 33:20–26; Psalms 89:36–37"> If these ordinances depart from before me, saith Jehovah, then the seed of Israel also shall cease from being a nation before me forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Jeremiah 33:24–26; Isaiah 40:12; Jeremiah 33:22"> Thus saith Jehovah: If heaven above can be measured, and the foundations of the earth searched out beneath, then will I also cast off all the seed of Israel for all that they have done, saith Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="Zechariah 14:10; 2 Kings 14:13; Jeremiah 30:18"> Behold, the days come, saith Jehovah, that the city shall be built to Jehovah from the tower of Hananel unto the gate of the corner.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Zechariah 2:1–2; Ezekiel 40:8"> And the measuring line shall go out further straight onward unto the hill Gareb, and shall turn about unto Goah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="2 Samuel 15:23; Joel 3:17; 2 Kings 11:16"> And the whole valley of the dead bodies and of the ashes, and all the fields unto the brook Kidron, unto the corner of the horse gate toward the east, shall be holy unto Jehovah; it shall not be plucked up, nor thrown down anymore forever.</span>
