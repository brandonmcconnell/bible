<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Then Mordecai said, These things are of God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> For I remember the dream which I saw concerning these matters, and nothing thereof hath failed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> As for the little fountain that became a river, and there was light, and the sun, and much water, the river is Esther, whom the king married, and made queen:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and the two dragons are I and Haman:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and the nations are those that were assembled to destroy the name of the Jews:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> and my nation, this is Israel, which cried to God, and were saved: for the Lord hath saved his people, and the Lord hath delivered us from all these evils, and God hath wrought signs and great wonders, which have not been done among the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Therefore hath he made two lots, one for the people of God, and another for all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> And these two lots came at the hour, and time, and day of judgment, before God among all the nations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> So God remembered his people, and justified his inheritance.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> Therefore these days shall be unto them in the month Adar, the fourteenth and fifteenth day of the month, with an assembly, and joy, and with gladness before God, throughout the generations forever among his people Israel.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3"> In the fourth year of the reign of Ptolemy and Cleopatra, Dositheus, who said he was a priest and Levite, and Ptolemy his son, brought the epistle of Purim here set forth, which they said was the same, and that Lysimachus the son of Ptolemy, that was in Jerusalem, had interpreted it.</span>
