<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 33:10–12; Exodus 15:27; Exodus 17:1"> And they took their journey from Elim, and all the congregation of the children of Israel came unto the wilderness of Sin, which is between Elim and Sinai, on the fifteenth day of the second month after their departing out of the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:10; Exodus 15:24; Psalms 106:25"> And the whole congregation of the children of Israel murmured against Moses and against Aaron in the wilderness:</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Numbers 11:4–5; Numbers 20:3–5; Exodus 17:3"> and the children of Israel said unto them, Would that we had died by the hand of Jehovah in the land of Egypt, when we sat by the fleshpots, when we did eat bread to the full; for ye have brought us forth into this wilderness, to kill this whole assembly with hunger.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="1 Corinthians 10:3; Psalms 78:24–25; Deuteronomy 8:2"> Then said Jehovah unto Moses, Behold, I will rain bread from heaven for you; and the people shall go out and gather a day’s portion every day, that I may prove them, whether they will walk in my law, or not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 16:22–23; Exodus 35:2–3; Leviticus 25:21–22"> And it shall come to pass on the sixth day, that they shall prepare that which they bring in, and it shall be twice as much as they gather daily.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 6:7; Numbers 16:28; Exodus 16:12–13"> And Moses and Aaron said unto all the children of Israel, At even, then ye shall know that Jehovah hath brought you out from the land of Egypt;</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Isaiah 40:5; Numbers 16:11; John 11:40"> and in the morning, then ye shall see the glory of Jehovah; for that he heareth your murmurings against Jehovah: and what are we, that ye murmur against us?</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Romans 13:2; 1 Samuel 8:7; 1 Thessalonians 4:8"> And Moses said, This shall be, when Jehovah shall give you in the evening flesh to eat, and in the morning bread to the full; for that Jehovah heareth your murmurings which ye murmur against him: and what are we? your murmurings are not against us, but against Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Numbers 16:16; Exodus 16:2; Exodus 16:8"> And Moses said unto Aaron, Say unto all the congregation of the children of Israel, Come near before Jehovah; for he hath heard your murmurings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 16:7; Numbers 16:19; Numbers 16:42"> And it came to pass, as Aaron spake unto the whole congregation of the children of Israel, that they looked toward the wilderness, and, behold, the glory of Jehovah appeared in the cloud.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11"> And Jehovah spake unto Moses, saying,</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Joel 3:17; Ezekiel 39:22; Exodus 6:7"> I have heard the murmurings of the children of Israel: speak unto them, saying, At even ye shall eat flesh, and in the morning ye shall be filled with bread: and ye shall know that I am Jehovah your God.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Numbers 11:9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:27–28"> And it came to pass at even, that the quails came up, and covered the camp: and in the morning the dew lay round about the camp.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Numbers 11:7–9; Psalms 105:40; Psalms 78:24"> And when the dew that lay was gone up, behold, upon the face of the wilderness a small round thing, small as the hoarfrost on the ground.</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 16:4; John 6:58; Exodus 16:31"> And when the children of Israel saw it, they said one to another, What is it? for they knew not what it was. And Moses said unto them, It is the bread which Jehovah hath given you to eat.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="Exodus 16:36; Exodus 16:18; Exodus 16:32–33"> This is the thing which Jehovah hath commanded, Gather ye of it every man according to his eating; an omer a head, according to the number of your persons, shall ye take it, every man for them that are in his tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17"> And the children of Israel did so, and gathered some more, some less.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Corinthians 8:14–15"> And when they measured it with an omer, he that gathered much had nothing over, and he that gathered little had no lack; they gathered every man according to his eating.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 12:10; Exodus 23:18; Matthew 6:34"> And Moses said unto them, Let no man leave of it till the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Mark 10:14; Numbers 16:15; Ephesians 4:26"> Notwithstanding they hearkened not unto Moses; but some of them left of it until the morning, and it bred worms, and became foul: and Moses was wroth with them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Proverbs 6:6–11; Ecclesiastes 12:1; 2 Corinthians 6:2"> And they gathered it morning by morning, every man according to his eating: and when the sun waxed hot, it melted.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="Exodus 16:5; Leviticus 25:22; Exodus 16:16"> And it came to pass, that on the sixth day they gathered twice as much bread, two omers for each one: and all the rulers of the congregation came and told Moses.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Leviticus 23:3; Exodus 31:15; Exodus 20:8–11"> And he said unto them, This is that which Jehovah hath spoken, Tomorrow is a solemn rest, a holy sabbath unto Jehovah: bake that which ye will bake, and boil that which ye will boil; and all that remaineth over lay up for you to be kept until the morning.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 16:20; Exodus 16:33"> And they laid it up till the morning, as Moses bade: and it did not become foul, neither was there any worm therein.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 16:23; Exodus 16:29; Nehemiah 9:14"> And Moses said, Eat that today; for today is a sabbath unto Jehovah: today ye shall not find it in the field.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:13; Exodus 20:9–11; Ezekiel 46:1"> Six days ye shall gather it; but on the seventh day is the sabbath, in it there shall be none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27" data-xref="Proverbs 20:4"> And it came to pass on the seventh day, that there went out some of the people to gather, and they found none.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Psalms 78:10; Psalms 106:13; Numbers 14:11"> And Jehovah said unto Moses, How long refuse ye to keep my commandments and my laws?</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 31:13; Isaiah 58:13–14; Ezekiel 20:12"> See, for that Jehovah hath given you the sabbath, therefore he giveth you on the sixth day the bread of two days; abide ye every man in his place, let no man go out of his place on the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Deuteronomy 5:12–14; Leviticus 23:3; Hebrews 4:9"> So the people rested on the seventh day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Song of Solomon 2:3; Numbers 11:6–9; Exodus 16:15"> And the house of Israel called the name thereof Manna: and it was like coriander seed, white; and the taste of it was like wafers made with honey.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Psalms 105:5; Luke 22:19; Psalms 111:4–5"> And Moses said, This is the thing which Jehovah hath commanded, Let an omerful of it be kept throughout your generations, that they may see the bread wherewith I fed you in the wilderness, when I brought you forth from the land of Egypt.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:4"> And Moses said unto Aaron, Take a pot, and put an omerful of manna therein, and lay it up before Jehovah, to be kept throughout your generations.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Exodus 25:21; Exodus 25:16"> As Jehovah commanded Moses, so Aaron laid it up before the Testimony, to be kept.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Nehemiah 9:20–21; Joshua 5:12; Deuteronomy 8:2–3"> And the children of Israel did eat the manna forty years, until they came to a land inhabited; they did eat the manna, until they came unto the borders of the land of Canaan.</span>
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 16:16; Exodus 16:32–33"> Now an omer is the tenth part of an ephah.</span>
