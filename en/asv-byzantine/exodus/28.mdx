<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Numbers 18:7; Hebrews 5:1–5; Numbers 17:2–9"> And bring thou near unto thee Aaron thy brother, and his sons with him, from among the children of Israel, that he may minister unto me in the priest’s office, even Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron’s sons.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="Leviticus 8:30; Exodus 31:10; Revelation 19:8"> And thou shalt make holy garments for Aaron thy brother, for glory and for beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Ephesians 1:17; 1 Corinthians 12:7–11; Isaiah 11:2"> And thou shalt speak unto all that are wisehearted, whom I have filled with the spirit of wisdom, that they make Aaron’s garments to sanctify him, that he may minister unto me in the priest’s office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Ephesians 6:14; Revelation 9:17; Isaiah 59:17"> And these are the garments which they shall make: a breastplate, and an ephod, and a robe, and a coat of checker work, a miter, and a girdle: and they shall make holy garments for Aaron thy brother, and his sons, that he may minister unto me in the priest’s office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Exodus 25:3–4; Exodus 39:2–3"> And they shall take the gold, and the blue, and the purple, and the scarlet, and the fine linen.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 39:2–7; Exodus 26:1"> And they shall make the ephod of gold, of blue, and purple, scarlet, and fine twined linen, the work of the skillful workman.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 39:4"> It shall have two shoulder pieces joined to the two ends thereof, that it may be joined together.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 28:27–28; Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 29:5"> And the skillfully woven band, which is upon it, wherewith to gird it on, shall be like the work thereof and of the same piece; of gold, of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; 2 Chronicles 2:7; Isaiah 49:16"> And thou shalt take two onyx stones, and grave on them the names of the children of Israel:</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="Exodus 1:1–4; Genesis 43:33"> six of their names on the one stone, and the names of the six that remain on the other stone, according to their birth.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Timothy 2:19; Revelation 7:2; Jeremiah 22:24"> With the work of an engraver in stone, like the engravings of a signet, shalt thou engrave the two stones, according to the names of the children of Israel: thou shalt make them to be enclosed in settings of gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="Exodus 28:29; Joshua 4:7; Exodus 39:6–7"> And thou shalt put the two stones upon the shoulder pieces of the ephod, to be stones of memorial for the children of Israel: and Aaron shall bear their names before Jehovah upon his two shoulders for a memorial.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13"> And thou shalt make settings of gold,</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 39:15; Exodus 39:17–18; 1 Kings 7:17"> and two chains of pure gold; like cords shalt thou make them, of wreathen work: and thou shalt put the wreathen chains on the settings.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 28:30; Exodus 26:1; Exodus 28:4"> And thou shalt make a breastplate of judgment, the work of the skillful workman; like the work of the ephod thou shalt make it; of gold, of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen, shalt thou make it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Foursquare it shall be and double; a span shall be the length thereof, and a span the breadth thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Ezekiel 28:13; Exodus 39:10–21; Proverbs 8:11"> And thou shalt set in it settings of stones, four rows of stones: a row of sardius, topaz, and carbuncle shall be the first row;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="Ezekiel 27:16; Job 28:6; Song of Solomon 5:14"> and the second row an emerald, a sapphire, and a diamond;</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Isaiah 54:12; Exodus 39:12"> and the third row a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst;</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ezekiel 1:16; Revelation 4:3; Daniel 10:6"> and the fourth row a beryl, and an onyx, and a jasper: they shall be enclosed in gold in their settings.</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="Revelation 21:12; Revelation 7:4–8; 1 Kings 18:31"> And the stones shall be according to the names of the children of Israel, twelve, according to their names; like the engravings of a signet, every one according to his name, they shall be for the twelve tribes.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And thou shalt make upon the breastplate chains like cords, of wreathen work of pure gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="Exodus 25:11–15"> And thou shalt make upon the breastplate two rings of gold, and shalt put the two rings on the two ends of the breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24"> And thou shalt put the two wreathen chains of gold in the two rings at the ends of the breastplate.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="Exodus 39:15; Exodus 39:4; Exodus 28:14"> And the other two ends of the two wreathen chains thou shalt put on the two settings, and put them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod in the forepart thereof.</span>
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26"> And thou shalt make two rings of gold, and thou shalt put them upon the two ends of the breastplate, upon the edge thereof, which is toward the side of the ephod inward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> And thou shalt make two rings of gold, and shalt put them on the two shoulder pieces of the ephod underneath, in the forepart thereof, close by the coupling thereof, above the skillfully woven band of the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28" data-xref="Numbers 15:38; Exodus 28:31; Exodus 39:30–31"> And they shall bind the breastplate by the rings thereof unto the rings of the ephod with a lace of blue, that it may be upon the skillfully woven band of the ephod, and that the breastplate be not loosed from the ephod.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 28:12; Exodus 28:30; Jeremiah 30:21"> And Aaron shall bear the names of the children of Israel in the breastplate of judgment upon his heart, when he goeth in unto the holy place, for a memorial before Jehovah continually.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Nehemiah 7:65; Ezra 2:63; Numbers 27:21"> And thou shalt put in the breastplate of judgment the Urim and the Thummim; and they shall be upon Aaron’s heart, when he goeth in before Jehovah: and Aaron shall bear the judgment of the children of Israel upon his heart before Jehovah continually.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Leviticus 8:7; Exodus 28:4; Exodus 39:22–26"> And thou shalt make the robe of the ephod all of blue.</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Nehemiah 4:16; John 19:23–24; Exodus 39:28"> And it shall have a hole for the head in the midst thereof: it shall have a binding of woven work round about the hole of it, as it were the hole of a coat of mail, that it be not rent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Exodus 39:24–26; Zechariah 14:20; 1 Kings 7:18"> And upon the skirts of it thou shalt make pomegranates of blue, and of purple, and of scarlet, round about the skirts thereof; and bells of gold between them round about:</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Song of Solomon 8:2; John 15:4–8; Colossians 1:10"> a golden bell and a pomegranate, a golden bell and a pomegranate, upon the skirts of the robe round about.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Hebrews 9:12; Leviticus 16:2"> And it shall be upon Aaron to minister: and the sound thereof shall be heard when he goeth in unto the holy place before Jehovah, and when he cometh out, that he die not.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Zechariah 14:20; Leviticus 8:9; Exodus 39:30"> And thou shalt make a plate of pure gold, and grave upon it, like the engravings of a signet, HOLY TO JEHOVAH.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 29:6; Exodus 28:4; Zechariah 3:5"> And thou shalt put it on a lace of blue, and it shall be upon the miter; upon the forefront of the miter it shall be.</span>
<span className="v" id="v38" data-v="38" data-xref="1 Peter 2:24; Numbers 18:1; Leviticus 10:17"> And it shall be upon Aaron’s forehead, and Aaron shall bear the iniquity of the holy things, which the children of Israel shall hallow in all their holy gifts; and it shall be always upon his forehead, that they may be accepted before Jehovah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v39" data-v="39" data-xref="Psalms 45:14"> And thou shalt weave the coat in checker work of fine linen, and thou shalt make a miter of fine linen, and thou shalt make a girdle, the work of the embroiderer.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v40" data-v="40" data-xref="Exodus 39:41; Exodus 28:4; Exodus 28:2"> And for Aaron’s sons thou shalt make coats, and thou shalt make for them girdles, and headtires shalt thou make for them, for glory and for beauty.</span>
<span className="v" id="v41" data-v="41" data-xref="Exodus 29:7; Leviticus 8:1–36; Exodus 40:15"> And thou shalt put them upon Aaron thy brother, and upon his sons with him, and shalt anoint them, and consecrate them, and sanctify them, that they may minister unto me in the priest’s office.</span>
<span className="v" id="v42" data-v="42" data-xref="Ezekiel 44:18; Leviticus 6:10; Leviticus 16:4"> And thou shalt make them linen breeches to cover the flesh of their nakedness; from the loins even unto the thighs they shall reach:</span>
<span className="v" id="v43" data-v="43" data-xref="Exodus 27:21; Exodus 20:26; Leviticus 5:17"> and they shall be upon Aaron, and upon his sons, when they go in unto the tent of meeting, or when they come near unto the altar to minister in the holy place; that they bear not iniquity, and die: it shall be a statute forever unto him and unto his seed after him.</span>
</div>
