<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="Exodus 36:8–19; Exodus 26:36; Exodus 26:31"> Moreover thou shalt make the tabernacle with ten curtains; of fine twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet, with cherubim the work of the skillful workman shalt thou make them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Samuel 7:2; Numbers 4:25; 1 Chronicles 17:1"> The length of each curtain shall be eight and twenty cubits, and the breadth of each curtain four cubits: all the curtains shall have one measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="Exodus 36:10; Colossians 2:2; Ephesians 2:21–22"> Five curtains shall be coupled together one to another; and the other five curtains shall be coupled one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="Exodus 36:11–12; Exodus 36:17; Exodus 26:10–11"> And thou shalt make loops of blue upon the edge of the one curtain from the selvage in the coupling; and likewise shalt thou make in the edge of the curtain that is outmost in the second coupling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5"> Fifty loops shalt thou make in the one curtain, and fifty loops shalt thou make in the edge of the curtain that is in the second coupling; the loops shall be opposite one to another.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="Exodus 36:13; Exodus 26:11; Exodus 26:33"> And thou shalt make fifty clasps of gold, and couple the curtains one to another with the clasps: and the tabernacle shall be one whole.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Exodus 26:14; Psalms 45:13; Exodus 26:9"> And thou shalt make curtains of goats’ hair for a tent over the tabernacle: eleven curtains shalt thou make them.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Exodus 26:13; Exodus 26:2"> The length of each curtain shall be thirty cubits, and the breadth of each curtain four cubits: the eleven curtains shall have one measure.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9"> And thou shalt couple five curtains by themselves, and six curtains by themselves, and shalt double over the sixth curtain in the forefront of the tent.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10"> And thou shalt make fifty loops on the edge of the one curtain that is outmost in the coupling, and fifty loops upon the edge of the curtain which is outmost in the second coupling.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="Exodus 26:3; Exodus 26:6"> And thou shalt make fifty clasps of brass, and put the clasps into the loops, and couple the tent together, that it may be one.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12"> And the overhanging part that remaineth of the curtains of the tent, the half curtain that remaineth, shall hang over the back of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="Exodus 26:2; Exodus 26:8"> And the cubit on the one side, and the cubit on the other side, of that which remaineth in the length of the curtains of the tent, shall hang over the sides of the tabernacle on this side and on that side, to cover it.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Exodus 36:19; Exodus 25:5; Exodus 35:23"> And thou shalt make a covering for the tent of rams’ skins dyed red, and a covering of sealskins above.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Exodus 36:20–33; Numbers 4:31–32; Exodus 40:17–18"> And thou shalt make the boards for the tabernacle of acacia wood, standing up.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16"> Ten cubits shall be the length of a board, and a cubit and a half the breadth of each board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="Exodus 36:22; Exodus 26:19; Exodus 36:24"> Two tenons shall there be in each board, joined one to another: thus shalt thou make for all the boards of the tabernacle.</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18"> And thou shalt make the boards for the tabernacle, twenty boards for the south side southward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="Exodus 38:27; Exodus 38:30–31; Exodus 26:37"> And thou shalt make forty sockets of silver under the twenty boards; two sockets under one board for its two tenons, and two sockets under another board for its two tenons:</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20"> and for the second side of the tabernacle, on the north side, twenty boards,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21"> and their forty sockets of silver; two sockets under one board, and two sockets under another board.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22"> And for the hinder part of the tabernacle westward thou shalt make six boards.</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23"> And two boards shalt thou make for the corners of the tabernacle in the hinder part.</span>
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="Exodus 36:29–30; Psalms 133:1–3; 1 Corinthians 1:10"> And they shall be double beneath, and in like manner they shall be entire unto the top thereof unto one ring: thus shall it be for them both; they shall be for the two corners.</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25"> And there shall be eight boards, and their sockets of silver, sixteen sockets; two sockets under one board, and two sockets under another board.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v26" data-v="26" data-xref="Romans 15:1; Numbers 4:31; Galatians 6:1–2"> And thou shalt make bars of acacia wood: five for the boards of the one side of the tabernacle,</span>
<span className="v" id="v27" data-v="27"> and five bars for the boards of the other side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the boards of the side of the tabernacle, for the hinder part westward.</span>
<span className="v" id="v28" data-v="28"> And the middle bar in the midst of the boards shall pass through from end to end.</span>
<span className="v" id="v29" data-v="29" data-xref="Exodus 25:11–12"> And thou shalt overlay the boards with gold, and make their rings of gold for places for the bars: and thou shalt overlay the bars with gold.</span>
<span className="v" id="v30" data-v="30" data-xref="Exodus 25:40; Hebrews 8:5; Exodus 25:9"> And thou shalt rear up the tabernacle according to the fashion thereof which hath been shown thee in the mount.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v31" data-v="31" data-xref="Matthew 27:51; 2 Chronicles 3:14; Exodus 36:35"> And thou shalt make a veil of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen: with cherubim the work of the skillful workman shall it be made:</span>
<span className="v" id="v32" data-v="32" data-xref="Exodus 26:37; Exodus 36:38; Esther 1:6"> and thou shalt hang it upon four pillars of acacia overlaid with gold; their hooks shall be of gold, upon four sockets of silver.</span>
<span className="v" id="v33" data-v="33" data-xref="Hebrews 9:2–3; Leviticus 16:2; Exodus 40:21"> And thou shalt hang up the veil under the clasps, and shalt bring in thither within the veil the ark of the testimony: and the veil shall separate unto you between the holy place and the most holy.</span>
<span className="v" id="v34" data-v="34" data-xref="Exodus 40:20; Exodus 25:21; Hebrews 9:5"> And thou shalt put the mercy seat upon the ark of the testimony in the most holy place.</span>
<span className="v" id="v35" data-v="35" data-xref="Exodus 40:24; Exodus 40:22; Hebrews 9:2"> And thou shalt set the table without the veil, and the candlestick over against the table on the side of the tabernacle toward the south: and thou shalt put the table on the north side.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v36" data-v="36" data-xref="Exodus 36:37; Numbers 9:15; Exodus 26:31"> And thou shalt make a screen for the door of the Tent, of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen, the work of the embroiderer.</span>
<span className="v" id="v37" data-v="37" data-xref="Exodus 36:38"> And thou shalt make for the screen five pillars of acacia, and overlay them with gold: their hooks shall be of gold: and thou shalt cast five sockets of brass for them.</span>
</div>
