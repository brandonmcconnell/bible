<div className="bt">
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v1" data-v="1" data-xref="1 Peter 1:5; 1 Thessalonians 5:23; 1 Peter 2:9"> Jude, a servant of Jesus Christ, and brother of James, to them that are called, sanctified by God the Father, and kept for Jesus Christ:</span>
<span className="v" id="v2" data-v="2" data-xref="2 Peter 1:2; 1 Peter 1:2; Revelation 1:4–6"> Mercy unto you and peace and love be multiplied.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v3" data-v="3" data-xref="1 Timothy 6:12; Philippians 1:27; Jude 1:20"> Beloved, while I was giving all diligence to write unto you of the common salvation, I was constrained to write unto you exhorting you to contend earnestly for the faith which was once for all delivered unto the saints.</span>
<span className="v" id="v4" data-v="4" data-xref="2 Peter 2:1–3; Galatians 2:4; 2 Peter 2:10"> For there are certain men crept in privily, even they who were of old written of beforehand unto this condemnation, ungodly men, turning the grace of our God into lasciviousness, and denying our only Master, God, and Lord, Jesus Christ.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v5" data-v="5" data-xref="Numbers 26:64–65; Hebrews 3:16–2; 1 Corinthians 10:1–12"> Now I desire to put you in remembrance, though ye know this once for all, that the Lord, having saved a people out of the land of Egypt, afterward destroyed them that believed not.</span>
<span className="v" id="v6" data-v="6" data-xref="2 Peter 2:4; Matthew 25:41; Ephesians 6:12"> And angels that kept not their own principality, but left their proper habitation, he hath kept in everlasting bonds under darkness unto the judgment of the great day.</span>
<span className="v" id="v7" data-v="7" data-xref="Deuteronomy 29:23; 2 Peter 2:6; Romans 1:26–27"> Even as Sodom and Gomorrah, and the cities about them, having in like manner with these given themselves over to fornication and gone after strange flesh, are set forth as an example, suffering the punishment of eternal fire.</span>
<span className="v" id="v8" data-v="8" data-xref="Hebrews 13:17; 1 Peter 2:17; 2 Peter 2:10–12"> Yet in like manner these also in their dreamings defile the flesh, and set at nought dominion, and rail at dignities.</span>
<span className="v" id="v9" data-v="9" data-xref="Zechariah 3:2; 2 Peter 2:11; Deuteronomy 34:6"> But Michael the archangel, when contending with the devil he disputed about the body of Moses, durst not bring against him a railing judgment, but said, The Lord rebuke thee.</span>
<span className="v" id="v10" data-v="10" data-xref="2 Peter 2:12; Romans 1:21–22"> But these rail at whatsoever things they know not: and what they understand naturally, like the creatures without reason, in these things are they destroyed.</span>
<span className="v" id="v11" data-v="11" data-xref="2 Peter 2:15; 1 John 3:12; Revelation 2:14"> Woe unto them! for they went in the way of Cain, and ran riotously in the error of Balaam for hire, and perished in the gainsaying of Korah.</span>
<span className="v" id="v12" data-v="12" data-xref="2 Peter 2:17–20; Matthew 15:13; Ezekiel 34:8"> These are hidden rocks in your love feasts when they feast with you, shepherds that without fear feed themselves; clouds without water, carried along by winds; autumn trees without fruit, twice dead, plucked up by the roots;</span>
<span className="v" id="v13" data-v="13" data-xref="2 Peter 2:17; Philippians 3:19; Isaiah 57:20"> wild waves of the sea, foaming out their own shame; wandering stars, for whom the blackness of darkness hath been reserved forever.</span>
<span className="v" id="v14" data-v="14" data-xref="Deuteronomy 33:2; Psalms 50:3–5; Hebrews 11:5–6"> And to these also Enoch, the seventh from Adam, prophesied, saying, Behold, the Lord came with ten thousands of his holy ones,</span>
<span className="v" id="v15" data-v="15" data-xref="Romans 2:16; Ecclesiastes 11:9; 1 Corinthians 4:5"> to execute judgment upon all, and to convict all their ungodly ones of all their works of ungodliness which they have ungodly wrought, and of all the hard things which ungodly sinners have spoken against him.</span>
<span className="v" id="v16" data-v="16" data-xref="2 Peter 2:18; 2 Peter 2:10; Philippians 2:14"> These are murmurers, complainers, walking after their lusts (and their mouth speaketh great swelling words), showing respect of persons for the sake of advantage.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v17" data-v="17" data-xref="2 Peter 3:2; 1 John 4:6; Ephesians 4:11"> But ye, beloved, remember ye the words which have been spoken before by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ;</span>
<span className="v" id="v18" data-v="18" data-xref="2 Peter 3:3; 2 Timothy 4:3; 2 Timothy 3:1–5"> that they said to you that in the last time there should be mockers, walking after their own ungodly lusts.</span>
<span className="v" id="v19" data-v="19" data-xref="1 Corinthians 2:14; Proverbs 18:1; Romans 8:9"> These are they who make separations, sensual, having not the Spirit.</span>
<span className="v" id="v20" data-v="20" data-xref="Ephesians 6:18; Romans 8:26–27; 1 Corinthians 14:15"> But ye, beloved, building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Spirit,</span>
<span className="v" id="v21" data-v="21" data-xref="1 John 4:16; Lamentations 3:25–26; Hebrews 9:28"> keep yourselves in the love of God, looking for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal life.</span>
<span className="v" id="v22" data-v="22" data-xref="James 5:19–20; Galatians 6:1; 1 John 5:16–18"> And on some have mercy, making a difference;</span>
<span className="v" id="v23" data-v="23" data-xref="1 Timothy 4:16; 1 Corinthians 15:33; Revelation 3:4"> and others save with fear, snatching them out of the fire; hating even the garment spotted by the flesh.</span>
</div>
<div className="bp">
<span className="v" id="v24" data-v="24" data-xref="2 Timothy 4:18; Ephesians 3:20; Colossians 1:22"> Now unto him that is able to guard them from stumbling, and to set them before the presence of his glory without blemish in exceeding joy,</span>
<span className="v" id="v25" data-v="25" data-xref="1 Timothy 1:17; Revelation 5:13–14; 1 Peter 5:10–11"> to the only wise God our Savior, be glory and majesty, dominion and power, both now and forevermore. Amen.</span>
</div>
</div>
